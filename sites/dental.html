Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask our 24/7 assistant, bottom right.

Do you take new patients?

Yes. New patients get a free consultation and treatment plan on the first visit.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call us or book an "Emergency visit". We keep slots open every day, including Sunday mornings.

Can I pay in instalments?

For treatments over $500 we offer 0% instalments over 6 or 12 months.

Is whitening safe for sensitive teeth?

Usually yes. We check your enamel first and use a low-sensitivity gel when needed.

Do you treat children?

Yes, from age 3. Lena runs kid-friendly check-ups with a short, playful first visit.
