Quick answers

Who performs the treatments?

Services are performed by board-certified providers or under clinical oversight. The practice is led by Novia, a board-certified Nurse Practitioner.

Do you serve Southwest Ranches as well as Pembroke Pines?

Yes, the site describes the clinic as serving Pembroke Pines and Southwest Ranches.

How long do dermal fillers last?

The site says reversible results that last 12 to 18 months. Your own plan is set at the consultation.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, there is a financing application with a soft-pull check that doesn't affect your credit score.

Is there a first-visit offer?

The Exclusive Offers page mentions a first-time client special when you reserve a consultation. Ask the team for current terms.

Do you offer a weight loss program?

Yes, a physician-supervised medical weight loss program with personalized plans, approved treatments and expert guidance.

Is body sculpting surgical?

No, it is described as non-surgical, using EMS sculpting and PCDC treatments with zero downtime.

What does the infrared sauna do?

The site describes full-body detoxification, muscle recovery and improved circulation, with deeper penetration than a traditional sauna.

Is there a gift option?

Yes, wellness gift cards are sold on the Be Env website.

Do you do hair removal?

Laser Hair Removal is listed for the face, body and bikini area.

Is the DPN removal for every skin type?

The site says DPN removal is tailored to your skin type, using safe, minimally invasive methods. Novia confirms what is suitable for you.

Do you do peptide therapy?

Yes. The board-certified NP designs custom protocols based on your lab work and wellness goals.

What are your hours?

They aren't listed on the website. Call 954-887-3368 to confirm.
