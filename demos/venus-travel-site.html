Our story

Three generations of trips

In December 1968 Vincenzo Conenna opened Venus Travel Agency on Belmont Avenue, in one of Chicago's oldest Italian neighborhoods. In 1977 his son John F. Conenna joined him to build the agency and keep the tradition alive.

Region notes

What the agency says about each place

Short notes taken from the destination pages of venustravel.com.

Florida

Hundreds of miles of coastline and activities for any type of vacation. Venus Travel can advise which part of Florida best suits your needs. Disney World in Orlando has the Magic Kingdom, Epcot, Animal Kingdom and Hollywood Studios, with resorts, villas, campgrounds, hotels and beach resorts.

Caribbean

A peaceful atmosphere with endless sunshine and effortless activities: beach, sports, golf, adventure, diving, spas or water sports, to fit every taste and budget. Cruises are available to most destinations.

Hawaii

Volcanoes, family adventure, romance, whale watching, golf and horseback riding. Kauai is a romantic and adventurous getaway, Oahu has Pearl Harbor, Molokai has the largest white sand beach in Hawaii, Lanai is a luxury and privacy island, Maui has waterfalls and beaches, and the Big Island has botanical gardens.

Mexico

An island feel but much closer to home: ancient Aztec pyramids, soothing beach resorts and authentic food, as a budget friendly trip for the entire family.

Europe

Cities and countries to choose from, from the Eiffel Tower in Paris to the Coliseum in Rome, the enchanting cities of Spain and the landscapes of Ireland. Itineraries are customized, including where to stay and how to get around.

Asia

Ancient temples and glorious coastlines of Southeast Asia, and India's food, music and lifestyle for a more spiritual journey.

Australia & New Zealand

The adventurous outback, the Great Barrier Reef, wildlife, islands and rainforests, then New Zealand's lakes, wilderness and national parks.

Why Venus

The name comes from Italy

The agency's name derives from the Roman goddess of love, and also from what Italy means to so many people: love, beauty and endless possibilities. The family at Venus Travel is from the region of Puglia, in southern Italy, and has shared that heritage and expertise since the day the agency opened.

Puglia is not very tourist driven, but it can be a great place to visit old world charm, and the hospitality is among the friendliest in the country. An Italian defines himself by village, province and region, and each region of Italy tells a story. The agency continues to offer its knowledge and guidance to its home country.
