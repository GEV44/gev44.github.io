About us

Pa'La invites its community to take part in a time-honored tradition: food as a daily pursuit, with a connection to ingredients as they were meant to be. The kitchen cooks over wood fire with local and specialty imported ingredients, and the menu changes daily with the seasons.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What are your hours?

Dinner Tuesday to Saturday 5pm-10pm and Sunday 5pm-9pm. Closed Monday.

When is happy hour?

Every day 5:00pm-6:30pm, and all day on Sunday.

Where can I park?

City Scape Block 23 Garage, 101 East Washington Street, or Collier Center Parking Garage, 201 E. Washington St.

How does the menu work?

Tapas, small bites and plates meant to be shared. Two people typically share 4-5 plates. The menu changes daily.

Is there live music?

Yes, a live jazz band plays in the upstairs Lounge Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 6pm-10pm.

Do you do catering and events?

Yes, catering and special events. Email events@palakitchen.com or call 602.368.3052.
