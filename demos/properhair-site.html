08

Questions

How do I book?

Online through the Proper booking page, by phone at 604.681.1487, or by email. Quick bang trims and clean-ups are booked by calling the salon.

What makes Proper different?

The salon describes itself as a collective of passionate artists who believe in celebrating individuality, with a goal of a bespoke look that reflects the true essence of each person in the chair.

Do I need a consultation first?

Not for a standard cut or colour. Extensions, bleach outs, special occasion hair and design work are priced after a consultation.

Do you work with colour on all hair types?

The services list covers base colour, multi-colour, gloss, highlights and bleach outs. Your stylist assesses your hair at the consultation and the salon confirms what is possible and the final price.

Do you do men's and barber cuts?

Yes. There is a full barber menu with cuts, fades, beard services, hot towel shaves and eyebrow shape-ups.

Can I get a quick bang trim between cuts?

Yes. Bang trims and clean-ups or refades are 15 minute appointments, booked by calling the salon.

Which products do you use?

Proper is a Davines Concept Salon, and the treatments menu is built on Davines products.

Is there anything for dog people?

Two comfort dogs are on site most days, according to the salon's careers page. If you have allergies or concerns about dogs, tell the salon before you come in.

What does a consultation cover?

Extensions, bleach outs, special occasion hair and design work are priced after a consultation, because the work is highly personalised.

Are you hiring?

The website has a Careers and Culture page about commission stylist opportunities. Questions go to management@properhairsalon.com, or text Samuel Bligh at 604.999.1185.
