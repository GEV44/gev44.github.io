FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you take same-day appointments?

Yes. Emergencies are prioritized and same-day treatment is usually possible. For the fastest appointment, please call.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes. A quick call before you head over helps the team expect you.

Do you take my dental insurance?

Most major dental insurance plans are accepted. Medicaid and other state-funded programs are not.

Does emergency care cost more?

No. There are no extra fees for care outside typical hours, and the team tells you the expected cost before any procedure.

Are you open 24/7?

No. The office is open 9:00am to 9:00pm every day and closes overnight. You can book online for the next morning.

Is financing available?

Yes. Several financing options are offered, and major credit and debit cards are accepted.
