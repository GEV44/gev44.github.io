Our story

From Greek town to Western Canada's expert travel agency

In September 1974 Nick Panos opened Omega Travel in the heart of Greek town in Kitsilano, to help members of the Hellenic community of British Columbia travel to Greece and spend their holidays with family.

Over four decades it grew into one of Western Canada's leading expert travel agencies. The Mediterranean still holds the biggest place in the team's hearts: "we're from there, so we're a little biased."

Good to know

Quick answers

Do I need travel insurance?

The team always recommends it. Medical issues, family health problems, weather delays and lost baggage can happen to anyone, and an agent can recommend a policy for your situation.

How early should I arrive at the airport?

At least two hours before domestic flights and three hours before international flights. Reconfirm your flight time 24 hours before departure.

Do I have to choose from the trips on the site?

No. Omega specialises in personalised, one-of-a-kind vacations built around your needs, interests and budget.

Can I book a trip that isn't listed?

Yes. Share where you want to go and when, and an expert on that destination designs the plan; the in-house team then books hotels and activities.

Who will plan my trip?

You are matched with an authority on your destination from the Omega team. Names and profiles are on the Your Agents page of the Omega site.

Will I need vaccinations?

Contact the office or your doctor to find out which are required. If you take medication or have an illness, make sure you are covered.
