Care beyond the standard session

Each patient gets a personalised plan of care from a highly trained physical therapist, with a comprehensive evaluation and evidence-based treatment.

Concussion management

Help with lingering effects of a head or neck blow, such as poor balance, dizziness, blurred vision or severe headaches, with a comprehensive exam and close contact with your referring physician.

Balance and fall risk

Questions plus standardised screening tests, then an individual program with hands-on mobility exercises and take-home material.

Sports medicine

Running (gait) analysis, golf swing analysis, return to play testing and athletic training outreach to local schools and community events on request.

Wellness

Continuation of care after rehab, for school athletes and active seniors alike, one-on-one or in small groups, starting with a functional mobility screening and your goals.

Graston Technique (IASTM)

Instrument assisted soft tissue mobilization using special stainless steel tools, a deeper form of manual therapy used to find and treat soft tissue restrictions.

Electrical stimulation

A modality used alongside your physical therapy program to help ease pain or muscle spasms. Not everyone can use it, so the therapist decides if it fits you.

Your first visit, step by step

New patient paperwork can be done at home or at the clinic.

  1. Request your appointment by phone, online form or the assistant here.
  2. Fill in the forms. Patient intake (English and Spanish) and the HIPAA notice can be downloaded from the website, or use the online patient form.
  3. Bring what you need: your referral if you have one, your insurance card and a photo ID.
  4. Arrive 15 minutes early if you have not filled in the forms, and wear comfortable, non-restrictive clothing.
  5. Evaluation and plan. Your therapist builds a plan around your input, goals and situation.

If you need to cancel, call (832) 400-2678 before 2 pm the day before (for a Monday appointment, by 2 pm on Friday). A cancellation fee applies otherwise.

Common questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?

Texas is a direct access state, so if your insurance plan allows self-referral, a physician referral is not needed. Many plans do require one for coverage, so check with your insurer.

Can I pick where I get therapy?

Yes. Even with a doctor's referral, the choice of where to receive treatment is yours.

How much will physical therapy cost?

It depends on your injury, the initial evaluation and your goals. After the first visit, the clinic can give you a clearer picture of the overall cost.

What is the difference between a copay and coinsurance?

A copay is a set amount due each visit. Coinsurance is a percentage of covered services. Your policy decides which applies.

Do you offer anything for older adults or fall risk?

Yes, a balance and fall risk assessment program with individualised exercises, including help with issues such as dizziness and vertigo.

Is there anything for people who are not injured?

Yes. The wellness program supports people who want to keep exercising after rehab, from school athletes to active seniors, one-on-one or in a group.

What forms do I need?

A patient intake form (English or Spanish) and the HIPAA notice of privacy practices, or the online patient form so a staff member can call you to schedule.

Does the clinic file my insurance claim?

Yes, as a service to patients. The clinic needs a copy of your insurance card to submit claims correctly.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, non-restrictive clothing, because the evaluation and treatment may need access to the area being assessed.

Do you work with schools or community teams?

On request and subject to availability, Dr. Perez provides athletic training outreach to local schools and at community athletic events.

Why see a physical therapist instead of a personal trainer?

The wellness program is led by a licensed physical therapist who is also a certified athletic trainer, and exercises can be modified around each person's mobility limitations.
