The intensive course

Seven skills, covered first

New learners work through the basic controls with an instructor who stays calm and keeps it stress-free. The school's aim: the essentials within 6 hours of lessons. How many hours you personally need depends on where you start.

  1. Steering techniques
  2. Reverse parking
  3. Three-point turn
  4. Kerb-side stop
  5. Safe following distance
  6. Responding to hazards
  7. Observation checks: head check and over-the-shoulder check

FAQ

Quick answers

How long until I can book a test?

That depends on your skills. The instructor can run a pre-test evaluation and tell you honestly when you are ready. Ask the team about the current test booking situation.

Are lessons in automatic cars?

The lessons advertised are in automatic cars, and the test car for hire is a dual-control automatic.

Is there a surcharge for weekends or public holidays?

No. The school states the same rate at all times, public holidays included.

What if I need to cancel?

Package cancellations need to be made more than 24 hours ahead. Within 24 hours there is a 50% surcharge.

Can I choose a male or female instructor?

Both are available. Mention your preference when you call or message.

Do you teach seniors or learners with special needs?

Yes. There are instructors for special needs learners, and RMS-approved Older Driver Assessors for drivers over 85.
