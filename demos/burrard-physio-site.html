About us

Burrard Physiotherapy is a downtown Vancouver clinic that has served the city since 1982. Its therapists have multiple levels of training beyond their original degrees and lead active lifestyles, which helps them understand what an injury means to your day-to-day life.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you direct bill my insurance?

We direct bill many insurers, such as Pacific Blue Cross, Canada Life, Manulife, Sun Life and Desjardins. If yours isn't listed, call the clinic.

Do you treat ICBC claims?

Yes, ICBC treatment is one of our services.

Can I get physio without leaving home?

Yes, telerehab is a live 1-on-1 online session from home.

What is the AlterG treadmill?

A NASA-designed, body-weight-supported treadmill that speeds return to activity after injury.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 7am-7pm, Saturday 8am-2pm, closed Sunday.
