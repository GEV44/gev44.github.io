Which licence class?

Short guide to the road tests the school prepares you for.

Class 7

The road test for your N (Novice) licence. The school makes sure you know what is expected of an N driver before test day.

Class 5

Possible after 24 consecutive months as a Novice driver with no prohibitions, or after 18 months with an approved GLP driver training course.

Class 4

Commercial licence for taxis, limousines, ambulances and shuttles. Pre-trip inspection and road test on the same day.

Quick answers

Or ask the assistant in the corner.

Do you offer lessons for beginners in Vancouver?

Yes. Beginner lessons start with basic vehicle control and gradually move to real-traffic driving, so you build confidence safely.

Which road tests do you prepare students for?

Class 7 (N licence), Class 5 and Class 4. The school practises on routes where road tests are usually held and offers a Mock Test assessed to ICBC standards.

Can I take the classes in any order?

Yes. The flexible class system lets students take courses in any order and at their own pace, which suits busy schedules.

Do you pick students up?

Pick-up and drop-off is part of the packages, and the school says it can pick students up from work, school and home.

Where do lessons happen?

Vancouver, Richmond and Burnaby. The head office is at 1165 E 61st Ave, Vancouver. Meeting points are by appointment only.

What if I get nervous?

The school gives extra support to anxious students and teaches a see-think-do approach, step by step.
