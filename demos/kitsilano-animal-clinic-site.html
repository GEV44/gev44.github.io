What we do

Tap a service for the clinic's own description.

NoteKitsilano Animal Clinic is not an emergency hospital. For urgent cases there are several nearby facilities, so call the clinic or an emergency vet right away.
General wellness and exams

Routine wellness checks can help diagnose, treat or prevent health problems. Prevention is the best cure for most ailments, so regular check-ups and vaccinations keep your pet in top shape.

Vaccines and preventive care

Vaccination protocols are designed around your pet's risk factors, avoiding blanket protocols and unnecessary vaccines. Puppies typically get DA2PP, Rabies, Bordetella and Leptospirosis. Cats get Rabies and FVRCP, and FeLV for outdoor or multi-cat homes.

Spay and neuter

The mainstay of pet overpopulation prevention, and early sterilization prevents several diseases. The youngest age for spays and neuters is about 6 months, and the vets will discuss sooner versus later.

Dental care

Full dental services, done under careful general anesthesia, with high tech equipment including dental radiography.

Surgery

A fully equipped surgical suite and most soft tissue surgeries. Orthopedic surgeons come in on call for bone and joint procedures when necessary.

Parasite control

Products for safe control of internal parasites (worms) and external parasites (fleas and ticks). Commonly dispensed: Revolution, Bravecto and Simparica Trio.

Laboratory and diagnostics

Most lab testing goes to external labs run to human hospital standards, giving access to clinical pathologists.

Nutrition and diet

Many lines of veterinary pet foods. If not in stock, the clinic can usually order within 24 hours.

Specialist referral

Good relationships with Vancouver's veterinary specialists for advanced diagnostics and therapeutics. The clinic stays involved in your pet's care when referred out.

End of life care

Pain medication, diet changes if needed and compassionate care to keep your pet comfortable and give your household dignified, peaceful final memories.

Your first visit

  1. Fill in the new client form. Include your pet's medical history and any recent behaviour changes. Tell the clinic which clinics you used before and they will request records.
  2. Request an appointment. Choose days and a time of day, and your preferred doctor.
  3. Annual check-up. A head-to-tail exam, with teeth and gum cleaning if needed, and tests if the doctor recommends them.
  4. Tech appointments. Nail trims, fluid treatments, anal gland expressions and similar services with a technician or assistant.

Quick answers

Are you taking new clients?

Yes. The clinic is open to new clients, starting with the New Client Form.

Which vaccines do dogs and cats usually get?

Dogs: DA2PP, Rabies, Bordetella and Leptospirosis. Cats: Rabies and FVRCP, plus FeLV for outdoor cats and multi-cat homes. Bordetella is usually required for boarding your dog.

When can my pet be spayed or neutered?

From about 6 months. The vets are happy to discuss earlier versus later.

Can I get flea and tick preventative without a visit?

Most are prescriptions and cannot be given to pets the clinic has not seen before.

Why does my pet need to be seen before I get preventatives?

Most of the preventative medications are considered prescriptions, which cannot be given to pets the clinic has not seen before. Health changes quickly, and the team wants to be sure each medication is still right for your pet.

Can the clinic help with travel paperwork?

Rules change often, so the clinic does not always have up to date travel information. Check with the appropriate travel authorities to confirm what documents you need, and the team will help fulfill the requirements per your request.

Do you work with pet insurance?

Yes. Trupanion (direct payment to the hospital for covered services), Pets Plus Us (flexible accident and illness coverage) and Petsecure (comprehensive Canadian coverage) are commonly used by clients.

What are your doctors like with nervous pets?

The site says the team makes an effort to understand your pet's personality while providing high-quality, modern medical care, and takes the time to explain what it sees. For a specific worry the team will confirm.
