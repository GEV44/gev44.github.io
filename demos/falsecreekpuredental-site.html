Dental services

What you can book here

Open a service for the clinic's own description, then ask the assistant a follow-up. It will not diagnose anything: symptoms and treatment advice stay with the dentist.

01Teeth cleaning and checkups+

A checkup starts with your concerns and health history, then an exam of teeth, gums, existing work and soft tissues, a clean by your hygienist and a plan for follow-up. X-rays are recommended only when they add information for your care.

02BioClear bonding+

Clear forms shape tooth-coloured composite for gaps near the gumline, selected chips or changes in tooth shape. It does not move teeth or treat active gum disease.

03Laser dentistry+

The clinic has a Fotona LightWalker laser system, used for particular tasks when it fits your diagnosis, not as a replacement for every instrument.

04Dental implants and peri-implantitis care+

Implant planning and placement, plus care for inflamed tissue around an implant using laser and PRF-supported options.

  • Comprehensive implant placement
  • Laser and PRF-supported treatment options
  • Focused care for inflamed implant tissues
05Wisdom tooth extraction+

The tooth and its position are examined first, then removal or monitoring is discussed.

  • Minor-trauma technique for less pain and swelling
  • PBM treatment to help reduce swelling
  • Clear after-care guidance for recovery
06One-visit laser root canal treatment+

SWEEPS and LightWalker laser technology, with a minimally invasive approach and thorough cleaning of the root canal system, in selected cases.

07Laser periodontal treatment+

Laser-assisted care for gum disease, bone defects or loose teeth, aimed at helping preserve natural teeth.

Questions

Before you come in

How do I book?

Book available appointment types online or call reception on 604-877-1712. To arrange a consultation with Dr. Xu, please call the office.

Are you open at weekends?

The clinic is closed on Saturdays and Sundays, and on Tuesdays. Call reception to arrange a weekday appointment or confirm holiday hours.

How can I get a treatment quote?

Fees follow the BC Dental Association's Suggested Fee Guide. Call 604-877-1712 to schedule a consultation with Dr. Xu for an assessment and a personalized quote.

Do I need a checkup every six months?

Not necessarily. Your history of cavities or gum disease, deposit buildup, home care and general health decide a suitable interval, and the team explains the recommendation.

Does a laser make treatment painless?

The clinic is careful here: a laser is one tool, used when it fits your diagnosis. Other instruments may still be needed, and the dentist explains benefits, limits and alternatives.
