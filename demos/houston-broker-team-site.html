What is my home worth?

A free valuation from people, not an algorithm

The team's own words: do not rely on a clunky algorithm. A valuation looks at similar homes sold and active nearby and produces a comparable market analysis. It takes about a minute to request. Have this ready:

  • Your contact information
  • Property type: single-family, condo, rental or multifamily
  • Condition, bedrooms and bathrooms
  • Special features and upgrades, such as a new roof or custom kitchen
  • Approximate size in square feet
  • Approximate age of kitchen and bathrooms
Valuation, inspection, appraisal
A valuation estimates market value. An inspection checks the condition of the house. An appraisal is what your lender requires on a mortgage. The team's valuation page explains all three.
