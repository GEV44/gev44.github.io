Training and tracking

Personal training, with data behind it

One-on-one with certified trainers

Trainers are certified professionals with knowledge in fitness, nutrition and injury prevention. They build a program around your goals, experience level and preferences, whether that is weight loss, strength, endurance or general wellness. Your custom program lives in the Level Up app so you can follow it any time.

The Evolt 360 body scan

A quick, non-invasive scan using bioelectrical impedance reads muscle mass, fat mass and hydration, and gives a calorie and macronutrient profile. After the scan a trainer talks the numbers through with you. A detailed nutrition plan is outside the scope of the scan itself.

FoodieFit x Level Up

Pick a FoodieFit meal plan (for example high-protein or vegan) and a Level Up program such as strength training or HIIT. Members get 25% off the first FoodieFit order and 15% off ongoing orders. Request your code at the front desk or in the app PT messages.

Level Up app

Your plan in your pocket

Workout library for home or travel, your trainer's custom program, goal setting, meal logging with barcode scan, and smartwatch sync. On iPhone and Android. Live occupancy tracking helps you pick a quiet time.

Questions

Straight answers

What makes Level Up different from other gyms?

It is an upscale, calm and beautiful space where you feel welcome, with limited memberships and a deliberately personal experience.

Do you offer group classes?

No. Members get quiet space and uninterrupted access to equipment, which suits independent training or one-on-one sessions.

Is personal training available?

Yes, as paid one-on-one sessions with certified trainers. They can track progress with the Evolt 360 and build a custom program in the app.

Will I feel part of a community?

There is a members lounge to connect with others, and the clubs occasionally host events.

Can I visit before committing?

Yes. Book a complimentary club tour and try the amenities first.

What is included in my membership?

Full access to top-quality gym equipment, infrared saunas, AI massage chairs, spa-like locker rooms, a lounge to relax or connect, towel service, complimentary coffee, on-site parking and the fitness app.

Does the app work for home workouts?

Yes. It includes a full library of workouts for home or on the go, your trainer's custom program if you have one, and a diet tracker with barcode scanning. It syncs with your smartwatch and works on Android and Apple devices.

Is the scan included with my membership?

All new members receive a complimentary scan as part of the initial fitness assessment with a personal trainer.

What if I want something the site does not mention?

Ask the assistant or call (604) 416-5116. The team confirms anything that is not published, such as prices, contract terms and holiday hours.
