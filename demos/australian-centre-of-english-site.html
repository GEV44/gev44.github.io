From enquiry to first day

You can apply directly to ACE, or through a private education agent who represents the school.

1. Ask your questions

Read the website, then contact the team. There is a Get a Quotation option and a free trial lesson form.

2. Student Enrolment Form

Download it, complete and sign it, read the terms and the Refund Policy, and return it by email or post.

3. Offer Letter

It lists the fees for your course: enrolment fee (if applicable), tuition, accommodation, airport pickup, materials and OSHC, and how to pay.

4. eCoE and visa

After full payment you receive your Electronic Confirmation of Enrolment, which you need to apply for your student visa.

5. Day one

Receive your OSHC card and Student ID card, sit the placement test and join orientation.

Quick answers

Where is ACE?

2nd Floor, Hyde Park Plaza Building, 38 College Street, Darlinghurst NSW 2000, Sydney.

Can I work while I study?

According to ACE's FAQ, international students can work part-time while studying. Check your own visa conditions on the Your Work Rights page.

Do you help with accommodation?

Yes. ACE can book homestay and airport pickup if you ask on your application. Many students arrange their own.

How do I enrol?

Apply directly with the enrolment form, or through an ACE agent. The Offer Letter and eCoE follow after payment.

What if I arrive and do not start?

If you have arrived in Australia and have not started within one week, ACE cancels your eCoE, as it would breach student visa conditions.

How are classes taught?

With the communicative approach: everything you learn in class is to help you communicate with others, so speaking, group-work, pair-work and presentations are central.

Who can take General English?

Students over 18. You are placed in a class after your placement test and move up as your weekly test results show progress.

What do I receive when I finish?

A Statement of Attainment stating your English level on the CEFR scale, and/or a Certificate of Attendance showing your enrolment and level.

Can I try a lesson first?

The website has an ACE English Free Trial Lesson form under How to Apply. The team will confirm available times.

Is there an agent list?

Yes. The website has an Agent List, and agents can use the agent application, renewal and review forms.
