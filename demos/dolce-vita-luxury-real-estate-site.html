What is your property truly worth?

A confidential appraisal returned in writing within 24 to 48 hours: a considered market appraisal rather than an algorithm, live comparable evidence from your micro-market, current buyer demand from the agency’s own registry, a recommended sale or letting strategy, and an honest view on timing. A significant share of prime property changes hands quietly, between agents and owners, before it reaches a portal. Your valuation says plainly which buyer the agency believes your property is for and how it would reach them.

For landlords and tenants

Lettings after the Renters’ Rights Act

Summarised from the long-lets page.

Periodic from day one

Since 1 May 2026, assured shorthold tenancies no longer exist. Tenancies run with no fixed term and no Section 21 notice.

Tenant notice

A tenant may end the tenancy by giving two months’ written notice, expiring on a rent day.

Possession

A landlord may recover possession only on one of the statutory grounds.

Rent in advance

No more than one month’s rent once the tenancy has begun, and none before the agreement is signed.

Referencing

Identity, right to rent, employment or source of income, and a previous landlord where there is one. A UK guarantor is usually asked of tenants without UK income.

Deposits

Capped under the Tenant Fees Act 2019 and protected with the Deposit Protection Service on managed tenancies.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the valuation free?

Yes, confidential, without obligation, and never shared.

Who will I deal with?

Your enquiry is read by Hanane or Chris Dawson, not a call centre, and the same person stays with you through to completion.

Can you help if I live abroad?

Yes. Appointments can be by video, WhatsApp is often quickest from abroad, and the team advises in English, French, Spanish and Arabic.

Do you only handle London property?

No. Sales and lettings run across the whole of the United Kingdom from Mayfair, and the team helps clients buy and rent abroad through partner agencies.

Do you work with partner agencies abroad?

Yes. For property abroad the team works with the best local agency in each market, and consults that partner before replying.

What is the history of the brand?

Hanane Dawson created Dolce Vita in 2014, acquired the Century 21 franchise in Putney in 2015, and in 2020 left the franchise to focus solely on the Dolce Vita luxury brand, working from Mayfair.

What are your accreditations?

The Property Ombudsman, Propertymark (ARLA and NAEA), and Propertymark Client Money Protection.
