About us

Soto is a Japanese sushi bar at 224 Westheimer Road in Montrose, Houston. The menu is built around seasonal fish from Tokyo's Tsukiji Fish Market, with nigiri, sashimi, maki rolls, hot dishes, omakase and desserts, plus lunch, happy hour, take-out and vegetarian menus.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How do I make a reservation?

Call (713) 485-4514 or book a table through OpenTable.

When is happy hour?

Monday to Sunday, 5:00 to 6:30 PM.

Do you have lunch specials?

Yes, every day from 11:30 AM to 3:00 PM, including Bara-chirashi and maki combos with miso soup and side salad.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes, there is a dedicated Vegetarian menu.

Can I host a party or order catering?

Yes. The website has pages for private parties, holiday parties and catering packages.

Can I order online?

Yes, use the Order button on the website. A Take Out menu is available.
