Small on purpose

Only 30 rooms, right by the Lower East Side, with Soho, Nolita, Chinatown and Little Italy within easy walking distance. Guest reviews keep coming back to the quiet, the beds and the staff.

Written guides are arranged for guests with one or two nights in the city, or a few leisurely days: an Old New York version of Chinatown plus a taste of the cutting-edge Lower East Side.

House rules, written plainly

The things guests ask most, straight from the hotel's FAQ.

Cancellation

Standard rate: free to cancel up to 48 hours before arrival. Inside 48 hours, the first night and tax. Non-refundable rates are not refunded. Groups of 4 or more rooms follow a separate schedule.

Non-smoking

100% non-smoking under New York City law. A minimum $400 cleaning fee may apply.

Pets

Pets and emotional support animals are not permitted. Verified service animals are welcome.

Parking

No on-site parking. The FAQ lists three local lots, two of them open 24 hours.

Extra bed

Roll-away beds in Superior Queen rooms only, $50 per night.

Room keys

Please return room cards at check-out. $20 for lost or damaged cards.

Accessibility questions

The hotel has an accessible entrance, an accessible elevator and an ADA Superior Queen room. The front desk is under temporary construction, so guests can check in at the lobby lounge. For specifics, call 24/7 at +1 (212) 343-4243 or +1 (917) 887-7000.

FAQ

How do I get to the hotel?

The lobby entrance is on Eldridge St, between Division St and East Broadway.

Can I store bags?

Yes. Arriving early, the team can hold belongings in the secure storage room until your room is ready, and also after check-out.

Is it noisy?

Windows have four panes of glass. Guests often say they cannot hear the nearby bridge. For full silence, free earplugs are at the front desk.

How many people fit in a Superior Queen?

Two adults and one small child under 12. Overcrowding is not recommended.

Late check-out or early check-in?

Subject to availability, and may incur additional charges.

Book directly, say hello first
