About us

Jul's was born in Ibiza in 2018 from a love of Mediterranean living, where exceptional food, thoughtful design and genuine hospitality come together. Jul's London brings that to St James's in a warm, design-led space shaped for long lunches and evenings that unfold naturally.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for card details with every booking and 24 hours' notice. A late cancellation or no-show can carry a charge of up to £50 per person.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual.

Can children come?

Yes, but guests under 18 must leave by 9pm, and the cocktail bar is 18+ only.

What if I am running late?

After 15 minutes we cannot guarantee your table will be held, so let us know as soon as you can.

Can I book a large group?

Groups of up to 10 confirm menus, dietary needs and numbers 72 hours ahead. For private events write to event@julslondon.com.
