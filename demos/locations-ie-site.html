Choosing an agent

Six questions Locations suggests you ask

  1. Is the agent fully licensed?Licensed agents have a PSRA licence number. Ask for it.
  2. Talk on the phone or in personDo not just engage by email or social media. Make sure you are confident in the people.
  3. Who will be your negotiator?Ask whether it is a junior or senior, experienced negotiator.
  4. What are the fees?Ask if there are hidden extras. Price is only one factor in the choice.
  5. What rent will they achieve?Be cautious of an agent who sets expectations too high to win the instruction.
  6. Use your intuitionIf you get a good gut feeling about the agent, trust your instinct.

Landlord questions

Good to know before you instruct an agent

What does a letting and management agent actually do?

The agent markets the property, sends details to registered tenants and corporate and relocation contacts, runs viewings for pre-qualified tenants, screens tenants, draws up the lease, transfers utilities, monitors rent and follows up if it is overdue, inspects during the tenancy, handles maintenance and tenant queries and keeps the landlord informed. In effect it takes on the responsibilities a landlord would have.

Are Irish letting and management agents licensed?

Yes, and it is vital to engage a licensed, regulated agent. The Property Services Regulatory Authority (PSRA) licenses compliant estate, letting and management agents, runs a complaints and redress system and maintains a compensation fund. Every licensed agent has a licence number, so ask for it.

How do I choose the right letting agent?

Ask if the agent is fully licensed, talk by phone or in person, ask who the negotiator will be (junior or senior), find out the fees and any extras, ask what rent they expect the property to achieve and be cautious of inflated valuations. Price is only one factor.

How is my property marketed for sale?

Your Senior Negotiator advises on the best ways to present the property and creates a tailor-made marketing plan. It is featured on myhome.ie, daft.ie, locations.ie and a number of associated websites, with professional photographs and a full video walkthrough. The negotiator handles negotiations and keeps you informed of feedback from viewings and every offer.
