Repair notes

What the garage says about the jobs people ask about most.

Clutch

Matters in manual cars: it lets you change gear smoothly and passes engine power to the wheels. Worn clutches slip, make gear changes hard, or fail completely.

Timing belt

Keeps the crankshaft and camshaft in step so the valves open and close at the right moment. A worn or broken belt can cause serious engine damage, which is why signs like misfires, hard starting or odd engine noise are worth checking early.

Battery

Batteries lose charge or fail inside over time. Voltage and load testing shows a weak one before it leaves you stuck, and a failing battery can be recharged or replaced.

Brakes

Worn pads, damaged rotors or leaking fluid affect braking. A spongy feeling under your foot is a sign it's time to get them looked at.

Exhaust

Mufflers, catalytic converters and pipes wear out over time. Left alone, you get more noise, higher emissions and poorer fuel efficiency.

Tyres and wheels

Tyres are your only contact with the road. Inspection, rotation and replacement keep wear even and grip good.

Three signs of misaligned wheels

Alignment adjusts camber, caster and toe to the manufacturer's specification.

1

Uneven tyre wear

Extra wear on the inner or outer edge of a tyre means an alignment problem, and can mean tyres replaced sooner than they should be.

2

Pulling to one side

On a straight road the car drifts one way. Annoying, and potentially dangerous.

3

Vibration or off-centre wheel

Shaking through the steering wheel, or a wheel that isn't straight when you're driving straight.

Inside a service

How every servicing visit goes, according to the garage.

  1. Inspection

    A full look at the car's overall condition to spot underlying issues, so the work matches what your car actually needs.

  2. Fluids

    Engine oil, coolant, brake fluid and transmission fluid checked for leaks or contamination, then topped up or replaced.

  3. Oil and filters

    High-quality oil and filters, following the manufacturer's recommendations. Air and cabin filters, fuel filter on diesels, spark plugs on petrol engines.

  4. Brakes

    Pads, rotors and callipers checked. If anything is wrong, they repair or replace it.

  5. Tyres and alignment

    Tread depth, wear patterns and pressure checked, with wheel alignment to help handling, tyre life and fuel efficiency.

Premier Pitstop is in Dublin 12, on Killeen Road.

What services do you offer?

NCT checks, car repair, car servicing, crash repairs, wheel alignment, tracking, tyre services and part replacement.

How often should I have my car serviced?

Generally once a year or every 12,000 miles, whichever comes first. Intervals vary by make and model, so check your car's manual or the manufacturer's recommendations.

What happens in a standard service?

Typically the oil filter, air filter and cabin filter are changed. On diesels the fuel filter is replaced too, and on petrol engines the spark plugs.

Will insurance cover crash repairs?

In most cases insurance policies cover crash repairs, but check your own policy. The team supports you through the claim process.

How often should I book wheel alignment?

Typically every 12,000 to 15,000 miles or once a year, depending on driving habits and road conditions.
