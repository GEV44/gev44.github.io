What sets the clinic apart

INDIBA® radiofrequency, first in Ireland

INDIBA® Active uses a 448 kHz radiofrequency to support the body's natural repair, easing pain and inflammation. It is used alongside manual therapy and dry needling, and Archview describes sessions as comfortable and non-invasive with no downtime.

Contraindications listed on the clinic site include pacemakers, pregnancy (discuss with your therapist), cancer and active infection. Your therapist decides if it suits you.

Good to know

Quick answers

Can I book outside office hours?

Yes. The online booking system lets patients book, cancel and rebook outside office hours.

What is the cancellation policy?

24 hours' notice is requested. A late cancellation or reschedule less than 6 hours before costs €40, and a no-show is charged the full fee. Emergencies are understood.

Is dry needling extra?

No. All therapists dry needle, and it is included in the cost of treatments and massages.

Do you treat women's health issues?

The clinic offers women's health physiotherapy, but its specialist is currently on maternity leave. Ask the team about current availability.

Do you see patients on weekends?

Yes, Saturday and Sunday 9am to 5pm, by appointment.
