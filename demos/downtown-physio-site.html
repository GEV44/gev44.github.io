About us

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

In most cases no. Physiotherapy, acupuncture, naturopath and massage therapy are direct access in BC, though some benefits plans may have their own requirements.

Do you direct bill my insurance?

Most private insurers, including Sun Life, Manulife, Great-West Life, Green Shield, Pacific Blue Cross, Desjardins, Industrial Alliance and Medavie Blue Cross. Direct billing can't be guaranteed for every appointment and only your primary insurer can be billed.

Do you treat car accident and work injuries?

Yes, ICBC car accident injuries such as whiplash, soft tissue damage and joint sprains, as well as WCB work injuries. The team can help you get started.

What about MSP Premium Assistance?

Qualifying patients can get partial coverage for up to 10 treatments per calendar year with registered acupuncturists and physiotherapists. You pay the remaining portion, and the office staff can help verify eligibility.

How do I book?

On the website, choose the appointment reservation option, pick your service, select a visit time, then enter your name and email or phone and create an account if you don't have one.

What is the cancellation policy?

At least 24 hours' notice is requested for any cancellation or change. Without adequate notice, or if you don't attend, a cancellation fee may apply.
