The hotel

Boutique hotel in the heart of Chelsea

San Domenico House is an intimate, very private boutique hotel close to Sloane Square, with 19 individually themed bedrooms and suites, each furnished with an extraordinary variety of valuable antique furniture and art.

A fine treasure hidden away in a quiet residential corner of Chelsea, it is a townhouse hotel brimming with an elegant mix of sumptuous interiors, eclectic antiques and Latin personality. Recently extended and redesigned, it offers a bespoke room service menu to complement the outstanding selection of restaurants nearby.

The building dates from 1887 and was a private residence before becoming the hotel it is today. The street is lined with classic Victorian townhouses with red-brick facades, in what the hotel calls one of the quiet, prized streets of Old Chelsea.

Rooms and suites

Every room is different

All 19 rooms and suites have individually themed interiors, with panelled walls, ragged paintwork, exquisite fabrics and antiques collected by the owners during their travels. Some have rare original pictures from 18th century Italian and English art schools, and several have a small balcony or terrace overlooking Chelsea.

Chelsea and Knightsbridge

Heritage, sophisticated, cultural

The hotel sits in one of the most elegant districts of London. A few steps away are the world-renowned shops of King's Road and, beyond them, the department stores Harrods and Harvey Nichols in Knightsbridge.

South Kensington is home to the Science Museum, the Natural History Museum, the Victoria and Albert Museum and the Royal Albert Hall. Nearby on foot there are also Whole Foods Market and Waitrose on King's Road, Boots at 60 King's Road, and Peter Jones in Sloane Square.
