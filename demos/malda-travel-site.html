Valid for 30 days before entry. Stay up to 60 days from arrival.

Visa questions

If my visa is issued in Dubai, can I enter through another Emirate?

Yes. Once issued, the visa works for entry via any Emirate: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah are one country.

I have a tourist visa. Can I extend it inside the country?

If the visa was issued through Malda, a 30-day visa can be extended up to 5 times and a 60-day visa up to 2 times, subject to immigration approval. If another agency issued it, only your sponsor can extend. For a visa on arrival, Malda recommends leaving and coming back with a pre-applied visa. Advice line: +971 50 88 666 49.

Which nationalities get a visa on arrival?

Use the checker above. It follows the 30-day, 90-day and 180-day lists on Malda’s visa page.

Anything else I should remember?

Never overstay. Malda wishes you an enjoyable stay in the UAE.
