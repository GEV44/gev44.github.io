How to apply

Four steps, with an advisor beside you

  1. Choose a program

    Decide what you want to study and talk with a student advisor.

  2. Send the form

    Complete the application form and send it to your advisor, or apply online.

  3. Pay tuition

    Options include Flywire and e-Transfer to the campus you chose.

  4. Apply for your visa

    After payment you receive an invoice and a letter of acceptance for the visa application.

Accommodation

Live with a local family

Homestay places you with Canadian hosts who provide three meals a day and a comfortable room with a bed, desk and furnishings, with public transport nearby. It is a good way to practise English every evening.

A residence option is also listed on the school's site. Ask an advisor for details and availability.

Good to know

Frequently asked

When can I start?

New students can start every Monday. The calendar on the school's site lists the dates.

How is my level decided?

On registration you take an oral and a written assessment, so you are placed in the class that matches your English. You can also take the free online test first.

Can I talk to someone in my own language?

Yes. The school lists advisors who speak Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Turkish, Mandarin, Russian, Thai, Italian and more.

Do I need IELTS to enter university?

Students who complete the University and College Pathway Program can enter SGIC partner universities and colleges in Canada without an IELTS or TOEFL score.
