How the practice works

Stability, and the right partners

The practice's strength is personal relationships and stability. Dr. Ito also has strong relationships with the specialists and the dental lab the practice works with, so that you get the best quality for your dental health.

Estimates before treatment

Quotes for recommended services show the total fee (usually a discounted fee set by your insurance company) and an estimate of what your plan is likely to cover. The balance is your estimated patient portion. Estimates can differ from the final insurance payment, since the insurance company makes the final decision when it processes the claim.

Patient resources

New patient forms, payment options and savings plan information are all published for patients to read at home. The practice also has a Refer Us page, because it grows through referrals from long-time, happy patients, and some patients drive from miles away to keep coming.

Quick answers

Before you call

Do you take my insurance?

The practice accepts all PPO dental plans and is in network with many of them. Call (480) 785-9191 and the team will check your plan and your coverage for you.

What if I do not have insurance?

The in-office Sonoran Hills Dental Savings Plan makes insurance-style discounts available, and there is a 10% self-pay discount for patients not using insurance or discounted fee plans.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes, third-party financing is available through CareCredit, and PayPal offers 6 months interest-free payment options. Your patient portion is due at the time of service.

How do implants work here?

The implant placement itself is done with an oral surgeon the practice partners with. Dr. Ito and the team then design, create and attach your replacement teeth or bridge once you have healed.

What whitening options do you have?

In-office Opalescence Boost (results in less than an hour, chemically activated with no hot light) or custom trays for use at home, made after a consultation.
