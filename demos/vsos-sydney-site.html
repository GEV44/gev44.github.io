FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need an appointment for an emergency?

No. Come straight in; no referral or appointment is needed. You can call (02) 8376 8767 for guidance on the way.

Are you open on public holidays?

Yes, we are open every hour of every day, 365 days a year, including Christmas, Easter and public holidays.

Do you accept pet insurance?

If your insurer participates in GapOnly, we can process your claim directly. For other insurers, you pay VSOS and claim reimbursement.

When do I need to pay?

A 50% deposit is required for admissions and procedures, including emergencies. The balance is payable when treatment is completed.

Can I send photos for a diagnosis?

Our vets cannot diagnose over the phone, even with photos or videos. Please call your vet or VSOS for advice.
