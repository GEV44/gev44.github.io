Airway-driven dentistry

Dr. Mitic has spent years studying sleep apnea and sleep-related breathing disorders and earned Diplomate status in Sleep Medicine in 2025. The practice runs a Sleep Apnea and Snoring Clinic for patients of all ages, starting with real test results rather than guesswork.

Symptoms patients bring in

Excessive daytime sleepiness and trouble concentrating, dry mouth or a metallic taste on waking, restless or broken sleep, and snoring that a partner notices.

Where it starts

A home sleep test runs for one night in your own bed. The consultation, testing and follow-through are laid out in 5 stages.

How it is billed

Sleep apnea treatment is billed to medical insurance, not dental. The team can explain what that means for you.

Replacing a missing tooth, metal-free

A missing tooth changes more than your smile. Chewing shifts to one side, certain words stop sounding right, and the jawbone under an empty socket begins to shrink while neighboring teeth drift toward the gap. At Healing Dentistry, ceramic zirconia implants are the standard option for patients who want a strong, biocompatible replacement without metal.

The bone

The jawbone under an empty socket begins to shrink over time.

The neighbors

Neighboring teeth drift toward the space, which can lead to bite problems.

The post

Once the zirconia post integrates with the bone, it anchors a crown, bridge or denture. Integration typically takes three to six months.

Ozone: a cleaner, gentler visit

If your gums bleed a little when you brush, or a taste keeps coming back, bacteria deep in the periodontal pockets may be staying put after a standard cleaning. Ozone is a naturally occurring form of oxygen with antimicrobial properties, applied as ozonated water or gas during your appointment.

No downtime

No recovery is needed afterward.

No numbing

Ozone needs no anesthetic on its own.

Every 6 months

For most patients, ozone is part of every cleaning.

Raised in a family dental practice led by her grandfather and mother, Dr. Mitic earned her dental degree in Serbia and practiced alongside her mother for three years before moving to the United States.

Quick questions

Is sleep apnea care billed to dental insurance?

No. According to the practice, sleep apnea treatment is billed to medical insurance, not dental.

How long does amalgam removal take?

A typical SMART amalgam removal appointment takes about 2 hours.

Does ozone hurt or need numbing?

Ozone needs no anesthetic on its own and there is no downtime or recovery afterward.

How long do ceramic implants take?

The post typically takes three to six months to integrate, across 5 stages from assessment to final crown.

Do you offer anything besides general dentistry?

Yes. The service list includes Invisalign, oral health, sleep apnea treatment, metal-free fillings and amalgam removal, Bioptron hyperlight therapy and anti-aging treatment, Fotona LightWalker laser, teeth whitening, dentures, ceramic dental implants, full mouth rehabilitation, ozone dentistry and Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF).

Who is Dr. Mitic?

Dr. Marija Mitic, DDS, D.ASBA, is the founder and clinical director. She is a member of several professional organizations, including ASBA, AACA, KOIS, gIDE and AAFE, and specializes in biomimetic dentistry and occlusal therapy.

Are you open on weekends?

Saturday is by appointment only. The office is closed on Sunday.
