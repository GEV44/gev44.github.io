Overseas licence conversion

Four steps to a Victorian licence

VicRoads requirements vary by country, licence, age and driving history, so check which steps apply to you.

  1. Learner permit knowledge testAlso called the Road Laws Knowledge Test. Booked through VicRoads.
  2. Verify your overseas licenceVicRoads checks your licence and identity documents.
  3. Hazard Perception TestA separate VicRoads test, with official practice tests online.
  4. VicRoads drive testFocused lessons on the local testing routes, then the test.

Quick answers


How many lessons should I book?

New learners: 5 to 10 to begin. Close to 120 hours: 3 to 5. These are recommended ranges, not minimums.

Do I need a test package?

If you want the instructor's car and support at the VicRoads appointment, choose a Lessons + VicRoads Drive Test package, whether your test is already booked or not.

How do I pay?

Book first. Your confirmation email has the booking details, your instructor's information and payment instructions. Use your name as the payment reference.

When are lessons and phone hours?

Lessons run 7am to 7pm. The phone is answered 9am to 5pm on weekdays; online bookings can be sent any hour and are answered from 9am until late, 7 days.

Is a bigger package cheaper per lesson?

Yes. Larger packages carry a lower rate per lesson. In the 60-minute range the 3-lesson package works out at $75 an hour and the 10-lesson package at $68 an hour, the school's lowest rate. Packages also make it easier to plan lessons consistently.

Do weekends cost more?

No. There is no weekend rate, no public holiday rate and no early-morning or after-hours surcharge. The price you see is the price you pay.

Is the VicRoads test fee included?

No. The package price covers the school's lesson, vehicle and instructor support. Fees for the official VicRoads drive test are separate and payable directly to VicRoads.

What if my test is already booked?

Choose a Lessons + VicRoads Drive Test package and enter the test date, time and VicRoads office in the online booking form. The same package applies if you are about to book the test.

Where does the lesson start?

From your home, your work, a local train station or the VicRoads office you are testing at. For focused test preparation, meeting at the VicRoads office can cut travel time and leave more of the booking for driving.

Do overseas drivers need many lessons?

Often not. Overseas licence holders usually need focused preparation on Victorian road rules, Melbourne conditions and the VicRoads test format rather than a long course. The school suggests choosing a plan by your driving experience; the team can advise.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes, there is an organisation bookings form for booking on behalf of a client.
