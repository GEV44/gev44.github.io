The honest version

Is Miami Iron for you?

You will feel at home if

  • You are a dedicated gym goer, bodybuilder or competitor with serious ambitions
  • You want heavy weights with no size limits
  • You train at odd hours and need the doors open 24/7
  • You like a gym where grunting is normal
  • You want a trainer who competes at IFBB or NPC level

It is not trying to be

  • A big and flashy health and fitness club
  • A weekend warrior, cardio-all-day environment
  • A place to text on the bench or socialize on a machine
  • A gym with noise complaints about heavy lifting

House rules

Ten rules. Read them.

  1. Respect each otherIf you want respect then you give respect.
  2. Respect the equipmentThis is your home, treat it as such.
  3. Pick up after yourselfRe-rack your weights. Your momma does not work here.
  4. No textingDo not take up a machine or bench texting or socializing. Others are here to put in work.
  5. Support and encourage each otherThis is your Iron family.
  6. Grunting is allowedNo one will tell you that you are training too loud. We encourage hard work.
  7. Drop your weights, do not slam themThere is a difference. Respect the equipment.
  8. Train, do not work outMake every session count. Come to put in work and get results.
  9. No curling in the squat rackUse the equipment for what it is intended for.
  10. Enjoy yourselfThis gym was made for you. Train hard and have fun.
Quick answers

Before you come in

Are these the only plans?

Previous specials and deals have existed. To stay up to date on new and upcoming specials, follow the gym on social media.

Are there any discounts?

Discounts are available for Military, Police and Fire Fighters who show proper ID at the time of sign up.

How much is a daily pass?

$25 plus tax, one-time access, available 24 hours a day. One-time use, no in and out.

Are there any sign up fees?

The site says there are no sign up fees or hidden costs. IRON and IRON PLUS list a $20 one-time gym fee.

What are the gym's hours of operation?

Miami Iron Gym is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Anything not covered here?

Parking, cancellation terms, equipment lists and locker details are not on the website. The front desk will confirm them, or ask the assistant in the corner.
