From savings bank to hotel

The old banking chamber is now reception, with its pressed metal ceiling restored. Rates and room availability are confirmed by the team.

  1. 1912Land acquired by the Government Savings Bank of NSW.
  2. 1914The Oxford Street building opens, with a bank and the manager's residence.
  3. 1931The branch becomes part of the Commonwealth Bank.
  4. 1996The bank sells the building and leases it back until 2013.
  5. 2015 to 2017Conserved and adapted as Mrs Banks Hotel, with a new three-storey rear wing.

Good to know before you arrive

The practical answers guests ask most, from the hotel's own information.

Check-in and check-out

Reception is open from 7am to 11pm daily. Check-out is by 11am; as a small, busy hotel it is not usually able to offer late check-out. Guests need valid photo ID and a matching credit card at check-in, and must be 18 or older to book.

Payment

Payment in full at check-in by Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay or American Express. The hotel is almost cashless. Prepayment is not required unless it was a condition of the booking, though a deposit of up to one night may be held to verify the card.

Parking

No onsite parking. Street parking is limited and time restrictions may apply.

Breakfast

Fresh fruit, tea and coffee are always available. A complimentary continental breakfast is no longer offered, and Paddington's cafes are on the doorstep.

Smoking

The hotel is entirely non-smoking, including vaping. A smoking fee applies if the rule is broken.

What's on nearby

Match days, race days, markets and theatre, all within a walk or a short ride. Check each venue for dates.

Paddington Markets

Every Saturday from 10am to 4pm, rain or shine. More than 150 stalls of local fashion designers, craftspeople, jewellery makers and artists, five minutes on foot.

The SCG

Test, one-day and T20 cricket, AFL, NRL, Super Rugby and A-League matches, plus concerts at the Sydney Cricket and Sports Ground, a short walk from the hotel.

Royal Randwick

The jewel in the crown of Sydney racing, with the first race held in 1833. About a 20 minute walk; see the Australian Turf Club for race dates.

Entertainment Quarter

Twice-weekly Cambridge Markets, Fratelli Fresh, Hoyts cinemas and many places to eat, 15 minutes on foot. The Hordern Pavilion nearby hosts popular gigs.

Paddington Town Hall

A heritage-listed Victorian hall two minutes away, with a foundation stone laid in 1890. It hosts regular sales events by fashion designers and label groups.

Capitol Theatre

A heritage-listed theatre in Campbell Street, Haymarket, with musicals, theatre and ballet.

Bondi Beach is the classic day out: swim, learn to surf, or do the Bondi to Coogee walk. A regular bus from Circular Quay to Bondi passes the hotel on Oxford Street.
