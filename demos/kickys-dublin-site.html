FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How do I book a table?

Book through the Reservations page or call 01 906 1008. For more than 8 guests please use the group booking form.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel at least 48 hours before your booking, online or by email or phone. A no-show fee of €50 per person applies.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

We can cater for most allergies and diets, and vegetarian or vegan mains are available on request.

What are the last order times?

Dinner: last reservation 9:30pm, last order 9:45pm. Lunch: last reservation 2:30pm, last order 2:45pm.

Do you have a Christmas menu?

Yes, a set menu for tables of 7 or fewer and a group menu for 8 or more, from November 23rd to December 24th.
