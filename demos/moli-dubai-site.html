About us

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What offers do you run each week?

Friday Brunch every Friday 8-11 PM, Ladies Night every Tuesday 8-11 PM, Happy Hour Monday to Friday 4-8 PM and Business Lunch Monday to Friday 12-3 PM.

Can I reserve a table online?

Yes, you can reserve on the website, by WhatsApp on +971 58 189 7445, or by e-mail at reservation@molibyshi.com.

Are there vegetarian options?

Yes, there is a range of vegetable dishes, such as tofu eggplant with black bean sauce and Sichuan vegetable pot, plus noodles with mixed vegetables.

What currency and taxes apply to the menu prices?

All prices are in UAE Dirhams, inclusive of VAT 5% and subject to a 7% municipality fee.

What about allergies?

Please let the team know when you book and tell your waiter. The dessert menu marks Dairy, Gluten and Nuts.

Do you host events?

Yes, the restaurant hosts events and celebrations, and the website has an events brochure.
