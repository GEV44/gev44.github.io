Dental emergency

Hurt, broken or swollen? Call first.

Emergency patients are seen the same day whenever possible. Phone ahead so the team expects you, and after hours a person answers rather than a machine. Typical urgent visits include tooth pain, broken teeth, swelling and injuries.

Right away

Gently rinse your mouth with warm water to clear debris from the area.

Broken or loose tooth

Keep the tooth or pieces in a cup of water or whole milk to keep them moist and bring them with you.

For the pain

The practice suggests ibuprofen or acetaminophen rather than aspirin, which can increase bleeding. Tell the team exactly what happened when you arrive.

General guidance from the practice's Emergency Dentist page. The team will advise you on the phone.

Your first visit

New patients fill in a registration form, then have a comprehensive exam. The team reviews your dental history, talks through your goals and builds a personal care plan. Appointments usually run 45 minutes to an hour.

Bring: a valid ID, dental insurance information and any relevant medical records.

Insurance and payment

Most dental insurance that lets you choose your own office is accepted. Call to confirm your plan. Cash, check, debit, credit and third-party financing are accepted, and Invisalign® can start as low as $95 a month.

Common questions

Answers from the practice's own FAQ. Ask the assistant for anything else.

Can I be seen the same day for an emergency?

Yes. Call ahead so the team knows to expect you. After hours there is staff available instead of an answering machine.

Do you accept my insurance?

Any dental insurance that allows the patient to choose their own dental office. Plans differ in limits and rules, so call before your visit to confirm your coverage.

I do not have insurance. Are there payment options?

Yes: cash, debit, credit, third-party financing and several payment plans. A discount is available when paying in full by cash or check.

Will it hurt?

The team uses local anesthesia and offers sedation if needed, and explains each step as it goes.

Who cleans my teeth?

Registered dental hygienists perform the cleanings.
