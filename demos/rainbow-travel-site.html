Quick answers

Do I need an appointment?

You can call or walk in during opening hours. For a tailor-made trip or a visa, the team can set aside time if you tell them what you need first.

Which destinations do you know best?

We specialize in flights and packages to Asia and Europe, cruises, and custom tours.

Can you help with a visa?

Yes, express visa services for China, Vietnam, Australia and Myanmar. The team confirms the documents for your case.

Is my money protected?

Rainbow Travel lists "100% money protected" among its reasons to book. Ask the team for the details of your booking.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Yes. Travel insurance for Canadians and visitors to Canada is on the services list, covering emergency medical and trip cancellation.

Are the posted prices final?

No. Deals are subject to availability, and packages are based on double occupancy with taxes included (gratuities and resort fees excluded). The team confirms the current fare.
