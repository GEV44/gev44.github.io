Arriving

From the airport: bus 16 goes to the city centre, or a taxi takes about 30 to 45 minutes. There is no car park at the hotel; two nearby car parks offer guests a discount, and reception validates the ticket.

Before you book

Quick answers

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is 11am. Early check-in depends on availability, and the only way to guarantee it is to book from the night before. Late check-out is subject to availability and a charge. You can leave luggage with the front office team.

Can I cancel a direct booking?

Contact the hotel by 2pm local time the day before arrival, or the first night is charged. Advance Purchase rates are prepaid, non-cancellable and non-refundable. For bookings through third parties, contact the booking provider.

Do you have family rooms, cots or interconnecting rooms?

No. The hotel does not offer family rooms, interconnecting rooms, cots or cribs. Guests must be at least 18 to check in, or accompanied by an adult.

Are pets allowed?

Only certified guide dogs and emotional support dogs at the hotel. Wren Apartments on College Green are pet friendly.

Is the hotel accessible?

There are wheelchair accessible rooms with an emergency cord in the bathroom, a lower sink, a raised toilet with grab rails, and the upper floors are accessible by wheelchair. The site has an accessibility guide.

Do I need a card at check-in?

A valid credit or debit card is needed at check-in. The hotel pre-authorises the room rate plus an amount per room for additional charges.
