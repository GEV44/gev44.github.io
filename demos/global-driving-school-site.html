EDT

Essential Driver Training, lesson by lesson

EDT is a mandatory course of 12 lessons that teaches the fundamentals to learner car drivers. It is part of the RSA's Graduated Driver Licensing scheme and is meant to make the roads safer.

Do you need it? If the date of issue on your first Category B learner permit is on or after 4 April 2011, you must complete EDT before you can take the full driving test. If your first permit was issued before that date, EDT is not required.
Keep in mind: EDT is only part of the learning process. You will need lots of practice, and you may need more lessons to become a safe driver.
Your time, your pace

What happens on a lesson

Learning to drive is not something to be rushed. Here is how your instructor works with you, in their own words.

  1. An assessment firstYour first lesson starts with your instructor checking what you already know about the rules of the road and any driving experience you have.
  2. A quiet spot to swap seatsYou are driven to a quiet place where you can comfortably change places and get behind the wheel.
  3. A plan for the dayAt the start of each lesson you talk through the lesson plan for that day.
  4. A review at the endEach lesson closes with what was taught and what to practise before the next session. Areas of concern are checked so skills are fully developed.
Your own car? If it suits you better, lessons can also be taught in your own car. Tell the team when you book.

Test day

Pre-test and car hire

For learners who are ready to take the driving test. Available in manual and automatic at the Dun Laoghaire and Gorey test centres.

How it works

  • Your instructor meets you at the test centre before your test.
  • You drive a mock test on test routes while they mark a test sheet.
  • They flag any serious mistakes before the official test and help you focus.
  • They accompany you to be signed over to the assessor, and wait while you take the test.

Refresher lessons

  • The first 30 minutes gauge your skills and confidence.
  • Closer to test day it runs as two sessions of two hours on a simulated road course.
  • Feedback and suggestions at the end of each session.
  • Also for drivers returning after a break or feeling tense behind the wheel.
Using your own car for the pre-test and the exam? Say so when you book, and the price is adjusted accordingly.

Yes. Lessons, pre-test and car hire are all available in both manual and automatic vehicles.

The school has both male and female RSA approved driving instructors. Tell the team your preference when you book.

The cars are modern late-model vehicles with dual controls, fully insured, and well looked after.

Do you teach theory?

The school offers comprehensive theory tuition on the rules and regulations on Irish roads. Ask the team how it fits with your lessons.
