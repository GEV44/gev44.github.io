Quick answers

Who are the lessons for?

First-time drivers and beginners are the school's specialty, and refresher training is available for people who already drive.

How long is a lesson?

One hour each. You can buy one lesson at a time or a package of 5, 7 or 10.

Do you pick me up?

Yes, pickup and drop-off services are offered. Ask the team to confirm your address is covered.

Are the cars dual-controlled?

Yes, lessons use dual controls for added safety.

Can I learn in the evening or at the weekend?

Weekdays 6:00 am to 7:00 pm, weekends 7:00 am to 5:00 pm. After-hours training options are available on request.

What if my suburb is not on the list?

The listed suburbs are the main areas. Message the team with your suburb and they will tell you if they can come to you.

Do I need to buy a package?

No. A single one-hour lesson is available, and the packages simply cost less per lesson.

How do I book?

Use the booking request in the assistant, call +61 420 307 268, or write to info@namastedrivingschool.com.au.
