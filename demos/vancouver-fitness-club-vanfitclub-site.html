Your plan

Built around you, not a template.

Every programme starts with a conversation about you, and the plan is yours alone. The club designs a plan and program specific to you by taking into account:

Your goalsStrength, weight loss, performance, or simply to feel and look good.
Your limitationsInjuries and anything your body needs you to respect.
Your lifestyleYour current routine, so the plan fits a real week and not an ideal one.
Your historyYour fitness level and what you've trained before.

Whether you are an athlete or an everyday mover, you keep moving toward your goals with the club behind you.

Through training you continuously receive education, motivation and support. As the club puts it: no dream is impossible with dedication and commitment. Nothing that comes easy lasts, so the work is daily, and the results follow the effort.

Mind, body, spirit

Energy comes first.

In a busy world that keeps pulling attention toward the material side of life, it is easy to forget our total well-being. Vesna teaches people to use the full potential of their body through transformational programs that train the mind to be limitless. When we are not aligned, the body communicates through symptoms such as weight gain, fatigue, pain or injuries. The work begins by bringing awareness to all three: mind, body and spirit.

Vesna describes her own turning point plainly: fitness became a tool to train not just the body but the mind, and to carry the real world and real life into every session. Her goal for every client is the same, to make fitness fun and a lifestyle for a lifetime, and to help each person feel and look good.

Good to know

Quick answers.

Is the first class really free?

Yes, the site says train with us, first class free.

I'm a beginner. Is that okay?

The application asks for your level: beginner, intermediate or advanced. Plans account for your goals, limitations, injuries and history.

Can I train online?

Yes. The online program works worldwide, with video instructions and nutritional guidance.

Do you work with injuries?

Plans take your limitations and injuries into account. For any medical question, please speak to your doctor and tell the team when you apply.

Can I bring a friend or partner?

Yes. Partner Training is built for training together: motivate, inspire and grow together.

Can I do yoga only?

Yes. The Yoga Membership is a yoga-only option with unlimited yoga classes in Kundalini, Vinyasa, Hatha, Yin, Power and Flow. The Unlimited Membership covers every class, yoga included.

Can I see the club before I join?

The site offers a Take a Tour option. Mention it in your application and the team will arrange it.

Who is the club for?

The club describes its training as elite training for the everyday athlete: strength, power, speed, focus, balance and breath, for people who want fitness to be a lifestyle for a lifetime.

What does it cost?

Pricing is not published on the website. The team will confirm costs for the option you choose.

Where is the club and when are classes?

VanFitClub is in Vancouver, BC. The street address and class times are not on the site, so the team will confirm both.
