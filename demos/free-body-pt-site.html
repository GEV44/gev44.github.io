About us

Free Body Physical Therapy is an independent, locally owned practice in Chicago, founded in 2015 to break the mold of high-volume, assembly-line physical therapy. Therapists investigate the root cause of an injury so patients understand their bodies and achieve a real recovery.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Which insurance do you accept?

Blue Cross Blue Shield PPO/EPO, Aetna, MedRisk, Corvel, Humana Medicare, workers' comp and auto, plus self-pay by cash or card.

How long is an appointment?

45 to 60 minutes, one-on-one.

What should I wear to my first appointment?

Shorts for low back, hip, knee or ankle injuries; clothing that shows the shoulder blades for neck or shoulder injuries.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed for most insurance plans.

What languages do you speak?

English, Spanish and Polish.
