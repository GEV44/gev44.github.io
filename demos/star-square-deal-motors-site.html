Before you drive over

Quick answers

Where is the workshop?

Al Quoz Industrial Area 3, Dubai, UAE. PO Box 113189.

What are the opening hours?

The website does not list workshop hours. It does say recovery and friendly service run 24/7. Confirm the workshop hours by phone before you visit.

Do you publish prices?

No. The service pages say cost depends on the car and the job, so the workshop gives an estimate after seeing the car.

Can you collect my car?

Pick-up and drop-off is available with a prior appointment, limited to certain areas.

Do you do inspections before I buy a car?

Yes: a pre-purchase full check-up with a 29 point check, plus a 360 degree inspection report and a valuation and condition report.
