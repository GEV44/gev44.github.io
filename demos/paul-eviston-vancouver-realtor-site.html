Quick answers

The same answers the assistant gives, pulled from the Eviston site.

What do I tell the team if I want to buy?

Your ideal neighbourhood, whether you want a townhouse, detached home, duplex or condo, minimum bedrooms and bathrooms, and your preferred moving date. Comments are welcome too.

What do I tell the team if I want to sell?

Your name and the address of the home, plus how soon you are thinking of selling: immediately, within 3 months, within a year, or just exploring the possibilities.

Which kinds of homes does the team focus on?

Townhouses, detached homes and condos in East Vancouver and the West Side, with experience across downtown, the waterfront and the mountains.

Can I just text a question?

Yes. The site says Paul is never too busy for inquiries and invites you to call, email or text. Cell: 604-313-0035.

How do I follow new listings and price reductions?

Sign up for the Inside Track (Insider’s Edge) on the home page, or browse Our Listings, Open Houses, Recent Sales and the MLS map search.

What does the team say about its approach?

That selling and buying should be a simple, straightforward and enjoyable experience, and that Paul’s associates integrate their skills to serve sellers, buyers and property investors.
