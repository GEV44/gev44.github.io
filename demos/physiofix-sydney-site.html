How a visit runs

Based on what the clinic describes: private, hands-on and with a clear path forward.

1

Book

Assessment, 60 minutes

A plan, not a quick fix

Quick answers

Do I need a referral?

The clinic's site does not say a referral is required. If you have one, scan or history, email it to info@physiofixsydney.com.au. For anything else the team will confirm.

Is the session really one-on-one?

Yes. All consultations are private, with no shared rooms or handovers.

Can I choose who I see?

Yes. The online booking lets you pick your practitioner and a time, and manage or reschedule bookings.

Do you treat children and pregnancy pain?

Yes, both are listed services. Sara and Harry both work with children and adolescents.

What if I am not sure which appointment to book?

Call (02) 9371 2750. The admin team will guide you through the process and answer questions.

Do you work with other health professionals?

Harry's profile says he collaborates closely with surgeons, sports physicians and local GPs, and Alex works closely with the physiotherapists to bridge hands-on treatment and independent training.

Is there anything on Saturdays?

Yes, 7:00 am to 1:00 pm, exercise physiology only.

How do I send a referral or scan?

Email info@physiofixsydney.com.au and the team makes sure it is added to your file.
