Questions

What people ask first

Anything not listed here, the team will answer personally.

How do I start planning?

Write to yourtravelagent@sdqtravel.net, call 305 635 6314, or use the contact form on the agency website with a subject (Vacations, Honeymoon or Others). You can also leave your trip idea in this assistant and the team follows up.

Can you plan a destination wedding or honeymoon?

Yes. Destination weddings, honeymoons and romantic getaways are one of the agency's specialties, and the owner holds a weddings and honeymoons specialist certification.

Do you book cruises?

Yes. The agency books cruises for families, couples and anyone who wants to explore different ports or be pampered at sea.

Do you do all-inclusive resorts?

Yes. The agency finds an all-inclusive resort that suits you, which saves booking each part of the vacation separately.

Can you help with Europe trips?

Yes. Reviews mention hotels, transfers, tours and train and plane tickets between European cities arranged by the agency.

Do you speak Spanish?

The agency's website has Spanish content and the owner's profile is bilingual, so you can write to the team in Spanish. The team will confirm for your specific request.

What do trips cost?

It depends on destination, dates and group. The promotions page lists "from" figures for set itineraries; for anything else your agent prepares a quote.

Where are you?

Santo Domingo Travel Agency is in Miami. Contact the team for the visiting address and opening hours.
