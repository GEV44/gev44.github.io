Quick answers

Before you write

How fast does the school reply?

Student Support replies within one working day, in English, French, Italian, Spanish, German or Portuguese.

How big are the classes?

Sixteen students at most. Cambridge classes are 16 or fewer, and one student describes having two teachers per class with different methods.

Is there a certificate?

Yes. General English includes an end-of-course level certificate, plus a grammar, writing and speaking test and weekly progress tests.

Can I study as a group or through my company?

Mini-Stay Groups are closed classes for groups of 16 or more with dates and timetable set by you, and Corporate Training is a syllabus written for your team after a consultation, around 12 weeks.

When do I arrive and check in?

Host families prefer weekend arrivals and departures, with check-in up to 22:00; flight details are shared before arrival. The premium residence has Saturday check-in from 4:00 pm, and the shared apartments offer self check-in from 14:00 with instructions after confirmation.

Is there a dietary option with host families?

Yes. Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, lactose-free and other dietary requirements are accommodated on request, at €40 a week. Tell the team in advance so they can match you with the right family.

When can I start?

Courses start every Monday and can be booked for 1 to 25 weeks.

I do not know my level.

Take the free online test: about 15 minutes of grammar and a short piece of writing, which gives your CEFR level.

Does the school help with visas?

The site has a Study Visa FAQ about Stamp 2 and lists visa paperwork under support. The Academic Year course is 25 weeks with the IELTS fee and insurance included. For your own situation the team will confirm what applies.

Can I study in the evening only?

Yes. Evening General English and Evening IELTS run on Tuesday and Thursday from 18:30 to 20:30, for 4 hours a week. Evening Spanish and Irish run on Tuesdays.

Can the school arrange my accommodation?

Yes. Tell the team when you book the course; they will book it, or advise on where to find a place yourself.
