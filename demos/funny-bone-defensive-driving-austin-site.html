About

Texas defensive driving since 1994

Funny Bone Defensive Driving School calls itself the number 1 online fast defensive driving course in Texas. The site says it has served Texas for over 28 years and helped over 2 million students dismiss tickets.

It is a one stop shop: you can take the online course or attend a classroom, order your Texas driving record, and have your certificate delivered by E-File, PDF download or mail. The main office is in Round Rock, and support is available seven days a week.

Customers rate the course 4.9 on the home page, and the state licence is shown on the site so you can check it for yourself.

Three steps

From ticket to dismissal

  1. Register online

Questions

Straight answers

Is the course approved for ticket dismissal and insurance discounts?

Yes. The school is state approved by the Texas Education Agency and acceptable by all courts in Texas to dismiss traffic tickets. You may also lower insurance rates by up to 10 percent. Check with your insurance company.

How long does it take to complete?

All courses approved by the Texas Education Agency take 6 hours. You can do it in one sitting or a little at a time, with a required 60 minute break. You have up to 90 days to finish.

What if my internet connection fails?

When you log in again you are taken to where you left off. You may lose the time on the section you were viewing, typically just a few minutes.

My ticket is from another state. Am I eligible?

Rules vary from state to state on how often you can take a driving safety course and what it is for. Verify with your state or court before completing a course.

How do I receive my Certificate of Completion?

By US mail within a few business days. For faster delivery there are E-File and Instant Download options that deliver the same day.

Can I get the certificate overnight?

Yes, overnight delivery via FedEx is available for an additional $25.00. Call the office by 3:00 pm CST the same day with proof of completion, on 1-512-255-8515.

Where can I get my driving record?

You can skip the line at the DPS office and order your State Approved Type 3A driving record through the school, using the Order Driving Record page.

How can I confirm my registration?

After you fill out the online form you should receive an email confirming your registration. If you cannot find it, call 1-888-331-2113 or 512-255-8515.

What did classroom students bring?

Their driver licence, a copy of the ticket (or the name of the court to mail the certificate to, such as Austin Municipal Court), payment by check, cash or money order, and a great attitude.

Do you offer classroom courses?

The site says classroom courses are not being held at this time and recommends the online course.
