Good to know

Small inns work differently from big hotels. These are the policies guests ask about most.

Check-in

From 3:00 pm Monday to Friday, from 2:00 pm Saturday and Sunday. Early and late arrivals can be arranged. If no innkeeper is present, you receive check-in instructions by email or text.

Cancellation

2 days' notice. Stays of 3 or more rooms or 4 or more nights need 7 days, holiday bookings 14 days; otherwise the full amount applies.

Guests

No children under 7, for quiet enjoyment and because much of the furniture is antique. No pets; service animals are welcome as the law requires.

Breakfast

Not served since March 2020, and no lunch or dinner either. A dozen restaurants are within walking distance.

Accessibility

The inn is not ADA compliant: the first floor dates from before 1900, with steps to the entrance and two steps down to the dining room.

Front desk

Not staffed 24/7. The innkeepers are happy to help, so it suits guests who do not expect round-the-clock concierge service.

A 17% hotel occupancy tax is added to room rates. Minimum stays of 2 to 4 nights can apply on major holiday weekends and during big conferences.

A house with a story

The house was restored and converted to a bed and breakfast in the 1980s. It was featured on the Heights Historical Home Tour in May 1986 and opened the next month as Sara's Inn, named after the owner's youngest daughter. A second story with a turret and widow's walk was added in 1980.

Today the inn welcomes guests from all over: over 35 countries so far, from Argentina to South Korea.

House rules, in plain words

The inn is a historic building with antique furniture, so a few rules protect both the house and your neighbors in the next room.

  • VisitorsOnly with prior innkeeper approval
  • Candles and incenseNot allowed; fireworks too
  • Confetti and glitterNot allowed, rose petals included
  • NoiseGuests who disturb others may be asked to leave

The innkeepers cater to the comfort, privacy and security of registered guests. Negligent damage is assessed at actual documented cost, so please report spills or damage to the innkeepers promptly.

Housekeeping is daily, except Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, when extra towels are left the day before. A tip for the housekeeping staff is always appreciated.

Questions

The assistant in the corner knows these and more.

Is there parking?

Yes, free: 10 spaces on site and more along Heights Boulevard.

Is Wi-Fi included?

Yes, free Wi-Fi in every room, cable TV, and a guest computer on site.

Do you have a pool or restaurant?

No pool, and no on-site restaurant. The neighborhood has dozens of places to eat.

Is the inn good for business travelers?

The innkeepers host numerous business travelers every week. Just note there is no 24/7 front desk.

How are rooms cleaned?

Daily, by a long-serving housekeeping staff. Hang the doorknob sign to skip a visit and receive fresh towels in a basket.

Do I need a rental car?

It depends on your plans. Some guests rent cars, some use a cab when needed, and others visiting family in the Heights get picked up and dropped off. The airports are about 18 to 22 miles away and there is no shuttle.

Can the inn host an event?

Yes, for up to 75 people: weddings, showers, retreats, reunions and parties. Write to Events@saras.com.

Why choose an inn over a big hotel?

The inn describes itself as more comfortable and friendlier than a big hotel, with free parking and free internet, and a historic setting you will not find downtown.

Can I check in early or late?

Early and late check-ins can usually be arranged. Please inquire with the innkeepers before you arrive.

Are candles or confetti allowed?

No. Because of the historic building, candles, incense and fireworks are not allowed, and neither are confetti, glitter or rose petals.
