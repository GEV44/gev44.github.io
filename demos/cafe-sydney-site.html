About us

Cafe Sydney is a rooftop restaurant at Customs House with views of the Harbour Bridge and Sydney Opera House. A seasonal lunch and dinner menu is paired with an awarded Australian wine list, and the Lounge and Cocktail Bar offers drinks and light bites.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Can I book just for drinks or dessert?

Join us in the Lounge, no reservation required. Main dining room and terrace bookings are for a full dining experience.

Do you cater for allergies?

Yes, tell the reservations team your requirements before dining. Allergen-free cannot be guaranteed.

Can I bring a cake or BYO?

No outside cakes or BYO, but the pastry team offers celebration cakes.

Are children welcome?

Yes, with a children's menu for under 12s. Request a highchair when booking.

What happens if it rains on the terrace?

A heavy awning covers most of the terrace; in extreme weather moving inside depends on availability.
