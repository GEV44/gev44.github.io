Plain-English Houston terms

Two of the questions that come up most for buyers in Cypress, Katy and outlying areas, answered the way Kevan's FAQ answers them.

What is a MUD?

A Municipal Utility District: a political subdivision that provides water, sewer and drainage to areas outside city limits. Many newer Greater Houston subdivisions, especially in Cypress, Katy and outlying areas, sit in MUDs. They levy property taxes, so check the total rate (ISD + MUD + county + any other special districts), not just the listed tax line.

What is the MLS?

A database of properties for sale that brokers share with each other. Houston is served by the Houston Association of REALTORS® MLS, which feeds public sites like HAR.com and Zillow, though those often lag the live MLS by hours or days. Kevan's app uses the live feed.
