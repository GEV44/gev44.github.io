Welcome to the Blue Horizon

A locally owned and operated independent hotel in Vancouver, BC. The Blue Horizon has been awarded a 3 Green Key Eco-Rating by the Hotel Association of Canada, and a Certificate of Excellence by TripAdvisor.

The hotel sits between downtown and the West End neighbourhood. Most of its 31 floors have some of Vancouver's finest views, and the entrance to the hotel car park is right off Robson beside the building.

Good to know

What is check-in time?

Check-in begins at 3:00pm. If your room is not ready, the hotel is able to store your luggage. Early check-in requests cannot be guaranteed.

What is check-out time?

Check-out is 11:00am. The hotel is able to store your luggage. Late check-out requests cannot be guaranteed. Fees may apply.

Are guest rooms non-smoking?

The hotel is 100% non-smoking, including the guest room balconies.

Do you allow pet dogs?

The hotel is dog friendly. Please call 604-688-1411 or email info@bluehorizonhotel.com for more information.

Do you have accessible guest rooms?

Yes. Please contact the hotel directly to find out more and for availability.

Do you have playpens or cribs?

Playpens and cribs are available upon request and are not guaranteed. There are no rollaways or cots.

Is there parking?

Parking is available at the hotel; the entrance is right off Robson beside the hotel. The parking height is 6 feet 2 inches. Please stop beside the side entrance before parking and register your vehicle with the Front Desk; your licence plate number is required. Rates are subject to change, so the team confirms the current one.

Is there an airport shuttle?

No shuttle service. A taxi stand is right in front of the hotel. The SkyTrain departs the airport every 5 minutes and takes about 30 minutes to reach City Centre Station; the hotel is approximately 4 city blocks (12 minutes' walk) away.

How do I get to the cruise terminal, train or bus station?

Canada Place cruise terminal is about 10 blocks away and there is no shuttle, but a taxi runs there. The Via Rail station and bus terminal are about 3 km from the hotel.

What about local transit and bikes?

A transit stop is in front of the hotel; ask the Front Desk for SkyTrain and bus schedules. Hotel guests get discounts on bike rentals at SPOKES, and Mobi bike share is just around the corner.

What is the Green Key programme?

The Green Key Eco-Rating Program recognises hotels committed to improving the environment and sustainable practices across five areas: corporate environment management, housekeeping, food and beverage operations, conference and meeting facilities, and engineering. The Blue Horizon holds a 3 Green Key Rating and is a Silver Level hotel in the TripAdvisor Green Leaders programme.
