Quick answers

How do I pay and schedule?

Pay by card or by Zelle (Zelle prices are lower). After payment the school contacts you for lessons, or you call or text (954) 624-2872 to schedule your appointment.

Do the packages include the road test?

The 4, 6, 8 and 10 hour packages are listed with the road test at the school location. For the larger packs, the office will confirm what is included.

Does the Full License package include lessons?

No. It covers written test preparation, the written test, the road test and the school car for the road test. Lessons are separate.

Can I take the traffic courses from home?

Yes. They run online on a PC, tablet or phone, and the certificate of completion arrives right away by e-mail.

¿Hay servicio en español?

Sí. El sitio de la escuela está en inglés y español. Llame o escriba al +1 954 624 28 72.
