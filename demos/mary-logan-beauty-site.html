Questions

How do I book?

Online through the studio's booking page, by phone on 0452 090 983, or through the inquiry form or live chat on the website.

Are services only for women?

No. The studio offers a men's haircut and a reviewer who is a man recommends the brow and facial work.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, gift vouchers can be purchased through the booking page.

What are the opening hours?

They are not published on the site I read, so the team will confirm them. Booking online shows the available times.

Which treatment is right for me?

Mary explains each step during the visit, and questions about suitability for treatments such as microblading, lip blush or tattoo removal are best asked to her directly.
