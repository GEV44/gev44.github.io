Good to know

Short answers from the school's FAQ and lesson policies.

Is there an age restriction for the knowledge exam?

You must be at least 15. Applicants under 18 may take the knowledge exam online through the website.

Must I hold a permit to schedule lessons?

Yes. By law a student must at least hold a valid Florida learner's permit.

What if I fail?

The knowledge exam can be retaken up to 3 times on the same day. The driving exam can be retaken 24 hours after each attempt. Attempts 4 and 5 are only at the Driver License Office.

Can you get me a Driver License Office appointment?

No. The state only allows the school to administer the knowledge and driving exams. Obtaining the appointment is the applicant's (or parent's) responsibility.

Do you teach manual transmission, CDL or motorcycles?

No manual transmission, no CDL classes (some CDL-related online courses exist) and no motorcycle classes.

Which languages can I take the driving exam in?

English, Spanish and Russian. The written test is available only in English.

Lesson policies

Packages are prepaid and valid for 90 days. Reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours before, or a $100 fee applies. Lessons aren't cancelled for weather except hurricanes or tornadoes. Expect a call after your first appointment to go over details.

BDI and ADI courses (online) and 3-in-3 Behind The Wheel Training for 3 crashes in 3 years.
