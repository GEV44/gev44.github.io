FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What is your cancellation policy?

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy. If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice you are charged 50% of the service price, and a no-show is charged 100%.

How often does blonde need a refresh?

Blonde usually needs a refresh every 10-16 weeks to keep its brightness, and balayage grows out softly so touch-ups are easier. Your stylist will recommend the right schedule.

Do you offer color correction?

Yes, advanced color correction and total hair makeovers. The price depends on the complexity, and a complimentary consultation is offered.

Can you service extensions from another salon?

No. Our stylists only service extensions that our team supplied and installed. We do offer a removal service for your current extensions.

Is parking nearby?

There is ample parking nearby, and the salon is steps from the Merchandise Mart.

Do you have gift cards?

Yes, gift cards are available on our website.
