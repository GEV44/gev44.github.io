How sessions work

The studio's own rules, so there are no surprises on day one.

How does scheduling work?

Regularly scheduled appointments can be arranged. Appointments can be made by the week or by the day.

What is the cancellation policy?

24 hours in advance for cancellations, or you are charged in full for the session. No-shows are charged for the session.

What about renewals?

The credit card on file is charged automatically for another package unless you notify the studio before your last session. Invoices on account status are available on request.

How long do packages last?

Clients have up to one year to use a package of sessions. Sessions are non-refundable and transferable.

Do I get one trainer?

Trainers work on a rotation with Hanson Fitness clients, because different approaches and training styles help. If you want one specific trainer exclusively, the Exclusive Rates apply.

Can I lift without a trainer?

No, weight lifting is only with a scheduled personal training appointment. Cardio equipment can be used any time during normal business hours at no extra charge.
