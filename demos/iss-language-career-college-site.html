Starting is three steps

If you are a new student, contact the team before you register: info@lcc.issbc.org or 604-684-2325.

1 Pick a course

Read the program page and check its entrance requirements.

2 Show your level

Send official scores (IELTS, TOEFL, CELPIP or CLBPT) or take the free online English assessment, about 1 hour.

3 Register

Online, or in person by appointment. A Letter of Acceptance is available if you need to apply for a study permit.

Free online English assessment

Use it as proof of English proficiency for an LCC application. The account works once, and results are emailed to you.

About 1 hourFreeOnline

Official scores may be enough: CLBPT results are good for 1 year, TOEFL, CELPIP and IELTS for 2 years.

CLB Placement Test (CLBPT)

Task-based test of reading, writing, listening and speaking, levels 1 to 8. In person at the college, 1.5 to 2 hours, with the report for all four skills straight after.

TESOL certificates

The 8-week online TESOL certificate is 128 hours of video lessons and activities, with live online classes Tuesdays and Thursdays 6:30 to 9:30pm (attendance is mandatory). It includes a practicum. A 12-week TESOL and Leadership certificate is the broader option.

StudentAid BC funding may be available to eligible domestic students for some programs.

Studying on a visitor visa

With a visitor visa you can study at LCC for up to 6 months. Programs longer than 6 months need a study permit. New study permit applications currently need a provincial attestation letter, so talk to the team early.

Common questions


When do classes start?

Classes start every week, depending on seat availability.

What if I do not know my English level?

Take the free online assessment, or send official test scores. Classes cover all levels from beginner to advanced.

Is there part-time study?

Yes. Mornings 9:30am to 12pm Monday to Thursday, afternoons 12:30 to 3pm or 4 to 6pm Monday to Thursday, and evenings 6:30 to 8:55pm Tuesday and Thursday. Full-time courses run four or five days a week.

Which languages can the office help in?

English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish and Vietnamese.

I was in a LINC class. Is there a path to LCC?

The college has a page for students affected by LINC Stage 2 funding changes, mentioning a tuition discount and monthly payment options. The team confirms eligibility and details.
