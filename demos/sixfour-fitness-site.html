Questions

Before you book

Is it only for women?

Yes. SixFour is small-group personal training built exclusively for women, with coaching and programming designed around women.

I've never trained before. Is that okay?

Very. Plenty of members started as beginners, and the coaches build your confidence with the equipment from the first session.

How many people are in a session?

No more than six per coach for small-group PT. SWEAT HIIT classes run at 8:1.

What happens after the Kickstart?

You can move onto flexible monthly membership options. The team talks them through on your free discovery call.

How do I book sessions?

Once you've enrolled, you choose your slots in the booking app: morning, lunchtime or evening, Monday to Saturday.
