FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How do I choose a stylist?

Take the two-minute Find your stylist quiz, browse the team page, or tell the front desk about your hair and goals and they will match you.

Can I book hair extensions online?

No. Extensions start with a consultation and application isn't bookable online, so call (305) 442-8136 and the front desk will book your consultation.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations more than 24 hours before your appointment have no fee. Within 24 hours there is a 50% cancellation fee and a no-show is charged 100%. Hair extension applications need 48 hours' notice.

How much do services cost?

Prices depend on the service, your artist and your hair's length and density. You see prices when you book online, and your artist confirms the final price at your appointment.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Call (305) 442-8136 to buy one, or buy one in person at the salon.

Where is Portfolio Salon?

At 281 Miracle Mile, a short walk from 155 Miracle Mile. It is open Tuesday to Friday 9am-7pm, Saturday 9am-6pm and Sunday 10am-6pm, and closed Monday.
