Up to $1,000 per child from Medicare (a Medicare letter is needed). A child is eligible if they are:

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to mention an offer when booking?

Yes. The online offers say to mention the special when you book. The $99 hygiene & clean is for new patients on weekdays and cannot be combined with other offers.

What does the $99 clean include?

A standard hygiene cleaning. Deep scaling and gum treatments are not included.

Who can use the children’s benefit?

Children aged 2–17 who are Medicare-eligible and in a family receiving Family Tax Benefit A or certain government payments. Eligibility is assessed at the start of each year.

Is there an emergency service?

Yes, during clinic hours. Several spots are reserved every day. Call the clinic and describe the situation to the team.

Is the website available in Chinese?

Yes, the clinic website has a Chinese version.
