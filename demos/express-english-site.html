About

Welcome to Express English

Express English Language Training Center offers English courses in Dubai to help individuals and families hone their writing and speaking skills. The institute is accredited by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) and is an official IELTS test partner, partnered with IDP Dubai.

The English language centre provides spoken English courses, private English lessons, business English courses, IELTS training, PTE training and expert training for other exams.

The curriculum

From the basics to phonetics

The curriculum starts with the basics of English: understanding and producing correct sentences. It then moves to more advanced topics such as phonetics and written comprehension. Students learn everything from verbs and adjectives to idioms and proverbs through real-life examples.

Lessons should be fun. Enjoyment is more of a focus in the spoken English classes, but IELTS and PTE preparation courses are meant to be fun too.

FAQ

Quick answers

Do you provide certificates?

Yes. Express English provides KHDA accredited certificates for each language level passed.

Are the teachers native speakers?

The institute guarantees native English teachers, mostly British and American, so students get used to the accents they will meet in IELTS and PTE.

Which IELTS version should I choose?

General Training is commonly required for visa and immigration applications. Academic is mainly used for university entrance or employment. Your tutor also helps you choose between paper-based and computer-delivered IELTS.

Can I study with friends or colleagues?

Yes. Semi-private lessons are available for a small group who want to study together.

When are the classes?

IELTS classes run on weekday mornings, evenings or weekends. For other courses, the team will confirm current timings.

What do the courses cost?

Prices are not published on the page you are viewing. The team will confirm them for your course and branch.
