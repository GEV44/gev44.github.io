Three things that stop people booking

The practice says the main barriers to starting treatment are fear, time and money, and that it works hard to find a way around each one.

FearNervous about the dentist? There is a page on dental anxiety, and the team says it understands the stress that often sits behind long-term dental problems.
TimeThe schedule keeps spaces open for emergencies, and an online appointment book lets you lock in a time without making an account.
MoneyHICAPS claiming on the spot, and plans that spread larger treatment over months, with interest-free options.

What the practice offers

Grouped the way the website groups them. Details of any treatment are confirmed by the team at your visit.

  • Rebuilding teeth and oral rehabilitation
  • Dental crowns, inlays and onlays

The focus stated on the site is keeping your remaining teeth in place and trouble free.
Gum care and hygienists
Dental hygienists Steph and Ros look after cleaning and gum care. The practice says prevention of disease is its primary focus.
Dental emergencies
Pain, accidents, a broken tooth, or a crown that falls off before a big day. Spaces are left in the schedule for emergencies without bumping regular patients. After hours, there is an Emergency eForm that the practice says is monitored very regularly.
Removing teeth, snoring and sleep apnoea
Tooth removal and sleep apnoea each have their own page on the website. The team will confirm what is suitable for you.

Quick answers

Do I need an account to book online?
No. The online appointment book does not need an account or sign-in. If you cannot find a suitable time, the request form takes two preferred times.
What if I have a dental emergency?
Call (03) 9867 1252 during opening hours. After hours, use the Emergency eForm on the website.
Do you do anything about dental anxiety?
The website has a dental anxiety page. Tell the team when you book and they will talk it through.
Can I pay it off over time?
Payment plans exist. Ask Mel or Ethan, the treatment co-ordinators, about the right plan for your treatment.
