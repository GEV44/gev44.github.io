Where you will live

Two options for adult students, both bookable with the course.

Host family

For students aged 16 and over, available all year. Single rooms, half board; twin or double rooms for friends or couples travelling together.

  • Arrival and departure: Saturday to Saturday or Sunday to Sunday
  • Suburban Dublin, around 60 minutes by public transport
  • Host families are police vetted and inspected by Apollo management

City centre residence

Single en-suite rooms at Canvas Point Campus, Point Village, Dublin 1, for students aged 18 and over. It is an independent residence that Apollo partners with.

  • Arrival and departure: Sunday to Sunday
  • Gym, fitness studio, rooftop gardens and study pods

Beyond the classroom

The school runs a social programme of outings to Irish attractions, bookable in the Apollo Student App, and a Jobs Club with free workshops every two weeks on getting a PPS number, writing a CV and interview skills.

Common questions

Do I need to be a certain level?

No. Real World English starts every Monday and is open to all levels from beginner to advanced. IELTS Focus needs a minimum level, and the school has a placement test.

How old do students have to be?

The adult school takes students aged 16 and over all year. From 30 May to 29 August, 15-year-olds can join the Young Adult programme.

What are the class hours?

20-hour courses run 09.00 to 13.15, Monday to Friday.

Does Apollo help with visas?

The school's courses are on the ILEP list, which is the list of visa-eligible courses for non-EEA students. Check your own case with the team.

Is there help finding a job?

Yes, the Jobs Club runs free workshops every two weeks: PPS number, CV, job search and interview skills.
