Getting here

40-47 Fleet Street, Temple Bar, Dublin 2. The airport is about 25 to 40 minutes away depending on traffic and transport.

Aircoach 700

Up to every 15 minutes, 24/7, both terminals, 25 to 35 minutes. Get off at O'Connell Bridge and walk south to Fleet Street.

Dublin Express

Every 30 minutes, 24/7, both terminals. Get off at Eden Quay and walk across O'Connell Bridge.

Taxi or transfer

Taxis wait outside the airport, or pre-book a private transfer with partner Welcome Pickups to skip the queue. Airlink and the number 16 bus stop close by.

Driving? The Fleet Street car park is 30 metres from the door, with a guest offer: ask for a discount card at reception.

Quick answers

The assistant at the bottom right can answer more.

Do I need ID?

Yes, a passport, national ID or driving licence for every guest. The minimum age is 18 unless staying with an adult family member or guardian.

What is the cancellation policy?

Non-refundable bookings are charged on booking day with no changes. Flexible bookings are paid on arrival, with cancellation at least 48 hours before arrival. A no-show is charged the first night.

Can I smoke?

Not inside, by law. Use the outdoor smoking area on the terrace.

Are group bookings possible?

Yes. Write to reservations@templebarinn.com and the team will organise it.

Is breakfast included?

The site says every direct booking includes a complimentary buffet breakfast. For other rates the team will confirm.

Where can I leave my luggage?

There is a complimentary luggage room in reception. Leave bags before check-in or after check-out.

How far is the airport?

About 25 to 40 minutes depending on traffic and transport. Aircoach 700 and Dublin Express both run 24/7 from both terminals.

Is there parking?

The Fleet Street car park is 30 metres from the door. Guests get a special offer, so ask for a discount card at reception.

Is there accessible accommodation?

Yes: wheelchair accessible rooms on each floor close to the two lifts, an entrance on the ground floor and a fully accessible restaurant. Tell the team before you arrive.

Can I book a tour through the inn?

Yes, the 24 hour tour desk advises on and books tours in Dublin and across Ireland.

Can I hire the bar for an event?

Yes: up to 50 seated or 70 standing. Email events@templebarinn.com.

Is there Wi-Fi throughout?

Yes, fast free Wi-Fi throughout the building at all times.
