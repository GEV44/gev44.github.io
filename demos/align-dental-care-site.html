Coverage and paying

The clinic keeps costs simple and gives clear estimates before treatment begins.

Canada Dental Care PlanCDCP is accepted. The team explains how coverage works and how it may coordinate with other benefits, and helps with the paperwork.
InsuranceAll major dental insurance is accepted, with direct billing to many providers. Bring your benefits card and photo ID.
Pre-determinationUnsure how your plan applies? The team can request a pre-determination and give you a written estimate to review at home.
FinancingPayment plans through iFinance Canada (Dentalcard) with terms based on credit approval. Your insurance applies first.
