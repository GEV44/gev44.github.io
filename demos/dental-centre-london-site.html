About us

Established for more than 65 years, The Dental Centre is a modern Denplan-accredited dental practice in the heart of Euston, London. It offers everything from a simple dental health examination to complex restorative and cosmetic dentistry, from a team of specialists.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How much is a new patient examination?

£69, and a routine examination is £61.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

Yes, call the practice and the team will arrange the earliest available appointment. A private emergency consultation is £40.

Can I spread the cost of treatment?

Yes, finance plans with interest-free payments are available.

Where are you?

139 Euston Road, opposite the British Library, between Euston and St Pancras/King's Cross stations.

Do you work with UCL students?

Yes, the practice has supported UCL staff and students for over 60 years.
