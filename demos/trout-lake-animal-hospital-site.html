Emergency & urgent care in detail

Emergencies can be a terrible experience for pets and pet owners, so we promise to communicate with you and make the process less stressful.

Emergency means

Things like accidental ingestion of rat poison, being hit by a car, or chocolate ingestion. If you ever feel your pet needs emergency treatment, do not hesitate to call or come in immediately.

Urgent means

The condition is not life-threatening, but you feel your pet needs to be seen before you can get an appointment. Our staff will work to accommodate you in between scheduled appointments.

What we can do

  • Emergency surgery
  • Hospitalization
  • Vital sign monitoring
  • Intravenous fluids
  • Supportive care
  • Blood transfusions and plasma
  • On-site diagnostics and bloodwork

After hours

For emergencies occurring after our hours, please go to your nearest emergency vet hospital. We are proud to offer pet families in our community an extra resource for extended-hours treatment.

Quick answers

Do I need an appointment?
No — walk-ins are welcome all day. Appointments are always preferred and take precedence over walk-ins.
Which pets do you treat?
We treat dogs and cats.
What if my pet has an emergency?
Please call (604) 559-1800 and we will do our best to help you through it. Emergency and urgent care is available during business hours; for emergencies after hours, go to your nearest emergency vet hospital.
When can I drop off my pet for surgery or a dental?
Drop-off timing for all surgeries, dental procedures, ultrasounds and medical procedures is 8am – 9am.
Can I book online?
Yes — through the Vello patient portal on our Appointments page, by requesting an appointment on the website, or by phone.
Is there a fee if I miss an appointment?
The website lists a $50 no-show fee.
