Think it might be an emergency? Call first.

Calling lets the staff get ready for your arrival. The hospital handles emergencies during regular office hours. Outside those hours, please contact an emergency animal hospital.

  • Allergic reactions, such as facial swelling or hives after a sting or vaccine
  • Heat stroke: spray with cool (not cold) water and come in right away
  • Poisoning, such as medications, chocolate, plants or cleaners, where timing is crucial
  • Animal attacks or bites with active bleeding: apply direct pressure with a clean dry bandage

Why the dental cleaning matters

Bad breath is not normal for a dog. It comes from bacteria in the mouth, and dental disease can release bacteria into the bloodstream.

Regular cleanings reduce the need for costly treatment later, such as extractions and oral surgery. Cleanings are done under general anesthesia, with blood tests beforehand to uncover hidden illness and constant monitoring during the procedure.

Good questions


How often should my pet have an exam?

Yearly for most pets, at least every six months for older pets. Puppies and kittens are seen more often in their first year. For pets over age 7 the hospital recommends exams and blood work every six months.

Why does my pet need a dental cleaning?

Bad breath is caused by bacteria, and dental disease can release bacteria into the bloodstream. A thorough cleaning needs general anesthesia, with blood tests beforehand to uncover hidden illness.

At what age should I spay or neuter?

The hospital recommends waiting until at least six months of age and discussing specifics for species, breed and size with the team.

My pet’s behavior changed. Should I come in?

Changes in appetite, energy, aggression, elimination or vocalizing can signal a problem, so contact the hospital for an exam right away.

How do I prevent heartworm?

Prevention is year-round, 12 months of the year. Dogs are tested annually. Cats should be tested before starting prevention, because there is no treatment for heartworm in cats.

Can I order food and medication online?

Yes. The website has an Order Food and Meds link and a pet portal.
