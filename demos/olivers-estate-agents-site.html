Questions people ask first

Is the valuation really free?

Yes. The valuation is free and carries no obligation, whether you are thinking of selling, letting, refinancing or just curious.

What does "no sale, no fee" mean?

For sales, Olivers works on a no sale, no fee basis, and the standard agreement includes a one-day lock-in period. For anything about fee levels, the team will confirm directly.

Where are my sale properties advertised?

On Olivers.ie, Daft.ie and MyHome.ie, with high-quality presentation to attract the right buyers.

Can you manage my rental if I live abroad?

Yes. Olivers works with Irish and non-resident landlords, including pension fund property owners.

Who handles repairs?

The team is the main contact for tenants and coordinates maintenance with trusted contractors. For major works you are consulted in advance.

What happens at the end of a tenancy?

The team coordinates key return, final inspection, utility readings and inventory checks, assesses any repairs and advises on deposit deductions where applicable, so it is handled fairly and transparently. They can also arrange cleaning, repairs and preparation for the next tenants.

How are tenants chosen?

All applicants are vetted, including reference checks and, where required, guarantors. The team then prepares the lease, tenancy documents and inventory report, hands over the keys and provides property and utility information on move-in.

Which areas do you cover?

South Dublin, County Dublin and North Wicklow, from the Sandyford office. Recent listings have included apartments in Tallaght, Ranelagh and Stillorgan.

Do you only work with large landlords?

No. Whether you own a single rental or a large portfolio, the service is tailored to you, including full management or support with specific parts of lettings.

Do you handle commercial property?

Yes: offices, retail units, mixed-use property, investment assets and development opportunities, plus commercial valuations.
