Our story

Opened in 1978. Still the standard.

“You could find yourself shoulder-pressing beside Arnold Schwarzenegger, Tom Platz and Dorian Yates.”

City Gym opened in 1978, the same year as the first Sydney Gay and Lesbian Mardi Gras protest. Through the 70s and 80s it became the cultural and social hub for the LGBTQI+ community, a haven for minorities, outsiders and all gender identities, and a mecca for the sporting elite that never became pretentious or intimidating.

Today that spirit is the same: a warm, inclusive atmosphere where weightlifters, yogis and group-class regulars share one supportive gym.

Quick answers

Is there a free pass?

Yes, the website offers a free pass (for Australian residents only) and a free try of each class.

How much is membership?

From $24.95 per week. The team confirms the plan details when you enquire.

Are classes included?

Unlimited classes are part of the offer: City Spin, Reformer Pilates, City HIIT, Mat Pilates, City Strength, City Box and Livestrong.

I can’t book a class. Who helps?

Existing members can call or see City Gym reception.

Is it beginner friendly?

City Gym describes itself as an inclusive gym for all backgrounds and levels, with coaches to guide you.
