Three ways to get a cut

Every cut includes a wash, a scalp massage and a styled finish. Fades are available with select stylists at each location; straight razoring is not currently offered.

Short cut

Cropped cuts with scissor over comb and clippers: short back and sides, fades, clipper cuts.

Detailed short

Short to medium styles needing extra attention, or thicker hair: sharp lines, blunt edges, mullets, micro bobs, pageboys.

Long cut

Medium to long hair or hair below the ear: bobs and lobs without heavy detail, with layering for shape and movement.

Before you come in

The questions guests ask us most.

Cancelling or running late?

Please cancel 48 hours ahead if you can (24 at the latest). No-shows, late cancellations and late reschedules carry a 50% fee. There is a 15-minute grace window; after that the appointment is forfeited.

Will my card be charged when I book?

No deposit is taken unless it's an unusual situation. Your card is only charged on a no-show or a cancellation inside the 24-hour window.

Washing hair around colour

Don't wash before a colour service unless your hair is extremely oily. After colour, wait 48 hours and use a colour-safe shampoo.

Kids, dogs and access

Select stylists cut children's hair. Well-behaved, quiet, friendly dogs may be allowed and service animals are always welcome. The salon is wheelchair accessible.

Gift certificates

Available by e-mail, phone or in person. They are final sale, with no refunds.

If something isn't right

Every case is handled individually: an adjustment, a complimentary re-service, or, if nothing else works, a refund.
