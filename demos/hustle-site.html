The LU Method

Prenatal and postpartum classes for mamas. Carefully designed across fitness modalities to build trust in the body, strength in the mind and support for the baby.

Babies welcome

You are welcome to bring babies to postpartum sessions. Bouncers, rockers, exersaucers, Jolly Jumpers and toys are available as needed. Babies can attend until they are crawling.

Late or leaving early

For prenatal and postpartum classes the coaches can help with late entry or leaving early, for the comfort of mama and baby.

Please check with your medical professional first. For which class fits you, speak with a Hustle coach.

Questions

Are there beginner classes?

Beginners are welcome in every class, so there are no beginner-only classes. Arrive 15 minutes early on your first visit.

How often should I come?

3 to 5 classes a week is recommended for real change, but there is no magic number. Rest matters too, especially if you are new.

How long until I see results?

About 2 weeks of 3 to 5 classes a week to feel motivated and confident; a consistent routine over 2 to 3 months optimizes results.

Packs or membership?

There are two options: packs of classes, or a membership. Check the pricing page; it depends on how often you plan to come. Student pricing is available for full-time students under 25.

Can I freeze my membership?

In general, if you commit to a month or more you can freeze for a doctor's order, pregnancy, extended travel, job loss or bereavement. Memberships are non-transferable; class packs can be shared.

Should I eat before class?

A small snack of about 200 to 300 calories an hour before, with protein and carbs.
