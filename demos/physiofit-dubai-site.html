Fine, sterile needles are inserted into myofascial trigger points, the tight knots in muscle that cause pain and restrict movement. The clinic says most patients feel only a brief twitch or dull ache. Good to know

Quick answers

Can I book a consultation first?

The clinic invites you to use the booking form or WhatsApp to book an obligation-free consultation.

How long is a sports massage?

Sessions run 45 to 60 minutes, focus on the areas that need it most, and pressure is always adapted to your tolerance. Many patients book a regular weekly or monthly maintenance massage.

Is chiropractic treatment painful?

The clinic describes chiropractic as non-surgical, non-invasive and non-painful, using gentle specific manipulations. Some people feel mild soreness afterwards, which the clinic says typically fades within 24 hours.

What if I need more than massage?

The massage therapists work alongside the physiotherapists and chiropractor. If something needs more than massage, such as a joint issue or nerve irritation, you are already in the right clinic.
