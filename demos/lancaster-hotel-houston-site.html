About the hotel and its history

The Lancaster is a small, historic luxury hotel built in 1926 in downtown Houston's Theater District. In 2026 it celebrates 100 years of hospitality, and it calls itself Houston's longest continuously operating hotel. It has welcomed actors, musicians, dancers, writers, newlyweds, circus and rodeo performers, and even a horse.

What guestrooms are there?

The hotel has 93 guestrooms, suites and public spaces across 12 floors. Room types include the Petite King, Classic King, Deluxe King, Deluxe Double Queen, Classic Penthouse King and Deluxe Penthouse King. The team will confirm which type fits your dates.

What is in the rooms?

Rooms include complimentary in-room snacks and beverages, Nespresso coffee, a complimentary shoe shine, Frette bedding, Bulgari bath products, Waterworks fixtures, a 55-inch smart TV, premium high-speed wireless internet and turndown service. A welcome glass of Champagne or sparkling water is offered on arrival.

Is breakfast included?

A complimentary gourmet breakfast buffet is served daily on the mezzanine from 6:30am to 10:30am for hotel guests.

When is Cultivated F+B open?

Cultivated F+B is the on-site contemporary American restaurant, open seven days a week. Breakfast 6:30am to 11:00am. Lunch Monday to Friday 11:00am to 4:00pm. Dinner Monday to Saturday 4:00pm to 10:00pm, Sunday 4:00pm to 9:00pm. Brunch Saturday and Sunday 11:00am to 4:00pm. Reservations are highly recommended, and there is a sidewalk dining area.

How does Afternoon Tea work?

Afternoon Tea is a three-course seasonal menu of tea sandwiches, fresh baked scones with Devonshire cream, housemade jam and lemon curd, and patisseries. Seatings are Saturday and Sunday at 12:00 p.m. and 2:30 p.m. 2026 seasonal dates: Saint Valentine's Feb 7-15, Autumn Sep 26-Nov 22, Holiday Nov 28-Dec 27. For parties of six or more or a private tea, email tea@thelancaster.com.

Tell me about the art collection

The hotel curates over 200 contemporary works by more than 100 Texas artists, including Mark Flood, Terrell James, Trenton Doyle Hancock, Aaron Parazette and Margo Sawyer, with pieces by Donald Judd, Donald Moffett, Robert Rauschenberg and James Surls, shown in the guestrooms, suites and public spaces.

Where is the hotel?

The Lancaster is at 701 Texas Avenue, Houston, Texas 77002, at the corner of Texas Avenue and Louisiana Street. It is the only hotel in the Theater District, directly across from Jones Hall and the Alley Theatre. Wortham Center is 0.1 miles away and the Hobby Center is a short stroll.

How far are the airports and attractions?

Distances from the hotel: Houston Bush Intercontinental (IAH) 20 miles; Houston Hobby (HOU) 11 miles; Houston Convention Center 0.9 miles; Museum District 3.1 miles; Texas Medical Center 5.4 miles; University of Houston 4.6 miles.

Is there parking?

Overnight valet parking for hotel guests is available 24 hours a day for a daily fee. Cultivated F+B valet and Theater District valet parking are available for an hourly fee.
