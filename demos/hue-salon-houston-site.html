FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Where do I park?

The Montrose Collective garage offers up to two hours of validated parking. Valet is available from 11:00 AM for $10.

How much do services cost?

Starting prices: haircuts $89+, single-process color $87+, color refresh/glaze $44+, blowouts $64+, Brazilian Blowout $333+, Kérastase Fusio-Dose $43+. Balayage, highlights, corrections and extensions are priced by result.

What is your cancellation policy?

24 hours' notice is required (48 hours for multiple services). Within 24 hours the fee is 50% of the scheduled services; a no-call, no-show is 100%. A credit card is needed on file to book.

Do you offer hair extensions?

Yes, keratin fusion and weft extensions, always starting with a personalized consultation.

What color products do you use?

L'Oréal Professionnel, including INOA, Majirel, Dia Color, Dia Light and Blond Studio lighteners.

What if I arrive late?

If you are more than ten minutes late, we may have to modify your service or have another available hairstylist complete it.
