Essential Driver Training

12 lessons. One approved instructor.

EDT is set by the RSA and every first-time Irish learner permit holder has to complete it before the driving test. Lessons 1 to 8 go in order; 9 to 12 can be taken in any order. Each lesson is one hour and your instructor signs it off on the RSA system.

1Car controls and safety checks
2Correct positioning
3Changing direction
4Progression management
5Positioning, more complex (reversing and parking starts)
6Anticipation and reaction
7Sharing the road
8Driving safely through traffic
9Changing direction, more complex
10Speed management
11Driving calmly
12Night driving
Lessons 1-8, in orderLessons 9-12, any order

Holding a recognised foreign licence? You may only need a reduced 6-lesson EDT. The team can tell you what applies to you.

Common questions

Before you book

Do I need EDT for an automatic licence?

Yes. EDT is required whether you learn manual or automatic. The 12 lessons are identical in structure; only the car changes.

Can I switch instructor mid-course?

Yes. Your EDT logbook transfers between any RSA-approved instructor and each lesson is signed off individually.

How long should I leave between lessons?

There is no minimum gap. Most learners do one a week, some two, and the full course often takes 3 to 6 months.

What if my learner permit expires during EDT?

You need a valid permit for every lesson. Renew it before the next session; completed lessons stay on record.

Do you charge extra for city centre pickups?

No. The rate is the same in Tallaght, Swords, the city centre or anywhere else in Dublin.

Can I get a refund or make a complaint?

Yes, both go through the contact page. Refunds are reviewed within 48 hours and a manager replies to complaints within 24 hours.
