Straight answers on what they do and do not do

Worth knowing before you write, so nobody wastes a week.

They do

  • Plan with no extra fee: paid through their travel partners and suppliers (100% commission-based)
  • Help while you travel, and before and after
  • Book flights as part of a trip with hotels, resorts, excursions or transport
  • Review each inquiry case by case, with no strict minimum spend

They do not

  • Book Airbnb or VRBO, as those platforms do not partner with advisors
  • Book flights only, or flights for groups
  • Book stays paid with points (flights with points are fine)
  • Compete with another agency’s quote for the same trip

Frequently asked

Taken from the agency’s own FAQ page.

Do you charge a fee?

No. They are 100% commission-based and get paid through their travel partners and suppliers, so planning is at no additional cost to you.

Do you have a minimum spend?

Not a strict one. Each inquiry is reviewed case by case, considering trip length, season, destination and number of travellers. As a luxury-focused agency they are best suited to clients seeking elevated travel; a strictly budget-focused trip may not be the right match.

Can you give me an itemized cost breakdown?

Their system does not show an itemized breakdown for packages or multi-part trips. Share a total budget or range and they will work to stay within it.

Can you help me while I am traveling?

Yes. Please review your itinerary carefully before you go, since time differences mean they may not always be able to answer instantly.

I only need flights. Can you book them?

No. Airlines do not pay travel agents a commission, so flights are arranged only alongside other trip components. They do not book flights for groups.

Do you book with points?

Not for accommodations. Booking flights with points is fine, and you can ask the team whether your trip still fits.

Are you a wedding planner?

No, they are wedding travel planners. They may be able to put you in touch with someone at your destination, and they shine at finding the resort and rooms for your whole party.
