Questions

Manual or automatic?

Both. Lessons in the instructor's car are manual only. For automatic lessons you use your own car.

What is EDT?

Essential Driver Training: the mandatory 12-lesson programme for learner drivers in Ireland. The school offers structured packages that cover all required modules.

How long are lessons?

Standard lessons are 60 minutes. Pre-test sessions are 90 minutes to cover test routes thoroughly.

When are you available?

Flexible weekday and evening slots. Send your preferred times and the team will find one that suits.

Do you offer weekend lessons?

Yes, subject to availability. Weekend slots are in high demand and priced at a premium rate, so contact the team for current rates.

Do you provide a car for the test?

Yes. Three options: meet at the test centre, local pick-up and drop-off, or car hire plus a pre-test lesson. The car is fully insured and roadworthy.
