01 / The model

Most physical therapy is three patients an hour. This one isn't.

You will not be left on a table with a heat pack while somebody else runs the room. Your plan is built around what your body is actually doing, not printed off a sheet for your diagnosis.

A proper first look

How far the joint moves, how strong it is, how much it can handle, and how everything around it is working.

A plan on day one

Exercises start at the first visit. You leave knowing what to do at home and why it helps.

Hands-on treatment

Massage, joint work and soft-tissue release to get the movement back. The exercises make it stay.

Progress you can see

Goals set on day one and checked again, tracked and logged every session.

Machines help, they don't do the work

Electrical stimulation, TENS and hot or cold packs support the treatment.

What a first visit looks like

From the first call to a plan in your hands

  1. You call, someone answers

    A couple of minutes about what happened and what you want back. You will know before you hang up whether this is the right place.

  2. A proper first look

    Range of motion, strength, load tolerance and the tissues around the sore spot, not just the spot that hurts.

  3. Your plan, that day

    Exercises start at the first visit, with what to do at home and why it helps.

  4. Hands-on work

    Massage, joint work and soft-tissue release where it helps, with machines only in a supporting role.

  5. Measured again

    Clear goals from day one, checked later. Progress is tracked and logged every session.

  6. Keep it from coming back

    At the end you know what to keep doing so the problem does not return.

Older patients

Rebuilding the confidence to walk without a stick. The founder leads the Geriatrics group of the Florida Physical Therapy Association.

Weekend athletes

Keeping training through an injury, then returning on test results rather than on the calendar.

Pro-level demands

The founder has worked with NFL players and UFC fighters. Different needs, same approach: find it, work on it, measure it again.

    03 / What you can book

    Four ways in. One clinician throughout.

    Service 01

    Personalized physical therapy

    The heart of the practice. One-to-one sessions built around your problem and your goal, so you get back to what you want to do.

    • A full one-to-one visit, every time
    • Your exercise plan on the first day
    • Massage, joint work, soft-tissue release
    • Goals tracked and logged each session
    • E-stim, TENS, hot or cold when they help
    • What to keep doing once you finish
    Service 02

    Strength & conditioning

    Getting stronger, safely, with someone who knows what your joints can take. Built to become a habit rather than a six-week burst.

    • Training in the clinic, with a plan written for you
    • Train on your own schedule at your gym, with text access to ask questions
    • Prehab: screening to spot what is most likely to injure you
    Service 03

    Sports injury rehab

    Back to competing with confidence. Testing says when you are ready, while the rest of you keeps training so you do not lose the fitness you built.

    • Field sports
    • Court sports
    • Water sports
    • Combat sports
    • Return to play is based on test results, not the calendar
    Service 04

    One-on-one Pilates

    Taught one-to-one by a Polestar-educated instructor who is also a doctor of physical therapy, at whatever level you walk in with.

    • Mat, reformer, trapeze and spine corrector in the studio
    • Chair and ladder barrel coming soon
    • Adapted for long-standing back pain or thinning bones
    • All levels, including first-timers
