Questions

Before you book.

What is Korean manual therapy?

It is rooted in traditional Korean healing methods and modern anatomical science, relying on a therapist's refined tactile perception to guide treatment with minimal force and maximum precision.

Can I book on WhatsApp?

Yes, the clinic takes reservations through WhatsApp on +971 58 579 2033, by phone on 04-370-3007 or by email at info@masterhands.ae.

Do I need to be examined before treatment?

Every treatment plan is personalized, and only a therapist can assess your case. The team will advise what to expect at your first visit.

Is there parking?

Yes, visitor parking in the Tower B basement. Weekday visitors take a coin from the green machine and show it at reception.

Who is on the team?

Eight Registered Physiotherapists, led by Medical Director Chungman Lim, all registered with the DHA in the UAE.

Do you work with insurance?

The team assists with insurance paperwork and claims. Please ask which insurance providers apply to your policy.

What if I need to ask something not listed here?

Call 04-370-3007, message on WhatsApp or email info@masterhands.ae. The team is happy to answer inquiries and assist with the booking.

Is the clinic only for manual therapy?

Yes. The clinic specializes exclusively in advanced manual therapy, nothing else, so your session is hands-on with a registered physiotherapist.

Do you treat children?

Onejoon Jung holds a pediatric physiotherapy certification, and Hanbyul Kim has developmental rehabilitation training. The team will confirm which therapist suits your child.
