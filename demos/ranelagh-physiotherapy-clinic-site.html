Before you come

Good to know

Do I need a referral?

No. Ranelagh Physiotherapy Clinic is a primary care clinic, so you can contact reception directly to make an appointment without a GP or consultant referral.

What happens at my first visit?

Your physiotherapist asks about your history and lifestyle, then carries out a physical examination to decide on treatment and rehab. You leave knowing about your condition, with a recovery time frame and a short and long-term exercise programme.

Does health insurance cover it?

You may get a rebate if you have physiotherapy, day-to-day or extras cover. It depends on your policy, so check with your provider. The clinic's physiotherapists are approved by VHI, Irish Life Health, Laya and Glo. You may also be entitled to a tax-back rebate through Revenue's Form MED 1.

How do I pay?

Payment is taken at the time of consultation by cash, debit card or credit card.

Do you do home visits?

Yes, for people who cannot attend the clinic. The current area covers Beechwood, Ranelagh, Rathgar, Donnybrook, Rathmines, Dartry and Milltown.

Can I join a pilates or yoga class?

Pilates is available 1:1, 2:1 or in classes of up to five. Each class is an hour, booked four weeks at a time, with mats and equipment provided. Yoga is offered 1:1 for beginners or in small groups of up to five with Jane.
