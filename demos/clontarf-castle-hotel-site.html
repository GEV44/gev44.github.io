Eat & drink

Three places to dine

Breakfast from 7am and evening meals until 10pm across the three venues. Allergens are identified on all hotel menus.

Fahrenheit Restaurant

Fine dining beneath the castle's original vaulted ceilings, with seasonal Irish produce and contemporary technique. Two AA Rosettes. Reserve online or at the front desk.

  • Thu to SatDinner 5:30 to 10pm
  • SundayLunch 1 to 3pm, dinner 5:30 to 10pm

Indigo Lounge

A purple-lit champagne bar with red velvet armchairs, opening onto a terrace. Walk-in only, and available for private hire.

  • Every SundayCraft Carvery 12:30 to 5pm

Knights Bar

Gothic panelling, green vaulted ceilings and a fireplace, with cocktails, wine and hearty classics. Walk-in only.

  • Mon to Thu12 to 9:30pm
  • Fri and Sat12 to 10pm
  • Sunday12:30 to 9:30pm

Getting here

Bus, DART, taxi or car

Dublin Bus 130Starts at the hotel and runs to Lower Abbey Street in the city centre. Every 10 minutes on weekdays, 20 at weekends, last bus 23:30.
DARTKillester and Clontarf Road stations are both about a 10 to 15 minute walk away.
Taxi and transfersA local 24-hour taxi firm via Reception or Concierge (10 minutes' notice). Airport and port transfers can be pre-booked. Dublin Airport is about 20 minutes away.
