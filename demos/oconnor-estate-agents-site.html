Asked a lot

Is the valuation really free?

The site offers a free, complimentary, no-obligation valuation to determine the value of your property. Independent formal valuations for bank funding, taxation or legal purposes are a separate service; call to discuss.

What does the lettings service include?

An inspection to set the advised letting value, photography, advertising on the main portals, a BER certificate if needed, tenant showings and screening, the lease, move-in handover with an agreed inventory, utilities, the deposit and first month’s rent, standing order set-up and PRTB registration.

Do you look after tenants’ repairs?

For managed properties, repairs and maintenance go through a network of skilled tradespeople, and the team advises on keeping the property’s value up. There is also a Report an Issue page on the site.

What happens at the end of a tenancy?

A move-out inspection to flag anything that could affect the deposit, key return, final meter readings, a property inspection against its initial condition, photos of any damage, a repair assessment, the deposit calculation, cleaning between tenancies and re-letting.

Do I need a solicitor?

Yes. Both sellers and buyers appoint one once a sale is agreed. They handle contracts and certificates while the agent coordinates with them.
