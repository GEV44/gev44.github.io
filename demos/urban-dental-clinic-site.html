Services

Sixteen service areas, listed on the clinic's own site. Pick a group to filter. This page repeats what the clinic publishes and does not diagnose anything.

Everyday

Oral hygiene and cleaning

Professional cleaning by a hygienist removes plaque and tartar (tartar is harder than bone and needs a scaler) and polishes stains, with fluoride where recommended. The site suggests a preventative visit every six months.

Everyday

Children's dentistry

Gentle care in a friendly, stress-free environment. First visit after the baby teeth emerge, exams and cleaning every six months, brushing and flossing education, molar sealants, and paediatric sedation if a child struggles with anxiety.

Everyday

Dental restoration

Composite fillings shaded to match your teeth, crowns (caps) and bridges, designed to be as durable as natural teeth and to match their shape and shade.

Urgent

Dental emergencies

Same-day emergency care where availability allows: injuries, severe toothache, broken or knocked-out teeth, damaged fillings or crowns. New patients welcome. After hours, listen to the office recording for the emergency contact.

Urgent

Endodontics

Root canal treatment removes the infected pulp, cleans and fills the tooth and seals it, then a temporary crown holds the place while a permanent one is made. It aims to save the tooth from extraction.

Urgent

Wisdom teeth removal

Assessment and extraction. Removal is done under local anesthetic (freezing), with a mild sedative possible, and the incisions are closed with sutures.

Smile

Cosmetic dentistry

Porcelain veneers, Lumineers (ultra-thin veneers placed without drilling or anesthetic, if you are a candidate), contouring and bonding to change the shape, shade and spacing of teeth.

Smile

Teeth whitening

An at-home kit with gel and trays (most patients see results after two weeks of daily use) or in-office chairside whitening in one appointment. It does not whiten crowns, bridges or fillings.

Smile

Orthodontics and Invisalign

Braces and aligners reposition the teeth. Invisalign aligners are unnoticeable and removable for eating and brushing, with regular check-ups for new aligners; treatment time depends on the correction needed.

Replace

Dental implants

Artificial tooth roots placed in the jaw to support crowns and bridges and help prevent bone loss. Treatment usually takes 2-4 months over two or three appointments; a bone graft may be recommended if there is not enough bone.

Replace

Dentures

Full and partial dentures that fit securely and look natural, and help reduce the sunken look of cheeks after tooth loss.

Replace

Implant-supported dentures

Full or partial dentures attached to implants so they never slip when you eat or speak, in attached or snap-on (removable) models.

Comfort

Sedation dentistry

For dental anxiety: a calm, welcoming environment and several options, including oral anti-anxiety medication taken before the visit. Arrange a ride home, since sedatives slow reaction times.

Comfort

TMJ

Diagnosis and treatment for jaw disorders that can cause headaches and jaw pain, which may include medication, stretching and exercise, stress reduction, bite correction or treatment for clenching.

Comfort

Guards and bite appliances

Custom-made sports guards and night appliances that fit comfortably, protect against impact, and prevent grinding and clenching during sleep.

Comfort

Periodontics

Gum infection and disease care: scaling and root planing, tooth extraction when a tooth cannot be saved, and frenectomy for tongue or lip tie in babies.

Insurance and new patients

Becoming a patient is easy

Call, email or fill out the contact form, and someone from the office will contact you to set up an appointment. A new patient exam looks at all your teeth, mouth and jaws, takes 30 to 60 minutes and is usually combined with a cleaning; X-rays and images may be taken.

Why patients choose the practice: flexible morning, evening and weekend hours, free local parking, an easy West Broadway location, longstanding family relationships, and a community-focused, socially active practice.

Insurance and payment

Urban Dental Clinic is an assignment office: it accepts assignment of benefits, so your insurer can pay the clinic directly for the covered amount. The team obtains a predetermination of your coverage to estimate your cost. You pay any co-payment at treatment, and payment is due at the time of treatment.

Your coverage is between you and your insurer, and knowing your benefits, exclusions and policy is your responsibility, but the front desk will gladly help. Tell them if your plan changes.

Good to know

Can a broken or knocked-out tooth be saved?

Sometimes, if you bring the tooth in time. The clinic says to bring a broken-off tooth with you; a knocked-out tooth should be placed back in the socket if possible, otherwise kept between cheek and gum or in a container of milk. Do not clean it or wrap it in paper towel or cloth. Call the office straight away.

When should I go to the emergency room?

The clinic says to go to the nearest emergency room or call 911 if you are bleeding heavily, suspect a broken bone, or feel faint or have trouble breathing. Call the office if you notice bleeding or severe swelling, or have difficulty breathing or swallowing.

What happens in an emergency or specific exam?

A short exam of the problem area, about 20 minutes, to find the source of the problem and discuss options. It may include an X-ray or other tests, with a follow-up appointment for treatment if needed. If there are several areas of concern, the clinic recommends a new patient complete exam.

Why do I need X-rays?

X-rays help the dentist spot problems that cannot be seen with the naked eye: between your teeth and beneath your gums, early decay, teeth that have not yet come through, jawbone health, cysts, abscesses and other signs of infection.

Can I drive after sedation?

No. Sedatives slow your reaction times and impair your ability to operate a vehicle, so arrange transportation home.

Can whitening treat my crown or bridge?

No. Whitening does not work on restorations such as bridges, crowns or fillings. Speak to the dentist about your options.

How long does implant treatment take?

Most patients take 2 to 4 months and two or three appointments, varying with whether a bone graft is needed and how long the implant takes to heal. Creating and placing the crown can take 1 to 2 weeks. With proper care implants can last a lifetime; crowns and restorations usually need replacing sooner.
