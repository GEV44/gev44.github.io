From infants and toddlers to young children, tweens and teens. The team goes the extra mile so young patients feel comfortable, safe and even excited about their visits.

The clinic takes part in Medicare's Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS), which covers eligible children aged 2 to 17. Eligibility depends on your family receiving Family Tax Benefit Part A or a relevant government payment.

FAQ

Good questions

How does the No Gap offer work?

It applies to the check-up and clean for patients with private health insurance. If your fund coverage exceeds $180 you pay nothing out of pocket. Without a health fund, a comprehensive check-up and clean including x-rays and fluoride is $270.

How do I book?

Online through the booking system on the clinic's website at any hour, or call (03) 9041 6384 during business hours. The team also offers same-day appointments when there is availability.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please call (03) 9041 6384 as soon as possible and give at least 24 hours' notice so the slot can go to another patient. You can also reschedule through the online booking system.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call (03) 9041 6384 straight away for severe tooth pain, a broken tooth or an infection. The clinic prioritises emergencies and aims for same-day appointments, and offers after-hours emergency services.

Am I suitable for dental implants?

The dentists examine you, with x-rays and a CT scan, to assess jawbone density and oral health. If extra procedures such as bone grafting are needed, they will talk you through the options at the consultation.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes: Humm (interest-free*, up to $5,000 pre-approval), onsite HICAPS, all major credit cards and government programs such as the CDBS.
