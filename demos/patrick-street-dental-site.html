Treatments in detail

In the clinic’s own words. Tap a treatment to read more.

Root canal treatments

Root canal treatment, or endodontics, is a procedure where a dentist or an endodontist (a specialist in root canal therapy) accesses the tooth through the crown, removes the infected pulp, cleans and shapes the canals, and fills the canal system. A rubber-like material called gutta-percha is used to seal the canals, preventing re-infection. Finally, the crown of the tooth is restored with a tooth-colored filling material.

Cold-plasma ozone treatments

Ozone therapy offers a solution for treating periodontal pockets by eliminating harmful bacteria, including resistant strains, that accelerate periodontitis. Using a specialized probe, ozone penetrates deep into all areas of the pocket, eradicating infection and stimulating natural growth factors for rapid tissue regeneration after mechanical cleaning.

Composite bonding

Composite bonding at Patrick Street Dental is a quick, gentle, and affordable way to refine the color, shape, and size of your teeth, with no invasive treatments required. Perfect for anyone looking to boost their confidence with a fresh, radiant smile that looks and feels completely natural.

Orthodontics: braces and aligners

Braces consist of individual brackets that are bonded to each tooth and connected by an archwire, which applies consistent, gentle pressure to guide the teeth into alignment. Aligners are transparent, nearly invisible orthodontic devices designed to gradually straighten your teeth over time, an excellent option for those who want to correct their teeth without traditional braces.

Dental implant solutions

A dental implant serves as a replacement for a natural tooth root. It is a durable, small post made from biocompatible materials such as metals or ceramics, inserted into the jawbone. The implant provides a stable foundation for attaching single crowns, multi-tooth bridges, or even full dental prostheses. The clinic uses minimally invasive implantation methods and works exclusively with top-tier implant brands.

Metal-free crowns and bridges

Crowns made entirely of high-quality ceramics, without any metal components, give a maximum aesthetic effect and match the colour and natural appearance of your teeth. They are biocompatible, so they do not cause allergic reactions or release ions that could discolour the gum tissue.

Dental hygiene and periodontic treatments

During a scale and polish appointment, the hygienist removes deposits and stains, scales off tartar build-up, and polishes your teeth for a smooth, clean finish. Periodontic or gum treatments are essential when periodontal or gum diseases are present: serious bacterial infections that can destroy the attachment fibers and supporting bone that hold your teeth in place. The clinic provides treatments to address and manage gum disease.

What is Dental Card?

An alternative to expensive dental insurance that covers various types of treatments without annual limits or costly premium payments. Ask the team or visit dentalcard.ie.

Can I claim tax relief?

You can offset the full cost of treatments like bridges, crowns, orthodontics, veneers, root canals, periodontal treatment and surgical extraction of a wisdom tooth against your income tax liability by filling in a Med 2 form. Our dentist signs and submits it on your behalf when treatment is completed.

Please give at least 48 hours’ notice. With less than 24 hours’ notice, or if you do not attend, a cancellation fee may apply.
