FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Are you open on weekends?

Yes, all offices are open seven days a week; Saturday and Sunday 9:00am-3:00pm.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes, emergency and same-day services are available for toothaches, cracked or chipped teeth, lost fillings or crowns, abscesses and jaw pain.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, numerous major PPO dental plans. The team assesses your benefits and estimates any out-of-pocket expenses.

What if I don't have insurance?

New patients with no insurance get a free evaluation, exam and X-rays. There is also a membership plan and in-house financing.

What payment and financing options are there?

Cash, major credit cards and online payments, plus CareCredit, LendingClub, Wells Fargo, Sunbit, Cherry and Basis.

Do you offer sedation?

Yes: nitrous, oral and IV sedation options.
