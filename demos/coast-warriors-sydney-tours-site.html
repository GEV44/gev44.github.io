The Blue Mountains day tour

How the day unfolds

Roughly in order, depending on traffic and the group. Tim Tams and Vegemite are on the bus for tasting.

  1. Meet in Haymarket 7:20am

    Be at 812 George Street, in front of St Laurence church with the big red door, close to Central Station and the light rail. Departure is 7:30am.

  2. The drive up

    About an hour through tunnels, motorways and western Sydney. Chat with your seat neighbours, enjoy the playlist or grab a bit of sleep.

  3. Glenbrook coffee stop

    A relaxed morning stop in the guide's home town for coffee, a smoothie or a chai, plus bathrooms and takeaway brekkie.

  4. Secret lookout

    Where you feel like you are standing on the edge of the world, looking over the Jamison Valley to Mt Solitary. Stay 2 metres from the edges, there is no fence.

  5. Wentworth Falls waterfall walk

    The guided Overcliff-Undercliff bushwalk, about 90 minutes to 2 hours, past multiple waterfalls including the 187 metre fall. About 15 minutes of steep stairs on the way back is optional to skip.

  6. Picnic lunch with a view

    Back up the Stairs of Doom for lunch overlooking the Jamison Valley. Bring a refillable water bottle.

  7. Three Sisters, Katoomba

    The iconic rock formations and the Aboriginal Dreamtime story behind them, with around 30 minutes of free time and wild white cockatoos for photos.

  8. Back to Sydney 5:10 to 5:40pm

    The tour finishes at Haymarket in time for evening plans, with plenty of dinner and drink options a short walk from the drop-off.

Straight from the FAQ

Know before you go

What should I wear and bring?

Enclosed walking shoes (hiking boots are not needed), clothing for the weather, a refillable water bottle, and sunscreen, hat and sunnies. Check the Katoomba forecast rather than Blue Mountains in general.

Can I bring luggage?

Small backpacks are fine. There are no luggage lockers on the minibus, but there is a storage facility a few minutes' walk away near Platform 1 at Central Station.

Do I need to print my booking?

No. They have your name and booking in the system. Bring a smile, you are on holiday.

Can I book last minute?

Yes, if there is space it shows on the website up until 60 minutes before the tour. Sometimes there are last-minute cancellations, so also try WhatsApp or email.

What is the cancellation policy?

For the public tour, changes and cancellations are accepted 24 or more hours before the start time on your voucher, usually through the website where you booked. No-shows and late arrivals are not eligible for a refund. Travel insurance is recommended.

Is it suitable for kids?

The public tour is advertised for 14 and over, with some flexibility on request. Private tours have no age guidelines.
