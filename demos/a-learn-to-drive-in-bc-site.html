Good to know

Questions and policies

What is your student passing rate?

The school's FAQ lists 96%.

Which lessons are available?

Class 4, 5 and 7 lessons, plus beginner, refresher and ICBC road test preparation.

Which languages and cars?

Lessons are available in English, Farsi, Pashto and Russian. You practise in a Tesla or a Toyota Corolla.

Do you help nervous or elderly drivers?

Yes.

Can I choose a male or female instructor?

The school has certified male and female instructors. Max (male) and Shan (female) are listed on the contact page. Say which you prefer when you book.

What are your operating hours?

7 am to 8 pm, Monday to Sunday.

Cancelling or rescheduling

At least 48 hours notice is required. Lessons cancelled or moved with less than 48 hours notice are non-refundable and cannot be rescheduled. Arrive 10 minutes late and the lesson continues for the remaining time with the full fee. More than 15 minutes late, the instructor may leave and the fee is not refunded.

Refunds

No refunds for lessons or road test sessions that already took place. Unused lessons can be refunded with at least 48 hours notice, minus a $25 processing fee. For packages, used lessons are charged at the regular single lesson rate.
