Dizzy, spinning, off balance?

The clinic offers vestibular rehabilitation therapy (VRT) for vertigo, dizziness and balance problems. Treatment is drug-free and starts with a detailed assessment.

  • Recurrent dizziness or imbalance
  • Sudden spinning sensations (vertigo)
  • Motion sensitivity and feeling unsteady
  • Falls caused by poor balance
  • Dizziness linked to neck problems
  • Balance after head injury or stroke

Common causes the clinic treats

  • BPPV, tiny inner-ear crystals that shift
  • Vestibular neuritis or labyrinthitis
  • Migraines and chronic headaches
  • Neck problems (cervicogenic dizziness)
  • Post-stroke or neurological conditions
  • Head injuries or concussions
  • Age-related balance issues

What happens in a session

  • Detailed assessment of triggers and medical history
  • Vestibular tests: eye movement, head movement and balance
  • Customised exercises and repositioning manoeuvres
  • Home exercise guidance to keep your progress
  • Follow-up monitoring and adjustments

Sessions include testing of eye and head movement and balance, repositioning manoeuvres where suitable, balance training, and a home exercise routine. Sudden severe symptoms need urgent medical care.

Enaya, Almadallah and Dubai Government are listed, along with several government employee groups. The team checks your card and referral requirements.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on the condition and is explained after your assessment. Many vertigo cases of BPPV are reported to improve within 1 to 3 sessions, while chronic cases may need more.

Is vertigo physiotherapy safe?

The clinic describes it as non-invasive and drug-free. A physiotherapist assesses you first, and anything that needs a doctor goes to a doctor.

Can physiotherapy cure dizziness completely?

According to the clinic, in most BPPV cases it provides lasting relief, and for neurological causes it reduces symptoms. Your own outlook is explained after assessment.

Is therapy covered by insurance?

The clinic says many plans cover physiotherapy when it is prescribed by a doctor. Bring your card and the team will check what applies.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Not as far as the website says. It advertises a discounted first detailed consultation of 60 to 90 minutes with same-day treatment; the team confirms the current price.

Can I come with a doctor's referral?

Yes, many insurance plans want one. If you are unsure whether your plan needs a referral, ask the team before the visit.
