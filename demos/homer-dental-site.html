Your first visit

  1. Welcome & history. We go over your medical and oral health history. Bring a list of your medications and doses, including vitamins and supplements.
  2. Your questions. We love to answer questions — tell us your concerns and what you would like to change.
  3. Comprehensive exam. May include X-rays, a non-invasive oral cancer screening and an oral health scan. If there are no signs of periodontal disease, you will have a routine cleaning.
  4. Your plan. Dr. Motamedian reviews her findings and builds a treatment plan around your wants and needs, which can be prioritised and staggered over time.

Common questions

Does Invisalign really work?
Yes — in orthodontic and dental practices worldwide, Invisalign has been effective at straightening teeth. Nine out of ten patients are “very or extremely satisfied” with the experience.
How does Invisalign work?
3-D computer imaging maps the complete treatment. A series of custom aligners is produced; each is worn for about two weeks and gradually moves your teeth before the next one takes over.
How much does Invisalign cost?
Only your dentist can determine the cost for your treatment — it depends on what you need and how long it takes. Monthly payment plans are available. The team will go through your options with you.
Do you see patients in the evening or on Saturday?
Yes. We offer evening and Saturday appointments — see the hours above.
What should I do in a dental emergency?
Call the office on (604) 408-2408. The Emergencies page of our website also lists general first-aid notes for knocked-out, broken or sore teeth. The team will advise you directly.
Do I need to bring anything to my first visit?
A list of any medications you take (with doses), including vitamins and over-the-counter supplements, and your insurance details if you have them.
