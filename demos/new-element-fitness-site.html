Questions

Quick answers

Is there a contract?

The monthly membership is contract free. The 6 month and annual options are free from sign-up fee.

I have never trained before. Is that fine?

Yes. The programs are for all fitness levels, and members mention how friendly and helpful the trainers are.

Who designs the training?

Niall and Ken, both sports scientists. The staff hold Sport & Exercise Science degrees.

What if I cannot get to the gym?

Online Personal Training: a goals form, a Zoom consultation, weekly check-ins and a program from Niall or Ken.

Where exactly are you?

Unit 1, Mount St. Annes, Miltown Road, Miltown, Dublin 6.

Can I join and pay monthly?

Yes. Pay monthly is €45 and contract free. Six months is €250 and annual is €450.

Is there anything on nutrition?

The 12 week transformation includes custom meal plans and teaches you to track your food. Members also say the staff happily answer nutrition questions.

Bank holiday hours, trials, day passes?

These are not on the website, so the team will confirm them. Call (01) 444 6956.
