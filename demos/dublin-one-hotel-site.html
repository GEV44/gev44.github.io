Getting here

No hotel parking, but the transport is easy.

  1. Aircoach 700Every 30 minutes from Dublin Airport, about 20 minutes to the Drumcondra Rail Station stop, then a 3 minute walk.
  2. Airlink ExpressStops at Parnell Square and O'Connell Street, then a short 10 minute walk to the hotel.
  3. Taxi from the airportAbout 30 minutes. From Heuston Station allow roughly 25 minutes, or the Luas Red Line to Abbey Street and walk.
  4. Bus and bikeA Dublin Bus stop is right outside and Dublin Bikes are beside the front door. Connolly Station is a short walk.

Parking: none at the hotel. Euro Car Parks at the Mater Private Hospital is 800 m away (24 hour multi-storey), Q-Park The Spire is 1.1 km, and Parkrite Irish Life 1.7 km.
