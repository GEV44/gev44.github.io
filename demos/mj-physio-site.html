Pelvic health and balance care

Pelvic floor physiotherapy

A specialist field that assesses and treats the pelvic floor muscles, internally and externally. Rajwant Chahal and Haritaa both offer it, with evidence-based information for managing pelvic floor concerns. People it can help include those with:

  • Bladder leaks
  • Urinary urgency or frequency
  • Pelvic floor prolapse
  • A weak pelvic floor
  • Prenatal and postpartum care

Vestibular and concussion rehab

Specialised therapy to relieve dizziness, vertigo and balance disorders, plus a structured concussion plan to manage symptoms and support a safe return to daily and sports activities. Krishna trained in concussion rehabilitation and vestibular rehabilitation, and Rajwant also specialises in vestibular rehab.

For anything urgent or sudden, please see a doctor or call emergency services first.

About MJ Physio

Our mission

MJ Physio is a full-service physiotherapy clinic. Whether it is a sports injury, recovery after surgery, a car accident, or an injury that just will not go away, the team is there to help. The clinics are in Vancouver, Surrey and Fleetwood.

The mission is simple: help you live pain-free, move confidently and perform at your best, with advanced physiotherapy techniques, personalised treatment plans and modern equipment.

Vision and philosophy

To be a leading healthcare clinic that provides satisfaction through a friendly staff environment, highly skilled physiotherapists and best practices in client care.

The clinic partners with groups and events in the communities it serves and focuses on preventive physiotherapy. Therapists put cutting-edge treatments into practice through continuing education and in-service training.

Quick answers

Do you work with ICBC and WorkSafeBC?

Yes. ICBC, WorkSafeBC, MSP (eligible clients) and private extended health plans are listed. Your clinic will confirm your coverage before the visit.

What does a first visit look like?

An assessment to find the root cause, a SMART goal you set with your physio, hands-on treatment as needed and a home exercise plan.

Can I book online?

The clinic also takes bookings through its online booking page, or you can call. Use the assistant to send a request to the team.

What is Class IV laser therapy?

A high-intensity laser used to reduce inflammation and speed tissue healing, also used in sports recovery.

What is IMS / dry needling?

Targeted needling to release tight muscles, reduce pain and improve movement patterns. Medical acupuncture is a separate service for pain, circulation and musculoskeletal recovery.

What is vestibular rehabilitation?

Specialised therapy for dizziness, vertigo and balance disorders, to help you move more steadily.

What is clinical Pilates here?

Clinical Pilates is combined with evidence-based physiotherapy for targeted, functional rehabilitation.

Do you treat workplace injuries?

Yes. WorkSafeBC rehabilitation and workplace return-to-duty programs are listed under billing and program services.

Can the physio help me prevent re-injury?

Prevention is part of the approach: personalised exercise programs, posture assessment and a preventive physiotherapy focus.

Do you offer orthotics?

Yes. Custom foot orthotics are offered to improve alignment, relieve pain and enhance walking mechanics.

Is massage therapy available?

Yes. Clinical massage relieves muscle tension, improves circulation and supports recovery. Chiropractic adjustments are also on the services list.

What should I bring?

Your clinic confirms exactly what to bring when the team contacts you, including any claim details if your visit is covered by ICBC or WorkSafeBC, so nothing is missed.

Where can I read more?

The Blog has articles on tennis injury prevention, physiotherapy for sciatic pain in Vancouver and exercises after pregnancy or postpartum.

Which clinic is open late?

Fleetwood is open until 8:00 pm Monday to Friday; Fraser Street until 7:00 pm.

Can I get help after surgery or a concussion?

Yes, post-operative physiotherapy and concussion management are both offered.

How much does a visit cost?

It depends on the service and how you are covered. The Pricing page lists fees and the team confirms the amount for your visit.
