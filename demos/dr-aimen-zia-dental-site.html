A cleaning without the scraping

The clinic is a Guided Biofilm Therapy certified practice. GBT is a minimally invasive approach to dental hygiene that finds and removes plaque (biofilm) gently, using Airflow devices with erythritol powder and plaque-disclosing dye.

  1. RevealPlaque is highlighted with a non-toxic dye so the bacteria are easy to see.
  2. PolishBiofilm and tartar are removed gently with Airflow tooth polishing.
  3. Scale if neededDeeper scaling with ultrasonic tools, only where necessary, without damaging gums or teeth.
  4. CheckA final verification to make sure the mouth is completely biofilm-free.

The clinic lists these benefits: minimally invasive and pain-free, safe for implants, orthodontics and crowns, can reduce inflammation and bleeding gums, and can reduce bad breath caused by bacteria.

Questions patients ask

Do you offer emergency dental care?

Yes. 24/7 emergency care for severe toothache, swelling, infections, broken teeth and other urgent problems. Call +971 505 215 971 and the team will guide you on the next steps. A children’s emergency line is on the same number.

I am nervous. Can you help?

Absolutely. The team is experienced in treating anxious patients and takes time to help you relax. Laser-assisted treatment and safe sedation options are available.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill out the appointment form on the website or call +971 505 215 971. The team contacts you to confirm a time that suits you.

How often should I visit?

Most patients should have a check-up and professional cleaning every six months. Your dentist may recommend more frequent visits depending on your oral health.

Do you have female dentists?

Yes. The clinic is led by Dr. Aimen Zia, and oral and maxillofacial surgery is performed by Dr. Monica Bichay.

I am new to Dubai. Can I register?

Of course. Book an appointment and bring your ID and any previous dental records. The English and Arabic speaking team will help you settle in.

What is Guided Biofilm Therapy?

A modern, minimally invasive way to clean teeth. Plaque is revealed with a harmless dye, removed with Airflow polishing, and the result is verified at the end. It is described as pain-free and suitable for children, seniors and anxious patients.

Which treatments can I get without being referred elsewhere?

The clinic has a general, cosmetic and pediatric dentist and an in-house oral and maxillofacial surgeon, so check-ups, children’s care, cosmetic work, implants and extractions can be handled in one place.

Do you work with insurance?

The website lists insurance partners, and a patient review mentions help with paperwork for reimbursement and insurance. Please ask the team whether your plan is accepted.
