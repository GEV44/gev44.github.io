FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Is the first Invisalign or implant consultation free?

Yes, the clinic offers free initial consultations for Invisalign and dental implants, and the team can check your suitability.

Which warranties do you offer?

Composite fillings 1 year, back tooth crowns 7 years, front tooth porcelain veneers 2 years and implants 2 years, with 6-monthly check-ups.

Can I spread the cost of treatment?

Yes, Denticare offers a no interest payment plan that you apply for with a single form.

Do you accept private health insurance?

We accept most major private health funds and verify your insurance before your visit.

How long does teeth whitening take?

Zoom! in-office whitening takes about 1 to 1.5 hours.
