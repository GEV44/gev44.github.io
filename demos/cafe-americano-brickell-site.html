About us

Located on the third floor of Brickell City Centre, Cafe Americano Brickell is an upscale Latin-inspired diner and cafe with a smart, sophisticated design, perfect for breakfast, lunch and dinner. It offers a bold mix of flavors and innovative dishes, and is a good place for a business meeting, lunch with friends or a casual dinner.
