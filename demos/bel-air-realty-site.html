If you are buying

The questions the team says buyers should ask, and how they help.

Are you looking in the right neighbourhood?

Each agent has particular neighbourhood expertise and can offer accurate market information, plus alternatives if an area does not work for you.

Is it in your budget?

The team works with a reputable, well connected mortgage advisor and helps match you with the best broker for your situation.

What is achievable within your budget?

They analyse what your budget buys and help you stay focused on what matters most.

Is this a good deal?

Market knowledge and negotiating skill to consult on the best possible deal.

What comes after accepting the deal?

Referrals to expert inspectors, a complete due diligence checklist, and a clear list of closing costs.

Can you still contact your agent afterwards?

Yes. The team says not to hesitate with any questions or concerns once the deal is done.

If you are selling

Complete transparency, with current information on market conditions.

To renovate or not?

It is not always necessary, and sometimes it is a waste of money. The agents help you decide what is worth doing.

Beyond the usual sale

Three situations where a boutique team with development roots helps.

Investment property

A different process from buying your dream home. Is the cash flow positive from day one? Is the area expecting long-term economic and geographic growth? Long-held investments usually see the most appreciation, and the advisors help you start or expand a portfolio.

Custom homes

It begins with locating and acquiring the right building lot. The team then works side by side with you so the home suits your lifestyle, with a personalised, hassle-free process and a view to resale value.

Land and development

Commercial, multifamily and bare land sales and acquisitions, and a main role in land acquisitions for developers in fast-growing areas of Metro Vancouver and Southern Vancouver Island.

Quick answers

Can you help me build a custom home?

Yes, starting with finding and acquiring the right building lot, then working side by side so the home suits your lifestyle and stays marketable.
