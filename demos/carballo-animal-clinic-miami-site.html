How a visit is run

The clinic describes its approach in four parts: record, prevent, diagnose, treat.

1Full electronic recordsBlood work, radiographs and medical history are available immediately in your pet's digital record, so nothing is missed when treatment is chosen.
2Prevention firstA complete physical exam can catch subtle signs early, for example a heart murmur, limping or inflamed gums. High-quality vaccines protect against serious diseases.
3DiagnosisAccurate diagnosis is treated as the key to a correct treatment, with in-house analyzers, imaging and a reference lab.
4TreatmentTreatment is applied according to the diagnosis, from medication and laser therapy to surgery, with clear communication with the owner.

Good to know, from the clinic's FAQ

Dachshunds and stairsGoing up and down stairs puts a deep extension on a dachshund's back, and an intervertebral disc injury may occur.
Pool waterChlorine and other harsh chemicals can be aggressive to a dog's digestive system.
Car coolantCoolant often contains ethylene glycol. It tastes sweet but can cause irreversible kidney damage in cats and dogs.
BonesSplinters can pierce the intestines or cause blockages, which often need surgery if found in time.

Quick answers

Do you take emergencies?

Emergency treatment is provided during regular service hours. After hours, call (305) 552-9121 and you will get the information you need for your pet's emergency.

What does the annual check-up include?

A physical check-up plus exams chosen according to age and state of health.

Is it better for a pet to live indoors?

The clinic's FAQ says pets generally live safer, healthier lives inside the house, where they can also be groomed and taught good habits.

Can I get my pet's medication online?

Yes, through the clinic's VetSource online store.

Are specialist scans available?

CT scan, fluoroscopy and echocardiography are available on request.
