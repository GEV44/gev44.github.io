Good to know

Do you see cats?

Not currently. Our doctor is allergic to cats, so we are only seeing dogs for now. We expect that to change when we hire additional doctors in the coming months, so feel free to keep checking in or reaching out if your cat has needs.

Is an exam required for vaccines?

Yes. Vaccines are $30 per shot and an exam is required. Give us a call or submit the online form if you are wondering which vaccines your pet needs.

What does the starting dental price include?

The most basic cleaning includes anesthesia, IV administration and X-rays, but no extractions. An exam gives you a dental estimate tailored to your pet's teeth.

Can you bill my pet insurance?

Not directly, because the clinic is not currently contracted with any insurance providers. You pay in full at the visit and submit the paperwork to your insurer for reimbursement. Let us know what information they need.

What if my dog shows signs of illness after exposure?

Please seek veterinary care. For example, if your dog shows vomiting, diarrhea or extreme lethargy, especially if not fully vaccinated, early treatment can be lifesaving. If your pet is bitten or exposed to wildlife, or bites someone, seek care right away. Call the clinic and the team will advise. This page cannot diagnose.

Vaccines

Protection for your pet at just $30 a shot

We administer the core dog vaccines recommended by the American Animal Hospital Association (AAHA), as well as others suggested based on lifestyle and geography. Exam required.

Rabies

A deadly virus that affects the nervous system of mammals, including pets and people, spread through bites from infected animals, most often wildlife like bats, skunks and raccoons. Almost always fatal once symptoms appear, but completely preventable with vaccination.

Source: American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA)

DA2PP (parvo)

Canine parvovirus is highly contagious and potentially deadly, especially for puppies and unvaccinated dogs. It attacks the intestines and immune system. Vaccination is the best protection and a core vaccine for all dogs.

Source: AVMA

Leptospirosis

A bacterial disease spread through water, soil or surfaces contaminated with infected animal urine. It can cause kidney and liver damage, and exposure can happen in everyday places like puddles and standing water.

Source: AVMA

Bordetella (kennel cough)

A highly contagious respiratory illness that spreads easily where dogs gather: boarding facilities, dog parks and grooming settings. Many cases are mild, but some dogs develop serious complications.

Source: AVMA
