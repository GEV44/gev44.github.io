Their FAQ

Quick answers

How quickly can the mechanic reach me?

Typically within 20 to 30 minutes in most service areas, depending on your location and traffic.

Can you repair my car at home?

Yes. Many repairs and maintenance jobs can be done at home: diagnostics, battery replacement, brake repairs, oil changes, A/C servicing and electrical work.

What if my car won't start?

Call +971 56 831 6301 for an inspection. It may be the battery, starter motor, alternator, fuel system or electrical parts, and they come to your location to fix it on the spot.

Do you replace batteries on site?

Yes: battery testing, charging-system checks and replacement at your home or preferred location.

How long does a repair take?

Minor repairs may take under an hour. Complex ones need more time and parts.

Which brands do you work on?

BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche, Lexus, Toyota, Nissan, Honda, Hyundai, Kia, Ford, Chevrolet, Volkswagen and many others.

Do you offer pick-up and drop-off?

The About page lists "pick-up and drop-off services across Dubai". How it works for your car is something to ask the team.

Is the team available at night?

The header and About page both say 24/7, and a review on the site describes a mechanic arriving within minutes of a call.

What does it cost?

The website does not list prices. The team gives an estimate once they know what the car needs.
