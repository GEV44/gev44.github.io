Questions

Quick answers

Do you only work on European cars?

Yes. The shop specialises exclusively in European and exotic brands: McLaren, Aston Martin, Bentley, Rolls-Royce, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Porsche, Land Rover, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi and Maserati.

Can you work with my insurance after an accident?

The collision department works with all major and specialty insurance carriers, and always in the best interest of the vehicle owner, with safety and quality first.

What does the 360 inspection include?

A master technician performs a multi-point visual inspection to find any safety and mechanical issues visibly present, then prepares an estimate for related repairs and flags which are most urgent.

Do you offer detailing and paint protection?

Yes: detail, wax and paint restoration, plus PPF and ceramic coating applications.

Do you restore classic cars?

Yes, mostly through the Renner Projekts brand, with ground-up and resto-mod builds. These projects are very time and labour intensive, so only a few select vehicles are accepted at a time. Ask the team about availability.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Friday from 8:30am to 5pm. Saturdays are by appointment, so book ahead if the weekend suits you better.

Where exactly is the shop?

1800 NW 23rd Street, Miami, FL 33142, in the Allapattah neighborhood next to Wynwood and the Health District, a few minutes from Downtown, Midtown and Brickell.

Can I talk to someone before I bring the car in?

Yes. Call (305) 351-2012 during opening hours or e-mail info@masterclassauto.com with the year, model and what the car is doing, and the team will reply with next steps.

Is the shop a dealer?

No. MasterClass is an independent facility and the shop positions itself as the leading alternative to costly dealer service for European and exotic owners in South Florida.

Do you service air conditioning?

Yes: A/C check, freon refill and condenser repair for most modern European vehicles, with practices that seal and capture freon gas.

Which brands have their own page on your site?

Aston Martin, Bentley, BMW, Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Porsche and Rolls-Royce each have a dedicated service and repair page.

How do I get a quote?

Prices depend on the car and the work, so the team confirms them after looking at your vehicle. Call (305) 351-2012 or send the details through the assistant.

Bring it to people who know it

Tell the team about your car and what it needs, and the team will get back to you.
