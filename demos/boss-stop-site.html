About us

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome, and you can also send a booking request through the website.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan dishes?

Yes, dishes marked VG include vegetable dumplings, vegetable spring rolls, crispy shimeji mushrooms and Mala Vege Soup Noodle.

Can I book for a large group or party?

Yes, there is a 3-course set menu at €29 per person for groups over 6, and events for up to 50 people. Contact the restaurant with your request.

What about allergies?

Please ask the staff for the in-house allergy guide, or message the restaurant before you come.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, vouchers can be bought on the website.

Is service charge added?

No service charge is added. A discretionary gratuity of 12.5% is added for parties of 5 or more.
