Quick answers

Do I need a doctor referral?

Not for ICBC injury therapy, according to the clinic's site. For anything else the team will confirm.

Can I text the clinic?

Yes. The site lists a text line at (778) 244-7788 next to the main number, (604) 661-8878.

Can I do physio over video?

Yes, e-Rehab (tele-rehabilitation) runs by video conferencing when an in-person visit is not possible.

Is bike fitting open to anyone?

The clinic's site says bike fitting is for current clients of the clinic only.
