What the road test checks

The Class E Driving Skills Test is held at our office. The team prepares you for each of these, and the rental car, dual brakes and insurance are included.

Three-point turnTurn the car around in a 20 to 40 foot space.
Approach of crossingGet in the proper lane and look in each direction.
Yield right-of-wayLet pedestrians cross, pull over for emergency vehicles, never block traffic.
Straight-in parkingCentered in the space, nothing sticking out into the lane.
Parking on a gradeUphill and downhill, with and without a curb.
Stop quicklyA quick, safe stop when the examiner asks.
Backing50 feet at a slow speed, looking to the rear.
Stop signs and signalsFull stop before the line; wait until the intersection is clear.
Signal and turnProper lane and a signal for the last 100 feet.
Passing and lanesLook ahead and behind; keep to the right lane except on one-way streets.
Following distanceA minimum of four seconds.
PostureBoth hands on the wheel, no elbow in the window.

If you do not pass, you will be asked to practice before returning for a re-test. Your road test includes 2 attempts.

Quick answers

The questions families ask first.

What do I need before I can sign up for lessons?

A learner’s permit. Complete the drug & alcohol course and pass the written test first.

How old do I have to be?

You can start the permit at 15. Minors take the road test after 12 months with the permit, or at 18.

Do you pick students up?

Yes for lessons, free of charge. For the road test, the student comes to our office.

Do you have female instructors?

Yes. Female and male instructors, all bilingual in English and Spanish.

How far ahead should I book?

Programs: about 1 month before Spring Break, 30 to 45 days before summer. Written and road tests: about a week.

I have a license from another country.

Complete the written test and the road test. You must still hold a learner’s permit for the road test.
