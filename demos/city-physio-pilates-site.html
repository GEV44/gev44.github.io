Your first appointment

Sleep, stress, training load, work demands, past injuries: all of it matters. Treating only the knee is a rubbish healthcare model.

  1. A proper history. The injury itself, your general health, lifestyle and recovery, the other practitioners on your team (GP, surgeon, coach) and your goals. Yes, they ask about dental history if jaw pain is involved.
  2. A thorough physical assessment. Movement analysis, strength testing, joint mobility, load tolerance, posture, breathing mechanics, running or sport-specific analysis, TMJ and jaw assessment, and vertigo testing where relevant.
  3. A clear explanation. Your working diagnosis, the contributing factors, why it developed, what needs to change, a realistic recovery timeline and a structured plan.
  4. Treatment from day one. Manual therapy, soft tissue work, joint mobilisation, dry needling, targeted exercise, load modification and nervous system regulation, depending on what you need.

Who they see: corporate professionals around Martin Place, local residents, marathon runners, strength athletes, executives with persistent neck and back pain, and people with complex or recurring injuries that have not resolved elsewhere.

Questions

What happens at my first appointment?

A proper history (injury, general health, lifestyle, your goals), a thorough physical assessment, a clear explanation you can understand, and treatment from day one. Single-area consults are 30 minutes; jaw, headache and dizziness are 45.

What should I bring?

Any imaging (MRI, X-ray, ultrasound), a list of current medications, a referral if you have one (not required), and any splints or oral appliances you wear. Clean sports shorts and singlets are in every room.

Can I claim with my private health fund?

HICAPS and digital claiming are on site, so rebates are processed at the time of payment.

What is the cancellation policy?

Physio: 3 hours notice. Pilates: 16 hours notice. A credit card secures the booking and nothing is taken at booking; it is only charged for a no-show or late cancellation.

Do you see workers comp and CTP patients?

Yes. Paperwork is required before you book: letter of liability, insurer details, claim number and case manager details. Matt and Nicola do not take CTP or Workers Comp appointments.

Is Pilates safe if I am pregnant or injured?

Small Group Equipment and Private/Duo sessions are pregnancy, postnatal and injury friendly. Group Reformer is not suitable for pregnancy or injury modifications.

Workers comp and CTP

The clinic is happy to be the calm in the workers comp storm, with a Preferred Provider side for injured workers and for employers.

To secure a booking, the team needs a letter of liability, insurance company details, the claim number and case manager details by email or fax. Bring all paperwork, scans and doctors letters to the first appointment. Matt Hagerty and Nicola Michell do not offer CTP or Workers Compensation appointments.

Corporate Pilates and physio

Recurring Pilates at your office, a one-off session for the exec team, a hotel room session or a company event.

The clinic supplies the equipment, sets up and packs down. Travel rates may apply for locations more than 1km from 25 Martin Place. Corporate physio is offered for teams working around Martin Place.

How booking works

Physio appointments are booked online. A valid credit card is required to secure the appointment, but no payment is taken at the time of booking. Card details are stored securely and only charged if you fail to attend or cancel with insufficient notice.

Physio needs 3 hours notice and Pilates 16 hours. The clinic runs back to back with active waiting lists, which is why late cancellations are charged.

Pilates, the practical bits

Group Reformer and Small Group Equipment classes are booked, cancelled and managed through Momence, including class packs and waitlist notifications. Private and Duo sessions are by appointment, by email.

Wear comfortable clothes, tights or bike shorts. Tie long hair up so you can lie on your back without a clip digging in. Socks are mandatory and grip socks are sold in the studio. Never done Pilates? Start with Small Group Equipment for a more gradual introduction.
