Home of sedation dentistry

If dental visits make you anxious, you are not alone.

Our doctors and staff are trained to keep the room friendly, attentive and non-judgemental. You meet the dentist for a consultation first, and together you choose the option that fits your anxiety, your procedure and your medical history.

Nitrous oxide

Laughing gas mixed with oxygen, breathed through a small mask over your nose. A mild sedative for anxiety and discomfort.

Oral conscious

A sedative you take an hour before your visit. You rest in a quiet room, and you can still tell the dentist how you feel. You need a ride home.

IV conscious

Given by the anaesthesiology team we have partnered with for years, across the street. A twilight state where you can still respond. You need a ride home.

General anaesthesia

"Sleep dentistry" for severe fear or a lot of work in one go, at the partner practice across the street while our dentist treats you.

Ask about sedation

What a sedation visit looks like

Written out, so nothing is a surprise

For adults

  • Your dentist administers the sedation method you chose together.
  • You feel calm and relaxed throughout the treatment.
  • Recovery time depends on the type of sedation.
  • Arrange for someone to drive you home after oral, IV or general sedation.
  • Avoid driving or operating machinery for the recommended period.
  • Follow the aftercare instructions your dentist gives you.

For children

  • An anaesthesiologist monitors your child continuously.
  • Sedation is often started through a mask before an IV is placed.
  • Parents may stay with the child until they fall asleep.
  • Afterwards your child wakes gradually in a recovery room.
  • Mild disorientation or upset feelings are normal after waking.
  • A responsible adult takes the child home, avoiding public transportation, and your child rests for the rest of the day.

Sedation is chosen after a consultation, based on your level of anxiety, the procedure and your medical history. Medical questions go to the dentist, not to this assistant.

Quick questions

Can I come in today for an emergency?

Same-day appointments are available for severe pain, broken teeth and infections. You need to call (604) 873-9794 first. Walk-ins and emergency cases are welcome.

Do you work with my insurance?

We direct-bill most insurance providers. Tell the team which plan you have and they confirm what is covered.

Do you publish prices?

No. Treatment costs depend on your exam and plan, and fees follow the BC Dental Fee Guide. The team gives you a figure after your visit.

I get very nervous. What can you do?

Four sedation options, from nitrous oxide to general anaesthesia, plus a team trained to be non-judgemental. You choose after a consultation.

Do I need a ride home after sedation?

Yes for oral, IV and general sedation: arrange for someone to drive you home and go lightly on activities afterwards. Nitrous oxide is a mild option; ask the dentist at your consultation what applies to you.

Is financing available?

Yes, 0% interest financing through Fairstone: no down payment, no collateral, high approval and no early payment penalty. The Financing page and the team can walk you through it.

I am a student with BC student insurance. Can you help?

We have a page on student dental insurance options in BC. Tell the team your plan and they confirm what it covers.

Who will I see?

Dr. Arezou Nejad or Dr. Kathem Talebian for treatment, and hygienists Nina, Laura and Beena for cleanings, with Kate at reception and Simmie Manhas as office manager.

Which areas do you serve?

All of Vancouver and surrounding areas, most convenient for Yaletown, Downtown, Shaughnessy and the Broadway and Kingsway area.

Are you open on weekends?

Saturdays and Sundays are closed, except that we are also open every third Saturday of the month.
