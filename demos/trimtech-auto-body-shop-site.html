Straight answers

The most asked questions, answered from the workshop's own FAQ.

What types of vehicles do you repair?

All makes and models of cars, vans and commercial vehicles.

How long will the repair take?

It depends on the extent of the damage. The team gives a timeframe estimate during the initial assessment.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes, with all major insurance providers. The team can help with filing claims and liaising with your insurer.

Can I get a quote before repairs start?

Yes. Bring the vehicle in for assessment and you get a detailed estimate. An estimate fee may apply depending on the process required, and all estimate fees are fully redeemable if you go ahead with repairs.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. All repairs are backed by a warranty.

Do you offer a courtesy car?

Depending on availability, a courtesy car or a transport solution can be arranged.

Will my paint match the factory finish?

Technicians use advanced paint matching techniques so the repair keeps the factory appearance.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Phone or email the workshop, and the team will book a time for the assessment and repairs.
