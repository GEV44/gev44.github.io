Prep and aftercare

A few points straight from the treatment pages. Your therapist gives you the full guidance for your own skin.

Express AHA peel

Pause retinol and AHA/BHA products for at least 2 days before, avoid heavy exercise for 24 hours after, and leave at least 2 weeks between the peel and any botox or filler.

Cosmedix Deep Sea Peel

Skin prep is essential, typically a month or more on a retinol serum and a tyrosinase inhibitor as prescribed by your therapist. Avoid sun, vigorous exercise, saunas and hot showers for 72 hours, and allow for 7 days of downtime.

Skin needling

Avoid makeup, heat, sweat and sun for 24 to 48 hours, use gentle hydrating skincare and SPF, and skip actives such as retinol, AHA/BHA and vitamin C for 3 to 5 days.

Lash and brow treatments

If you have contact lenses, please make sure you can take them out before lash tinting. Brow shaping is recommended every 3 to 5 weeks, and a lash lift lasts up to 8 weeks.

Questions

Good to know

Do you treat men too?

Yes. The Hive is a unisex clinic.

Are facials safe in pregnancy?

The Initial Consultation Facial, Customised Facial and Express Hydration Facial are listed as pregnancy-safe. Strong peels and needling are not suitable, so tell the team when you book.

Where exactly is The Hive, and when is it open?

The Hive is tucked away in a charming laneway in South Melbourne. It is appointment only, and the pages I read don't list a street address or opening hours, so the team confirms both when you book: 0431 328 112.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give 24 hours' notice by SMS. This lets people on the waiting list be notified and your spot filled. You are also welcome to send a friend or family member in your place.

Can I buy a gift?

Yes. Gift vouchers (physical and e-vouchers) and treatment packages are in the online shop.

What should I do after a peel or needling?

The website has a Post-Treatment Care guide for peels, microneedling, waxing and facials. Your therapist will also talk you through it.
