Questions

Or ask the assistant in the corner.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly at either clinic. If your extended health plan needs a referral for reimbursement, check with your provider first.

Do you offer direct billing?

Yes, to most major extended health providers. ICBC and WorkSafeBC claimants are accepted with adjuster approval.

Can I have a virtual appointment?

Yes. The clinic offers virtual physiotherapy for patients across BC, as well as in-person sessions at both clinics.

What happens at the first appointment?

A comprehensive assessment: medical history, symptoms and goals, movement testing to find the root cause, then a plan. Most patients get hands-on treatment that day.

Which clinic should I pick?

Downtown is at Unit 508 - 1160 Burrard Street and Fairview is at Unit 201 - 1416 W 8th Avenue. Both take bookings online, so pick whichever is closer to home or work, or choose a virtual appointment.

Can my workplace book something for the team?

The clinic lists corporate workshops and a mobile physiotherapy clinic for workplace wellness. The team can tell you what is possible for your office.

Who are the physiotherapists?

The clinic has 12+ licensed practitioners, certified in areas including sports medicine, pelvic health, vestibular rehabilitation and manual therapy. See the Team page on the clinic's site for profiles.

Where can I see prices?

On the clinic's own price list page. The assistant here does not quote numbers, to avoid showing you something out of date.
