Questions people ask

What is a visa change?

It extends your stay by getting a new UAE tourist visa, with the aim of avoiding overstay fines and absconding records.

What does the A2A option include?

An air ticket and a new tourist visa.

What does exit by road include?

Transport and a new tourist visa, with the Oman exit by road (B2B).

Is it only for tourists?

Can I also book a flight with you?

Yes. Air ticket booking is one of the five services on the website.

Do I get a new visa or just an exit stamp?

Both routes (A2A and B2B) include a new tourist visa. The team explains the exact steps for your nationality.
