Quick answers

The same answers the assistant gives.

Do I need a permit before the driving exam?

Yes. You must have a Florida learner's permit to complete the driving exam.

How long is the driving exam?

The route is chosen by the State and takes about 15 to 20 minutes.

What happens after I pass?

Head to the State office with your certificate. They trade your permit for a driver's license for free.

I just moved to Florida. Can you help?

Yes. The school says it can help you get a Florida driver's license in 24 hours.

Do you speak other languages?

English first, but the school is also bilingual. Ask the team which language you need.

Is the traffic school course available online?

Yes, courses are offered in person, online, and the 4-hour course also on DVD.
