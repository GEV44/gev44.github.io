About Shiva

Shiva Driving School runs lessons in Clayton and the surrounding south-east suburbs. A team of experienced instructors certified by the Driving Instructor Authority (DIA), fully insured, in cars fitted with dual-control pedals.

Learning to drive is stressful for a lot of people. Instructors here are used to nervous and anxious learners and to people who would rather be taught in their own language.

  • Train for your driving test with full confidence
  • Highways, freeways and urban streets
  • New hybrid vehicles (Toyota Corolla and Camry)
  • Performance reports after lessons
  • Free pick-up and drop-off
  • Notes for techniques you can practise

Questions

Straight from the school.

How many lessons do I need to pass?

Most students need 5 to 10 lessons. The school says the average learner passes after about 7.

Do you pick up from home?

Yes. Free pick-up and drop-off from home, work or uni across the south-east suburbs.

Can I learn in Hindi or Punjabi?

Yes. All instructors speak Hindi, Punjabi, Gujarati and English fluently.

What car do you use?

Modern dual-control Toyota Corolla and Camry hybrids, the same type as VicRoads test cars.

Do you teach on weekends?

Yes, 7 days a week from 8AM to 8PM.

Can I use your car for the VicRoads test?

Yes. The Test package includes car hire and a 1 hour warm-up.

Do you help convert an overseas licence?

Yes, overseas licence conversion is a specialty.

Are instructors police checked?

Yes, all have a current Police Check and Working With Children Check.

What if I'm a nervous driver?

The school specialises in nervous and anxious learners. Instructors are patient, calm and encouraging.

How do I book?

Call 0415 707 182 or use Book Now. The school answers 7 days a week. You can also send a request through the assistant on this page and the team will follow up.

What do the packages cost and how long are they valid?

Single lessons start at $55 for 45 minutes. Packs of 5 and 10 are prepaid and valid for 3 months. See the pricing table above for every length.

Is there a charge to change a booking?

A $25 charge applies for any change or cancellation within 24 hours of the booking.
