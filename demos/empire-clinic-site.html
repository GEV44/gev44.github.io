FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. You get a personalized treatment plan and exact quote at no cost.

Do you accept medical cards?

Yes, HSE and PRSI medical cards, with eligibility assessed during the consultation.

How can I pay?

Visa, MasterCard, cash and medical cards.

Can I claim tax relief?

You can claim 20% tax relief on non-routine dental treatments such as implants, crowns, root canals, orthodontics and bridges.

Are your prices all-inclusive?

Yes, they include materials, standard anaesthesia, PPE and sterilization. The final quote is confirmed after an examination.
