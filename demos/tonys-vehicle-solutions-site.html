Signs to watch for

Time for an A/C service

  • Weak or warm airflow
  • Strange odors when the A/C is on
  • Moisture or leaks around the vents
  • Unusual noises during operation

Time for an alignment

  • The car pulls to one side
  • Uneven or rapid tire wear
  • A crooked steering wheel while driving straight
  • Squealing tires or shaky handling

Brake problems

  • Worn-out pads or rotors
  • Brake fluid leaks
  • A spongy or hard pedal
  • ABS warning lights

Electrical problems

  • Dead battery or charging issues
  • Faulty alternator or starter
  • Dashboard warning lights
  • Power windows or locks not working
  • Wiring and fuse issues

The alignment process

  • Computerized diagnostic check
  • Suspension and steering inspection
  • Adjustment of camber, caster and toe angles
  • Post-alignment road test for accuracy

Quick answers

Do I need an appointment for an oil change?

Walk-ins are welcome, an appointment cuts wait times. Most oil changes take 30 to 45 minutes once in the bay.

How long does A/C repair take?

Diagnostics usually take 30 to 60 minutes. Repairs range from same-day recharges or leak fixes to 1 to 2 days for parts like compressors.

How much is a brake job?

The site says brake pad replacement starts at $389 plus tax for most vehicles. Final price depends on front or rear, rotor condition and OEM or aftermarket parts. You get a written estimate.

Do you work on European cars?

Yes, including BMW, Mercedes-Benz and Audi, with factory-grade diagnostics and OEM-approved fluids.

What is in a diagnostic check?

A 20-point multipoint inspection, OBD-II fault-code scan, Carfax service-history review, battery and charging test, fluid, brake and steering checks, tire and pad measurements and a road test if needed.

Can I bring my own parts?

Not recommended, and labor on customer-supplied parts is not warranted.

Are free estimates available?

Estimates are written, after a professional inspection or diagnostic, and a diagnostic fee may apply.
