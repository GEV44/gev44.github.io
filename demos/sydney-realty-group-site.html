Quick answers

Questions we hear a lot.

Do you only manage rentals?

No. SRG provides property management as well as property sales services to investors, landlords and tenants.

When can I reach the team?

Business days, 9am to 5:30pm, by phone or email, or by dropping into either office.

How do I find a place to rent?

Current properties are on the For Rent page. The support hub explains inspections, applications and what happens from holding deposit to signed lease.

Where can I read about the 2025 rental reforms?

In the support hub under Getting Started: a guide that explains the legislative changes without the legal jargon.

What does it cost to have my property managed?

The website does not publish fees. The team will confirm what applies to your property when you speak with them.

Can I visit without an appointment?

The team says to drop by whenever suits you and they will make time to provide a solution, whether it is renting, leasing, buying or selling. You can also schedule an appointment using the form on this page.

Which office should I go to?

The Sydney CBD office at 3/368 Sussex St sits on the corner of Sussex and Liverpool St. The Zetland office at 24a Gadigal Ave is next to Joynton Park. Both are staffed by the SRG team.

Who do I speak to about selling?

William Tianguang Bai is the Sales Director. Ask for a call using the form and the team will arrange it.

Who handles commercial property?

Yandi Zhou is the Commercial and Residential Property Manager. Please contact the office to confirm what is possible for your property.

I am a tenant with a repair. What do I do?

See the Repairs section of the support hub. For anything urgent, phone the office on (02) 9264 2599.

What if I am an investor who feels ignored by my current agent?

That is exactly the problem SRG was set up to fix: clear communication, reasonable resolution times and a team that knows your property. Ask for a call and tell them what has gone wrong.

Is it a chain?

No. Sydney Realty Group is a family-owned and operated company, in business since 2000.
