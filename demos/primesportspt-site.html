Why we are different

Boutique, not assembly line.

Eugene Jang founded Prime Sports Wellness after deciding he wanted something better than the high-volume approach. In the clinic's words, your therapist does not make a cameo appearance: the same person works with you for the whole session, watches how your body reacts and adjusts the treatment as you go.

There is no one-size-fits-all method here. Eugene's whole body approach takes your age and goals, your physical and athletic abilities, even your personality into account, and aims past the clinical diagnosis to the root cause of pain.

The clinic also values privacy, which is part of why its client list runs from students and pro players to celebrity clientele, in its own words.

You take part too. You give feedback on how your body responds, and your therapist uses it to fine-tune the plan.

FAQ

Questions and answers.

Does physical therapy hurt?

It depends on the treatment. Some rehabilitation can be uncomfortable, some techniques give immediate relief. Tell your therapist about pain intensity, frequency and duration.

How should I dress?

Loose clothing that exposes the area being treated: shorts for a knee, a tank top for a shoulder, a loose shirt and pants for the lower back.

Is it covered by insurance?

In most cases, per the clinic. Call (305) 798-5612 so the office can clarify your plan.

Will I need a referral?

The clinic does not require an outside medical referral. Your insurance plan may differ, so confirm with the office.

How many sessions will I need?

The clinic calls it a crystal-ball question: treatment length is as unique as each patient. Your therapist can tell you after seeing you.

Can physical therapy help me avoid surgery?

The FAQ says that for some conditions it has been found as effective as surgery. Whether that applies to you is for your therapist and doctor to say.

Why is physical therapy a good idea?

The clinic's FAQ points out that the modern body takes a beating, from repetitive work injuries to sport, and that physical therapists are trained to treat movement disorders and find the source of pain, not only the pain itself.

What does a physical therapist do?

According to the FAQ, therapists help the body after injury and surgery, evaluate lifestyle and goals, and design programmes to avoid future injury. Therapeutic exercise and functional training are the cornerstones, and patients are taught to care for their bodies on their own too.

Do I need to see my doctor first?

The clinic does not require an outside medical referral. If you are unsure whether physical therapy is right for you, the clinic suggests asking your doctor.

Can I ask a medical question here?

No. This page and its assistant cannot diagnose or give medical advice. Please speak to the therapists.
