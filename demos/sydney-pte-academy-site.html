How it works, start to finish

No sign-up maze. The first step is simply telling a teacher where you are.

  1. Send your numbersCurrent PTE scores (or that you have not sat the test), the score you need and what it is for: visa, professional registration or university. Add your test date if it is booked.
  2. Get a recommendationA teacher replies with the course that fits, usually Express, Advanced or private sessions, with no obligation.
  3. Join live on ZoomSmall group of fewer than 10, or one hour 1 on 1. Pick evenings or weekends if that suits your week.
  4. Practise between classesUse the free PTE Practice Hub for all 22 question types and the 5 full mock tests, and replay the recorded sessions.
  5. Sit the test with a planStrategy for Speaking, Writing, Reading and Listening, so you know what to do with every question type and every minute.

Tip from the school: if you are within 5 to 10 points of your target, the gap is almost always exam technique rather than English ability.

Quick answers

What score do I need for Australian PR?

It depends on your visa subclass (189, 190, 491, partner, employer sponsored). Check the English requirement on the Department of Home Affairs website, then message the team with your target score.

Do you teach general English?

No. The academy teaches PTE Academic only: Speaking, Writing, Reading and Listening, aligned to the real test format and scoring.

Are classes in person?

Classes run live online on Zoom, in small groups and 1 on 1, for students anywhere in Australia or overseas. The academy is based in Glebe, Sydney.

Can I review a missed class?

Yes, sessions are recorded so you can review them afterwards.

Is the practice platform really free?

Yes. Every student practises on PTE Practice Hub, the free platform built by the teachers. It covers all 22 question types with instant scoring on the 10 to 90 scale and 5 full mock tests.

How big are the groups?

Every Express Course intake is capped at fewer than 10 students. The school calls that a guarantee, not a guideline.

Can I choose evenings or weekends?

For 1 on 1 sessions, scheduling works around your availability, including evenings and weekends. The school is open Monday to Sunday, 9am to 9pm.

I am not sure which course to take.

Take a free mock test on the PTE Practice Hub, then send your score on WhatsApp. A teacher recommends the option that fits, with no obligation.
