About us

The Majestic Hotel directly faces the beach in the heart of Miami Beach's famous Art Deco District. Albert Anis designed it in classic Art Deco style, it was completed in 1940, and it has been welcoming travelers ever since. Guests enjoy a smoke-free environment, an on-site restaurant and bar with seating that takes in the ocean breezes, and top-end services in a modern, newly renovated setting.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 4:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. The front desk is open 24 hours.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, a complimentary hot breakfast is included for up to two guests per room. If there are additional guests, breakfast is included in the extra person fee.

What is your cancellation policy?

There is a 3-day cancellation policy: cancel or modify by 12:00 PM (noon), 72 hours before arrival. After that, or for a no-show, one full night plus tax is charged. Non-refundable reservations cannot be canceled or modified.

Is parking available?

Yes, valet parking is available for $39 per day. Public parking is also available nearby, and fees apply.

Are pets allowed?

No pets are allowed, but ADA-defined service animals are welcome.

Is there a security deposit?

A security deposit of $150 is paid on arrival by credit card and returned in full at check-out, subject to a damage inspection. Cash is not accepted for the deposit.
