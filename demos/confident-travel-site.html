Quick answers

Can I book a holiday directly on this page?

No. The company states that its holidays are bookable only through its travel agent partners. The assistant can take your details so the team can point you to an agent.

Why phone instead of booking flights online?

The team says long and short transits that come with online booking create hassle, and it prefers to advise on the best fares and routes.

Is there a number for emergencies abroad?

Yes. A 24 hour emergency contact is published: +353 86 236 3130.

Who regulates the business?

Confident Travel is a trading name of World Travel Centre Ltd, licensed by the Irish Aviation Authority under TA 0388 and TO 256.

Can I get insurance as a backpacker?

Yes, backpacker cover is one of the three types on the services page, next to single trip and multiple trip cover. The team confirms terms and quotes.

Can you help with an OCI card or attestation of documents?

Both are listed under the Indian Embassy consular services the team can assist with. Call before you gather paperwork so you bring the right things.

Do you only do India?

No. India is a speciality, but the flights desk covers all five continents, and the home page also features Dubai, Australia and Manila.

How long has the company been going?

The services page says the company has worked in management consultancy for 16 years and is now widening into tourism and education.

Opening hours?

Hours are not published on the site. The assistant will say the team confirms them by phone.
