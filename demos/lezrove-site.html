FAQ

Quick answers

What do the tour packages include?

Visa processing or assistance, flight booking, comfortable transfers, hotel reservations and guided visits to key sights, as per your requirements.

What payment options are available?

Payment links or online transfers, cash at the office, and the option to spread the cost over 4 instalments with Tabby.

What if I need to cancel?

Once payment is made, the agency's cancellation policy applies. If visa on arrival is denied, or a flight is cancelled or boarding refused, the package is non-refundable and non-transferable, and unused services are not refunded. Ask the team for the full terms.

Do you help with visas for other countries?

Yes. The list includes Saudi, Turkey, US, UK, Schengen, China, Bahrain and more. Their FAQ also mentions Japan and Oman.

Can I book without visiting the office?

Yes. Online customers can pay through payment links or online transfers.

Which destinations are most popular?

Georgia, Azerbaijan, Armenia and Turkey are listed as the most popular packages.
