The P&P Method

Five ways to work with us

01

Physiotherapy

Your first consult is a history and physical examination. The physio diagnoses the injury, explains it clearly, and creates a treatment plan to reach your goals and prevent re-injury.

  • Joint mobilisation
  • Manipulation
  • Massage
  • Neural stretches

Classes are led by a Clinical Pilates trained musculoskeletal physiotherapist, with exercise based on your body, your history and the goals you set. No prior experience needed; let the team know your level.

Before you come in

Quick answers

What happens at the first physio visit?

The physio takes your history and does a physical examination to diagnose the injury, explains the problem clearly, then creates an accurate treatment plan.

Do I need Pilates experience?

No. Please call or email ahead to say what level you are at and what you would like to achieve. Group classes begin with a 50 minute consultation and physical assessment.

Who leads the Pilates classes?

A Clinical Pilates trained musculoskeletal physiotherapist, who builds the exercise around your body, history, aggravating factors and goals.

How fast does migraine treatment work?

The clinic's stated goal is to reduce symptoms within 3 to 4 sessions and to manage symptoms through to complete resolution within 3 to 6 months. Results vary and your physio will assess you first.

Who is Safe Train for?

Everyone who has trained at a gym or on a sporting field and been injured by the intensity or frequency of the exercise, and anyone who wants to progress their training or regain peak performance under a qualified physiotherapist. It also works as a full-body workout for weight loss, with nutritional guidance.

What does the Golf Fit assessment involve?

A golf-specific physical examination, 3D motion capture data analysis to measure swing efficiency, and a customised workout program. The TPI certified assessment runs 90 minutes and includes a video assessment.

Which treatments might I receive?

Massage, joint mobilisations and manipulation, injury-specific exercise programs, neural stretches, strengthening and injury prevention exercises, electrotherapies, western dry needling or acupuncture, ergonomic advice, sport-specific training, video gait analysis and Clinical Pilates. Your physio chooses after assessing you.
