Your journey with us

From first contact to full recovery

  1. Initial assessment

    A comprehensive 60-minute session. We take your full history, identify movement patterns and map a clear picture of your condition before any treatment begins.

  2. Treatment plan

    A personalised, phased plan combining manual therapy, targeted exercise and technology, with clear milestones and a realistic timeline for recovery.

  3. Active rehabilitation

    Regular sessions with your dedicated practitioner, adapting the plan as you progress. Every session is purposeful, with no filler.

  4. Discharge & prevention

    When you are ready to leave, you get the tools, exercises and knowledge to maintain your recovery and prevent future injury.

Why Kinetika: unlike high-volume chains, your practitioner does not rotate. You see the same face every session, and that continuity helps recovery.

What sets us apart

Six principles of the practice

01

Root cause diagnosis

Every journey begins with a detailed, research-backed assessment that looks for the true origin of pain or movement dysfunction, not only its symptoms.

02

Bespoke care plans

No two patients are the same. Each protocol is designed around your body, your history, your goals and your timeline.

03

Manual therapy expertise

Practitioners lead with hands-on technique, movement analysis, mobilisation and soft tissue work before introducing technology.

04

Rehabilitation technology

Technogym, BTL, Magnetolith and Sidas are integrated to speed up outcomes beyond what manual therapy alone can do.

05

Collaborative care

The team works alongside your GP, surgeon and other healthcare professionals, so care is co-ordinated and not isolated.

06

Long-term accountability

Recovery does not end when the pain stops: discharge plans, return-to-sport protocols and preventive programmes keep you moving.

Questions we hear often

How long is the first appointment?

The initial assessment is a comprehensive 60-minute session.

Will I see the same therapist each time?

Yes. Unlike high-volume chains, your practitioner does not rotate.

Which languages do you speak?

English, Arabic and French at the clinic, plus Turkish with Ali.

Is the clinic licensed?

Yes, Kinetika is a DHA licensed clinic and every practitioner is individually registered.

Do you only treat pain?

No. Physiotherapy here is also a proactive step towards optimal health and movement, including prevention programmes and return-to-sport protocols.

Do you work with my doctor or surgeon?

Yes. The team works alongside your GP, surgeon and other healthcare professionals so your care plan is co-ordinated.

What does a typical plan look like?

Assessment, a phased plan combining manual therapy, targeted exercise and technology, regular sessions with your therapist, then discharge and prevention.
