Questions landlords ask

How often are inspections conducted?

Every six months unless you want them more often, with a detailed digital report and photos after each one.

What if the tenant stops paying rent?

An automated arrears protocol starts on Day 3 and the tenant is contacted immediately. If it is not resolved, tribunal processes follow in line with NSW law.

Do you manage short-term or Airbnb stays?

No. Lifestyle focuses on long-term residential rentals.

Do you manage strata properties?

Yes, including liaising with strata managers and following by-laws.

Do I need landlord insurance?

Lifestyle strongly recommends it, because building insurance does not cover rental defaults, malicious damage or lost rent.

Is there a minimum term?

No lock-in. If the service does not deliver, you are free to leave with no penalty.

Can I approve the tenant myself?

Yes. Lifestyle shortlists applicants and gives you summaries with recommendations.

Do I need to register for Rental Bonds Online?

Not if Lifestyle manages the property. The team lodges all bonds through the NSW Rental Bonds Online platform.

Can you advise on renovations or adding value?

Yes. They assess rental uplift potential, manage trades and guide your strategy based on what local tenants are paying for.

What happens when a lease expires?

Discussions begin well before expiry: renewal or a rent review. Since May 2025 NSW rules require a valid reason and correct notice to end a tenancy.

What if I want to sell?

The director also runs an established Sydney sales practice, so Lifestyle can handle it or refer you to a trusted, non-conflicted agent.

Questions tenants ask

Can I offer more rent to secure a property?

You may voluntarily offer above the asking rent, but under NSW law agents cannot solicit higher offers.

Are furnished properties available?

Yes, some are furnished. Check the listing for details.

How can I get my bond back?

Return the property in the same condition, fair wear and tear excepted, then request a refund via Rental Bonds Online.

What counts as fair wear and tear?

The gradual deterioration from ordinary living, such as carpet worn in a hallway or paint faded by the sun. Damage from carelessness or accidents is different.

What if I am behind on rent?

Contact the team early. NSW tenancy laws allow a termination notice if rent is 14 or more days overdue.

What is an emergency repair?

Burst pipes, power outages and dangerous damage. Contact the team immediately.

When can I renew my lease?

The team contacts you around 60 days before expiry with renewal options.

What notice do I give to vacate?

At least 21 days written notice if you are on a periodic lease. The website has a Notice to Vacate form.

Can I hang pictures or change the property?

Any alterations, even hanging pictures, need landlord approval.
