Good to know

Do I need a GP referral?

No. If you have referral letters or scan reports (MRI, X-ray), bring a copy for your file.

Can I come the same day?

Often, yes, most days, but it is not guaranteed. Call or book online to check today’s availability. Sundays are home visits only.

How long is the first visit?

Generally 20 to 40 minutes. The physiotherapist can give a closer estimate by phone.

Insurance and medical card?

Major Irish insurers refund treatment by a chartered physiotherapist, but check your policy first. You pay at the end of the visit and claim with the receipt; the clinic does not bill insurers directly. The medical card is not accepted.

Do you treat children?

Yes. A parent needs to be in the treatment room.
