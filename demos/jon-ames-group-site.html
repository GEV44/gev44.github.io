Two short checklists to start from

Drawn from the buyer's and seller's guides on the site.

BEFORE YOU BUY
  • Set aside a down payment, at minimum 5% of the price
  • Meet a mortgage broker and compare rates
  • Write a needs versus wants list
  • Think about schools, recreation and the neighbourhood
  • Line up a home inspection once an offer is accepted
BEFORE YOU SELL
  • Talk to your lender about your current mortgage
  • Interview more than one agent
  • Ask for the reasoning behind the listing price
  • Ask about staging before the listing goes live
  • Have your lawyer ready for the closing paperwork

Common questions

How do I find out what my home is worth?

The site has a home evaluation tool to help set a listing price that attracts offers while getting you a fair price. You can also ask here and the team will follow up.

How much do I need as a down payment?

The buyer's guide on the site says at minimum 5% of the purchase price. A mortgage broker will tell you what you can afford.

Can you help if I am not in Vancouver?

One client sold a family home remotely with Jonathan's help. Ask the team how they would handle your situation.

What does Jonathan's commission or fee look like?

Fees are not published online, so the team will confirm them directly.

Should I list with the agent who suggests the highest price?

The seller's guide warns against exactly that. Shop around, and look for an agent who can price the home to sell, answer "when is a good time to sell?" and knows the market in depth.

What kind of home inspection happens when I buy?

After an offer is negotiated, you conduct a home inspection and deal with any repairs, the cost of which you can also negotiate with the seller. Then you finalise your mortgage and sign, typically in a lawyer's office.

What type of homes can I search for?

Single family, townhome, condo, house with acreage, or a custom search. Listings include Richmond, Kitsilano in Vancouver and Squamish.

Does the group handle both buying and selling at once?

Yes. One client wrote that Jonathan quickly sold their property and found them a dream home within a few weeks after that.
