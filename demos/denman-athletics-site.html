FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I come if I am new to training?

Yes. Beginners are always welcome. You start with a consultation, where a coach talks through your experience, goals and any injuries, and adjusts the workout to match.

Can I drop in?

Conditioning classes: yes. SGPT strength sessions: no, they are only available through the trial or a membership so you can train consistently and see measurable progress.

How do I book a session?

Once you have the trial or a membership, book in advance through the website or the MindBody app, where the schedule is shown.

What is the cancellation policy?

24 hours for both SGPT and conditioning. Cancel or reschedule with 24 hours or more notice for a full credit back; later cancels are charged as if attended. Cancel online in MindBody under My Info, My Schedule.

How do waitlists work?

If a session is full, add your name to the waitlist. If a spot opens, you are added automatically and receive an e-mail confirmation. Remove yourself if you can no longer attend.

How often should I train?

For progress, consistency matters: the coaches recommend three times per week, adjusted for your goals, lifestyle and any injuries.

What should I bring?

Come dressed for a workout with a water bottle and a small towel.
