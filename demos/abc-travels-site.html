Before you ask

Good to know.

How long has ABC Travels been around?

The agency was established in 1974 by Vinod Shah and is privately owned, headquartered in Houston, Texas.

Do you only do leisure trips?

No. Planning can be stressful or overwhelming whether you travel for business or leisure, so the agency offers highly personalized services for both.

Can I book a business class fare?

Yes. Special business class fares are available when you call in and speak to an agent. Fares are subject to change.

What if something goes wrong abroad?

There is a 24-hour emergency service in case you are in need of help when you are abroad.

Do you arrange insurance and car hire?

Visitor's coverage and Travelex insurance links, plus CarTrawler car rental and Viator tours, are linked from the website.

Which cruises are listed?

An Iceland cruise and a 5-night Western Caribbean cruise are shown, each with a downloadable flyer on the site.

Are prices on the website?

The site notes that prices are subject to change, so the team confirms current options when you call or enquire.

Do I need to visit the office to book?

You can call (832) 563-0919, email the agency, or use the quick contact form on the website. The team will confirm how best to proceed for your trip.

Can you handle a group or family trip?

Tell the agent how many people are travelling and the dates you have in mind. The team will confirm what can be arranged for your group.

Do you help with visas, passports or vaccinations?

That is not covered on the website, so the team will confirm what they can advise on when you call.

Is this a big chain?

No. ABC Travels is privately owned and headquartered in Houston, Texas, founded by an entrepreneur in 1974 and still known to its clients as a personal, local agency.

Who will I speak to?

The site lists Vinod as the contact on the main phone line, (832) 563-0919. Email enquiries go to pragna@abctravels.com.

How are cruise details shared?

The Iceland cruise and the 5-night Western Caribbean cruise each have a downloadable flyer on the website, and the team can talk you through the options.

Can I ask about a trip that is not listed here?

Yes. The listed cruises and fares are only examples of what is on the website. Tell the agent where you want to go and the team will confirm what can be arranged.

Is there a quick way to ask a question?

Use the assistant on this page for instant answers drawn from the agency's own website, or send a message through the quick contact form on the main site.

What are the opening hours?

Opening hours are not published on the website. The team will confirm them.
