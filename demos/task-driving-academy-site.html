Good to know before your first lesson

The main points of the academy's published policies. The assistant can answer questions about them too.

Bring
Your learner's licence to every lesson. For a road test, 2 pieces of valid government ID plus your licence.
Lesson length
One hour is 60 minutes with at least 55 minutes behind the wheel, then a debrief from your instructor.
Cancelling
48 hours of notice for a lesson, otherwise a $50 fee. Less than 24 hours: the full lesson amount. Road-test bookings made by TASK need 72 hours.
The 15-minute window
Instructors may arrive up to 15 minutes early or late, and you still get your full hour.
Payment
Fees are paid in advance. A lesson or package is reserved once payment is received in full.
Safety
Only the instructor and student in the car, and training vehicles have an in-cabin dashcam.
