Miami is hard on cars

When to bring it in

Heat, humidity, salt air and stop-and-go traffic on US-1, the Dolphin and 836 wear a car faster than most places. The shop's own checklists for three common jobs:

Brakes

  • Squealing or grinding when you brake
  • Pulling to one side under braking
  • Pulsing or vibrating pedal at highway speed
  • A pedal that feels soft or sinks
  • Brake or ABS light on the dash

A/C

  • Lukewarm air, especially in traffic
  • Slow to cool down after a long drive
  • Clicking, hissing or grinding on start
  • Musty or sour smell from the vents
  • Weak airflow at full fan

Tires

  • Vibration in the wheel or seat
  • Car pulling to one side
  • A tire losing air faster than the others
  • Bulges, cuts or sidewall cracks
  • TPMS light on the dash

Rotation every 5,000–7,500 miles, or every other oil change. New tires by wholesale order when you are ready.
