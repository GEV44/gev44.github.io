About the school

Based in central London

Sohel's Driving School is based in central London. Its driving instructors are fully qualified, government approved driving instructors (ADIs), and the school describes them as patient, reliable, professional, knowledgeable and organised.

Every instructor will teach you the relevant road rules and regulations, in order to adhere to the rules enforced by the DVSA. The aim is stated plainly: to make you a safe, competent driver and help you pass first time.

Lessons are available in both manual and automatic cars, with evening and weekend classes, refresher courses, an intensive or 1-week course and a mock test before the exam. The school focuses on your success, no matter your age or experience.

Questions

Good to know

What is the cancellation policy?

Notify the instructor of a cancellation within 48 hours of the scheduled lesson. Instructors are self-employed and only paid for the work they carry out. If the instructor has to cancel, they will do their best to reschedule at no extra cost.

How long do credited lessons last?

Credited driving lessons or courses must be used within 6 months.

How can I pay?

Cash or bank transfer. Lessons are booked pre-paid.

How long is a lesson?

A full hour from the pick-up location, unless agreed with the instructor before the lesson.

What do I need on the first lesson?

A current valid driving licence or provisional licence to show the instructor.

Can the instructor refuse a lesson?

The terms say the instructor reserves the right to refuse a lesson if they think you are not fit to drive, are under stress, or are a risk on the road.

Do you help with nerves?

Yes. A tailored approach is offered to nervous learners, and the instructor has dual controls in the car to help ease nerves.

Can I choose the area I am picked up from?

Lessons start from your pick-up location within the areas covered. Call 07931 952281 if your area is not on the list and the school will tell you whether it is covered.

Can I bring someone along?

No passenger is taken during the lesson.
