Quick answers

Also available in the assistant, bottom right.

Which languages does Sandra speak?

English, Swedish, French and Spanish.

What does the home valuation include?

A custom Competitive Market Analysis that looks at location, size, features, condition and updates, with comparable homes in your area and surrounding towns.

Do you work with out-of-state and international clients?

Yes. The agency says it works with international, out-of-state and local clients.

What does hosting cost?

The 305 Stays says there are no fees to join and they earn a commission only when you receive a reservation. The team will confirm the details.

Which areas does the agency serve?

South and Central Florida. Sandra's own focus is Miami and South Beach, Coral Gables and Fort Lauderdale, and the agency says it is expanding its team of agents.

How do I get a free guide?

The Buy, Rent and Relocation pages each have a short form: choose the property type and when you plan to purchase, then download the guide.

Does the agency manage properties?

Yes. It offers comprehensive property management for landlords and investors, including financial reporting, guest service, and maintenance and maids for short-term rentals.

Can I also look at real estate outside Miami?

The International section lists Costa Rica, Georgia and Spain. Contact the team for details on each.

Is there a notary on the team?

Sandra is a Certified Public Notary and the site has a Notary page.
