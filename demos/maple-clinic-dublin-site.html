How a visit works

Forty minutes, start to finish, every time

1

Assessment of the real cause

The area of injury is assessed, and so is how you use it in your sport or activity, looking for weakness or imbalance that makes you load it unevenly.

2

Hands-on treatment

Trigger point release, fascial work, joint mobilisations or stretching. Dry needling is offered for those who like it, and skipped if you do not.

3

A personal rehab plan

Emailed to you after the appointment, with a video and clear explanation for each exercise. Open it in an app on your phone or download a PDF.

4

Follow-ups that fit the problem

As a rough example, a knee problem might mean 5 to 6 visits over 10 to 12 weeks, spaced out to every 2 to 3 weeks once you are working on the exercises.

Dry needling uses fine acupuncture needles on tender trigger points. It combines with hands-on treatment and rehab where appropriate. If you do not like needles, they will not be used on you.

Read more about dry needling

It is called “dry” needling because it developed from an older practice of injecting trigger points. Trigger points are knots of tight tissue that are tender to touch and can cause pain and dysfunction. Today fine needles are used with no injection. The needles affect the chemical mix of the trigger point so that it releases and eases out. The clinic describes it as very useful for back and neck pain, sports injuries, and assisting with chronic pain syndromes, and many clients find it effective for tender or acute areas that can be too sore for hands-on treatment.

Before you come

Questions people ask first

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

No. Appointments can be made without a referral. If the assessment shows your condition needs medical attention, you will be referred on.

What should I wear?

Loose-fitting clothing. For the lower back, hips or legs, shorts help, and the clinic keeps spare shorts if you forget a pair. For back, rib, shoulder or neck problems you may be asked to remove your top (except underwear). Towels preserve modesty, so tell the team about any concerns.

How many treatments will I need?

It depends on the condition, how long it has been there and how you respond. Your therapist will discuss the expected number after the first appointment.

What will I feel after treatment?

Sometimes some discomfort that is short term and quickly subsides. For any concern, call the clinic on 01-5441225.

Is it covered by private medical insurance?

Physical therapy is recognised by all the major insurance companies, but the amount you can claim depends on your healthcare plan. Your insurer can advise. Some insurers recognising the clinic: Irish Life Health, Laya, VHI.

Do I have to have the needles?

No. Dry needling is part of the clinic's physical therapy treatments, but if you do not like needles they will not use them on you.

Is there parking?

There is parking outside the clinic. Paid parking applies until 6pm, Monday to Friday.
