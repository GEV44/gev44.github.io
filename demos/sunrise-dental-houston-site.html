FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Which insurance do you accept?

We're in-network with several plans, including Cigna PPO and Delta Dental PPO. Even if we're not in-network with your plan, we'll help maximize your benefits and file an out-of-network claim for you.

I don't have insurance. What are my options?

The in-house Clerri dental savings plan has no deductibles, waiting periods or yearly maximums. Adult Complete is $38 a month ($456 a year) with 2 cleanings, 2 exams with X-rays, a 3D X-ray, an oral cancer screening and an emergency exam, plus 15% off procedures.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, CareCredit and Cherry, with zero to low interest options and customizable payment schedules.

What happens at a first checkup?

About an hour: X-rays, a thorough exam with an intraoral camera, a chat about the findings with photos on a chairside monitor, and a cleaning. New patients are asked to fill in the forms at home beforehand.

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes, same-day, and you can call or text (713) 869-9973 any time. New patients are welcome.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you can't keep your appointment. A fee may be charged for a no-show without enough notice.
