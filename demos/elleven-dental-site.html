FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How long does the first appointment take?

Please allow around 45 minutes for a comprehensive assessment.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

Yes. If you're in pain or have an urgent concern, contact the practice and the team will do their best to see you the same day.

Do you accept nervous patients?

Yes, the team is highly experienced in supporting anxious patients and can discuss sedation options.

Where can I park?

There is street parking outside, payable by Ringo, with a 4 hour maximum stay. The practice is in the congestion zone.

Can I book online?

Yes. New patients can book a consultation online, and registered patients can book directly into the diary.
