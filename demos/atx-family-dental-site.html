Services

A welcoming, full-service practice, from routine family care to cosmetic treatments. Tap a service to see what the practice says to expect.

Routine care

1 hour, established patients2 hours, new patients

Routine cleanings and exams keep a healthy smile, and contribute to overall health. Registered Dental Hygienists are committed to educating you every step of the way: clear, kind and thorough.

Visits typically begin with low-radiation digital X-rays and a comprehensive examination of teeth and gums. Cleanings use hand instruments and an ultrasonic scaler to gently break up plaque and tartar, and you get home-care guidance so future cleanings are easy breezy. If there is anything to address, the team builds a treatment plan customized to you.

Emergency dentistry

45 minutes or longer

Dental emergencies do not follow a schedule. The team leaves room in the schedule each day to see Austinites with tooth or gum pain, whether you are a current patient or new, and sometimes stays late or adjusts appointments.

You start with a quick but thorough evaluation: an X-ray and a focused 15-30 minute limited exam. A dentist walks you through clear images and your options, and you leave with a plan. In pain right now? Call (512) 717-3147.

Invisalign

A comfortable, discreet process using advanced technology and a custom treatment plan. The practice describes Invisalign as able to straighten teeth in as little as six months. Straight teeth are not only about looks: correcting alignment can improve your bite, reduce wear on teeth and support long-term oral health.

Dental implants

Sometimes a tooth cannot be saved. An implant can support the surrounding teeth, assist chewing and maintain your smile. The steps: an initial consultation with X-rays and scans; implant placement of a titanium post, typically under local anesthesia; several months of healing (osseointegration); then an abutment with a custom crown, bridge or denture matched to your natural teeth; and ongoing routine care.

Dental veneers

Custom-made, ultra-thin porcelain shells covering the front of the teeth to change colour, shape and length. The approach is conservative: enhance your natural teeth, not replace them. It typically takes a few visits: digital scans and a custom design, a tiny bit of tooth prep, impressions and temporary veneers, and a couple of weeks later the final veneers are bonded.

Cosmetic dentistry

Unhappy with dark fillings, old crowns, food stains or pronounced white marks? Tooth whitening with Zoom or KoR, bonding and natural-looking fillings are among the tools used. The aim is results that look totally natural, so that no one knows you had work done.

Root canals

Up to 3 hours including restorative treatment

A root canal aims to save a badly infected or decayed tooth and get you out of pain. A range of pain-management options is offered. Afterwards the tooth usually needs a crown, because removing damaged tissue can leave it more fragile.

Dental crowns

1st appointment: 2 hours2nd appointment: 30 min

A crown fits over a damaged tooth to restore its shape, size and strength, often for a cracked or worn tooth or after a root canal. At the first visit you are numbed (nitrous is offered if you feel nervous), the tooth is prepared, a digital impression or 3D scan is taken, and a temporary crown stays about two weeks. At the second visit the final crown is tried on and fitted.

Which treatment suits you is a clinical decision for the dentists at your visit; this page only repeats what the practice publishes.

Do we see kids? You bet.

Kid-friendly care that helps children feel at ease from start to finish, and everyone in the family, whatever their age. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends a first dental visit by age one or within six months of the first tooth appearing.

First visit, around age 1

May be brief. The dentist checks for signs of cavities or teething problems, examines the gums, and may offer advice on infant oral care, such as cleaning gums and starting a soft toothbrush.

Check-ups, ages 2 to 3

A more thorough check-up as teeth come in: cleaning, a look for cavities and early signs of orthodontic problems, and getting used to the sounds and equipment so future visits are easier.

Quick answers

How long is a first visit?

The practice plans about 2 hours for new patients and 1 hour for established patients for routine care.

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes. Room is left in the schedule each day for tooth or gum pain, including for new patients. Call (512) 717-3147.

Do you take my insurance?

Check the PPO carrier list above; for anything else call and the team will help. HMO, DMO and Medicaid plans are not accepted.

I feel nervous about the dentist.

Let the team know when you book. Dr. Najafi is known for helping anxious patients, nitrous is offered for crowns, and root canals come with pain-management options.

What does the Wellness Plan not include?

Panoramic or CT scan X-rays, Invisalign orthodontic treatment, Zoom whitening and bleach trays, nitrous oxide, fluoride, sealants, desensitizing bond, and products such as CariFree toothpaste, electric toothbrushes and bleaching gels. Child X-rays are taken as needed, as determined by a doctor.

How does the team help with insurance?

When you schedule, the team collects your insurance information, verifies your eligibility and benefits with your provider, and files the claim. Before treatment you are told any out-of-pocket costs for the non-covered portion. Even if the practice is not a preferred provider for your plan, it can still check your benefits, and the cost of preventive care such as exams, X-rays and cleanings is often the same in or out of network.

What if my insurer does not pay as expected?

The practice is upfront that estimates are not guarantees, that your policy is a contract between you and the carrier, and that claims are sometimes not paid as expected, in which case the balance is the patient's responsibility, as at any other medical office.

Why bring a young child in early?

Early visits let the dentist catch potential problems before they become serious, give parents guidance on oral hygiene habits, allow early intervention for orthodontic concerns, and help children get used to the dental office so later visits cause less anxiety.

What counts as a dental emergency?

The practice describes it as any urgent dental issue needing immediate attention to relieve pain, prevent further damage or protect your oral health, and suggests acting if pain is 5 or above on a scale of 1-10. For anything urgent, call (512) 717-3147; the team, not this assistant, decides what is needed.

Can I pay online?

The practice site has a Make Payment link, and an online booking link as well. Accepted payment: most PPO insurances, all major credit cards, CareCredit third-party financing, and FSA and HSA benefits.
