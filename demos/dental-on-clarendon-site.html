Health funds

Claim on the spot

We use HICAPS for on-the-spot claiming and accept all major health funds. We are a preferred provider for Bupa, Medibank, NIB, HCF and CBHS. Your rebate depends on your cover, and reception will confirm it.

Ask about my fund

Payment plans

Interest-free payment plans are available through third-party providers such as humm, with terms and eligibility criteria applying.

To follow the National Dental Board and AHPRA guidelines, third-party payment plans are reserved for general, preventative and restorative care. For elective cosmetic treatments we accept credit card, EFTPOS or cash upfront.

Good to know

Quick questions

Can I get an emergency appointment today?

Emergency same-day appointments and urgent priority bookings are available. Call 03 9690 3285 as early as you can.

Do you publish fees?

No. Costs vary by patient and need a clinical assessment, and rebates depend on your fund. The team gives you a figure after your check-up.

Are payment plans available for veneers or whitening?

No. Third-party plans such as humm are only for general, preventative and restorative care. Elective cosmetic treatments are paid upfront by credit card, EFTPOS or cash.

Is my child covered?

Children under 17 can be bulk billed under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule if eligible. Call to check eligibility.

How do I get there?

Free street parking, or tram 12 to stop 129 and a three minute walk.

Which languages do your dentists speak?

Dr Laith: English and Arabic. Dr Linda: English and Vietnamese, with conversational Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien and Teochew. Dr Xu: English and Mandarin.
