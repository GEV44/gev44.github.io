What we do

Four services, done in house.

Tap a service for what is included. Anything about your own pet’s health goes to the vets, the website and this page never replace an exam.

01

Pet wellness

+
Services designed to keep your pet healthy and happy at every life stage, so problems are caught before they start.
  • Vaccinations
  • Deworming
  • Heartworm and flea prevention
  • Comprehensive bloodwork
Routine blood work can detect problems early; your vet chooses what fits your pet.
02

Pet radiology

+
In-house digital radiography (X-ray), over-read by a board certified veterinary radiologist. Ultrasound is available by appointment only. Imaging can reveal what a physical exam alone can’t.
03

Pet surgery

+
From routine to complex, with a personalized anesthetic protocol, advanced monitoring and dedicated surgery technicians.
  • Spay and neuter
  • Mass removal and biopsies
  • Eye and ear surgery
  • Amputation
  • Orthopedic and soft tissue surgery
The vets explain the risks and benefits with you before any procedure. A surgery release form is available online.
04

Pet dentistry

+
Oral health is tied to overall health. Check-ups and cleanings remove tartar and plaque, with scaling and polishing, dental X-rays when recommended, and extractions if needed. Your vet walks you through each step.

Good to know

Quick answers

What type of pets do you see?

Dogs and cats, plus select exotics.

Boarding or grooming?

No, we don’t offer those at this time.

Can I drop my pet off?

Yes. Call and a client service coordinator will set up a drop-off appointment.

Do you have an online pharmacy?

Yes, through VetSource. It is linked from the Online Pharmacy page.

My pet is having a medical emergency.

Call us during business hours for an emergency appointment, based on availability. After hours: Gulf Coast Veterinary Specialists 713-693-1111 or Pearland 288 Animal Emergency (713) 482-4592.
