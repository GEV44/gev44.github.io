Programs

What we teach

Traditional and modern styles under one roof, in what the school calls one of the cleanest, most professionally run schools in Mesa.

Kids & adults

Martial Arts

Tae Kwon Do, Kickboxing, Jiu Jitsu and Fit Defense in one dynamic program. Kids build respect, self-discipline, responsibility, confidence, self-control and focus.

Ages 8-12 · teens · adults

Kickboxing

Punching, kicking, blocking and combinations, plus fitness and stress relief. Adults train it inside Fit Defense: an intense workout with real striking skills. Trainers with over 10 years of experience.

Ages 7-12 · teens · adults

Jiu Jitsu

Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, Gi and No-Gi. Grappling, leverage and submissions: a strong self-defense tool for both kids and adults.

Ages 5-7 · 8-12

Ninja Trix

Tumbling, parkour, martial arts, obstacle training and freestyle movement. Kids run, jump, kick, spin and climb. A teen program is on the horizon.

Adults

Adult Fit Defense

Adult classes combining Tae Kwon Do, Kickboxing, Jiu Jitsu and Krav Maga. Build strength, learn self-defense and relieve stress in a welcoming community.

Camps · teens

Camps & STORM

Summer and other day camps for school breaks, plus the STORM (CIT) program. Ask the team for current dates and details.

Our philosophy

More than kicks and punches

The goal at Precision Martial Arts is to empower as many people as possible to be successful in anything they set out to accomplish. We aim to arm students with the skills, confidence and discipline to defend themselves and achieve success in life, using holistic and traditional methods.

Boost confidenceBuild self-esteem and carry yourself with it.
Improve fitnessStrength, flexibility and stamina through engaging training.
Enhance focusDiscipline and mental clarity for school, work and personal goals.
Learn self-defensePractical skills drawn from several martial arts styles.
Respect and disciplineCore values that shape respectful, goal-oriented people.
Stress reliefBalance and calm through focused practice and controlled movement.
Good to know

Quick answers

Can a complete beginner start?

Yes. The trainers have taught athletes, fighters and people with no experience, in a clean, professionally run school.

What is the class schedule?

It changes from time to time. Request the current schedule and the team emails or texts it to you.

How much do classes cost?

Class pricing is not listed on the website, so the team will confirm it. Party packages are priced above. New students can ask about the special offer.

What ages do you teach?

From 5-year-olds in Ninja Trix to adults in Kickboxing, Jiu Jitsu and Fit Defense. After-school pick-up is for kindergarten through 6th grade.

Is the after-school program safe?

Pick-up is by the team in the Ninja Bus, parents get a text when the child boards, and kids are supervised throughout.

Are you hiring?

There is a Join Our Team page on the website. You can also call 480-833-5537 or email info@pmamesa.com.
