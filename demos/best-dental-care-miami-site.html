Good to know

Plain-language notes from the service pages on the practice site. General information only, not advice for your own mouth.

Dental implants

A titanium root is attached to the jaw by surgery, topped with a porcelain part that looks like the rest of your teeth. The site lists three benefits: they keep other teeth aligned, they stimulate jaw bone regeneration and they look natural.

Not for everyone: a healthy jawbone with enough density is needed, which is why a consultation comes first.

Emergency examination

The doctors assess the damage and, if a tooth is broken or there's no visible infection, take an X-ray. Then you decide together on the next step, be it a root canal or an extraction. The site says anesthesia keeps the procedure pain free.

Hurting right now? Call the office instead of waiting.

After an extraction

There's a Post-extraction Instructions page for patients to read once they're home. One patient on the site mentions being given wonderful instructions after an extraction.

Anything that worries you afterwards is a question for the team.

Quick answers

Can I book online?

Yes, the website links to an online appointment page. It notes that requested dates and times are not guaranteed and a representative will reach out to confirm.

Do you offer same-day crowns?

Yes, Same-Day Crowns are listed: a custom crown in a single appointment.

I'm nervous about the dentist. What helps?

Nitrous oxide is listed under sedation dentistry, and patients on the site describe doctors who explain every step. Mention it when you book.

What if I have a dental emergency?

There is an Emergency Examination service. Call (305) 642-6330 right away. For serious swelling, trouble breathing or heavy bleeding call 911.

Do you treat children?

The website doesn't say, so ask the front desk when you call.

Do you offer All-on-4?

Yes, All-on-4 is listed: a technique for full-arch tooth replacement using four implants. A consultation tells you whether it suits you.

Can I get aligners here?

Clear aligner therapy is listed, using CandidPro aligners.

Do you do Botox?
