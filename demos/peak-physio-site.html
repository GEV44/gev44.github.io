Reformer Pilates, led by physios

Classes of six people maximum, led from start to finish by a qualified physiotherapist, so modifications fit your goals, aches or injury.

Fundamentals

For complete beginners or anyone going back to basics. Complete at least 5 Fundamentals classes before progressing.

Level 1

For those with 5 or more Fundamentals classes or some reformer experience, with more challenging variations.

Level 2

For experienced reformer clients: a dynamic flow for strength, stability and mobility. At least 10 Level 1 classes are recommended first.

Stretch & Flow

Restorative stretching with dynamic Pilates on the reformer. Complete 2 to 3 Fundamentals classes first.

Postnatal

Helps new mothers rebuild strength and balance after childbirth, with core stability, pelvic floor strengthening and posture work.

Yogalates

A mix of yoga and Pilates movements on the reformer for flexibility, body awareness and coordination. Complete 5 Fundamentals classes first.

Why a reformer? The reformer has a moving carriage that allows smooth movement, with springs giving subtle resistance to challenge the muscles effectively. You get support to keep posture and alignment, and the resistance can be adjusted for your goal. After a few classes people report stronger core, better functional movement and lower risk of injury in daily activities.
Who is it for? Anyone managing aches, pains or injury, improving sporting performance, or simply building strength and flexibility. Instructors are qualified in managing pain and injury through the Pilates method, and give individual feedback during class so each exercise is done safely.

Quick answers

How long until I get an appointment?

An appointment time is guaranteed within 24 hours of booking, and usually you can be seen the same day.

Do I need a GP referral?

No. If you have a referral, MRI or X-ray report, bring it.

Am I covered by health insurance?

The team is covered by all the major health insurers. The amount you can claim depends on your level of cover, so check with your insurer.

Who gets the student discount?

Anyone in full-time attendance at a third-level institution.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes. For a knee or ankle injury, shorts are advisable, and spare shorts are available.

Where is the Ballsbridge clinic now?

The clinic has moved around the corner to a brand new clinic at 56 Lansdowne Rd, D04 A6R2, beside the Aviva stadium.

Is the Reformer Studio a separate place?

Yes, The Reformer Studio is at 6 S Lotts Rd, Beggar's Bush, Dublin 4, D04 FR83. Classes have a maximum of six people.

How will I remember my exercises?

Your exercises are emailed with written instructions and videos, and can be opened on a tablet, smartphone or laptop.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

Yes, there is a gift vouchers page on the website.
