What you get

From the clinic

Physiotherapy

Low back and neck pain through to joint, muscle, tendon and ligament sprains. Chartered physios skilled in hands-on treatment and rehab programmes built on evidence and experience. Pre and post surgery and pre/post natal rehab too.

They liaise with orthopaedic surgeons, GPs and sports medicine physicians

Physical & Sports Therapy

Soft tissue and trigger point therapy combined with mobility and progressive rehab. Return-to-play rehab for long-term or recurring injuries, and recovery support before and after events.

Athletes and weekend warriors alike

Sports Massage & Dry Needling

Deep hands-on work to release tension and cut recovery time, also useful against the tension of long periods of sitting. Dry needling targets trigger points and may not suit everyone, so the team advises on the right treatment.

Get back to training faster

Personal & Small Group

One to one sessions built around your goals and what your body needs. Small group or semi-private for up to 6 people brings the push of a group with personal attention. All abilities.

Recovering, new to the gym, or chasing a goal

Performance Training

Athletic development to find your personal best. The coaching team have performed at the highest level in their own sports, and plans connect gym work with field work, for individual or team sports.

Returning from injury or raising your game

Pre/Post Natal

Guided strength training through pregnancy, focusing on low back, hips and pelvic floor. After birth, corrective exercise and pelvic floor strengthening whether you had a vaginal delivery or a C-section.

Coaches and physios address any concerns

FAQ

Quick answers
What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 7am to 8pm. Weekend hours are not listed, please ask the team.

Is it only for athletes?

No. Athletes and non-athletes alike. One to one training suits beginners, and the gym team says all abilities and levels are catered for.

Can I book online?

Online booking for training and therapy is listed as coming soon. For now, contact the clinic directly by phone or through the contact form.

Do I need a referral?

Not stated on the site. The physios work closely with orthopaedic surgeons, GPs and sports medicine physicians. Please ask the team.

I have never trained in a gym. Is that fine?

Yes. One to one training is a good way to learn how to train properly and build confidence in a safe, motivating environment. The coaches tailor sessions to your lifestyle and cater for all abilities.

Do you work with team sport players?

Yes. Performance Training suits individual or team sports, and the site features Dublin senior footballers who train with the clinic.

Do you offer dry needling?

Yes. Trigger point dry needling is often used alongside physiotherapy and physical therapy to reduce pain, increase movement and restore strength. It may not suit everyone, so the team advises on the most suitable course of treatment.

How much does it cost?

Prices are not published on the site. The team will confirm when you get in touch.
