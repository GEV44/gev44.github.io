Renting in Dublin: tips from the team

From the firm's tenants guide.

Set a realistic budget

Renting in Dublin does not come cheap, so count monthly bills as an unavoidable cost.

Be organised

Have employer, former landlord and character references ready, because without them the move-in is delayed.

Know what you pay for

Concierge, parking, a gym, a water view: ask yourself whether the extras are good value for you.

Remember the deposit

Normally one month's rent, paid up front and fully refunded when you return the home in agreeable condition.

Find a community

Developments with resident events help friendships form, and happy residents tend to stay longer.

Treat it like home

Landlords want good tenants who take pride in the space, so check with them before changes.

Questions we hear

What is the difference between lettings and full management?

Lettings markets the property and secures tenants. Full property management adds rent collection, maintenance and tenant care. The firm offers both.

Can you help first-time landlords?

Yes, with pricing, legal requirements and tenant management, from marketing to lease agreements.

Do you manage apartment blocks?

Yes: block management for Owners' Management Companies, covering finances, maintenance oversight, compliance and day-to-day operations.

Do you handle luxury properties?

Yes, with a service tailored to attract premium tenants and buyers and to maximise results.

Do you offer new homes marketing?

Yes, from small infill developments to 300+ unit apartment schemes: data-led advice, design advice, show house design and marketing strategy.
