Your road to a licence

The Irish steps in order, with where Art of Driving helps at each one.

  1. Pass the Driver Theory Test

    Booked online. It checks the Rules of the Road and traffic signs, and comes before your learner permit.

  2. Get your learner permit

    From age 17, through NDLS online or at an NDLS centre. Bring it to every lesson.

  3. Complete 12 EDT lessons

    Essential Driver Training with an Approved Driving Instructor. Your free logbook is filled in lesson by lesson.

  4. Practise between lessons

    With someone who has held a full licence in the category for at least two years, and L plates on the car.

  5. Warm up, mock test and hire the car

    A mock test shows where you lose marks. On the day, a one-hour warm-up and a test car from us.

  6. Book and take the test

    Book online with the RSA once EDT is done and the permit is six months old. The test lasts about 30 to 40 minutes.

Test day, step by step

Nothing on the day should be a surprise.

1. Warm-upYour instructor meets you for a brief warm-up lesson before the test.
2. Drive to the test centreYou drive there and meet the examiner. Hire a test car from us if you need one.
3. Questions and inspectionQuestions about the car and the Rules of the Road, then a brief visual inspection.
4. The practical testAbout 30 to 40 minutes, including one manoeuvre: reverse around a corner, hill start or turnabout.

Quick answers

How many lessons do I need?

First-time learners must complete 12 EDT lessons. That is the mandatory minimum; most learners need extra lessons and private practice to be test-ready.

What should I bring to my first lesson?

Your valid learner permit and EDT logbook, comfortable closed-toe shoes, and glasses or contact lenses if you need them.

What if I fail my test?

It is very common. The examiner's report shows where you lost marks, and your instructor builds a tailored lesson plan around those areas before you re-apply.

What will I be asked to do on the test?

One of: a reverse around a corner, a hill start or a turnabout, plus questions on the car controls and the Rules of the Road.

When can I book the practical test?

Online through the RSA, after completing all 12 EDT lessons and holding your learner permit for at least six months. Check with your instructor first.

Can I start with a few lessons only?

Yes. Besides the 12-lesson and 6-lesson EDT blocks, there are single 1-hour and 2-hour lessons for extra practice, plus refresher lessons for returning drivers.

Where do lessons start?

At home, work, college or school: the door-to-door service is free. Lessons can run on the actual test routes so the test environment feels familiar.

Can I get a gift voucher?

Yes. Contact the team directly and they will help you choose and organise it.
