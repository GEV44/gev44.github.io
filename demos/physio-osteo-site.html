Two disciplines, one plan

Dr. Ben combines physical therapy with European osteopathy, looking for the root of the problem instead of only treating symptoms.

Physical therapy

Relieving pain and restoring movement through manual therapy, physiotherapy and rehabilitation exercises, with techniques tailored to your needs.

Therapeutic massage, guided stretching, gentle joint mobilization and precise adjustments to relieve pain, improve function and support structural balance.

Exercises customized for strength, mobility, flexibility and endurance, to rehabilitate injuries and boost physical health.

Training with advanced equipment, including the Huber 360 Evolution. Each program starts with an assessment of flexibility, mobility, balance, strength and coordination. Used for sports injury recovery, post-surgical rehab and posture, for athletes, post-op patients, desk workers, seniors and children.

Fine needles at trigger points to ease muscular pain and tension and promote healing.

Specialized garments to improve circulation, reduce swelling and aid recovery, including for chronic venous insufficiency, lymphedema and post-exercise recovery.

Kinesiology and other taping to support joints and muscles without restricting movement.

European osteopathy

Osteopaths see the body as a unified whole. Three families of techniques restore overall body motion.

Structural manipulation realigns the body's framework to improve function, ease pain, improve joint mobility and restore balance. Described as effective for back pain, postural imbalances and joint stiffness.

A gentle, hands-on approach to the central nervous system, using subtle work on the cranial bones and sacrum to release tension. Headaches, stress and relaxation are mentioned.

Specific, gentle pressures to improve the mobility and function of the internal organs and address restrictions in the visceral system.

Out-of-network, on purpose

Physio Osteo does not work directly with insurance companies, which keeps sessions one-on-one with the doctor. The clinic says this often means effective treatment in fewer sessions. Most insurers allow out-of-network care, so check your reimbursement options.

Not sure about your benefits?

Ask your insurer about your deductible, out-of-pocket expenses, co-payment and whether physical therapy needs pre-authorization. The team can help: send a photo of your insurance card (front and back) and your date of birth.

Questions

The assistant knows these too.

What is the difference between physical therapy and European osteopathy?

Physical therapy focuses on relieving pain and restoring movement through manual therapy, physiotherapy and rehabilitation exercises. European osteopathy employs craniosacral therapy, visceral manipulation and musculoskeletal techniques to restore overall body motion. They complement each other in addressing the root cause.

How do I schedule?

Phone the clinic, fill in the contact form on the website, or book online. The team finds a convenient time.

How long is a session?

The first session is 1 hour. Follow-ups vary with your plan, from 30 minutes to 1 hour.

How should I prepare?

Arrive on time, wear comfortable clothing that allows easy movement, and complete the intake form beforehand.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

Not to begin. In the state of Florida you have direct access to physical therapy services for up to 30 days without a physician's referral, so you can start care at Physio Osteo right away.

How do I know if I have out-of-network benefits?

Contact your insurance provider about your deductible, out-of-pocket expenses and co-payment requirements, and ask whether physical therapy needs pre-authorization. If you prefer, the team can assist: send a photo of your insurance card (front and back) and your date of birth.

Why is Physio Osteo out of network?

Not working directly with insurers lets the practice focus on tailored one-on-one sessions with the doctor, which often leads to effective treatment in fewer sessions.
