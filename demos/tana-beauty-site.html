Before and after

  • BeforeSkip harsh exfoliants and chemical peels on the brow area for 7-10 days.
  • On the dayTopical anaesthetic, then consultation and design. You agree the shape before any pigment goes in.
  • First daysPigment is most vibrant at 24-48 hours, then peels for 3-7 days. Don't touch the area; apply antiseptic cream twice daily.
  • RetouchRecommended within 6-8 weeks. Results last about 3-5 years depending on skin and care.

Lip blush

A naturally tinted, fuller-looking lip. We design a deeper outline that melts into a softer centre. Pigment gets stronger as it heals, so plan around weddings, birthdays and big meetings. Taking lysine or a cold sore preventative 7-10 days beforehand is recommended.

The Tana story

Tana Beauty began as a humble lash extension studio, built on the belief that everyone deserves to flaunt their unique beauty. Attention to detail earned a loyal clientele, and the studio grew into cosmetic tattooing to offer more personalised results.

Our commitment

Comfort and satisfaction come first. The salon is cozy and welcoming, and the team keeps training to stay current with industry trends and techniques, so each visit feels relaxed and the result is something you are happy to wear.

Good to know

The salon's policies in one place, so there are no surprises on the day.

Deposit

A 30% deposit secures your booking and is non-refundable. If you reschedule in time it transfers to your new appointment.

Cancelling or rescheduling

Please give at least 48 hours notice by phone, email or Instagram. Less is a late cancellation; not attending without notice is a no-show.

Arriving late

There is a 15-minute grace period. A late session may be shortened, with the full fee still applying. Beyond 15 minutes counts as a no-show and 50% of the service fee is due upfront to rebook.

Lash refills
  • Refills are recommended every 2 weeks.
  • Less than 20% of lashes remaining means a new set.
  • Outside 4 weeks also means a new set.
  • Online refills are for existing clients only.
  • Changing style, length, thickness or curl: removal, then a new set.
  • We don't work on or refill other technicians' sets.
