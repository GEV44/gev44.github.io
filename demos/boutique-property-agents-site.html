Before you ring

Quick answers

Do you manage apartment blocks?

Yes. The agency says it manages several of Sydney's key apartment blocks and is the preferred agent for sales and property management in them.

Can landlords get a rental appraisal?

Yes, a rental appraisal is listed under Rent. The property management team also takes enquiries from owners who want their property managed.

Is there a way to hear about new listings first?

There are Priority Buyer Alerts and Rental Alerts on the site, and a newsletter with insider tips and market updates.

Do you list commercial properties?

Yes. The Buy menu has a commercial section and the Rent menu has commercial leases. The team confirms what is currently available.

Can I see what has already been leased?

There is a Leased Properties page, alongside Recently Sold, so you can see recent results.

Who looks after trust accounting?

The team page lists Leanne Gorman as Trust Accountant. For questions about your statement, the assistant routes you to the office.

Is there a borrowing capacity calculator?

Yes, under Buy, then Calculators. For advice on your own finances, speak to a lender.

Is the website the only way to enquire?

No. You can call the office on 02 8815 8195, email, or visit Shop 1 at 251 Elizabeth Street. The assistant simply gathers your details so the right person can reply.

Where is the office?

Shop 1, 251 Elizabeth Street, Sydney NSW 2000.

How do I contact the team?

Call 02 8815 8195 or email info@boutiquepropertyagents.com.

What are the opening hours?

They are not published on the website, so the team will confirm them.

How do I report a repair?

Use the maintenance request page, or the tenant emergency repairs page for urgent issues. In a life-threatening emergency call 000.

Can I get a free appraisal?

Yes, a free market appraisal is offered. The assistant takes your address and a good time to call.
