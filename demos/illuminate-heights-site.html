Coaching & services

Stop guessing.
Train with direction.

Coaching is available to members. Not sure what fits? Start with a consultation and tell the team where you are and what has not worked before.

Personal training

One-on-one coaching built around your goals, schedule and experience level. Good for learning fundamentals, building strength, fat loss or muscle gain, and accountability.

Semi-private training

Small-group coaching with structure, accountability and expert guidance. Good for building consistency, training with energy and clear programming.

Contest prep

Detailed coaching for physique athletes preparing for the stage: bodybuilding, physique competition, posing support and nutrition structure. Built for competitors only.

Nutrition coaching

Practical guidance for performance, body composition and consistency: fat loss, muscle gain, better habits and meal structure.

Lifestyle support

Accountability and habit support for people who want progress beyond workouts alone.

Questions

Before you decide.

Do I need to be advanced to join?

No. You do not need to be an athlete or a bodybuilder. You need to want a better place to train and be ready to take your progress seriously.

Is Dark Side included?

Yes. Dark Side comes with every membership.

Do I need a membership to book coaching?

Yes, coaching services are for members. You can add personal training, semi-private training, nutrition or lifestyle coaching any time.

Is the gym staffed 24/7?

No. Staffed hours are listed above; members get 24/7 access after signing up.

Do 2-day and 3-day passes need to be consecutive?

Yes, multi-day passes must be used on consecutive days.
