What we treat

Six areas, one clinic. Not sure which fits? Ask the assistant or call the team.

  1. 01

    Physiotherapy

    Diagnosing, rehabilitating and improving movement disorders with evidence-based, natural methods: exercise, manual therapy, motivation, adapted equipment and advocacy.

  2. 02

    Sports physiotherapy

    Prevention, evaluation and management of sport and exercise injuries, fresh, old, recurring or post-surgical, coordinated with surgeons, sports physicians and coaches when needed.

  3. 03

    ACL rehabilitation

    Exercise before surgery to restore range of motion and strength, then rehab from day one to return to sport. The clinic works closely with three ACL surgeons.

  4. 04

    Shoulder rehabilitation

    Rotator cuff and shoulder blade strengthening for overuse problems, and coordination and strength training after dislocations or surgery.

  5. 05

    Pilates

    Mat-based and equipment-based classes tailored to the individual, to complement treatment, improve posture and build core strength.

  6. 06

    WorkCover and motor vehicle accidents

    All WorkCover and CTP injuries are treated. Bring your claim number and your doctor's and insurer's details to the first visit.

Who we see

Patients arrive from desks, pitches, pools and operating theatres. A few of the situations the clinic describes:

Pain at your desk

Prolonged sitting often leads to poor posture and back or neck pain. Treatment is hands-on and based on you, not a label.

Just had surgery

Rehab planned with your surgeon in mind, using the gym on site, from day one through to return to activity.

A sports injury

Fresh, old or recurring. Evaluation and treatment first, then neuromuscular control and strengthening to avoid re-injury.

Getting back to sport

The aim is to return you to the field, coordinating with orthopaedic surgeons, sports physicians and coaches when needed.

Pilates at the clinic

Pilates is a gentle form of exercise that improves posture, flexibility and core strength, using mat-based exercises and specialised equipment to retrain and restore movement. The clinic offers classes to complement treatment, tailored to individual needs.

  • Recovering from injury or surgery: regain strength, balance and motor control
  • Office desk setting: correct posture and stretch sore or tight areas
  • Sports enthusiast: fine-tune skills and help prevent injuries
  • Maintaining strength and balance as you age

Common questions

The same answers are in the assistant, so you can just ask.

Do I need a referral?

Not as a private patient. A current referral is needed if you are claiming under WorkCover or CTP.

What should I bring?

Any recent, relevant x-rays or reports. If you need to change clothing, the clinic has shorts, t-shirts and gowns you can borrow, or bring your own.

How often will I need to come?

It varies with the condition. The physio will give you an idea of the timeline at your initial visit.

What if I have WorkCover but missing paperwork?

Bring your doctor's referral, claim number, insurer and claims officer details. If the insurer has not accepted liability, or you don't have them, you are billed at the time of the consultation.
