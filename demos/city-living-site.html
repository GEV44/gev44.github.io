Tenant FAQ

Need to know before you rent

How do I apply for a property?

On a property listed on our website, use the Apply Now link to a PDF form, fill it in and send it to us. If you found it on a third-party site such as Domain or realestate.com.au, use the Apply Now link there, which goes through the Ignite platform.

What documents help an application?

Examples: three recent payslips, a bank statement or summary, council or strata levy (if you own a home), rental ledger (if you rent), passport, driver licence, Medicare card, accountant letter or tax return. The landlord wants to see you can pay the rent diligently and look after the property as your own.

How do I report maintenance or repairs?

Tell your Property Manager right away by phone and by email, with photos and as much detail as you can. For an after-hours emergency (no lights, no hot water, a major leak) you will be given emergency contact details, and you should follow up in writing.

Can I make changes, or keep a pet?

Ask your Property Manager first, who seeks the landlord’s permission. That includes holes in walls for pictures. Every property has its own pet policy, and in an apartment building the Strata Committee also has to approve, which can take time.

What if I need to break my lease?

Give your Property Manager as much notice as possible. The break-lease penalty depends on how far into the lease you are:

First quarter of the lease4 weeks rent
Second quarter3 weeks rent
Third quarter2 weeks rent
Fourth quarter1 week rent
When is rent due, and where do I pay?

Weekly, fortnightly or monthly, as agreed when the lease starts. The bank account details are on your Lease Agreement. Ask your Property Manager if you want a different payment method.
