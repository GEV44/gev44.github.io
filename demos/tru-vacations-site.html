FAQ

Questions Kristy hears most

Do you charge a fee?

Sometimes, not always. If you already know exactly what you want to book, a planning fee is typically not charged and she is generally compensated by the supplier. Trips needing more research or custom itineraries carry a non-refundable professional planning fee based on complexity. Destination weddings have a flat planning fee. Kristy confirms the exact amount for your trip.

Why use a travel advisor instead of booking directly with a cruise line?

Access to special group pricing and exclusive amenities, plus personalised recommendations and someone in your corner if anything goes wrong.

How far in advance should I book?

It depends. Cruises and guided tours often open up to two years ahead, which is usually when pricing and cabin selection are best. Airfare generally opens about 11 months out. Short-notice trips are possible too.

Do you book airfare?

Yes, as part of a vacation package she puts together. Standalone flights are not booked.

Do you recommend travel insurance?

Yes, strongly. She will walk you through your options.

What if something goes wrong while I am travelling?

Every client receives a contact number for in-destination support, often through one of the preferred suppliers used for the booking.
