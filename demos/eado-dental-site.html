FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you offer same-day appointments or take walk-ins?

Yes. We do same-day appointments, welcome walk-ins and can take dental emergencies. For the fastest help call (713) 474-2334.

Which insurance do you accept?

We accept most major insurances. Tell us your plan and the team can check your coverage and explain your treatment plan in plain terms.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We offer payment plans and easy financing through CareCredit.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We are a family practice for all ages, with family and pediatric dentistry and dental sealants.

Can I fill out new-patient forms before my visit?

Yes. New patients can submit the forms online before the visit, which keeps check-in quick.

Do you offer Invisalign?

Yes. Dr. Williams or Dr. Ma will create a custom Invisalign treatment plan for you.
