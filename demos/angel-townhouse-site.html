Welcome

A stylish retreat in the heart of Islington

Nestled on a peaceful residential street in the heart of Islington, Angel Townhouse offers a stylish and relaxing retreat just moments from some of North London's most vibrant restaurants, cafés, bars, theatres and cultural attractions.

Combining boutique character with modern convenience, the property provides a welcoming base for both leisure and business travellers seeking comfort, privacy and easy access to central London.

Each suite has been thoughtfully designed with spacious layouts, contemporary furnishings and elegant decorative touches. Selected suites feature luxurious spa baths or whirlpool tubs, the perfect way to recharge after a day exploring the city.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is until 11:00 AM. Please let us know your expected arrival time in advance.

Is Angel Townhouse adults-only?

Yes. Angel Townhouse is an adults-only property and the minimum age for check-in is 21.

Is Wi-Fi free?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.

Is there parking nearby?

Public parking is available at a location nearby. Reservation may be needed and charges may apply.

How close is the nearest station?

Essex Road station is just a 500 m walk, and Angel Underground station is about 1.4 km away.

Do you allow pets?

Pets are not allowed at the property.

How do I pay?

Payment before arrival by bank transfer is required. We will contact you after booking with instructions. Cash is not accepted.
