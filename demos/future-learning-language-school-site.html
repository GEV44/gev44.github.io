The school

Not a traditional language school

Future Learning (FL) specialises in teaching English through digital media projects, with a strong focus on collaboration along with communication skills. It has developed its own curriculum and describes itself as the first dedicated technology-enhanced language school.

Lessons are project-based and designed to encourage social interaction, with links to subjects such as science, history, geography, sociology, business, engineering and ICT. FL is an EAQUALS accredited school, with year-round adult campuses in Dublin and Athlone.

Where

Two year-round campuses

Dublin

Abbey House, Jervis Street

In the centre of Dublin, near the Jervis Shopping Centre and Henry Street. Newly refurbished, with 22 classrooms across four floors, student zones, a kitchen area and high speed wifi.

Athlone

Lyster House, Deerpark Road

Beside the River Shannon and close to the town centre, in a renovated building that was once part of a distillery and brewery complex. Visits are welcome if booked in advance.

Where you will live

Residence or host family

Dublin

FL Residence

For adults 18+: a single en-suite room in a five-bedroom shared apartment in a modern student residence, within walking distance of the school. A small number of rooms, so book early.

Athlone

Glen Abhainn Residence

A gated student complex of 96 apartments, 350 metres from TUS Athlone. About a 30-minute walk to school, or a regular local bus.

Both

Host families

Live in a real home and be looked after for your stay. Dublin homestay is best for students aged 15 and over, with journeys of about 30 to 65 minutes.

Questions

Quick answers

How many students are in a class?

A maximum of 16 on the Adult English course.

What if I do not know my level?

Courses run from A0 to C2. Tell the team when you enquire and they will advise.

Is the school accredited?

Yes, Future Learning is EAQUALS accredited.

Can I visit?

Prospective students and agencies are welcome at the Athlone campus, and booking in advance is recommended.

How do host family students get to school in Dublin?

By train, bus or Luas, usually 30 to 65 minutes depending on the time of day. On the first evening the host family shows the route. Homestay in Dublin is best for students aged 15 and over; younger students aged 12 to 14 can have extra commute support, arranged when booking.

Where do Mini-Stay groups sleep in Dublin?

At Clink Hostel, two minutes from the school, in 4 to 8 bed dorms separated by gender and shared only with their own group. Group leaders stay on the same floors, and students are in their rooms by 21:00.

Where do I find prices?

The website has a Prices page. The team confirms the figure for your course and length.
