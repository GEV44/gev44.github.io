Selling in Dublin

Ten steps from valuation to keys

Straight-talking advice before, during and after the sale. You will always hear the truth about prices, and the agent who values your home is the one who shows it.

Start with a valuation

  1. ValuationAsk any agent to explain how they reached their figure. A flattering number that cannot be delivered helps nobody.
  2. Choose your agentPick someone with the integrity to give straight-talking advice and keep you informed throughout.
  3. Instruct a solicitorHave the title deeds taken up from the bank early so there is no delay once a buyer appears. Extensions may need a Certificate of Compliance.
  4. Prepare the propertyKerb appeal counts. Many buyers drive by before they ever book a viewing.
  5. MarketingA professional photographer and floor plans, with the listing placed on both myhome.ie and daft.ie.
  6. ViewingsAlways accompanied by an experienced agent, not part-time viewers.
  7. OffersThrough an online bidding package you are notified instantly. Bidders upload proof of funds and ID, so there is a permanent record.
  8. Offer acceptedA booking deposit is paid and held subject to contract. The buyer arranges the survey and the lender's mortgage valuation.
  9. Exchange contractsThe solicitors agree the legal detail, the buyer signs first and pays the balance of the 10% deposit, then you sign.
  10. CompletionA few weeks later, on the date agreed between the solicitors, the sale closes and you hand over the keys.

Red Book standard

Valuations for every reason

As members of SCSI and RICS, every valuation follows the international Red Book framework.

Mortgage valuation

Commissioned by your lender, a short pro-forma report on value and any defects that affect the security. On the panels of most lenders.

Probate valuation

An independent valuation as at the date of death, for executors and solicitors applying for the grant of probate.

Legal and court

Expert, independent valuations where a property value is needed in a court of law.

HSE Fair Deal

For the Nursing Homes Support Scheme, to Red Book standard, considering condition and date of entry to the nursing home.

Capital gains tax

Historical valuations when purchase prices or deeds are lost, including as far back as 1974.

Marketing valuation

The likely sale or rental value if you are thinking of selling or letting. Not suitable for lending or the uses above.

Good to know

Quick answers

Where do you advertise properties?

All properties offered for sale on the open market are placed on both myhome.ie and daft.ie, with professional photography and floor plans.

Who attends viewings?

An experienced agent always accompanies viewers. Part-time or inexperienced viewers are not used.

How do offers work?

Through the online bidding package you are notified as soon as an offer arrives. Bidders upload proof of funding and identity, special conditions are stated in writing and the buyer's ability to proceed is checked before you decide.

Can I track my sale?

Yes. You can follow the sale online at any time, see appointments in real time and call or email with any question.

Can landlords see their income online?

Managed landlords get a 24/7/365 login to review income and expenditure details, and the team oversees non-resident landlord withholding tax.

What if my property has an extension?

You may need a Certificate of Compliance. The office can put you in contact with a suitably qualified building surveyor or architect to prepare it.

Do you only work with houses?

No. The team deals with a huge range of properties across Dublin: city-centre apartments, family homes, new developments and commercial property or business premises further afield.

How are tenants chosen?

Applications are thoroughly vetted with comprehensive reference checks, and tenants are communicated with regularly. Regular inspections keep the property in good order and vacancies are kept short with the latest letting technology.

Are you regulated?
