About us

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Patient forms are on the website so you can fill them in before your visit, and please bring your insurance information.

Do you offer direct billing?

Yes, direct billing is offered to most private insurers and the team can help estimate your benefits. It depends on your specific plan, so tell the team your insurer and they will confirm.

Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP)?

Yes. CDCP uses its own fees, so you pay the balance after its payment, which may include a co-payment.

How can I pay for treatment?

Cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and debit are accepted. For treatments where your portion is over $500, interest-free payment plans are available with no application fee.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Same-day emergency care is offered. Please call 604-681-6699 as early in the day as possible so your booking can be fast-tracked; after hours, leave a voicemail or follow the prompts to reach Dr. Bae.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes, oral sedation is available for patients with dental anxiety, and Dr. Bae is known for his calm, kind demeanor.
