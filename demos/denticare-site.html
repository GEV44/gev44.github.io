What we do

The treatments listed on the clinic's own pages. The dentist decides what is right for you after an examination.

Check-ups and hygiene

Dental exam, scale and polish, deep cleaning. We recommend a routine check-up every 6 months to catch problems early.

Fillings and root canal

Tooth-coloured composite fillings, inlays and onlays, and root canal treatment to save a natural tooth.

Composite bonding

Repairs chips and closes small gaps, usually in one visit and with little or no drilling of healthy tooth.

Whitening

Laser whitening in the clinic or a home kit. Results typically last 1 to 3 years depending on coffee, tea, red wine and smoking.

Crowns, bridges, veneers

Zirconium, PFM and e-max crowns, plus bridges and veneers for a complete smile makeover.

Implants and dentures

Single implants under local anaesthetic, All-on-4 and All-on-6, full and partial dentures and fast denture repair.

Extractions and wisdom teeth

Routine and surgical extractions, including impacted wisdom teeth.

Braces and orthodontics

Orthodontic care is offered. Ask at your consultation how it would work for you.

Nervous patients

Extra time, every step explained, and no judgment about how long it has been.

Dental emergency in North Dublin

Delaying treatment for dental pain can lead to serious infection, so please do not wait it out. Call 087 415 4835, ideally early in the morning.

Severe toothache

Sharp, throbbing or constant pain that keeps you awake. We focus on getting you out of pain first.

Swelling or abscess

Swollen gums, jaw or face can mean infection and needs urgent assessment by a dentist.

Knocked-out tooth

Keep it moist in a glass of milk or tucked inside your cheek, and reach us within 60 minutes if you can.

Broken or chipped tooth

From a fall, sport or an accident. Repair can include composite bonding to restore shape and strength.

Lost filling or crown

An exposed tooth can become very sensitive and risk further damage, so we reseal and protect it quickly.

Quick answers


Do you accept PRSI?

Yes. Eligible patients get a free annual exam and a EUR 15 scale and polish. Give reception your PPS number and date of birth and they check eligibility.

Do you take the Medical Card?

No, DentiCare is fully private.

Can I get a same-day emergency appointment?

We always try, depending on daily capacity. Call 087 415 4835 early. There is normally no extra emergency fee.

Is there parking?

Yes, a dedicated lot opposite the clinic, plus on-street parking nearby.

Do I need X-rays?

In most cases, yes. They show hidden decay, infections and broken roots. A small X-ray is EUR 35 and a panoramic one EUR 70, and we only take them with your consent.

Does composite bonding damage my teeth?

No. The resin is applied to the tooth surface, so in most cases there is no drilling or shaving of healthy tooth.

Is the implant procedure painful?

It is done under profound local anaesthetic. Most patients report minor pressure only, and aftercare discomfort is usually managed with standard painkillers.

Can I get a treatment plan without coming in?

No. The dentist has to examine your mouth in person to give an accurate plan and quote.

Do you offer payment plans and tax relief?

Yes. Spread the cost with Humm, and claim 20% tax relief on qualifying treatments through a Med 2 form that we complete for you.

Do you treat children?

Yes, gentle paediatric check-ups are welcome.
