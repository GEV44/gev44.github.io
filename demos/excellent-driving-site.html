About us

Australian owned and run

Excellent Driving School was established by Con Alexandropoulos, who is also the principal instructor with decades of experience. The business has grown steadily into one of Melbourne's leading driving schools.

The team is patient and well qualified. They teach every student to their own level, covering all road rules and the car-control skills that become good habits for life. The fleet is modern, with dual controls for braking and handling, so students feel safe and in control from the first hour.

Questions

Good to know

Do you pick me up?

Lessons come to a place that is convenient for you. Tell Con your suburb when you book.

What cars do you use?

Modern cars with dual controls for braking and handling. Ask whether an automatic or manual car is available for your lessons.

How long is a lesson?

There are 60-minute and 45-minute lessons, sold singly or in packages of 3, 5 and 10.

Can you help with my licence test?

Yes. The licence-test option includes a one-hour lesson with the test, and every lesson plan covers the area where you will be tested by VicRoads.

I have an overseas licence. Can you help?

Yes, the school helps drivers transitioning from an overseas licence as well as first-time learners.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Sunday is not listed.

What if I fail my test?

Under the guarantee conditions, a re-sit means you pay the VicRoads booking fee. Ask your instructor for the details.

I am from overseas. Is that a problem?

The school says it caters for every personality and background, from school students to people arriving from overseas. Ask Con if you need something specific.

How many lessons will I need?

It depends on you. Instructors teach each student as fast as they can pick up the skill. Con can advise when you book.

Can I get a callback instead of phoning?

Yes. Use the assistant or the contact form on the school's site, leave your name and number, and Con will ring you back.

Do I need to book far ahead?

The site does not give a booking lead time. Lessons run Monday to Saturday between 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM, so call Con or leave your details and he will find a slot.

Where will the test be held?

Your lessons include the area where VicRoads will test you, so the roads feel familiar. Con can tell you more about your test location.
