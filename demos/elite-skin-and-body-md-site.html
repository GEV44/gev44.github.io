Answers before your first visit


How do I book?

Book online any time through Mindbody, or call (281) 251-2225 and the front desk will find a time. Walk-ins are welcome, but availability cannot be guaranteed.

Can I choose who does my service?

Yes. Request a specific esthetician or therapist when booking. If you have no preference, the team matches you with the person best suited to your treatment and skin goals.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please let the team know 24 hours in advance so they can adjust their schedule and help you avoid fees.

Do you offer flexible payment?

Yes. Financing is available through CareCredit and Cherry, and memberships start at $60 a month with $0 enrollment.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, online and in-store.
