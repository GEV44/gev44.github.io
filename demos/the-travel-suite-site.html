FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need an appointment?

You can book an appointment through the site or the assistant. The team will confirm the time.

Can you plan a honeymoon?

Yes. Honeymoons are a speciality, with Maldives packages and a Luxury Travel and Honeymoon brochure for Autumn 2026.

Which regions do you cover?

The Indian Ocean, the Caribbean, the USA, Europe and river cruising, plus Business Class fares.

Are you licensed?

Yes, licensed by the Irish Aviation Authority, TA0443.

Is there anything for people travelling alone?

Explora Journeys has solo Mediterranean sailings in the packages. Ask the team about solo options for your dates.

Do you offer Business Class flights?

Yes, the agency has access to exclusive Business Class fares alongside its holidays and cruises.

Can I visit the office without an appointment?

The office welcomes visitors during opening hours. An appointment helps the team set aside time for you, especially on a Thursday evening.

Where do I find the latest offers?

The website lists suite picks of the week, cruise offers and resort packages. Ask the team to match one to your dates.

How do I get a price?

Prices depend on dates and the room or suite. The team prepares a tailored proposal after you share your plans.
