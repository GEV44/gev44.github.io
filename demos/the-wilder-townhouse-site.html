The house

Tucked away on Adelaide Road, just moments from the vibrant streets of Dublin city centre.

The Wilder Townhouse sits on a quiet residential street a short stroll from St. Stephen's Green, with the cafes, shops and cultural life of the capital on the doorstep.

It suits a weekend getaway, a city shopping break or a discreet, homely base for business. With only 42 rooms, mornings begin in the light-filled Garden Room and the secluded garden terrace offers a rare pause in the city.

In 2025 it kept its Michelin Key for the second year running, and it is the one Irish property listed with Mr & Mrs Smith, Small Luxury Hotels of the World and Ireland's Blue Book.

A day at the townhouse

From soda bread to the snug

  1. Morning

    Garden Room breakfast

    A buffet-style table of fruit, yoghurt, granola, pastries and house-baked breads, including the Guinness soda bread, plus cooked dishes to order: Full Irish, Eggs Benedict or brioche French toast.

  2. Afternoon

    Light bites

    Cheese and charcuterie boards, soup of the day, fresh salads and toasted sourdough, served in the Garden Room for the in-between moments.

  3. Evening

    Gin & Tea Rooms

    A darker, candlelit snug with leather seating. Every guest receives a complimentary G&T here on arrival. Dinner is a short walk to one of Dublin's restaurants.

  4. Night

    Turndown and poetry

    A chocolate treat and a booklet of 42 poems by Poet in Residence Paul Yates.

Ingredients come from Irish suppliers such as Quigley's Family Butchers, Keelings, La Rousse Foods and Atlantis of Kilmore Quay.

Quick answers

What guests ask first

Is there a restaurant?

Breakfast and light bites are served in the Garden Room, and drinks in the Gin & Tea Rooms. For dinner guests head to nearby Dublin restaurants.

How many people sleep in a room?

Every room type lists a maximum of 2 guests.

Can I add a late check-out?

Check-out is at 12:00. For early check-in or late check-out, call +353 1 969 6598 and the team will let you know.

Is there a lift or step-free access?

That is not stated on the website, so the assistant passes it to the team rather than guessing: stay@thewilder.ie.

Can I bring a car?

There is a limited car park on a first come first served basis, and on-street parking around the house. Complimentary parking is part of booking direct, subject to availability.

Is breakfast served in the room?

Breakfast is served in the Garden Room, overlooking the garden terrace. Serving times are confirmed by the team.

Do I need to bring a card?

Yes, an in-person credit card is needed at check-in.

Do I get anything on arrival?

Yes, a complimentary G&T in the Gin & Tea Rooms.

I have an Ireland's Blue Book voucher.

Mention it when booking and you receive a complimentary room upgrade.
