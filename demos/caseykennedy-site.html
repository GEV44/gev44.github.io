Sensitive sales

Probate and Fair Deal.

Selling the family home as an executor is described on the site as an emotive process. CKP explains how Irish probate works, including grants of probate and of letters of administration, and provides probate valuations.

For the Fair Deal nursing home support scheme, a written valuation of the home is part of the HSE's financial assessment, and CKP can organise one.

Good to know

CKP states openly that clients may be referred to third-party providers such as Moneysherpa for mortgage advice, that CKP may earn a commission on those referrals, and that you are under no obligation to use them.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is the valuation really free?

Yes. CKP says it provides free valuations with no obligation to use their services afterwards.

What is a Blue Book valuation?

A valuation to European TEGoVA standards, used for purchases and lending or refinancing. CKP are TEGoVA Residential Valuers.

I am an executor. Can CKP help?

Yes, there is a dedicated page for probate and selling a property as executor, and CKP also performs probate valuations.

Do you do Fair Deal valuations?

Yes. A written valuation is needed for the Fair Deal scheme, and CKP can arrange one.

What does OMC stand for?

Owners' management company. CKP has an OMC department managing common areas and finances of developments.

Where can I read more about buying and selling in Ireland?

The FAQ on the CKP site covers around sixty topics, including BER, Local Property Tax, stamp duty, mortgages, solicitors and booking deposits.

How long does it take to sell?

CKP quotes an average of 29 days on the market for its sales. Timing from sale agreed to closing is covered in the FAQ on ckp.ie.

Do you handle commercial property?

Yes. CKP lists commercial sales, commercial rentals and short-term office rentals, and has pages on commercial investments, from converting use to financing a purchase.

Can I buy property overseas through CKP?

CKP lists overseas properties in Cyprus, Portugal and France on its site.

Who manages the common areas of an apartment block?

That is handled by the owners' management company, often with a managing agent. CKP's FAQ explains who is responsible for common areas and for damage in multi-unit developments in Ireland.

Do I need a solicitor, and when?

CKP has an FAQ on how to choose a solicitor when buying or selling, and on conveyancing, legal searches and Land Registry fees.

Is the office open outside working hours?

The site lists 9:00 to 17:00. Anything outside that, the team will confirm, and for a property issue use the Report Property Issue button.

Which areas does CKP cover?

The property search lists Stillorgan, Blackrock, Dundrum, Sandyford, Foxrock, Dun Laoghaire, Bray and more, with agents based in the City Centre, on the Northside and on the Southside.

Can I register for new listings?

Yes, there is a property alerts sign-up on the site.

What do you charge?

Fees are not published on the site. The team will confirm them in a consultation.
