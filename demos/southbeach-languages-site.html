How to join

Three routes for F-1 students, and a simple one for tourists.

From your countryApply to the school, receive the I-20 form, then book your US embassy interview. Apply for the I-20 early; you can enter the US no earlier than 30 days before the start date.
As an F-1 transferAlready studying on an F-1 in the US? Apply as a transfer student. Transfer students are welcome.
Change of statusAlready in the US and want to become an F-1 student? Use the change-of-status route and ask the school official about the steps.

Tourists on English Vacation just choose a start month and a campus. No registration fee.

Good to know

How do I know my level?

A placement test is given on the first day of classes. A free 25-question sample test on the website is just for fun.

Do I qualify for a student visa?

Consulates decide. Applicants must show they live abroad with no intention of abandoning that residence, intend to leave the US after the course, and have sufficient funds.

When should I apply?

Embassies can issue an F-1 visa up to 1 year before the start date on the I-20. Wait for the I-20 before booking the interview.

Can my spouse or family come?

Qualifying spouses and dependents may join as F-2 holders. Please contact the school official.

Do I need health insurance?

Students are responsible for their own health insurance.

How do I pay?

In advance (for a discount) or monthly.

Which languages is the website in?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, German, Russian and Japanese. Classes are taught in English only.

Does the school arrange housing?

No. The school does not provide or refer student housing. Hostels, hotels, home-stays and long-term apartments are the usual choices.

What are the office hours?

Both offices are open Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday, 8am to 2:30pm.

Who are the teachers?

Experienced, certified American teachers. The school teaches American English and focuses on conversational, practical English for real-life situations.

Can I join in the middle of the month?

New students can join English Intensive classes on Mondays. Please confirm your start date with the school.

What age can students be?

The school welcomes adult international students, 16 years and older, from Europe, Latin America, Asia and all parts of the world.

Is there a registration fee?

There is no registration fee for tourists or locals on English Vacation. For other fees, the team will confirm.

Can F-1 students take a vacation?

Yes, 12 weeks of vacation annually.
