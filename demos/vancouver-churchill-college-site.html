Languages and communication

English in six levels, with a placement test first

The ESL pathway runs from Elementary to IELTS 6+ preparation. Each level is 120 hours, about 12 weeks, and the full route is up to 36 weeks depending on where you start.

ESL levels

Elementary, Pre-Intermediate, Intermediate, Upper Intermediate, IELTS 5–6 Prep and IELTS 6+ Prep.

120 h per level~12 weeks each

Healthcare English

Medical terminology and communication for non-native speakers who work, or plan to work, in health care.

French as a Second Language

FSL B1–B2 for intermediate to upper-intermediate learners: complex grammar, fluency and spontaneous conversation.

Not sure of your level?

A placement test decides where you start. Ask the team to arrange it.

How to start

Four steps from question to first class

Requirements differ by program. This is the general route from the college's FAQ.

AskCall, write or use the contact form. Mature students (19+) without Grade 12 are welcome to ask.
Check entryMost programs: Grade 12 or mature student status, plus English at Grade 10 level (IELTS 5.0–5.5).
ApplyDiploma programs have an application fee; certificate programs are confirmed by admissions.
Choose deliveryIn-class, distance or blended, depending on the program.
