Services

What the clinic does

Tap a treatment to see how the clinic describes it. Prices are not published, so the team confirms costs when you ask.

General dentistry

Fillings, ceramic restorations, partial and full dentures and extractions, plus complete exams, X-rays and cleanings, fluoride treatment, oral cancer screening and gingivitis treatment.

Cosmetic dentistry

For unequal, discoloured or crooked teeth, to create a more harmonious smile.

Veneers

Thin porcelain shells, or hand-layered composite, bonded to the tooth for chips, gaps, staining or poorly shaped teeth. In some cases the tooth does not need to be filed down.

Teeth whitening

Take-home whitening with custom trays (90 minutes or overnight, as advised) or in-chair whitening. Your shade is recorded first and extra visits can be added until you reach the shade you want.

Dental implant restoration

Titanium implants placed in the jawbone to support a crown, bridge or denture, usually in two stages. The dentist checks whether you are suitable first.

Crowns and bridges

A fixed, reliable solution for damaged or missing teeth.

Root canal treatment

Saves a tooth when the nerve is inflamed, injured or infected. Local anaesthetic and a dental dam are used, over one to three appointments.

Tooth extraction

For a chipped, broken or badly decayed tooth that cannot be kept.

Emergency dental care

If something hurts or breaks, call the team on (03) 9654 4455 and describe what is happening. The assistant cannot assess symptoms.

Children's dental

Care for infants and children in a comfortable setting, with advice on teething, brushing and habits such as thumb-sucking.

Not sure which treatment you need?

Start with a check-up. The dentist examines your teeth, explains your options and agrees a plan with you before anything begins.

For families

Little patients welcome

Following Australian Dental Association guidance, the clinic suggests a first visit around the first tooth or at 12 months, and a checkup by age two.

Good to know

Quick answers

How fast will I hear back?

The clinic states that it confirms booked appointments within 2 hours.

Is teeth whitening safe?

The clinic says whitening done by its dentists is safe: rubber shields and protective goggles protect your gums during in-chair whitening.

How often should I have a check-up?

The Australian Dental Association recommends visiting about twice a year for a cleaning, an examination and a consultation. Regular visits help catch problems before they become severe.

Does a root canal hurt?

The clinic describes root canal treatment as almost painless, performed with local anaesthetic. Everyone is different, so the dentist will talk it through with you.

Do you publish prices?

No. The dentist presents all the options before recommending one, and the team can confirm costs on (03) 9654 4455.
