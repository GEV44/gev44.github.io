About us

Physical Therapists NYC is a thriving outpatient physical therapy practice serving downtown Manhattan, Union Square and Brooklyn. As a hands-on facility focused on manual therapy and personalized care, its therapists offer natural treatments without drugs or surgery, supported by a full gym and rehab center.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Which insurance do you accept?

Most plans, including commercial, no-fault and workers' compensation. Medicaid, HMO Medicaid and Medicare are not accepted.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, programs such as CareCredit and the Wells Fargo advantage credit card program.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give 48 hours' notice if you can't keep your appointment.

Do you have weekend hours?

The Brooklyn office is open Sundays from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

What conditions do you treat?

Back, neck, shoulder, knee, hip and other joint pain, sciatica, sports injuries, headaches, arthritis, post-operative rehab and more.
