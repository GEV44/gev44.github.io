About us

ReSync is a team of Chartered Physiotherapists, Athletic therapists, Strength and Conditioning coaches and Sports Scientists. They combine physiotherapy, sports physiotherapy, manual therapy and strength and conditioning to ease pain first and then resolve the injury.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need a referral?

No, you do not need a referral to see a therapist.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes, we are accredited by all major health insurance companies.

Is there free parking?

Yes, a large free car park right outside, with a 2 hour limit.

What should I wear?

Shorts and a sports top (men), shorts and a string top (women).

Can I come if I have never done strength training?

Yes, we take it slow.
