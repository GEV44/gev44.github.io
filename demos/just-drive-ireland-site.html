Questions

Before you book.

What is EDT?

Essential Driver Training. It is a 12-module course for new drivers, and every EDT pack here includes the logbook.

Do I get an automatic or a manual licence?

Learning in an automatic gives an automatic licence, so afterwards you can only drive automatic cars, including rentals.

Is pick-up included?

Yes, pick-up and drop-off are part of the lessons listed.

Can I hire the car for my test?

Yes, the pre-test lesson packages include car hire.

Can the lessons fit around my work or school?

Lessons can be customised around your schedule. Hours run Monday to Friday 7:00 to 22:00 and Saturday 6:00 to 16:00. Your instructor agrees exact times with you.

Where will my instructor pick me up?

From your chosen location in the areas covered, and they drop you off after the lesson at no extra charge.

I passed years ago but have not driven since. Can I take lessons?

Yes. Refresher lessons are tailored to your needs and can include dual roads, motorways, congested areas and parking.

Which test centres do you know?

The school specialises in the Tallaght and Dun Laoghaire test centres, and the instructors are acquainted with the Dublin test routes.

What is the cancellation rule?

Give 48 hours notice. Inside 48 hours the lesson is charged in full.

Why automatic

What learners gain.

  • Accelerated full licence
  • No clutch or gear challenges
  • Suits people with physical disabilities
  • Suits older learners
  • More attention for hazards, passing, positioning, crossing and manoeuvring
  • Fewer lessons needed
  • Less petrol in heavy traffic
  • Cheaper to maintain

Remember: an automatic licence means automatic cars only, including cars you buy or rent later.

Test day

Six habits your instructor works on.

Relax and concentrate

Forcing yourself raises anxiety. Routines help only if you use them properly, and your instructor shows ways to keep calm.

Natural observation

A smooth flow, not too many forced mirror checks. Practise it in the pre-test lessons.

Scan effectively

Weak observation means less information and slower reactions. Ask your instructor to guide your scanning.

React safely

Allow yourself time to react to what you see around you.

Know your car

Own car or the instructor's car on test day, learn what it can and cannot do, and ask for the technology to be explained.

Have a coping strategy

Nerves are natural. Agree a plan with your instructor, such as pre-test mindfulness, to get back on track fast.
