Safe and responsible travel, before, during and after

Health and safety are a top priority. Partners confirm they follow the guidelines of their local health authority, and also abide by World Health Organization and World Travel and Tourism Council standards.

Suppliers meet or exceed provincial standards for sanitation, with guest rooms thoroughly cleaned and high-touch points such as door knobs and light switches disinfected.

Good to know

Can I get insurance through Fresh Tracks Canada?

Yes. Insurance is highly recommended as the trips are non-refundable. Packages include all-inclusive and non-medical options. Ask your advisor.

What is an insurance quote based on?

The packaged trip price per person, each traveller's birth date and the duration of the vacation.

Can I change a sample itinerary?

Yes. Each can be personalized with service levels, choice of accommodation, extra nights, exclusive tours and activity level.

What do you ask of your travel partners?

Partners follow their local health authority guidelines and World Health Organization and World Travel and Tourism Council standards.

Can I combine two kinds of trip?

Yes. Sample itineraries are starting points, so an Insider can add extra nights, exclusive tours or a second region.

Which season are train trips available?

The train itineraries listed run April to October, with some ending in September.

When can I see polar bears?

The classic Churchill trips run October to November, and a summer belugas and bears trip runs July to August.

How do I get a price?

Prices depend on the itinerary, season, service level and travellers, so an Insider will prepare a personalized quote.
