Questions

Do you offer free valuations?

Yes, a free, no-obligation letting valuation. Ian or Robbie visits personally. Sales valuations are also part of their service.

What hours is the office open?

9.30am to 5.30pm, with longer hours used for viewings.

Can I bid online?

Yes, many for-sale listings allow you to make an offer or bid online.

Who handles repairs on a managed property?

A 24/7 maintenance service with qualified, insured and certified tradesmen.

Do you only cover Dublin 8?

Dublin 8 is the specialist postcode, but listings and recent sales span Dublin, from Malahide to Stillorgan and Rathfarnham.

Can I rent out my home if I am a first-time landlord?

Yes. First-time landlords get extra time at the first meeting and a full walk-through of obligations.

Do you do inspections of rented homes?

Yes, at move-in and every six months, with a report and photos emailed to the landlord.

What does it cost?

They mention a competitive commission structure, but no figures are published. Ask during the free consultation.
