Good to know

From the salon's own policies.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give 24-hour notice. Less than 24 hours is charged 50% of the reserved appointment, and a no-show is charged 100% of the service. The salon understands emergencies and will do its best to work with you.

Do you confirm appointments?

Yes. The salon texts and e-mails to confirm the date and time 48 hours before.

Why do prices differ between stylists?

Levels reflect the hours of education and the demand of the stylist, from Level 1 up to the owner.

Are you hiring?

The salon has a careers page for stylists, coordinators and assistants, and mentioned tryouts for new stylists in January and February.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, gift cards are available through the salon's booking page.
