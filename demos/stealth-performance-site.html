A small group session

The group follows one program, but your training details are individual. Before class, coaches prepare your weights, reps and exercise variations.

1 · Arrive with a plan

Your coach knows you are booked in. Your assigned weights and current training details are displayed on the screens, so there is no guessing which weight to pick or which version of an exercise to do.

2 · Warm up together

Work through the warm-up with your group, with adjustments where you need them.

3 · Train at your level

Follow the group program with your assigned loads and movements while a coach watches technique.

4 · Record and progress

Your weights, sets and reps are tracked so your coach can plan the next increase in load or volume through the cycle.

Semi-private and one-on-one

For people who want a program that is only theirs.

Semi-private

Typically 1 to 3 members per coach, never more than 4. Your exercises, loads, sets and reps are planned around your goals, experience and movement needs, and your coach has your previous results at hand all session.

A lot of adult members land here long term: people in their 40s, 50s and 60s, people working around an old injury, and anyone who wants a custom plan without paying for one-on-one every session.

One-on-one

Working around pain, coming off physical therapy, aiming for a wedding, a season or a physical test, brand new to lifting, or on a tough schedule. A coach watching only you can adjust in real time.

Start with a free assessment: goals, history, injuries and a look at how you move. Then a weekly schedule that fits your life.

“Build you up, not beat you down” is how the site puts it: steady progress over random hard workouts.

Questions

I'm out of shape. Can I keep up?

Yes. Every movement is scaled to your level, and your coach adjusts weights and exercises for you.

What if I have an old injury?

Tell the coach at your first session. Exercises are modified so you can keep training without flare-ups.

How many people are in a semi-private session?

Usually 1 to 3, never more than 4. Each person follows their own program.

Is there a trial?

New clients can try 4 weeks for $97. After that, pricing depends on how many days a week you train.

Can I switch group times?

Yes, once you're placed you can move to another group time.
