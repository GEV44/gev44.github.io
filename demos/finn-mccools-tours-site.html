Questions

Is lunch included?

No. The tours have no lunch stops, so bring a packed lunch or pick something up at a stop on the way to the main attraction.

Can I bring luggage?

Yes, there is a storage compartment underneath the coach or at the back of the minicoach. Tell the guide if you are not returning to the starting city.

Is there a discount for groups or booking more than one tour?

Not at the moment. Big groups need to book a private tour; email the team with the date and group size.

I have limited mobility. Can I join?

The Cliffs of Moher, Glendalough and Titanic tours involve less walking and are more relaxed.

I booked the wrong date. What do I do?

Subject to availability and the cancellation policy, the date or tour can be changed. Email the team with your request so they can amend the booking.

I left something on the bus.

Send the tour date, tour name, seat location and a description. If it is found you can collect it from the pick-up point the next morning.

Is there WiFi on board?

Some coaches have it, but it is not advertised or guaranteed because the vehicle depends on the day.

Can I choose my seat?

Seats are first come, first served. Special needs can be noted on your booking, and people who get motion sickness often prefer the middle of the bus.

Where can I pay in cash?

The head office is not open to the public. Visit the official tourist offices: Things to do in Dublin (12 Aston Quay, Dublin 2) or the Belfast Welcome Centre (9 Donegall Square N).
