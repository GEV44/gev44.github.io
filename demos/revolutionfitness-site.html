The 3 Pillars Method

Fitness for busy people with busy lives

If you have 45 minutes 2-3 times a week and know how to turn on an oven, you can follow the plan.

Pillar 1

Exercise

With a trainer 3 days a week. Live heart-rate tracking with MyZone shows your zone and effort, so you work hard enough without blowing up, and you see your own progress.

Pillar 2

Nutrition

A custom meal plan built around your body, schedule and tastes, adjusted weekly. Prefer not to cook? Approved meal prep partner Parallel Meals can do it for you.

Pillar 3

Accountability

A personal accountability coach you can contact any time, with weekly check-ins and a monthly review of your targets and progress.

Questions

Frequently asked

Will I be fit enough to start?

Absolutely. Whatever your fitness and strength level, your workouts are designed and tailored to match.

I have a bad back, knee, hip or shoulder. Can you help?

The site says Revolution Fitness has a reputation as a gym of last resort for people dealing with injuries, from minor sprains to spinal fusion surgery, as well as members who are post stroke or have MS.

How long are sessions and how often can I train?

About 45 minutes, 3 times a week. You can train up to 4 times a week.

Will you tell me what to eat?

Yes. You are shown what to eat, how to cook and how to shop, or you can order the week's meals from Parallel Meals.

Can I pay by the class, or just do my own thing?

No. The coaching, accountability and group make the results, so there are no drop-ins. If you want to do your own thing, the team will recommend somewhere local.

Will I get too big?

You will not turn into a mass-monster unless that is what you want. It takes a lot of effort at the dinner table and a lot of heavy lifting.
