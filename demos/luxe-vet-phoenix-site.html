Services

Comprehensive care, personally delivered

A range of services designed around each pet. The veterinarians decide which suit your own pet after an exam.

Personalized wellness care

Individual wellness plans for every stage of life. A wellness exam covers weight, coat, heart, lungs, eyes, ears and dental health, plus any lab work needed.

Vaccines and titer testing

Customized vaccination schedules and titer testing, so your pet receives only the immunizations it needs.

Gut biome and genetic testing

Insight into digestive health and genetic predispositions, for more precise treatment and prevention.

In-house lab work

An on-site laboratory for rapid, accurate diagnostics and immediate results. Reference laboratory testing covers specialized tests.

In-house radiology

X-rays that let the team assess your pet's internal health quickly. Digital dental X-rays are part of the dental program.

Ultrasound

Non-invasive, high-resolution imaging, with findings reviewed with you during the same visit, including abdominal ultrasound.

Pet dentistry

Cleanings under anesthesia, with scaling, polishing and an oral health assessment, plus oral checks at each wellness visit.

Dermatology

Care for pet skin, coat, nails and ears, from allergies to more complex skin conditions.

Surgery

General surgery, emergency surgery and board-certified surgical services. Estimates are provided for all surgeries and hospitalizations.

Board-certified cardiology

Cardiology services led by board-certified specialists.

Walk-in urgent and emergent care

Same-day care from minor concerns to life-threatening emergencies, with no appointment needed for emergency visits.

In-home complete care

Physical exams, vaccinations, diagnostic blood testing and compassionate euthanasia, in the comfort of home.

Palliative and end-of-life care

Gentle, dignified care in a peaceful setting, with pre-euthanasia counseling and aftercare guidance.

The doctors

Two veterinarians, one clinic

Co-founder

Dr. Jamie Lenberg

Originally from Albuquerque, New Mexico. A Colorado State University graduate who worked as an emergency veterinary technician and took part in spay and neuter programs in underserved rural areas. Her research on canine parvovirus was published in the Journal of the American Animal Hospital Association.

After an internship in small animal internal medicine and surgery at VCA West Los Angeles Animal Hospital and nearly 11 years as an associate, she opened her own clinic. Special interests: nutrition, internal medicine, neurology and emergency medicine.

Co-founder

Dr. Cindy Barnes

A fellow CSU alum and a trailblazer in the field: founder of Northern Arizona's first 24/7 emergency animal hospital and, in 2016, of Shepherd Veterinary Software, a cloud-based practice management solution.

She spent 15 years as a veterinary nurse and has volunteered in underserved communities.

Urgent care

If your pet needs help now

Call (480) 542-2882. The practice asks that urgent matters be handled by phone, not by the website form.

During business hours the clinic is equipped for emergency and critical care, with walk-in visits and no appointment needed.

After hours, LUXE.VET recommends its trusted partner emergency clinic and can request the records to follow up.

Pet dentistry

More than fresh breath

The practice explains that oral disease can affect a pet's heart, kidneys and overall quality of life, and that pets tend to hide dental pain. Oral health is assessed at every wellness visit, and findings go into the pet's health record so changes can be tracked over time.

Services include cleanings under anesthesia, digital dental X-rays to see what is invisible during a visual check, and walk-in care for dental emergencies during business hours.

Pet surgery

Planned around your pet

Every surgical candidate receives a pre-operative evaluation, with Dr. Lenberg or Dr. Barnes examining the pet personally. The surgical plan takes account of age, existing health conditions and the home environment.

Surgical services range from planned procedures such as spays and neuters, to orthopedic and general surgery, to emergency surgery. Telemedicine and tele-triage connect you with a veterinarian by call or text when you are unsure what to do.

Quick answers

Do I need a membership?

No. Non-members are welcome. Membership adds priority scheduling and extra services.

Do you see exotic pets?

Some doctors see pocket pets such as guinea pigs, gerbils, rats and rabbits, but not birds or reptiles. Please call ahead to confirm.

Can I drop my pet off?

Drop-off appointments are a membership benefit: bring your pet in the morning and the team updates you when it is ready for pickup.

What does a dental cleaning include?

Scaling, polishing and an oral health assessment, performed under anesthesia for comfort and safety.

How do I schedule?

By calling the office or booking online. Members get priority scheduling, and the practice aims for same or next-day availability for urgent care.

What is telemedicine?

A phone or video consultation with the veterinarians for non-emergency questions, from home.

Do you accept pet insurance?

The team prepares the paperwork, invoices and records for your claim. You pay up front and file for reimbursement with your insurer.

Is the practice owner-run?

Yes: LUXE.VET describes itself as a private, women-owned veterinary practice, focused on patient care and exceptional client service.

Do you offer in-home visits?

Yes: in-home complete care covers physical exams, vaccinations, diagnostic blood testing and compassionate euthanasia. In-home visits are not included in the unlimited exams of the membership.

Which areas do you serve?

The clinic is on E Cactus Rd in Phoenix and serves nearby cities including Paradise Valley, North Scottsdale, Scottsdale and Tempe.

Can the assistant tell me what is wrong with my pet?

No. It can't diagnose or give medical advice. Health questions go to the veterinarians: 480-542-2882.
