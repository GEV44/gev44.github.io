Urgent care

Dental emergencies do not wait

The team reserves time each day for emergency care and does its best to see you the same day. Call the nearest office right away.

Severe pain

Severe toothache, dental abscess, acute infection, emergency root canal therapy.

Injury and trauma

Knocked-out or fractured teeth, loose teeth, soft tissue injuries, sports injuries.

Urgent repairs

Lost fillings, broken crowns, damaged dentures, emergency extractions, temporary restorations.

Facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding or a knocked-out adult tooth need care right away. Walk-ins for emergencies are welcome at the Glendale office during business hours.

Good to know

Questions new patients ask

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. All three Phoenix metro offices welcome new patients of every age. You can book online or call the office that is most convenient.

What should I expect at my first visit?

A comprehensive exam, digital imaging as needed, a gentle cleaning, and time to talk through your concerns and goals. No pressure and no surprise treatment plans.

Do you see children?

Yes, patients of every age, from a first visit onward.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call the nearest office right away. The team reserves time each day for emergency care and does its best to see you the same day. Walk-ins for emergencies are welcome at the Glendale office during business hours.

Do you accept insurance?

Most major PPO dental insurance plans. Call the office and the team will verify your benefits before your visit.

What if I do not have insurance?

There is transparent self-pay pricing and flexible payment options. Ask about membership and financing.

Will I know the cost before treatment?

Yes. You get a clear treatment plan and cost estimate before anything is scheduled.

Ready to visit?

Pick the office that works best. New patients are always welcome.
