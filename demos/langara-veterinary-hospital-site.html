Services

From routine wellness visits to ongoing medical care, supporting your pet's health at every stage of life.

1

Consultation

Behavioural and nutritional consultation for puppies and kittens, and wellness examinations for senior pets.

2

Surgery

Elective surgeries including spaying and neutering, and other procedures such as brachycephalic breed nasal resection, with a focus on safety and post-operative comfort.

3

Dental

Essential dental procedures including cleanings, exams and extractions, using advanced techniques to keep your pet's mouth healthy.

4

Grooming

Nail trims, lion-cut grooming, ear cleaning, teeth trimming and anal gland expression.

5

Traditional Chinese Veterinary Medicine

TCVM includes acupuncture, herbal medicine and food therapy. It is often used to support chronic conditions, improve mobility and comfort, enhance overall wellness and complement conventional treatments.

The team

Passionate animal lovers who enjoy getting to know patients and their families.

Founder and veterinarian

Dr. Vivien Leung (Dr. Vi)

BSc, DVM, Certified Veterinary Acupuncturist, Certified Veterinary Chinese Herbalist, Certified Food Therapist. Dr. Vi graduated from the Western College of Veterinary Medicine in Saskatchewan and developed a passion for small animals and exotic pets. Her experience in Uganda, Hong Kong and Singapore shaped a compassionate, whole-patient approach, and she trained further in Traditional Chinese Veterinary Medicine at Chi University in Florida and in international animal welfare at the University of Edinburgh.

She founded the hospital with a vision of harmony in health, and values lasting bonds with pets and their humans.

Good to know

Answers taken from the clinic's own pages. Anything not listed there, the team confirms.

Which animals does the clinic see?

Dogs and cats, small mammals (rabbits, guinea pigs, ferrets, hamsters, chinchillas) and reptiles (bearded dragons, geckos, turtles).

My pet gets stressed at the vet. Is that a problem?

The clinic's approach is gentle, low-stress handling, adapting to your pet's pace in a calm, welcoming environment, with clear communication and shared decision-making.

What is TCVM and when is it used?

Traditional Chinese Veterinary Medicine includes acupuncture, herbal medicine and food therapy. It is often used to support chronic conditions, improve mobility and comfort, enhance overall wellness and complement conventional treatments. Dr. Vivien Leung is a certified veterinary acupuncturist, Chinese herbalist and food therapist.

Who can I talk to in my own language?

Dr. Vivien is fluent in English, Cantonese and Mandarin. Eva speaks English, Cantonese and Mandarin, and Heather speaks Korean and English.

Is there a groomer on site?

Grooming services are listed on the clinic's services page: nail trim, lion-cut grooming, ear cleaning, teeth trimming and anal gland expression.

What if my pet needs help when the clinic is closed?

The clinic lists two emergency hospitals for after hours: VCA Canada Vancouver Animal Emergency and Referral Centre at (604) 879-3737 and Intercity Animal Emergency Clinic at (604) 321-8080.

Can I send a message instead of calling?

Yes. The contact form asks for the owner's name, the pet's name, an e-mail and a message, and the clinic is also reachable at info@langaravet.com or 604-245-3833.

Ready to book?

The clinic's contact form asks for the owner's name, the pet's name, an e-mail and a message. The assistant asks the same, plus the kind of visit.
