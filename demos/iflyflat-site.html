2 November 2026.

The Points Whisperer®, with 13 years helping business owners fly flat. iFLYflat has been helping business owners since 2012.

No. You can start with the System and choose ongoing support later, or join the Club directly with System onboarding included.

Yes. The amount paid for the System is credited towards Club membership when you upgrade during the programme or within 90 days of completion, under the existing terms.
