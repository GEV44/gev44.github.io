Our classes

Hard only as you want them to be

Our LGBT+ group fitness classes in London offer strength and conditioning, functional training and HIIT. Every class is 55 minutes, caters to all levels, and ends with an optional post-workout social.

Functional Strength

55 minutes

Strength training that readies your body for daily activities. The class uses all planes of movement, creating resistance from body weight and various equipment.

  • Squats and deadlifts
  • Pushing and pulling exercises
  • Medium weight, high reps

Strength Circuits

55 minutes

A circuit class with a Workout with PRIDE twist. Move quickly through 8-10 exercise stations to work different muscle groups with little to no rest, with partner drills and maybe an AMRAP (as many rounds as possible) to keep it interesting.

  • Whole body by the end of the circuit
  • Work at your own pace with different weight options
  • Builds strength, flexibility and mobility

#PROUD Cycle

55 minutes

An all-new spinning experience: hill climbs, sprints, power and performance, set to the sounds of icons from Missy Elliot to Madonna. A great way to start your fitness journey.

  • Caters to all levels
  • Work at your own pace

Personal training

Extra motivation, your way

If you are looking for extra motivation, Workout with PRIDE offers LGBT personal training in London with something for every budget: small group personal training to 1-2-1.

1-2-1 personal training

Our personal trainers are highly experienced in designing truly effective well-being programmes. We focus on unblocking what has been holding you back and give you the tools to be successful.

Starting with your first consultation, we discuss your goals, habits, lifestyles and blockers. Together we devise a plan that is simple to follow, fun and sets you up to succeed.

  • A combination of strength, endurance and mobility training
  • Our classes included, plus frequent check-ins
  • Tracked every session, updated every two weeks

Small group personal training

Train in small groups of up to 5 people, where you are matched on goals and strength level, and benefit from the expert attention of our trainers for less than half the price of 1-2-1 sessions.

We can compromise on price but not on good form. Check our Instagram to see how fun the classes are, then ask the assistant about a session.

Your first class

What to know before you come

Bring

Workout kit, suitable trainers and water. Not all studios have towels, so bring one.

Lockers and showers

Most locations have lockers: some need a padlock or £1, others use a code. There are showers, so bring a towel.

Where

We hire studios across London. The full address is on the booking form.

Level

All classes cater to all levels. Rest when you need to and join back in when you are ready.

Access

All studios have ground-floor or lift-accessed disabled changing rooms and showers, but some studios are reached only by steps. Get in touch to discuss your needs.

Who

Absolutely everyone is welcome, as long as you follow our values and are an ally to the community.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Where are you located?

We hire studios across London. Check out the full address on the booking form.

I'm not part of the LGBTQIA+ community. Can I still join?

Absolutely everyone is welcome at Workout with PRIDE. As long as you follow our values and are an ally to the community, you are welcome.

I'm new to fitness. Will I be able to keep up?

All our classes cater to all levels. Everyone works at their own pace. If you need to rest or take a break, please do so, then join back in when you're ready.

Do I have to attend the post-workout social?

Our after-class socials are optional. We have arranged discounts at all our local restaurants to keep the price point low. Feel free to bring a friend to the social even if they didn't come to class.

Do you have lockers?

Most of the locations have lockers. Some require a padlock or £1, and others operate on a code combination setup. Alternatively, you can bring your belongings to the studio.

What do I need to bring?

Workout kit, suitable trainers and water. Please note that not all our studios have towels. Please bring one with you.

Are there showers?

Yes, but please bring a towel.

Do you have disabled access?

All the studios we hire have ground floor or lift-accessed disabled changing rooms and showers. Some studios are only accessed via steps, though. Don't hesitate to get in touch with us to discuss your requirements.
