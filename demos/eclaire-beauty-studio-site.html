Our philosophy

Tailored to your natural nails

True beauty lies in the harmony of expertise, artistry and personalised care. Every service is thoughtfully tailored to each client's natural nail condition and lifestyle, so results are beautiful, long-lasting and healthy.

The team trains under Claire's guidance to keep the studio's distinctive style and standards. Even seasoned professionals refine their skills through this process.

"What began as a tiny 5m² studio has grown into a sophisticated beauty space with a highly trained team."

Founded by Claire Lee, who trained at a beauty academy in Korea and studied Visual Communication at Monash University. More than 12 years of nail and lash artistry sit behind the studio. There are no junior staff: every service is performed by an experienced technician.

The éclaire experience

Three promises

Experienced professionals

Every service is performed exclusively by highly experienced technicians. The studio does not employ junior staff, so each client receives consistent, expert-level care, with precise, elegant results tailored to individual needs.

Artistry and precision

An artistic vision combined with meticulous craftsmanship. Inspired by Korean beauty trends, each design is created to complement your natural features and personal style, from subtle elegance to intricate nail art.

Commitment to quality

Premium products and the highest standards of hygiene. Every detail is considered from consultation to completion, for long-lasting results in a comfortable environment.

About the name

éclaire combines two meaningful words: éclair and Claire. In French, éclair signifies a flash of lightning or a radiant burst of light. Claire is the name of the founder, whose vision and passion form the heart of the brand. Together they describe a place where beauty shines with refined brilliance.

Salon policies

Before you book

A short summary of the studio's terms. The full text lives on the Salon Policies page of the studio's website.

Deposit

A $50 deposit is required to secure your booking and is deducted from your final payment. It is refundable only if you cancel more than 48 hours before the appointment ($1.18 processing fee excluded).

Cancellations and rescheduling

Changes or cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before your appointment. Changes within 48 hours lose the deposit, and a new deposit is needed for another booking. Deposits are non-refundable for no-shows.

Running late

Please let the studio know. More than 10 minutes late and your service or design may be adjusted; more than 20 minutes late and the appointment may be cancelled and the deposit forfeited.

Designs and nail art

Nail art is handmade, so it cannot be guaranteed to be 100% identical to reference photos. Designs may be adjusted for colours, products, nail condition and appointment time. Extra nail art charges vary by design.

Removals, refills and repairs

Removals are available only together with a new service at éclaire. For quality, hygiene and safety reasons, the studio does not do infills, repairs or touch-ups on work from other salons.

Warranty

Nails and pedicures: complimentary repairs within 14 days for eligible issues such as lifting, chipping or detached nail art; contact the studio with photos first. Lash lift: complimentary adjustment within 7 days if the lift did not process correctly or is excessively curled. The warranty covers only services done at éclaire.

Health and aftercare

Tell the studio before your appointment about nail conditions, allergies, injuries, infections, eye or skin conditions. Services are cosmetic only and are not intended to diagnose or treat medical conditions.
