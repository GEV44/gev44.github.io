FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Can I have a consultation and a service on the same day?

Absolutely, although first-time clients need a colour patch test to check for an allergy to PPD, which is present in hair colour products.

Are consultations free?

Yes, we offer complimentary consultations online or in the salon.

Are blow dries included with colour?

No, blow dries are not included with hair colour services.

Are the prices fixed?

Our prices are a guide, and fitting prices may differ depending on which stylist you book with.

How do I get to you?

We are a few minutes' walk from Oxford Circus and Tottenham Court Road tube stations, at 28 Market Place.
