Services

What the clinic offers

Active Release Techniques (ART)

A manual soft-tissue therapy for muscles, tendons, ligaments, fascia and nerves.

McKenzie-based treatment

A movement-oriented method for spine and limb pain that teaches you to help yourself.

Certified strength and conditioning

Sport-specific testing, strength programs and injury prevention guidance.

Functional movement analysis

A biomechanical look at the movements of your daily life to find the source of the problem.

Joint manipulations

Hands-on joint techniques as part of a treatment plan.

Kinesio taping

Taping to support movement during rehab and training.

Post-surgical rehabilitation

A plan for regaining mobility and function after your procedure.

Pre- and post-race training and rehab

For amateur athletes, weekend warriors and professionals getting to the start line or recovering after the finish.

Primary care injury evaluation

Doctors of physical therapy assess and recommend treatment, and refer to other medical professionals when necessary.

Running gait analysis and correction

Video analysis and motion capture of your stride, foot strike and posture.

Dry needling

Sterile, thin monofilament needles in trigger points to ease tight or spasming muscles.

What to expect on day one

  1. Welcome and paperwork

    Complete your medical history and insurance information online beforehand if you can.

  2. Evaluation and examination

    Questions about your history and symptoms, then range of motion, strength, flexibility, balance and joint stability.

  3. Goals and plan

    Your therapist explains their findings, sets achievable goals with you and outlines a tailored plan.

  4. Treatment and home program

    Hands-on treatment if needed, a home exercise program, and scheduling of your next visits.
