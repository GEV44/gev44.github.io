Questions

Which areas do you cover?

Vancouver, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, Burnaby and Coquitlam.

Do you work with first-time buyers?

Yes. Many of Casey's clients are first-time and condo buyers; he also handles presales, new developments and luxury homes.

How is the list price decided?

Together with you, through market stats and sales history shown openly.

How fast do homes sell?

The team says it sells a home on average every six days, and that its listings typically get over 15,000 impressions in the first week.

Can you help with staging or repairs before listing?

Yes. They supply staging resources where needed and professional services such as painting, handyman repairs or consulting.

Are presales and new developments covered?

Yes, along with luxury homes and condos.

Do you help people moving to Vancouver?

Chantel Machnik moved from Calgary herself and specialises in relocations and first-time buyers.

What does it cost?

Not published online. Ask in the free consultation.
