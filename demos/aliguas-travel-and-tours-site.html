The agency

A Vancouver agency since 2013

Aliguas Travel is a wholly-owned corporation established in September 2013 by Deo Tan, providing travel and tour packages, products and services from Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. It prides itself on reasonable deals, from airfares, cruises, car rentals, hotels and tours to travel insurance and other travel needs, with great customer service and satisfaction.

The agency caters generally to trips and vacations from Canada to the Philippines and back, and equally offers travel deals to destinations around the world, such as the USA, Europe, Mexico, Australia, within Canada and the rest of Asia.

Its mission is to provide the ultimate travel planning experience while becoming a one-stop shop for every travel service available in the industry.

Questions

Quick answers

Where can I find your promotions?

On the Aliguas Travel & Tours Facebook page. The website invites you to follow it for promos and travel packages.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, per the contact page.

Do you only do flights to the Philippines?

No. That is the main focus, but the agency also books flights across Asia, the Americas and Europe, plus cruises, tours, hotels, car rentals and travel insurance.

Can I build my own trip?

The home page has a Create Your Tour option to build your next holiday trip with the team.

Where do I check my trip updates?

The Travel Links page lists CheckMyTrip for trip updates.

Where can I exchange currency?

The Travel Links page points to ICE International Currency Exchange.

Is there a Philippine consulate link?

Yes. The Travel Links page lists the Philippine Consulate General, the consulate office in Vancouver.

Which airports are on the travel links?

Vancouver International Airport (YVR), Toronto Pearson and the Calgary Airport Authority.

Do you book trips within Canada?

Yes. Within Canada is listed alongside the USA, Europe, Mexico, Australia and the rest of Asia, and the agency also sells bus and train tour packages.

Is it a new company?

No. Aliguas Travel was established in September 2013 by Deo Tan in Vancouver, British Columbia.

Do you have a street address?

The website does not list one. Call the team and they will tell you how to find them.
