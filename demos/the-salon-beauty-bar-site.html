Good to know

How do I book?

Use the request form on the salon's contact page (name, preferred date and time, service, number) or call 604-682-4645. The salon confirms by reply.

Do you do gift cards?

Yes, the website sells a gift card.

What should I do after waxing?

The salon's aftercare list: avoid tanning, swimming, saunas, hot baths and workouts for 48 hours, avoid touching the area for 24 hours, wear loose clothing, skip shaving between waxes, and wax every 4 to 6 weeks.

What should I do after a lash lift?

Avoid getting lashes wet or touching them for 24 hours, avoid swimming, saunas and extreme heat, do not use an eyelash curler or oil-based products, and avoid sleeping face down. Results last about 4 to 6 weeks.

How long does Bio Sculpture Gel last?

The salon says up to 2 to 4 weeks depending on nail growth and care, and it soaks off in 15 to 20 minutes.
