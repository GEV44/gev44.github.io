Good to know

Questions

What can I expect on my first visit?

You talk through your goals, review your health and history, and map out a plan that connects dentistry, vitality and esthetics. You won't feel rushed.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. CareCredit and Cherry financing. The team can discuss deposits and payment options that fit your budget.

Is my deposit refundable?

Yes, if no treatment has been rendered. Ask the team about the cancellation and scheduling terms for your appointment.

Are same-day appointments available?

Yes. Contact the team to check same-day availability.

Are you kid-friendly? Can I bring my dog?

Children are welcome. Friendly dogs are welcome too but stay in the reception area with the staff.

Are the Vitality programs medically supervised?

Yes. Plans are personalized using diagnostics such as bloodwork, with clear expectations set up front.

Will esthetic results look natural?

Natural is the whole point: features are enhanced without changing what makes you look like you. Downtime varies by treatment, and many options have little to none.

Do porcelain veneers look natural, and how long do they last?

They are custom-designed to look and function like natural teeth, and with good care can last for many years.

Is Invisalign right for me?

It is a great fit for a lot of patients. The team checks your bite and goals to confirm whether clear aligners or another option works best.

Do you treat TMJ pain or snoring?

Yes, TMJ treatment and snoring and airway options are part of the special services.

Will my dental care connect with my other treatment?

Yes. As an integrated practice, your dental plan works alongside your vitality and esthetics care.

Do I need lab work before starting Vitality?

Usually yes. Diagnostics like bloodwork are used to personalize your plan.

Can esthetic treatments be combined?

Yes, often, for example microneedling with PRF for better results. How long results last depends on the treatment, and the team walks you through it.
