Good to know

Before you call

Can I talk to someone in my language?

The team lists English, Spanish, Russian, Persian (Farsi), French, Czech and Slovak. Tell the assistant your language and it passes the request to the right agent.

Do you handle pre-construction?

Yes. Pre-construction is one of the curated lists and the site has a pre-construction condos section. Jessica Turner specializes in pre-construction and new-construction luxury condos.

What is my home worth?

The site has a "Your home's value" request. An agent follows up, so the assistant collects your address and contact details.

Do you help with rentals?

Yes, there is a Rent section and a Short-term Friendly list. The team confirms what suits your building and dates.

Can I browse before I contact anyone?

Yes. The platform is built so you can search condos, view floor plans and read neighborhood intelligence on your own, and reach out when you are ready.

Who handles investment purchases?

The brokerage specializes in residential, pre-construction and investment properties. The assistant can route an investment enquiry to the team.

Do you work with buyers from abroad?

The team speaks several languages, listed above. How they support out-of-town buyers is something the team confirms directly.

Where can I find condo association contacts?

The blog has a Miami Condo Association HOA contact page listing associations with their phone numbers and addresses, compiled to help current and future condo residents make informed decisions.

Do you support fair housing?

Yes. Blackbook Properties states that it fully supports the principles of the Fair Housing Act and the Equal Opportunity Act.
