FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Is there a charge if I miss my appointment?

Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are charged 50% of the service cost. A no-show without notice is charged the full amount.

What colour lines do you use?

Suki's uses Goldwell professional colour.

Can I bring my own colour?

Yes, a professional colour is welcome and the cost stays the same, but work is only guaranteed with Suki's own colour lines.

Do you have a stylist for Asian hair?

All stylists are trained to be comfortable with all hair types, including fine, coarse and curly.

What is the difference between a blow dry and a dry off?

A dry-off is a complimentary 10 minute service after a technical cut or colour, with no styling tools. A blow-dry is a 45-60 minute paid service for a finished, styled look.
