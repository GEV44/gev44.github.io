Quick answers
Questions


Do you handle residential?
Not yet. The residential division is coming soon and will be a curated practice. For now the focus is commercial real estate.


Can I reach someone after hours?
Chariff’s trademark service is a live agent on call, around the clock, at 305.576.7474.


Do you work with both landlords and tenants?
Yes. Chariff represents landlords, tenants, buyers, sellers, investors and developers.


Do you work with other brokers?
Chariff recognizes a cooperating broker only where exclusive tenant or buyer representation was disclosed and acknowledged in writing before first contact, tour or offer. Read the broker notice on the main site for the full terms.


Which property types do you list?
Office, retail, retail/office, warehouse, office/warehouse, flex, restaurant/retail, cafe, development sites, multifamily, residential income, special purpose, turn-key and investment properties.


Can I get access to off-market spaces?
Yes, that is part of the service. Chariff gives buyers and tenants access to on- and off-market opportunities, drawing on local relationships and market intelligence.


How do I get started?
Call 305.576.7474, use the brief builder above, or tell the assistant what you need and it will prepare your requirements for an agent.


Is all the property data guaranteed?
No. Data comes from sources believed reliable but is not verified. Ages and square footage must be checked independently by buyers and tenants and their advisors.
