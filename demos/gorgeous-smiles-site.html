FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you take dental emergencies?

Yes, same-day appointments are offered. Please call (03) 9042 0483 before you come.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, interest-free plans through Afterpay, Humm and Zip with terms from 1 to 24 months, subject to the providers' eligibility criteria. You can also pay upfront.

Do you accept private health funds?

Yes, all major funds, with claims processed on-site with HICAPS. Medicare does not cover Invisalign.

Do you treat children?

Yes, adults and children, including fissure sealants and orthodontic treatment for children.

Do I need a consultation before cosmetic work?

Yes, all cosmetic work starts with a consultation to check suitability.

Are new patients welcome?

Yes. Suggest a day and time or call (03) 9042 0483 to confirm a spot.
