FAQ

Quick answers

Is the driver also the guide?

No. Every tour has a professional driver and a separate Irish guide.

How many people are on each tour?

It varies with season and bookings. Busier months use full-size luxury coaches, quieter periods may use smaller vehicles. Every tour is fully guided.

Is there Wi-Fi on board?

Yes, free Wi-Fi on all coaches, though the signal can vary in remote areas.

What is the sheep farm stop?

A traditional working sheep farm where a local farmer explains how sheepdogs are trained and gives a live herding demonstration. Guests may be able to hold lambs. About 30 minutes.
