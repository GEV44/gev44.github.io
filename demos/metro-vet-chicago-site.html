About us

Metropolitan Veterinary Center provides comprehensive veterinary care to pets in the Chicago community. The staff treat you like family and each pet like their own, and believe that preventative care with wellness exams is the key to a longer, healthier life. It is a full-service hospital with testing and diagnostics, advanced care and surgery.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What should I do in an emergency?

During business hours, call us immediately. Outside our hours, we recommend Veterinary Emergency Group, 1114 S Clinton St Unit B & C, (872) 710-5226, or 755 W North Ave, (312) 757-5444.

How often should my pet have an exam?

Yearly for most pets, and every 6 months with blood work for pets over age 7. Puppies and kittens need more frequent visits in their first year.

What payment methods do you accept?

Major credit cards, cash, personal checks with ID, pet insurance, CareCredit and Scratchpay. Full payment is due at the time of service.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Complete the New Client Form beforehand and bring your pet's records, medications, feeding information and any questions.

Which pets do you treat?

We provide comprehensive medical care for dogs and cats.
