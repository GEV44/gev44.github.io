FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How much does a haircut cost?

A Master Stylist Cut starts at $125+ and a Men's Haircut at $67+. Final pricing varies by stylist, hair length, density and condition.

Is there parking nearby?

Metered street parking and public parkades are available in Yaletown; availability and rates vary.

How do I get there by transit?

The salon is near Yaletown-Roundhouse Station on the Canada Line, and bus routes 6 and 23 serve the area.

Do I need a consultation?

A consultation may be needed before colour correction, major lightening or a significant transformation.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, Pink Lime gift cards are available.
