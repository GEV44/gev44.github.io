The company

A Houston classroom that went online

Comedy Driving, Inc. began with a defensive driving classroom in Houston, Texas in 2002. Spanish classes were added to the course in 2006, and in 2008 the Texas online defensive driving course went viral at ComedyDriving.com.

The course was developed by professional comedians alongside driving safety specialists, so that students learn in a fun and relaxed way and stay awake. The company says it is the number one driving safety course provider in Texas, with a Better Business Bureau A+ rating and almost 100,000 positive reviews from people who took the course.

Customer care is available by phone and email, and the classroom is open to anyone who arrives on time, with no appointment necessary.

For a ticket

From ticket to certificate

  1. Get the court's permission

    Take proof of insurance and your driver's license to the court first. The officer who wrote the ticket cannot give permission.

  2. Sign up

    Click Sign Up and follow the registration. You need the county and court, and your due date.

  3. Take the course

    6 hours, including a required 1 hour break. Chapter questions show right away whether you passed.

  4. Send the certificate to the court

    You get a court copy and an insurance copy. Most courts also want your driving record.

Questions

Quick answers

Do I need to talk to the court first?

Yes. You must get permission from the court before taking the class for a ticket dismissal.

What happens if I fail the chapter questions?

You get a second set, then a third and final set. If you fail the third, you fail the course. Call 713-465-0042 if you are struggling.

Can I take it if I do not live in Texas?

If the court that issued your ticket allows it, yes. You will submit a notarized Statement of Assurance.

What should I bring to the classroom?

Your Texas driver's license, the court paperwork, and your sense of humor.

What if my court needs an instructor signature?

A few smaller Texas courts ask for one. Fax the paperwork to 713-465-0058 and it is returned with the signature.

What if I make a mistake while registering?

Contact the help desk at 713-465-0042 and they will correct it. You can also review delivery options at the end of the course.

Do I need to save my work?

Progress is saved as you click Next on each page, and when you log back in you pick up where you left off. If you log out before the time requirement for a page is finished, you lose the time on that page.

Is there a notary?

Yes. A notary is available at the Katy Freeway, League City, Willowbrook and Sugar Land locations listed in the help center.

When is Express certificate delivery closed?

There are no pickups or deliveries on weekends or holidays. The site lists Easter, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year Eve and New Year Day.

What is the difference between a driving safety course and a defensive driving course?

In the state of Texas, there is no difference.

Do I need a printer?

No. Mail and overnight certificate options are available.

How do I pay?

Credit card online. The classrooms take cash, check or money order, and you can mail a check or money order to the Katy Freeway address.
