Welcome

Who did your hair?

Our friendly team makes sure your visit is unforgettable and has everyone asking that question.

Welcome to Cream Melbourne. We’re privileged to be in the heart of bohemian Fitzroy, offering a peaceful oasis above the bustling Brunswick Street. Our philosophy is to understand your individual preferences and create a look that complements your unique features and fits your lifestyle.

Since 2002 our salon has been built on excellence in hairdressing and a dedication to the best possible customer experience. The team shares a vision of continuous self-education, to stay up to date with the latest trends, treatments and services. Excellence is our culture.

Good to know

Questions we hear often

How long will my appointment take?

For new clients we allow 3½ hours for a cut and a basic colour. Colour correction or long, thick hair takes longer, so give the team as much information as you can when booking.

Can I book a consultation first?

Yes, and for anything beyond a simple refresh it is recommended. Consultations are complimentary.

Can I request a specific stylist?

Yes. Choose a stylist when you book, or tell the team what you are after and they will match you.

How often should I get a haircut?

Every 6–8 weeks keeps the shape sharp. Solid colour regrowth is typically every 4–6 weeks; balayage every 8–12 weeks.

Do you offer buy now, pay later?

Cream lists Afterpay and Zip. The team can confirm other payment options.

Ready for hair you adore?

Ask the assistant anything, or leave a booking request and the team will confirm.
