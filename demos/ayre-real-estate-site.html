Tenants and landlords

Quick answers.

How do I report a repair?

Use the Tenancy Mobile App to submit a normal repair request; it is the fastest way. Photos of the issue help. Log in with your PropertyMe email and password.

What counts as an emergency?

Something broken or damaged that causes immediate danger to you, others or the property. If you cannot reach the office, check your lease for emergency trades, then email your property manager.

What can the tenancy app do?

View property and lease details, see when rent and invoices are due, log maintenance requests, message your property manager, see inspections and download documents.

Which suburbs do you cover?

Sydney City, Pyrmont, Millers Point, Barangaroo, Ultimo, Hyde Park, Darlinghurst, Surry Hills, Potts Point, Redfern, Alexandria, Zetland and nearby inner-city suburbs.

Can I see what my property is worth?

Yes. Request a sales appraisal or a rental appraisal and the team will have a confidential conversation with you.
