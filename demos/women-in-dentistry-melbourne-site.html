About the practice

Trust, education and compassion

Women in Dentistry has been a trusted name on Collins Street for decades. The practice believes preventive care is the foundation of long-term oral health, and takes the time to teach patients how to look after their teeth and gums.

The goal is to become your lifelong partner in a healthy smile: high-quality care that supports health, function and overall well-being, from a routine check-up and restorative treatment to a complete smile transformation.

Good to know

Questions patients ask

Where can I park?

Complimentary valet parking at the Sofitel Melbourne on Collins, next door, for up to two hours during your appointment.

Can I fit an appointment into a work day?

Yes. Weekdays open at 8am, lunchtime appointments are kept for city workers, and Saturday mornings are available.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

The team keeps time for same-day emergencies. Call (03) 9650 2863 as early as you can.

Do you accept health funds?

All major health funds, with on-the-spot HICAPS claims at reception.

I am nervous about the dentist.

Tell the team when you book. They add extra time, explain every step before it happens and never rush you.

Do you only treat women?

No. The clinical team is all female, but men, women and children are all welcome.

Is there a complimentary consultation?

Yes, for porcelain veneers and Invisalign.
