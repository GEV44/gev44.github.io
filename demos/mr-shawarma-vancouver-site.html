About us

Mr. Shawarma is the first Middle Eastern and shawarma food truck in Canada, serving Vancouver since 2009. Everything is prepared daily from scratch, with nothing preserved or processed, in a certified 8,000 square foot kitchen using fresh meats and vegetables from trusted local BC markets. Executive Chef and founder Mahmoud brings over 25 years of culinary education and experience in hotel and restaurant management.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Is the meat halal?

Yes, Mr. Shawarma is Halal certified. Halal meat is prepared according to specific Halal guidelines.

Where are your locations?

800 Robson Street (Robson & Hornby, Robson Square), 655 Burrard Street (entrance of Burrard SkyTrain Station), Mr. Supreme at Granville & West Georgia (entrance of Granville SkyTrain Station) and 1075 West Georgia Street.

Can I order for pickup or delivery?

You can order for pickup online from 1075 West Georgia Street, 800 Robson Street or 655 Burrard Street. Delivery is through DoorDash (Robson, Mr. Supreme and Georgia) and Uber Eats (Robson and Mr. Supreme).

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. The Famous Fresh Falafel is made in-house and comes as a pita wrap, SAJ, rice platter or Spicy Box.

How does food truck catering work?

Mobile catering with food trucks and food carts, with up to 3 hours of service. The minimum spend is $2,000 CAD plus a $250 loading and travel fee based on your location. A 25% deposit is due once your invoice is issued, and the balance 24 hours before the event.

What do the menu items cost?

The website menu doesn't list item prices, so the team confirms current prices. Catering has published per-guest pricing.
