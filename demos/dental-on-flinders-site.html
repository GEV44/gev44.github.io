Nervous? You set the pace.

  1. Anxiety questionnaire
    Tell the team what triggers it so the visit is tailored from the start.
  2. Empathetic diagnosis
    Everything is explained before it is done, with check-ins throughout.
  3. Oral sedatives
    Prescribed before your appointment if you need more support.

    Day surgery at Glenferrie Private Hospital with a visiting oral surgeon.

Common questions

Do you accept health fund claims?

Yes. HICAPS is on site for instant claims, and the practice is a preferred provider for HCF and CBHS.

Do you see children?

Yes. The practice is registered for the Child Dental Benefits Scheme (CDBS) for eligible children.

Are you open on weekends?

Saturday 9am to 4pm and Sunday 9am to 12pm. Press the intercom at the entrance outside weekday office hours.

Can I pay over time?

Interest-free payment plans of up to 12 months are available. Call before your appointment to arrange one.
