Treatments in focus

Three things the clinic explains in detail on its own pages.

Zoom whitening

A light-activated gel in three fifteen-minute sessions. Teeth typically become 8 to 10 shades brighter in about an hour, and often keep brightening for a few days.

Laser dentistry

Precise beams of light for soft tissue and gum care, canker and cold sore relief and treating infection in root canals, with less damage to surrounding tissue and quicker healing.

Dental implants

Titanium posts that act as tooth roots and support crowns, bridges or dentures, replacing missing teeth with a long-lasting, natural-looking result.

Whether a treatment suits you is for the dentist to decide at a consultation.

Care after the doors close

The team says it checks in on patients after a procedure to make sure everything is going as planned, even after the office has closed for the evening, and treats every patient the way it would treat family. Clients are the priority, and the doctors mentor other dentists so the standard keeps rising across the profession.

Questions

From the clinic's own pages. Ask the assistant for the rest.

What is a veneer?

A thin porcelain shell, about half a millimetre thick, bonded on top of the natural tooth. Preparation is more conservative than for a crown, which is why veneers are the treatment of choice here.

How long do porcelain veneers last?

Usually 10 to 15 years, sometimes longer, and they resist stain better than natural teeth.

What is a smile makeover?

Widening and building out the teeth, often with veneers, to create a more dynamic, youthful smile, described as a facelift from the nose down without the scalpel.

How does financing work?

Many treatments are covered by insurance. For the rest there are payment plans through a special financing system, and the clinic accepts Visa, MasterCard, American Express and Interac debit.

Why might I need a root canal?

If the pulp inside a tooth is infected or injured, a root canal removes the infection and fills the space, preventing the infection spreading. The dentist will advise whether it applies to you.

What can dental lasers treat?

The clinic lists pain relief for canker and cold sores, infection in root canals, gum disease and inflammation, gum shaping, help exposing wisdom teeth and removal of benign oral growths.

How does Zoom whitening work?

After your lips and gums are isolated, a light-activated hydrogen peroxide gel is applied in three fifteen-minute sessions. Teeth typically end up 8 to 10 shades whiter, and often brighten a little more over the following days.

Should I arrive early?

Yes, fifteen minutes before your appointment, with insurance documents and a form of identification, to speed up registration.
