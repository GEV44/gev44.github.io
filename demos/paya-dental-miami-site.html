Services

Treatments for every stage of your smile

Find the dental care that fits your needs, from routine visits to specialized treatment.

General

  • Dental cleaningsPrevent cavities, gum disease and bad breath.
  • Dental fillingsRestore decayed teeth and protect long-term oral health.
  • Tooth extractionsGentle, comfortable extractions when a tooth cannot be saved.
  • Wisdom teeth removalRelief from wisdom tooth pain and discomfort.

Restorative

  • Dental implantsDurable, natural-looking replacement for missing teeth.
  • All-on-FourA full arch supported by implants.
  • Crowns and porcelain crownsRestore shape, strength and appearance.
  • BridgesFill the gaps left by missing teeth.

FAQ

Quick answers

Which insurance plans do you accept?

Many plans, including Aetna, Cigna, Delta Dental and MetLife. Medicaid is accepted; contact your location to confirm your coverage.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. Financing is available through CareCredit and Sunbit.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes, the team is bilingual and the new patient form is available in English and Spanish.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A valid photo ID, your dental insurance card, any previous dental records and your completed new patient form.

What are your office hours?

Hialeah: Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri 8:30 AM - 3:30 PM, Tuesday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, two Saturdays a month 8:30 AM - 1:30 PM. Miami (7th Street): Monday to Friday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM. Please call to confirm availability.

Do you offer same-day implants?

Same-day implants may be available for qualifying patients. The doctors decide after a consultation and 3D imaging.

Is the whole family welcome?

Yes, Paya Dental provides personalized, high-quality dental care for the whole family in Hialeah and Miami.

I am in pain. What should I do?

Please call the office right away so the team can advise you. This page and its assistant cannot give medical advice or diagnoses. For a life-threatening emergency, call 911.

How much does treatment cost?

Prices are not listed online. The team will explain costs and options for your treatment; call your office.
