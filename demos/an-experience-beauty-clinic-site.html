Good to know

Before your first visit

What happens at a first facial?

You will spend time chatting about your skin, health and well-being, because they have a huge impact on your skin. Ashvinie shares her hints and tips, then you decide together which facial best addresses your concerns.

Will I be pressured to buy products?

Guests describe getting advice and tips without being pushy. Products are available if you want them, in store and online.

Can I add LED light to my treatment?

Yes. Omnilux LED light therapy can be added to facials, microbiome treatments and microdermabrasion for $79.

Are the prices fixed?

The listed prices can change, so the team confirms the current price when you book.

How do I book?

Use the Request an Appointment form on the clinic's website, call 0401 139 754 or email anexperience2020@gmail.com. The assistant in the corner can prepare a request for you.
