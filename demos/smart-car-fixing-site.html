Quick answers

Do you offer 24-hour car repair?

Yes. The site says you can call or WhatsApp any time, day or night, and a mechanic inspects the car and gives a clear estimate before work begins.

What does it cost?

It depends on the car model, the service, the parts and the location. The mechanic checks the vehicle first and shares an estimate before starting. The assistant has no price list.

How often should a car be serviced?

The site says most cars need maintenance every 5,000 to 10,000 km or as the owner's manual advises, and Dubai heat and traffic may call for earlier checks.

What are signs my car needs attention?

Warning lights, weak AC, slow starting, strange noises, brake noise, vibration, overheating, fluid leaks, poor fuel economy or a rough drive.

Can you come to my building's parking?

The site mentions home, office, roadside, basement parking and other nearby locations.
