FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What happens at a first visit?

Plan for at least two hours. A patient coordinator collects your forms, the dentist and assistant answer your questions, digital x-rays are taken, you get a full dental health report with an intra-oral camera tour if needed, and a hygienist does a cleaning or starts gum therapy if recommended.

Which insurance do you accept?

Most major dental PPO insurances. Tell the team which plan you have and they will confirm your coverage.

How can I pay?

Cash or check, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover, plus short-term interest-free and longer-term financing with monthly payments. A 3% convenience fee applies to credit card payments; debit, HSA, FSA, cash and check have no fee.

What is a virtual smile consult?

It's free for new and existing patients. You upload a smile photo and say what you'd like to change, and the dentists record a personalized video with your options and price estimates.

What is a CEREC crown?

A crown designed, milled and bonded in a single visit, with no temporary crown and no second appointment. Most patients with enough tooth structure to support a crown are candidates.

Do you help patients with dental anxiety?

Yes. Mild sedatives can calm apprehension, and sedation can be arranged for any procedure. The dentists and technicians are DOCS certified.
