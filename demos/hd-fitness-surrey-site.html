FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Are you a 24-hour gym?

Yes. 24 Hour members can access the gym any time, including weekends and holidays.

Can I try the gym first?

Yes. BC residents can buy a 3 Day Visit Pass for $5 or a 1 Day Drop In for $15 (by appointment), and we offer free consultations.

Do I need experience for personal training?

No. Our certified trainers work with beginners to advanced athletes.

Are there contracts?

Memberships come as a 12-month commitment or open-ended. Open-ended plans need 60 days' notice to cancel. A $70 annual maintenance fee applies.

Is there a fee for paying by card?

Credit card payments carry a 2.4% surcharge, which is credited back if you pay by bank account.

Is parking free?

Yes, free parking is included.
