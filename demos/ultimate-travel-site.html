How booking works

Three steps from "maybe" to "see you at the airport".

Hold your spotPay the low deposit at the payment step on the tour page. Visa or Mastercard, charged in AUD.
Pay the balanceThe remainder is due 45 days before your tour. Instalments are possible.
Plans changed?Free date or tour changes more than 45 days out. Deposits can stay on file for 24 months, or you can book Open-Dated.

Payments can take up to 72 hours to show on your booking. Tour arrangements are confirmed by the team once payment is received.

Quick answers

Who are the tours for?

Mostly 18 to 35s. Some adventure tours are open to ages 18 and over. Each tour page shows the age range for that trip.

How much is the deposit?

A deposit holds your place. The amount is shown on each tour page at the payment step.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Pay the deposit now and the balance before you travel.

Can I change my dates after booking?

In most cases yes, and it is free more than 45 days before your start. Talk to the team as early as you can.

When will I hear back?

Most enquiries are answered the same working day, Monday to Friday.
