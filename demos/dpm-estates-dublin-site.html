About DPM Estates

DPM Estates is a residential and commercial sales, letting and property management agency on Glasnevin Hill, Dublin 9, operating for over 15 years. It is fully licensed with the Property Services Regulatory Authority (licence no. 002357) and a full member of IPAV, the Institute of Professional Auctioneers and Valuers.

How do valuations work?

Valuations are free and no obligation when you engage DPM as your sales agency, and the agency gives a 24 hour turnaround on valuation instructions. An experienced agent meets you at the property, walks through it noting condition, location, size and type, and bases the valuation on comparable properties both sold and currently on the market, plus decor and past renovations such as extensions or attic conversions.

What is the selling fee?

DPM Estates currently offers a 1% fee ex VAT when selling. That fee includes free marketing, free photography and free advertising, with a No Sale No Fee policy and no upfront fees.

What is No Sale No Fee?

Yes: DPM has a No Sale No Fee policy with no upfront fees when selling.

What is included when selling?

When selling, the fee includes marketing, photography and advertising. You also get a full colour brochure with photographs and floor plans, and your property is shown in the agency's shop front window. DPM can also arrange BER certificates, cleaning and clearance, and repairs, maintenance and painting to enhance the property for viewers.

What does letting include?

Landlords have two options: the Let Only Service or the Complete Management Package. Let Only includes a free verbal rental valuation, an initial inspection and fit-out advice, advertising on dpmestates.ie and Daft.ie plus a brochure in the shop front window, attendance at all viewings, tenant vetting and documentation, a signed lease, meter readings and utility transfer help, collection of the deposit and first month's rent, and lodgement to your bank account, with a financial statement and an end-of-year summary for your accountant.

What does full property management cover?

The full management service suits investors and absentee landlords. DPM acts as the landlord's representative with tenants, collects the monthly rent and lodges it to your account, organises its maintenance team for repairs, carries out property inspections, handles lease renewal ahead of expiry and the inspection before the deposit is refunded.

Maintenance and facility services

DPM runs Property Maintenance and Facility Services alongside sales and lettings. The agency can also arrange cleaning and clearance, and repairs, maintenance and painting before a sale.

Do you handle commercial property?

DPM handles residential and commercial property, including commercial units and office space, and offers Commercial Valuations.

Probate and Fair Deal valuations

DPM offers Probate valuations and Fair Deal valuations, in addition to residential and commercial valuations. Call 01-8362954 to discuss your situation.

Which areas do you cover?

DPM covers all of Dublin and surrounding areas, including Dublin city and North County Dublin, with Ashbourne, Ratoath, Dunboyne and Celbridge mentioned. Its listings range from Finglas, Glasnevin and Drumcondra to Ballyfermot and Lucan.

Buying and viewings

Current listings for sale, for rent, on view and recently reduced are on the Properties pages of the website, searchable by beds, baths, size and property type. To arrange a viewing, call 01-8362954 or send your details.

Where is the office?

How do I contact DPM?

Where does DPM's business come from?

About 90% of DPM's business comes from referrals, which the team puts down to its professional approach and personal service for new and existing clients.
