Pink slip / eSafety

Pink slip, step by step

Most light vehicles older than five years need an eSafety inspection before they can be registered in NSW. Your registration renewal paperwork says whether one is required.

1. Inspection

Your car goes through safety checks. If it passes you get a pink slip, which shows the car is roadworthy.

2. 42 days

A pink slip is valid for 42 days, and you must renew your registration within that period.

3. Failed?

The workshop also does mechanical repairs to fix what was found. A follow-up inspection is needed after repairs, with no charge if you return to the same eSafety station that first failed the vehicle.

Cost and mobile option

The team quotes the pink slip cost by phone or e-mail once you tell them the make and model. Short on time? The mobile pink slip mechanic comes to you and hands over the certificate the same day.
