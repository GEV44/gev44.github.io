Their FAQ

Quick answers

Do you do mobile battery and repair service in Dubai?

Yes, 24/7 mobile service anywhere in Dubai. The technician comes to you for battery replacement, inspections, A/C repair and other services.

How long does a battery replacement take?

Most take 10 to 15 minutes once the technician arrives, plus a quick system check.

Which battery brands do you use?

High-quality, trusted brands suitable for all car types, including Japanese, American, European and luxury vehicles.

What do the services cost?

The site calls the pricing transparent and affordable but does not list figures. Ask for a free quote when you call.

Is there a warranty?

The About page says all services come with a warranty. The terms are not published, so ask the team what is covered.

What if my area is not in your list?

The list on the site says "some of the areas we cover", and the text promises service anywhere in Dubai. Other emirates are not mentioned, so ask before you count on it.

Do you fix flat tyres at the roadside?

The site mentions flat tyres under car repair at home. The details of what they can do on the spot are best checked by phone.

Can you replace a car key battery?

Yes, car key battery replacement is on their battery page.
