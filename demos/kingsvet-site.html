Good to know

Quick answers

Is there an emergency service?

Yes. Kings Vet advertises 24/7 emergency pet care. In an emergency, call the clinic straight away.

Can a vet come to my home?

Yes, home consultations are offered, as well as emergency home visits.

I cannot bring my pet in. What can I do?

The Pet Taxi picks your pet up and drops them back at your doorstep.

Which pets do you see?

Dogs, cats and rabbits are listed on the booking form, and the clinic also cares for exotic pets. Call to confirm your species.

How much does a visit cost?

Prices are not listed on the website. The clinic says it offers affordable care, and the team can quote you on request.

Let's get your pet seen

Tell the assistant which clinic, your pet and the day you prefer, and the request goes to the team.
