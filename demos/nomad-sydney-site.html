About us

NOMAD Sydney sources exceptional produce from local growers, with food inspired by travels through Spain, Morocco and the Middle East and cooked with fire and smoke. The wine list focuses on small growers making great wine sustainably, sourced worldwide.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What are your opening hours?

Lunch Wednesday to Sunday 12pm to 2:30pm, and dinner 7 nights a week: Sunday to Friday from 5:30pm and Saturday from 5:00pm.

How many guests can I book online?

NOMAD Sydney accepts online reservations for 1 to 40 guests.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Most allergies and dietary requirements can be accommodated with advance notice. Vegan options are available.

Do you have private dining?

Yes, the Wine Cave seats up to 24 on one table, and exclusive hire of the venue seats up to 120.

Are there surcharges?

A 10% surcharge applies on Sundays and 15% on Public Holidays.
