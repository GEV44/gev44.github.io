FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you take dental insurance?

We welcome PPO dental insurance and help you maximize your benefits. Please call the office to verify your specific benefits before your visit.

How do I get emergency dental care?

Call us. We offer same-day appointments for cracked teeth, severe toothaches and similar problems. For life-threatening symptoms call 911 or go to the ER.

What if I can't afford a dentist?

We offer dental insurance, special offers, an in-house savings plan, and CareCredit and Cherry financing.

Do you have a female dentist?

Yes. Dr. Ferreira graduated at the top of her class from the Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health.

What does the in-house savings plan include?

Two adult cleanings, two exams and one set of X-rays a year, plus discounts on a variety of services.

Where are you located?

702 E Bell Rd #118, Phoenix, AZ 85022, across the street from Moon Valley Towne Center and Fountain Square.
