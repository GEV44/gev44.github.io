What a visit looks like

Your pet is examined by a veterinarian, who discusses any concerns or changes you have noticed and may recommend diagnostic tests or treatment options. Depending on the reason for the visit, your pet may receive vaccinations, medication or other care, and you can ask about follow-up.

Not sure whether it is urgent? Sudden collapse, difficulty breathing, trauma, severe vomiting or diarrhea, or a suspected toxic substance are the kinds of situations the clinic describes as emergencies. Call 050 440 2217 and the team will guide you. The assistant on this page never diagnoses.

Quick answers

What are the opening hours in Dubai?

Monday to Saturday 8:00 am-10:00 pm and Sunday 9:00 am-9:00 pm.

Do you see exotic pets?

Yes, exotic animal exams are on the services list. Mention the species when you book.

Can a vet come to my home?

Yes. Veterinary home visits are offered in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Can I get food or medication delivered?

Yes, veterinary food, prescription medications and supplements are delivered to your door.

What if my pet needs urgent help?

Call the Dubai emergency line, 050 440 2217. The assistant on this page cannot diagnose and will always send medical questions to the staff.

How often should my pet have a dental exam?

The dental page recommends a dental examination once a year, more often if your pet is prone to dental issues.

What does grooming include?

Shampooing, nail trim, ear cleaning, anal gland extraction, blow-drying and brushing, plus haircuts, flea control treatments and medical shampoos.

Which tests does cardiology include?

Echocardiography, ECG, chest X-rays and cardiac biomarker testing, led by Dr. Khalid Gouda.

What happens on a telephone consultation?

You discuss your pet's symptoms, behavior or health concerns with a qualified vet, who gives professional guidance and recommends treatment or medication. Emergencies should go to the emergency line instead.

What does the home visit include?

A general health examination, basic diagnostic evaluation, vaccinations and medication administration.

Does Pure Life look after pets overnight?

Pet handlers care for pets staying at the clinic, including feeding, walking, cleaning and monitoring. Ask the team about your pet's needs.

Is there a clinic in Abu Dhabi too?

Yes. The group has a main branch and a second branch on Defense Road, Al Nahyan Camp: 02 444 3002 and 02 642 1999.
