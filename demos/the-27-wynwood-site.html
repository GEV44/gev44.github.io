Good to know

Questions guests ask

What makes The 27 Wynwood different?

Apartment-style accommodations with design-forward hospitality: full kitchens, private balconies, rooftop amenities, co-working spaces and Italian dining at Casa Tua Cucina.

What is included with my stay?

High-speed Wi-Fi, daily housekeeping, the rooftop pool, fitness center, co-working spaces, library lounge, concierge services and other amenities included through the nightly service fee.

What types of suites are there?

Studios, one-bedroom suites and two-bedroom suites, all with full kitchens, dining areas and spa-style bathrooms.

Check-in and check-out times?

Check-in starts at 4:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available depending on availability; additional fees may apply for late departures.

Where exactly is the hotel?

259 NW 27th St, Miami, FL 33127, in the heart of the Wynwood Arts District.

Does the hotel have a restaurant?

Yes. Casa Tua Cucina is on the ground floor, with fresh pasta, pizzas, grilled specialties, crudos, charcuterie, desserts, Italian coffees, wines and craft cocktails.

Can I host a meeting or celebration here?

There are six event spaces: Speakeasy, Meeting Room, Sun Deck, Parlor Area, Private Lounge and, soon, the NoMad Rooftop Bar. Events and sales: sales@the27wynwood.com.

Are there discounts for booking direct or ahead of time?

Yes: book direct, advance purchase, Flex Early Booker, AAA/CAA, Senior, Stay More Save More and a Florida Resident Special are listed on the hotel's offers page.

Something not listed here (parking, pets, cancellation, airport)?

The hotel site does not state these, so this page will not guess. The team confirms them: +1 305-676-4958 or reservations@the27wynwood.com.
