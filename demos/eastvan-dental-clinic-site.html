First-time patient?

Glad to have you on board

Here is what to expect on your first visit, so it is as comfortable and efficient as possible. The team contacts you within the next business day after an online request.

  1. Arrive about 15 minutes early to complete the paperwork. Bring valid ID and your insurance card if you have one.
  2. Fill out the new patient registration forms: contact details, treatment consent, insurance and payment information.
  3. A staff member brings you to the consultation room, where the dentist discusses your history, concerns and goals.
  4. A comprehensive exam, which may include a visual inspection of teeth and gums and X-rays if necessary.
  5. A hygienist may clean your teeth and give oral hygiene instructions, and the dentist builds a personalised treatment plan.
  6. Book any follow-up care; check-ups are usually every six months.
  7. At the front desk you handle payment or insurance claims, and make sure you understand costs and coverage before leaving.
While you call us

Tips for common dental emergencies

Knocked-out adult tooth

Keep it moist at all times. If you can, place it back in the socket without touching the root, and get to EastVan Dental right away.

Cracked tooth

Rinse your mouth with warm water and put cold compresses on the face to keep swelling down. If you bite your tongue or lip, clean gently and apply a cold compress.

Toothache

Rinse with warm water and gently floss out trapped food. Do not put aspirin on the tooth or gum, and do not use sharp instruments to remove stuck objects.

First aid tips from the clinic, not a diagnosis. Call (604) 251-4000; walk-ins are accepted.
