Not sure which one?

Match your situation to a program. Everything here comes from the school's course pages; call the school to confirm.

I am 14 to 17 and want a classroom feel

Choose the online course with instructor-led lessons: 32 hours online, then 7 hours driving and 7 observation at a Houston center.

My parent can teach me

The parent taught course gives you the 32-hour online class, printable instructor guides and an online driver log. The school suggests it when the parent can be approved by TDLR.

I am 18 or older with no permit

Take the 6-hour course online or in person on a Saturday, then add 2-hour lessons. Ages 18 to 25 must take the class by law.

I only need the road test

Book the DPS authorized test at the Hillcroft center. Add preparation if you want an instructor to refresh your memory first.

Quick answers

The assistant in the corner answers these too.

Do I need a permit before driving lessons?

Yes. Lessons are scheduled after the first 6 hours of the course and getting the permit from the DPS. Lessons need a valid instructional permit or a valid driver license.

What do teens need to bring?

An original birth certificate, state ID, current US passport or naturalization certificate; the original Social Security card; and a Verification of Enrollment (VOE) from the high school.

Can I use my own car for the road test?

Yes. You can drive your own vehicle, or choose an HDS training vehicle.

Is the adult permit class required?

It is required by law for ages 18 to 25 and recommended for ages 26 and over. The online 6-hour course is also offered.

How fast do I get my certificate?

The school delivers certificates almost instantly after you finish the online course. A lost or stolen certificate can be replaced at the local office.

Where are the lessons held?

At the two Houston centers, Hillcroft and Chimney Rock. Call to schedule and check availability.

What if I need more driving practice?

Teens can add extra behind-the-wheel hours at $57 per hour, with gas and the instructor included. Adults can book 2-hour lessons. Call to schedule and check availability.

Do you offer Spanish?

The school says Se habla espanol and offers Spanish translation on the online adult course.
