About us

Established in 1932, Harcourt Dental is one of Dublin's premier dental practices. A multi-disciplinary team provides day-to-day care as well as complex treatment including periodontics, prosthodontics, orthodontics, endodontics and oral surgery.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, by appointment only.

How much is a dental exam?

A dental examination is €75. A PRSI examination with a general dentist is free.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, the team is happy to discuss payment plans.

Where do I park?

Street parking is limited; the Q-Park Stephen's Green car park is nearby, and the Harcourt Luas stop is close.

What if I have a broken tooth?

Call 01 475 1313 and the team will help arrange suitable treatment.
