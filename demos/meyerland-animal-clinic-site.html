What we do

Care for dogs and cats, under one roof

From a regular wellness visit to surgery and imaging, with boarding when you travel. Questions about your own pet always go to the doctors.

1

Pet wellness

Thorough exams, vaccines, flea, tick, heartworm and parasite prevention, lab work for older pets, nutrition and exercise advice, and behavioral counseling.

2

Advanced surgery

Advanced dentistry, soft tissue, gastrointestinal and reproductive surgery and oncologic tumor removal. Every patient gets a pre-operative exam and diagnostic testing.

3

Advanced imaging

An in-house digital x-ray unit with immediate images that can be shared by email, and ultrasound for real-time views of internal organs.

4

Diagnostics

Diagnostic testing to catch serious issues early and start treatment as quickly as possible.

5

Emergency medicine

When minutes matter, call 713-723-8612 right away. The clinic is available 24/7 through its after-hours emergency team powered by Guardian Vets.

6

Pet boarding

Cat condominiums and dog play areas with veterinary oversight, in a clinic where the doctors are on site.

Quick answers

Before you call

Are you open on Sundays?

The clinic is closed on Sundays. For an emergency call 713-723-8612; the after-hours emergency team is powered by Guardian Vets.

Do I need to register first?

The site has a New Client Registration Form, along with a Boarding Agreement, an Anesthesia/Procedure Consent form and a CPR form.

How do I book boarding?

Call 713-723-8612 to request a tour and a stay. Make sure your pet is vaccinated, and tell the team about any special diet or medication beforehand.

What payment options are there?

Cash, check, Discover, MasterCard and Visa, plus Cherry payment plans and CareCredit. Payment is due at the time of service.

Do you have imaging in the clinic?

Yes, an in-house digital x-ray unit and ultrasound. X-ray images appear immediately and can be shared by email.

Do you treat cats?

Yes. Meyerland is a Cat Friendly practice, and cats have their own boarding condominiums.

Can I get my pet's prescriptions here?

Yes, and the clinic says they are often cheaper than at online pharmacies. It can also mail them.
