Good to knowQuestions we hear a lot

My pet has an emergency. What should I do?

Call (604) 221-5858 during open hours. The team triages urgent and same-day cases as they arrive and reserves capacity for them every open day. Outside hours, contact a 24-hour veterinary facility in Vancouver; reception can give a referral when you call during hours.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

The team tries to accommodate walk-ins during open hours, particularly urgent ones, but scheduled appointments are seen first. Calling ahead is recommended.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Previous veterinary records including vaccination history and bloodwork, a list of anything your pet takes (supplements too), and notes on your concerns. Cats travel best in a hard-sided carrier.

How often should my pet have a wellness exam?

Yearly for healthy adults, twice a year for pets aged 7 and over, and several visits in the first year for puppies and kittens.

Do you take pet insurance?

Alta Vista is a Trupanion direct-billing hospital. For other Canadian insurers the team provides itemized invoices. Financing through iFinance and ScratchPay is also accepted.

Is dental cleaning under sedation safe?

The site calls general sedation the safest way to clean all tooth surfaces, including below the gumline. The team offers pre-procedure bloodwork, monitors vital signs continuously and tailors the sedation protocol to your pet's age and health.

When should my dog be spayed or neutered?

It depends on breed and expected adult size: small and medium breeds are typically 6 to 9 months, large and giant breeds may benefit from waiting until 12 to 18 months. The vet advises after an exam.

Do you see rabbits or birds?

No. Alta Vista cares for dogs and cats only.
