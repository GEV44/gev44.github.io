Quick answers

Do you only work in Phoenix?

The search covers the Phoenix area, with featured pages for Ahwatukee, Tempe, Chandler, Gilbert, Mesa, Queen Creek, Sun Lakes, Maricopa, Arcadia, Scottsdale and Paradise Valley.

Can you help if the owner lives out of state?

Yes. The probate program includes a field service manager who visits the property and sends condition reports, and there is a relocation department connected with companies like Altair and BrookField.

What if I'm not happy with the listing?

For probate listings, the Easy Exit Listing Program lets you cancel with 24-hour written notice.

How do I find out what my home is worth?

Use the free Home Valuation on the R·House site, or ask the team for a follow-up. Fee and commission details are not published, so the team answers those directly.

Do I need to be pre-approved before I start looking?

The buying guide recommends it. Qualification is only a guess based on what you tell a lender, while a pre-approval gives a better idea of how big a loan you qualify for.

Can I save homes and searches?

Yes, sign up on the R·House site to keep saved searches and favorite properties.

Do you handle estate sales and clean-outs?

The probate page lists estate sale and clean-out coordination, plus a concierge team member for handyman work, cleaning and remodeling.

What are your hours?

Office hours aren't published on the website. Call 480.576.4404 and the team will tell you.
