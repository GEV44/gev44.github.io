Good to Know

What is the cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice. Less than 24 hours’ notice, or a no-show, is charged 50% of the reserved service and must be paid before booking again. A card is kept on file at booking for this. Reminder texts go out 24 to 48 hours before your appointment.

What if I am running late?

Please arrive on time to receive the full service. If you are running late, the team will do everything it can to accommodate you, but may have to reschedule.

Do I need a consultation for extensions?

Yes. Extensions add fullness, dimension and length, and a consultation is required to find the best option for you.

Is a haircut included with color?

No. Haircut and blow-dry services are not included with color and chemical services. Add your preferred finishing service after you choose the main service.

How does pricing work?

It varies with your stylist’s experience, demand, the time needed and your hair goals. For color, Arcane uses SalonScale, which charges for the product used plus labor, so costs are laid out clearly.

Which products do you use?

Eufora, CULT+KING, Pulp Riot, Wella Professional and Siphon Draw Apothecary skincare.
