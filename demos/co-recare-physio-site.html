Questions

Before you book

Do I need a referral for dry needling?

The clinic says that in the UAE you typically do not need a doctor's referral to start a dry needling session with them. It is performed by certified physiotherapists.

What is the McKenzie Method?

A system for assessing and treating spinal conditions, often reducing the need for extensive imaging. The clinic uses it for chronic back pain, sciatica and neck stiffness.

What is the difference between manual therapy and exercise?

Manual therapy is hands-on treatment by a licensed physiotherapist to reduce pain and improve range of motion. Therapeutic exercise is customized, guided movement to restore function and prevent recurrence. The clinic combines both.

Can you help after surgery?

Yes. Post-operative programs cover joint replacements and sports injuries such as ACL and rotator cuff, with a focus on functional recovery and a safe return to activity.

How does the clinic approach chronic neck pain?

It starts with a thorough assessment. Treatment typically combines manual therapy to relieve stiffness, specific therapeutic exercises to strengthen supporting muscles and, potentially, dry needling for relief, tailored to your lifestyle.

How is chronic pain managed?

With comprehensive plans that include manual therapy, therapeutic exercises and patient education, to help you manage and reduce pain.

What types of manual therapy are offered?

Joint mobilization, soft tissue manipulation and myofascial release, to help ease pain and improve mobility.

Do you treat sports injuries?

Yes, sports injury rehabilitation is a specialty, with treatment tailored to the injury to promote recovery and help prevent future injuries.

Why use moist heat, electrotherapy and ultrasound?

The clinic uses a moisture heat pack from South Korea to relax tight muscles and improve circulation, electrotherapy from Zimmer, Germany to ease pain and support tissue healing, and Zimmer ultrasound therapy to stimulate deep tissues and reduce pain. They are chosen for each personalized plan.

Is dry needling safe?

The clinic describes it as a safe, evidence-based technique performed by certified physiotherapists to target myofascial trigger points, used when appropriate for your case.
