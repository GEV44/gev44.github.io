Good to know

Common questions

What happens at my first appointment?

Your dentist discusses your needs and concerns, you can ask questions, and you receive a personalised treatment plan, printed or by email.

How often should I visit?

At least twice a year for a dentist and a hygienist. Your dentist will recommend what suits you.

How long does Invisalign® or braces take?

Most patients wear them for 6-18 months. You get a clear estimate at the consultation.

Do you offer emergency care?

Yes. Same-day emergency appointments whenever possible. Call the practice and the team will do their best to see you quickly.

What if I am not happy with the result?

The practice has a satisfaction guarantee and will work with you to make it right.
