Example: Dhow Cruise Dinner Creek

The details guests ask about first

A 2 h 30 min dinner on a traditional dhow along Dubai Creek between Bur Dubai and Deira. Welcome drink, international buffet with BBQ items, open-air upper deck and air-conditioned lower deck.

  • Departs 8:30 pm. Without transfer, report 30 minutes before departure.
  • Departure point: Al Seef Road Marsa, Al Seef Walk, Bur Dubai, opposite the British Embassy.
  • Hotel pick-up from Dubai hotels or residences only, not the airport.
Policies on that page
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure, then 100% charge.
  • Refunds, if eligible, return within 7 working days.
  • Under 3 free, 3 to 10 child rate, over 10 adult rate.
  • In Ramadan or on dry days: no live entertainment or alcohol.

Quick answers

Before you ask the team

Where is the office?

Shop no. 6, Al Quoz Mall, Al Quoz Industrial Area 3, P.O. Box 251352, Dubai, UAE.

How do I reach you?

Phone or WhatsApp +971 4 347 0227, or info@eurotravel.ae.

What are your opening hours?

The website does not list them. The team will confirm by phone or WhatsApp.

Do you handle visas?

Yes, UAE tourist visas and worldwide visa assistance. Processing times depend on the case, so the visa team confirms.

How can I pay?

By card through a payment link from the pay-online form, through the back-office call centre if you have no booking yet, or in cash at the Al Quoz office.

Is hotel pick-up included?

Where a tour offers it, you choose sharing or private transfer when booking. On the Dhow Cruise page, pick-up is from Dubai hotels or residences only, not the airport, and timing can move 30 to 60 minutes with traffic.

What if I cancel?

The Dhow Cruise page allows free cancellation until 24 hours before departure and charges 100% after that. Unused inclusions are non-refundable. Other tours can differ, so the team confirms.

Can children join the tours?

Each tour page states its child policy. The Dhow Cruise page treats under 3 as infants (free), 3 to 10 as children and over 10 as adults.
