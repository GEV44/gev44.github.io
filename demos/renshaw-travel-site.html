Seventy-odd years, three generations

From a young chauffeur arriving in Vancouver to the family agency on West 4th Avenue.

1948Renshaw Travel is established.
1954Don Renshaw founds Cavalier Tours, the first wholesale travel company in Western Canada.
1974He reopens in Pacific Centre with his own name on the door.
1980sThe agency moves to 4th Avenue, then a less glamorous street, and builds a west-coast glass office.
1993Don Jr. buys the agency from his father.
1997 and 1998A cruise department opens, and Renshaw is invited to join Virtuoso.
TodayThird-generation family members work as advisors alongside the team.

Good to know before you call

What is Virtuoso?

A luxury travel network of more than 1000 hotels, cruise lines, tour operators and travel agents. Booking through a member agency brings upgraded accommodation, extra perks and VIP recognition.

Can you arrange more than the cruise or hotel itself?

Yes. For cruises the team lists flights, land transfers and pre and post accommodation among the things it looks after.

Can I come in and meet someone?

The office is at 2175 West 4th Avenue in Kitsilano. The agency says its owner is open to meeting clients personally, so call ahead to arrange a time.

What are your opening hours?

They are not published on the website. Call 604 733 1010 and the team will confirm.

How do I choose an advisor?

Filter the directory above by interest, or send a trip request and the team routes it to the right specialist.
