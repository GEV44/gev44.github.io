About your teacher

Oceane De Decker

Born in France and a dynamic traveller of the world, Oceane has been sharing her love of French culture since 2016. She accepts any client with a passion for learning French, from children to adults and from beginners to advanced.

She has been teaching AP French students with a 100 percent success rate, and she also prepares students for the TEF and DELF. Since becoming a DELF examiner in June 2021 she knows exactly what you need to study and how to practise.

Her philosophy: learning French is like going on a diet. You can do it alone, but it is far easier with someone with you every step of the way. You will learn French not as books describe it, but the way we really speak it, with slang and plenty of expressions.

Besides French, she gives Spanish and English classes and does translation from French to English and English to French. Her marketing background also keeps her busy with content writing, web design and community management. She has lived on the West Coast since 2016, travelling across the States and Canada.

Questions

Quick answers

Is everything online?

Yes. Tutoring and classes are 100% online, so you can join from your living room.

Do you accept adults only?

No. Oceane has taught clients of all ages, from children to adults, and accepts anyone with a passion for French.

I have never spoken French. Is that fine?

Yes. Every level is welcome, from beginner to advanced.

Do you teach children?

Yes, Oceane has taught clients of all ages, from children to adults.

Do you offer in-person courses?

The site shows an intensive in-person course in Lille in April and May 2024, with morning classes and afternoon activities. That edition is past, so ask whether a new one is planned.

Which tools do we use for online classes?

Skype or Zoom are the favourite tools, and if you would like to use another medium that is not a problem.

What if I miss a class?

Reschedule or cancel at least 48 hours before the session. With less than 48 hours notice the class is completely due, though exceptions can apply and need to be discussed first.

Can my company book classes or an event?

Yes. Companies can hire a French teacher, or ask for a French event for employees. Email contact@havefunlearningfrench.com.

Do you only teach French?

French is the main subject, but Oceane also teaches Spanish and English, and translates between French and English.

What is the one rule in the workshops?

Speak French, and only French, for the entire time. Beginners are welcome.

What languages do you teach?

French mainly, plus Spanish and English classes, and translation between French and English.
