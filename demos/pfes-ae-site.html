Where we started

Prime Focus Educational Services was founded in 2015 with a simple vision: personalised, high-quality education for students of all ages. We grew from a passion for making learning accessible, engaging and effective into a team of dedicated tutors who work one-to-one with students, helping them reach their goals and unlock their potential.

Mission and vision

Our mission is to deliver tailored, results-driven programmes in languages, school subjects, IT and music, in a supportive environment that inspires growth. Our vision is to be the leading provider of personalised, high-quality educational services, empowering people of all ages to thrive in a world of continuous learning.

Good to know

Questions and policies

Can we meet the tutor before paying?

Yes. Step two is a free 30-minute consultation with the proposed tutor, in person or online. You approve the tutor, then choose a lesson package.

Can I choose a specific tutor?

You can request one. PFES makes the final tutor assignment based on the learner's needs, preferences and availability.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule a lesson?

Please give PFES or the tutor at least 24 hours' notice. Without it the session may be charged in full; changes inside the 24 hours depend on approval and availability.

What is the refund policy?

A learner who stops within 2 weeks of booking is refunded the remaining unused hours, with the original receipt. After 2 weeks a credit note is issued for any future PFES course. If a learner misses classes for 3 continuous months without notice, the contract ends without refund. Promotional or discounted rates are handled under the full policy on the site.

Is my information kept private?

Information shared between PFES, clients and tutors stays confidential and is used only for educational purposes. It is not sold or shared with third parties without consent.

Do you work with schools and companies?

Yes: school support, staffing, training, and customised corporate language and professional development courses.
