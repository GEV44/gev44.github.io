Quick answers

Before you call

Do you come to me?

For battery replacement, yes: the website says the service is done at your location. For other jobs, ask the team when you call.

Are you really open all night?

Their website and contact page both say 24 hours, 7 days.

What does a repair cost?

No prices are published on the website. The team gives you an estimate after hearing what the car needs.

Can I just send the car details first?

Yes. Use the assistant button: it asks for the service, the car, your location, timing and phone, then passes it on.

Which jobs can be done without towing the car?

Battery replacement is the one the website says is done at your location. For anything else, describe the problem on the phone and the team will tell you whether the car needs to come to Al Satwa.

Which languages does the team speak?

The website does not say. Call or send a WhatsApp message and see; the assistant here only answers in English.

Do you only work on one kind of car?

The website lists services, not brands or vehicle types. Mention your car when you call so the team can confirm.

Do you fix accident damage?

The site says they repair accident damage and lists body denting and painting.
