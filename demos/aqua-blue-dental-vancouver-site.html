Canadian Dental Care Plan

Using CDCP at Aqua Blue

The CDCP is a federal program offering partial coverage for essential dental services to Canadians without private dental insurance. It is not free for everyone, and coverage depends on income.

Who can use it

  • Annual household income under $90,000
  • No existing private or public dental insurance
  • Eligibility is rolling out in stages by age group and category

What it covers

  • Preventive: cleanings, fluoride, sealants
  • Diagnostic: exams and X-rays
  • Restorative: fillings, crowns, root canals
  • Emergency: pain relief, extractions, infection management

How the team helps

  • Verifies your eligibility
  • Guides the application
  • Handles paperwork and claims
  • Some procedures may need pre-approval and co-payments may apply
Dental emergencies

If something happens

Same-day care

Fast-track appointments for patients in pain: severe toothaches, abscesses and knocked-out teeth.

Walk-ins welcome

No appointment? The clinic offers walk-in dental care in Vancouver.

Knocked-out tooth

Handle it by the crown, not the root. Rinse gently, and if you can, place it back in the socket. If not, keep it between cheek and gum, or in cold milk for young children. Within one to two hours gives the best chance.

Aftercare

After the emergency is dealt with, you get guidance to support healing and reduce complications.
