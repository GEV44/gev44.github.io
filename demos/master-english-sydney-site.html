Questions

Clear answers before you choose

Is the English level assessment free?

Yes. It is currently free and takes about 15 to 20 minutes. You can meet by Microsoft Teams, phone or at the Sydney classroom.

Can I study in a Sydney classroom or online?

IELTS is available in the classroom and online. PTE private lessons are in the classroom or online, while PTE group classes are online only for now. Contact the team for current CELPIP options.

How large are the group classes?

Groups are small, typically three to six students, so you get more time to ask questions and receive feedback.

Can I pay weekly?

Yes, weekly payments are available for group programs. The team explains the schedule and payment dates before enrolment.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, card through Stripe, and PayPal. Bank-transfer enrolments are confirmed by the team.

When can I start?

Group programs generally start on a Monday. Availability can change, so contact the team to confirm the next start date.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

The team reviews your test, target score and contact details, then replies the way you prefer. You can choose a free assessment time before deciding to enrol.

Not sure which test fits?

Ask the assistant, or request a free assessment and a teacher will help you compare IELTS, PTE and CELPIP.
