From first lesson to licence

Five steps to test day

Most learners need around 10 to 20 lessons, depending on confidence and previous practice. Your instructor builds the plan around you.

  1. Book

    Call, message on WhatsApp or use the form. Pick a time that suits your week.

  2. Get picked up

    Pick-up and drop-off can be arranged, so you start the lesson relaxed.

  3. Learn the basics

    Patient one-on-one coaching in an automatic, dual-control car.

  4. Practise routes

    Real route practice and mock driving tests for the VicRoads test.

  5. Pass

    Test-focused training so you walk in prepared and confident.

Inside a lesson

Safe, confident, test-ready

Every lesson is structured to build road awareness and the confidence to drive in all traffic conditions. Your instructor explains the do's and don'ts and corrects habits as they appear.

  • 1
    Road safetyThe habits that keep you and others safe, from blind spots to road rules in Victoria.

    Parking skillsPractice with the manoeuvres that tend to come up on test day.

Quick answers

Questions learners ask

How many lessons do I need before the test?

Most learners need 10 to 20 lessons, depending on confidence, previous practice and skill level. Your instructor makes a personal plan.

Are the lessons in automatic cars?

Yes. Lessons are in modern automatic vehicles with safety features and dual controls.

Can I book evenings or weekends?

Yes. Flexible times include evenings and weekends.

Do you help with the VicRoads driving test?

Yes: test-focused training, mock driving tests and real route practice.

Do you offer refresher lessons?

Yes, for licensed drivers who want more confidence or need to get used to busy Melbourne traffic.

How do I book?

Call 0434 520 543 or contact the school through the website. The team will help you choose a time and a package.
