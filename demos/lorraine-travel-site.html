Our story

From Havana, 1948, to the world.

John and Luisa Guiteras opened Lorraine Travel in Havana in 1948, when air travel was still a rite of passage. In 1960 the family carried the firm and its standard of care to Coral Gables, Florida, where it grew into one of the largest privately owned travel companies in the state.

Few travel houses survive nearly eighty years. Lorraine Travel did, by doing it the old way: by hand, by relationship, by name.

Considering a travel advisor?

Frequently asked

Do travel advisors cost more?

In most cases, no. Luxury suppliers pay the advisor, not the traveler. For complex bespoke itineraries a modest planning fee is quoted in advance.

What happens during a flight cancellation or missed connection?

You call your senior advisor. Advisors have direct supplier lines and rebooking authority, and are reachable 24/7 during active travel.

Which cruise lines do you book?

Across the industry, from Royal Caribbean and Norwegian to Explora Journeys, Ponant, Lindblad, Quark and Uniworld River Cruises.

Can you plan honeymoons and milestone trips?

Yes. Honeymoons, anniversaries, milestone birthdays, family reunions and bucket-list journeys are all planned by hand.

Where are you located?

377 Alhambra Circle, Coral Gables, Florida 33134, serving clients across the United States and worldwide by phone, email and text.

Is Lorraine Travel a Four Seasons Preferred Partner?

Yes. Clients receive the Preferred-Partner amenity package at every Four Seasons hotel and resort worldwide.

Why book a cruise through an advisor?

Pricing is identical to the cruise line's, but advisors layer on onboard credit, prepaid gratuities, group rates applied to individual passengers and complimentary shore excursions on select sailings.

What does an advisor actually do for me?

Listens to what you want, designs a tailored itinerary, books air, hotels, cruises, transfers and guides, secures preferred-partner perks, and manages rebookings and supplier mistakes in real time.

How do advisors get paid if I am not paying more?

Hotels, cruise lines and tour operators pay a commission for booking with them, at no additional cost to you. The rate is the same as, or better than, booking direct.

Can I reach someone when I am traveling?

Yes. During active travel your senior advisor is reachable by phone, text and email around the clock, and an after-hours emergency service covers clients in transit anywhere.
