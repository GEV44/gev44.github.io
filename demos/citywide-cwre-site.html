Common questions

How does the "no sale no fee" basis work?

Citywide only charges when a property sells, and the complete marketing package is included in the overall sale fee. The team will confirm the fee for your property.

Can you value a commercial property?

Yes. Citywide handles both residential and commercial property, including investment properties.

Do you arrange viewings in the evenings or at weekends?

Yes, flexible viewing times are part of the service, with minimum disruption to the owner.

I'm already letting my property. Can you take over?

Yes. Citywide can take on management mid-tenancy, and also offers letting only.

How do I report a maintenance problem?

Use the Maintenance link in the menu of the Citywide website, or call the office on (01) 902 0777.

Who can recommend a solicitor or surveyor?

Citywide can personally recommend surveyors, solicitors, BER assessors, tradesmen and maintenance professionals.

Which areas do you cover?

Dublin 1, Dublin 7, Dublin 15 and Dublin City Centre from the Stoneybatter office, plus the Blackrock office in Co. Dublin.

Is Citywide independent?

Yes. It describes itself as an independent, multi-discipline estate agency, established in 1994 as Citywide Auctioneers.

What does the online bidding platform do?

It is part of the marketing package for sales, and the Online Bidding link in the website menu opens it. The team can explain how it works for a given property.

Who regulates Citywide?

The Property Services Regulatory Authority (PSRA licence no. 001485). The agency is also a member of the Institute of Professional Auctioneers & Valuers (IPAV).
