Good to know

Booking policy

The essentials from the clinic's policy page. Pre and post-care instructions are sent in your booking confirmation e-mail.

Request an appointment

Deposit or card on file

Every appointment needs a credit card on file or a $50 deposit that goes toward your appointment. If you book online through the Yocale system and your card is on file, no deposit is needed.

Rescheduling

48 hours notice is required to reschedule, otherwise the deposit is forfeited. A no-show without notice adds a $50 fee to the card on file.

Arriving

New clients, including makeup clients, should arrive 10 minutes early to fill out forms. More than 20 to 30 minutes late means an extra $50 charge or a cancelled appointment.

Guests and pets

No pets, because of cross-contamination. Please limit to one guest, and ideally come without one.

Results

Results vary by person depending on skin, lifestyle, medications and aftercare. Following the care instructions is the client's responsibility.

Financing

Spread the cost

Skin District partners with Beautifi for cosmetic procedure financing: loans from $1,000 to $25,000+, terms from 6 months to 6 years, an instant decision online, and applying does not affect your credit score. Gift cards are also available.
