Teaching languages on the banks of Dubai Creek

The Polyglot Language Institute was established overlooking Dubai Creek as the first educational institute in the city. It specialises in English and Arabic for non-native speakers, with computer courses alongside, and its courses are accredited by the KHDA and the UAE Ministry of Education.

Over the years its teaching has served school students, visitors, residents of Dubai, businessmen, and companies and institutions of all kinds.

A clear ladder of 16 English levels

Each level is a one-month course of 30 hours. Three levels make one international level (A1, A2, B1 and so on) of 90 hours over three months.

Beginner, Elementary, Pre-intermediate, Intermediate, Upper-intermediate, Advanced and beyond: 16 levels in all. The certificate for an international level is awarded when you complete all 90 hours of that level.

Questions we hear most

Can't find yours? Call +971 4 222 3429.

How much do the courses cost?

Fees depend on the language, the level and whether you study in a group or one-to-one. Call +971 4 222 3429 for the current fees and any offer running now.

How long is a course?

English has 16 levels, each a one-month course of 30 hours; three levels make one international level of 90 hours. Each Spoken Arabic level runs for one month (20 hours) or three months (60 hours).

When does the next group start?

Groups start once they are full and according to the approved schedule. Contact us for the nearest start date for your level.

What class times are available?

Morning and evening groups are arranged within our opening hours, Saturday to Thursday from 10 am to 9 pm, depending on demand and the availability of teachers and classrooms.

Is there a placement test?

Yes. Every student takes a placement test before registering. Ask us whether the test is free under the current offer.

Do you offer private or online lessons?

Private one-to-one lessons are available. Classes are held in person at the institute in Deira; ask us whether an online option exists for the course you want.

Do you provide a certificate?

A certificate of completion can be issued according to the programme's attendance and pass requirements. In English the certificate for an international level is awarded when you complete all 90 hours. Please confirm the type of certificate before you register.

Is the institute accredited?

Yes. It was the first institute licensed by Dubai's KHDA and the UAE Ministry of Education, and its courses are accredited by both.

How do I register?

Call +971 4 222 3429, message us on WhatsApp, email info@polyglotdubai.com or visit the institute. We will assess your level and recommend the right course and time.
