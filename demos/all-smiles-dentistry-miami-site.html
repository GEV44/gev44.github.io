Questions

Good to know

What should I expect at my first visit?

Dr. Gonzalez conducts a comprehensive oral examination, with X-rays if necessary, discusses your dental history and concerns, and creates a personalized treatment plan.

How often should I get a check-up?

Every six months is recommended; Dr. Gonzalez may suggest more frequent visits depending on your needs.

Do you accept my insurance?

All Smiles is an out-of-network provider but accepts most insurances, and the team handles claim filing, verification and paperwork. Call to verify your specific coverage.

What if I have dental anxiety?

The team is trained to provide a calming experience, and sedation options are available.

How do I book?

Call 305-371-6064 or use the online appointment request.
