Teen programme, the Texas way

Texas asks teens aged 15 to 17 for 46 hours of driver education from a licensed school. Safeguard's teen package covers the state requirements.

AgeStudents must be at least 15 by the first driving lesson. Enrolling at 14½ is possible, but you need to be 15 to qualify for the permit.
AttendanceNo more than 5 missed classroom dates. Missed sessions must be made up by the last day of class unless special arrangements are made.
TimelineCourses are usually finished in under 2 months and must be completed within 6 months.
Practice30 hours of behind-the-wheel practice with a parent or qualified adult is also required before the licence.

Possible insurance saving of up to 15% (10% for the course, 5% for alcohol training). Confirm the details with your insurer.

Road test: who needs what

The requirements below come from the school's road-test page. Bring your certificates and the team will check them with you.

Age groupYou will need
16 and 17At least 16 years old; a learner permit held for 6 months; a 24-hour driver education course (in person or online); 7 hours driving and 7 hours observation; the parent or guardian supervised 30-hour driving log; a valid DE-964 certificate; the 2-hour IMPACT Texas Teen Drivers course.
18 to 24At least 18 years old; the 6-hour Adult Driver Education Course with an ADE-1317 certificate; the 1-hour IMPACT Texas Adult Drivers course; a valid Texas learner licence with a "B" restriction.
25 and overWritten exam at the DPS office or the 6-hour Adult Driver Education Course (ADE-1317 certificate if you took it); the 1-hour IMPACT Texas Adult Drivers course; a valid Texas learner licence with a "B" restriction.

Questions drivers ask

Taken from the school's own site. For anything else, the team will confirm.

Do you teach in Spanish?

Yes. Classes are offered in English and Spanish.

What does the adult course include?

The required 6-hour state-approved driver education course, the written DPS driving test, application assistance and forms, and lunch.

Can I take behind-the-wheel lessons as an adult?

Yes, once you hold your instructional permit. Lessons are scheduled at a time that fits your week, and the number of lessons depends on the programme you need.

Can I book a road test only?

Yes. Road tests are available for all ages. Call or text 713.777.7783 to schedule.

What certificates do I need for a road test?

It depends on your age: teens bring a DE-964 certificate and complete the 2-hour IMPACT Texas Teen Drivers course; adults 18 to 24 bring an ADE-1317 certificate and complete the 1-hour IMPACT Texas Adult Drivers course. The team will confirm your exact list.

Can I pay in instalments?

Payment arrangements are available for teen driver education classes. Ask the team how it works.
