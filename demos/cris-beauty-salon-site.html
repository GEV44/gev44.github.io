Extensions

Three ways to add length

Every extensions visit begins with a consultation to choose the right type for your hair and goals, followed by a color match, application, blending and aftercare tips.

Tape-inSemi-permanent wefts with medical-grade adhesive that lie flat against the scalp. Good for volume, length or highlights, and they last 6 to 8 weeks with proper care.
I-tipIndividual strands attached with microbeads or micro-rings, with no heat or glue. Secure, reusable and made for long-term wear.
WeftWide sections sewn, glued or clipped to your natural hair. A fast way to get volume and length for an event-ready look.

Questions

Before you book

What is a Russian manicure?

A dry technique that uses a professional electric file to clean and refine the cuticle area without water. It gives a cleaner finish that typically lasts 2 to 3 weeks.

Are your keratin treatments safe?

The salon uses a formaldehyde-free formula that smooths and reduces frizz without the harsh fumes of traditional formulas.

How long does balayage last?

Between 3 and 4 months, depending on your hair type and care, since it grows out without harsh lines.

How long do hair extensions last?

Tape-in extensions last 6 to 8 weeks with proper care. I-tip extensions are reusable and need no glue or heat. Wefts are sewn, glued or clipped in for quick volume.
