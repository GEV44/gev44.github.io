Thinking of selling?

Three first steps

Selling can feel overwhelming. Broken into steps, it is manageable.

  1. Find a trustworthy estate agentChoose someone who listens, communicates clearly and understands your goals.
  2. Appoint a solicitorA good property solicitor handles the legal side and keeps things moving smoothly.
  3. Gather your documentsHaving everything ready early avoids delays. Open each group below.
Legal and ownership
  • Title deeds
  • Land Registry compliant map
  • Planning documentation
  • Certificate of Compliance (Building Regulations)
  • MUD Act documentation, for multi-unit developments
Energy and safety
  • BER certificate (Building Energy Rating)
  • Boiler service certificate
Financial and tax
  • LPT history (Local Property Tax)
  • Bank statement, for identity verification
Personal identification
  • Proof of ID: passport or driver’s licence
  • Proof of address: utility bill or similar
  • Marriage certificate, if applicable

Quick answers

Questions we hear

Is the valuation really free?

Yes. The website offers a free, no-obligation valuation. Standard market appraisals and specialist valuations (HSE, probate, divorce) are both available.

How quickly will someone call me back?

Every call is returned by the end of the day, or the following morning at the latest.

Can you help get the property ready to sell?

Yes. Dublin Homes can arrange trusted tradespeople for painting and improvements, choose furnishings for staging, and organise a BER assessment.

Do you only act for sellers?

No. There is a buyer’s agent service that represents your interests exclusively through the purchase, including off-market searches.

What are your opening hours and fees?

Neither is published on the website. The team confirms both when you get in touch.
