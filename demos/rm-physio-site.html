In plain words

What the techniques mean

Sports massage
Deep tissue manipulation aimed at reducing muscle tension, improving circulation and speeding recovery. Unlike a relaxation massage it targets specific muscle groups.
Myofascial release
Works the fascia, the connective tissue around muscle, to release tension, reduce adhesions and improve blood flow.
Neuromuscular therapy
Relief from pain and dysfunction that starts in the nervous and musculoskeletal systems.
Lymphatic drainage
Soft, gentle strokes that encourage lymph to move, since the lymphatic system has no pump of its own.
Oncology massage
Massage adapted to a person's health, treatment plan and cancer type so that it is safe and gentle.
Abdominal massage
Listed in Raul's bio as part of his work for women's health.

Before you come

Common questions

How long is a session?

Sessions typically run 60 to 90 minutes and can be adjusted to your needs.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing that allows easy movement. Athletic wear is ideal. For a specific injury, make sure the area is easy to reach.

What conditions do you treat?

Sports injuries, chronic pain, post-surgical rehabilitation, muscle and joint pain, and mobility issues.

Will sports massage fix anterior pelvic tilt?

It can help by loosening tight muscles, improving circulation and reducing pain, but it will not fully fix it. Strengthening, stretching and posture correction are needed too.

Therapeutic or relaxation massage?

A relaxation massage is mostly for unwinding. Therapeutic massage targets specific muscle issues, pain relief or recovery from injury.
