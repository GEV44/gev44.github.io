Art is everything

The Penny partners with the non-profit LAND Gallery. Guest rooms show hundreds of works by New York artists with developmental disabilities, among them Byron Smith, Garrol Gayden, James Rosa, Robert Latchman and Racquel Albarran.

Step outside

Williamsburg began as an industrial waterfront and became a home for artists, musicians and makers. The neighbourhood page lists favourites within a short walk:

Eat

Frankel's Delicatessen for a smoked-fish bagel or a deli sandwich to take to McCarren Park. Leo, ten minutes away, for handmade pizzas and pastas. The Four Horsemen for natural wine and small plates.

Walk

McCarren Park, the Williamsburg waterfront and Domino Park. Further out: Brooklyn Bridge Park, a 1.3-mile stretch along the East River, and Prospect Park with its 60-acre lake.

With your dog

Dog runs at McCarren Park (12 min), Marsha P. Johnson State Park (15 min) and Domino Park (20 min). Dog-friendly bars: Lucky Dog, Blinky's backyard, Tacocina on the waterfront, Dolly's Swing and Dive, Horses & Divorces.

Shop

Independent boutiques and vintage finds are a short walk from the hotel, along with galleries and cafes.

FAQ

Is the hotel good for longer stays?

Yes. Kitchenettes, work space and flexible layouts make many rooms ideal for a few nights or an extended Williamsburg stay.

Is there a gym?

Not on property. Guests can buy discounted day passes from nearby Chalk Gym for $25 per day through the QR code on the in-room corkboard.

Is the hotel accessible?

ADA-accessible Queen, Supreme Queen and suite rooms are available. Mention it when you book so the front desk can confirm.

What is Penny Mart?

The hotel's digital minibar, available via QR code, 24 hours, with items delivered to your room.

Can I buy things I like from my room?

Yes, please ask at the front desk.

Which animals are allowed?

Dogs only, in dog-friendly rooms: up to two per room, each under 75 lbs. Any damage or extra cleaning caused by a pet may be charged.

How do I contact the hotel for group sales?

Email the hotel and your request will be directed to the appropriate member of the team.

What is there to do nearby?

Cafes, galleries, independent shops, McCarren Park and the Domino Park waterfront, with Manhattan a short subway ride away.
