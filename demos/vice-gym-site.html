how can we help?

frequently asked questions

Where is Vice Gym located?

Vice Gym is located in The Sawyer Heights neighborhood of Houston, TX. Our exact address is 2122 Center St, Houston, TX 77007.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 5am-11pm, Saturday 8am-10pm, Sunday 8am-9pm. Any modified hours or changes will be posted on our Instagram page.

Where can I park?

We have 2 parking lots as well as street parking. Please see the parking map to confirm where you park is legal.

Can I tour the facility?

Our staff regularly gives tours of the club. Come on in.

Is Vice Gym beginner-friendly?

Absolutely. While we cater to competitive bodybuilders, our gym is open to all fitness levels. Our welcoming community and expert trainers will help you get started.

Do you offer day passes?

Yes, for those who want to try out Vice Gym before committing to a membership. If you sign up the same day, the day pass cost is credited back to you. The day pass is valid for the same day until close. Visit our front desk or membership page for pricing.

What membership options do you offer?

Various plans, including monthly and annual options, and a no-contract monthly membership. For pricing details, visit the membership page or stop by the gym.

Are there any contracts or cancellation fees?

We offer flexible membership options including a no-contract monthly membership. Please refer to the membership terms or speak with staff for details on cancellations or freezes. Cancellations are handled via email: memberships@vicegymhtx.com.

Do you offer military, veteran, police and first responder discounts?

Yes, we value those who give and have given to our country. Please stop by the club to take advantage of these offers. Family and corporate membership offers are also available in club. We do not offer student discounts.

Is there an age minimum?

Yes. All members and day passes must be 18 years or older.

Can I bring my dog?

Only service animals are allowed. Emotional support animals and pets are unfortunately not allowed.

Is filming allowed in the club?

Personal filming is allowed; keep it courteous to others' space, and speak to a manager if you need additional lighting or stands. For a professional shoot, DM us on Instagram, email beth@vicegymhtx.com or speak to a staff member.

Is Vice Gym bodybuilding friendly?

Yes. Owned by IFBB pros, Vice Gym is designed to support NPC and IFBB competitors with top-tier equipment and a dedicated posing room.

Do you have a lost and found? Do you host events?

Please see a front desk associate for lost and found items. We regularly host community events, so follow us on social media or check the website for upcoming events.
