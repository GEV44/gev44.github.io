Welcome to Hair Healers

Every client, a personalized project

The salon specializes in color correction and extensions. It has been featured in Miami's best salon lists, including The Best Salon & Spa Services in Miami and Best Humidity-Proof Cut & Botched-Color Repair.

Color correction

Single and double process, balayage, babylights, fantasy tones and color removal, planned around what your hair can take.

Extensions

Tape, fusion and micro-link extensions, plus a hybrid system. Pricing is custom, so book a consultation.

Restore and smooth

Keratin, Hair Botox, bond builders, hydration and Yuko Japanese straightening for dry, brittle or frizzy hair.

Brands and methods the stylists list: Yuko, Brazilian Blowout, Platinum Seamless, SoCap, Cinderella extensions, Bjorn, Donna Bella, Olaplex, Kapyderm scalp treatments, Ybera, Leaf & Flower, Truss and Malibu C.

Good to know

Quick answers

When is the salon open?

Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday from 10:00AM to 6:30PM. Closed Sunday, Monday and Wednesday.

How do I book?

Use the Book Now button on the salon website, call 305-643-2252, or leave your details with the assistant on this page for the team.

Do you price extensions online?

No. Tape, micro-link, fusion and hybrid systems are listed as custom, so the stylist quotes after seeing your hair. Appointment times run 180 to 240 minutes.

Can you correct color done elsewhere?

Yes, color correction is one of the two things the salon specializes in. Color remover is a 120 minute service with custom pricing, and the colorists are Adryanna, Yili and Noel.

What can you do for dry, brittle hair?

Restorative treatments at 60 minutes each (bond builder, Truss, Malibu, Mender, Rewind), plus keratin and Hair Botox with Jannie, the hair therapist.

Which brands and methods are the stylists certified in?

Yuko, Brazilian Blowout, Platinum Seamless, SoCap, Cinderella extensions, Bjorn, Donna Bella, Olaplex, Kapyderm and Ybera, between Dania and Adryanna.
