Good to know

Or ask the assistant in the corner. For anything medical, the vet team answers directly.

When is the clinic open?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

How do I request a prescription?

Call, e-mail or text the clinic. You can pick it up in the clinic, order through the VetSource online pharmacy, or have a prescription sent to the pharmacy of your choice.

Do you have a pet portal?

Yes, the clinic website links to a pet portal for clients. Ask the team if you need help getting started.

What can I buy for my pet's diet?

The clinic offers Purina Pro Plan VetDirect, delivered right to your door. The veterinary team can advise which food fits your pet.

What if my pet is sick or hurt?

Call (713) 297-8200 during opening hours and describe what you see, so the team can guide you. The website lists no after-hours service, so outside those hours please contact a 24-hour emergency animal hospital.

How much does a visit cost?

Prices are not published on the website. The team will confirm when you call or e-mail.

Can I use an outside pharmacy?

Yes. Choose the pharmacy, then call, e-mail or text the clinic to request the prescription. The clinic e-mails and/or prints it for you to send, and the pharmacy confirms and ships your order. That transaction is between you and the pharmacy.

Is the clinic open on weekends?

No. EaDo Veterinary Clinic is closed on Saturday and Sunday and open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

The website doesn't say, so the team will confirm. A quick call to (713) 297-8200 before you come will save you a trip.

Which animals do you see?

The clinic says it loves the EaDo pets of the neighborhood. The website doesn't list species, so ask the team about yours.

Where exactly are you?

2704 Polk St, Suite C, Houston, Texas 77003. Look for Suite C on Polk Street.

Who will I meet at the clinic?

The team on the website includes Ari, Monica, Sarabi, R'Monie, Dr. Farmer, Lizbeth, Tania and Sabrina. The clinic will tell you who is looking after your pet when you call.

How do I get directions?

Use the Directions button in the Get in touch section, which opens 2704 Polk St, Houston, TX 77003 in Google Maps.

Can I order pet food through the clinic?

The clinic offers Purina Pro Plan VetDirect, with nutrition delivered right to your door. Ask the team which diet suits your pet.
