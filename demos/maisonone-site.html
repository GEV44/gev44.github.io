Questions

Good to know

The salon
Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, booking ahead is recommended to secure your preferred date and time. Walk-ins may be available depending on availability.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee.

Who is Maison One for?

Every woman: bold beauty lovers, busy mums and graceful older clients are all welcome. Each treatment is adjusted to your energy, lifestyle and schedule.

How do I book?

Through the booking link on the website, by phone on 01 513 1144, on WhatsApp at 083 407 6382, or on social media. Vouchers can also be bought online.

How can I pay?

Cash and card are both accepted.

Nails & pedicures
What are BIAB nails and how long do they last?

BIAB (Builder In A Bottle) is a strengthening gel that helps natural nails grow, with a smooth, long-lasting finish. It typically lasts 2 to 4 weeks.

Does Shellac damage nails?

When applied and removed correctly it does not damage the natural nail, and professional removal is always recommended.

What does a pedicure include?

Cuticle care, exfoliation, foot filing, massage and polish. A pedicure usually lasts 2 to 3 weeks.

Brows, lashes & waxing
How long does brow lamination or a lash lift last?

Brow lamination usually lasts 4 to 6 weeks, and a lash lift 6 to 8 weeks depending on your natural lash cycle.

Brazilian or Hollywood?

Brazilian leaves a small strip or shape, and Hollywood removes all hair. Results last 3 to 5 weeks, and waxing can be tailored for sensitive skin if you tell the team in advance.

Skin & body clinic
How many sessions will I need?

Most clients see best results from 3 to 6 microneedling sessions and 6 to 10 IPL sessions, depending on skin goals, area and hair type.

Does the laser work on different skin tones?

The IPL system can treat a wide range of skin tones and hair types, and a consultation and patch test may be required.
