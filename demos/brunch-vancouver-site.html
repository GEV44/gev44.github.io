About us

Brunch Vancouver opened during one of the most challenging times for restaurants, with a simple idea: bringing people together over a great breakfast. The menu brings a Mexican touch to the brunch Vancouver knows and loves, with thousands of reviews and many returning guests.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you take reservations?

Monday to Friday, reservations and walk-ins. Saturday and Sunday are walk-ins only. Call +1 236 476 5682 to reserve.

Do you offer catering?

Yes, for parties and offices in downtown Vancouver. Send an enquiry or call us.

Can I order delivery?

You can order in through Uber Eats and DoorDash.

Do you have vegan or gluten free options?

Yes, dishes are marked on the menu, for example the vegan Avocado Toast and the vegan, gluten free Brunch Salad.

Is the Chilaquiles spicy?

It's marked spicy and is a house favourite.
