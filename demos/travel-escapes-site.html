Quick answers

Or ask the assistant in the corner.

Which airports do flights leave from?

Dublin, Cork or Shannon, depending on the holiday. The team confirms the departure airport for yours.

What does an escorted tour include?

The team arranges flights from Ireland, hand-picked accommodation, coach transfers and knowledgeable local guides. Exact inclusions are listed on each holiday page.

What does the Andalucia tour include?

Return flights from Dublin with airport taxes, 7 nights at the 4-star Sol Principe in Torremolinos on half-board with wine and water, a tour leader and local guides, and visits to Nerja, Malaga, Gibraltar, Ronda and Granada.

Is my money protected?

Yes. Travel Escapes is licensed and bonded by the Irish Aviation Authority (TA 0348).

Are deposits refundable?

Per the terms and conditions, deposits are non-refundable unless otherwise stated. Ask the team about your specific holiday.
