How rehab works

From assessment to return to sport

The clinic's knee page lays out its approach. The same pattern runs through the rest of the work.

1. Initial assessmentMovement, strength and flexibility testing, plus a review of any imaging you have.
2. Tailored rehab planPain and swelling management, strengthening for quads, glutes and hamstrings, then balance, agility and return-to-sport progressions.
3. Techniques usedManual therapy and taping, exercise-based rehab and injury prevention education, with objective monitoring along the way.

Strength gains are charted with regular Force Frame testing, and plyometric and power progressions are quantified with Output testing, so progress is measured, not guessed.

Insurance and receipts

The majority of health insurance policies give money back on physiotherapy sessions, depending on the insurer and policy, so the clinic suggests checking yours before you attend. There is tax relief on physiotherapy appointments, claimed when you file your tax return (see revenue.ie).

After each appointment a PDF receipt is emailed to you, which insurers ask for.

Cancellations: the clinic needs at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule without a charge. Inside 48 hours, or for a no-show, the cancellation fee equals the appointment fee.
