How a car accident or work claim runs

The same three steps for ICBC and WorkSafeBC, as described on the clinic site.

  1. First visit. A comprehensive assessment of your injury. For work injuries the physio asks about your job, how it happened and what it limits.
  2. Plan. Your physiotherapist explains your condition, advises on when and how much to move, and sets out an individual plan.
  3. Billing. The clinic bills ICBC or WorkSafeBC directly for authorized or accepted claims and works with your doctor and adjuster.

Extended health: what to have ready

Two ways the clinic can handle your private plan, from the Insurance page.

Option 1: direct billing

In many cases the clinic bills part or all of the treatment cost straight to your benefits plan through your extended medical insurer.

Some plans have restrictions and the clinic may not be able to bill online. If an online claim is denied, rejected or suspended, the patient covers that day’s cost.

Option 2: you claim

After therapy you submit your receipts and documentation to your insurer yourself for reimbursement. Check your own benefits first.

Have ready

  • Name of your extended health insurer
  • ID number and policy number
  • Your relationship to the policyholder
  • Date of your injury
  • Physician referral, if you have one

Also accepted

  • ICBC, for authorized treatments
  • WorkSafeBC, for accepted claims
  • MSP, RCMP
  • National Defence and Veterans Affairs

Before you come

How do I book?

Click Book Appointment, register on the site once, then book online, paperwork free. New and existing patients use the same page.

What do I need for extended health insurance?

Your insurer name, ID and policy number, your relationship to the policyholder, the date of injury and a physician referral if you have one.

What if I need to cancel?

Appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellation and no-show fees apply without notice.

Is the clinic accessible?

Yes. Spaces for clients with disabilities are close to the front door of the Superstore, and there is elevator access from inside the store up to the 2nd floor.

What happens at the first visit after a car accident?

The physiotherapist completes a comprehensive assessment of your injury, educates you about your condition, advises on when and how much to move, and develops an individualized treatment plan. The sooner treatment starts after an accident, the better, according to the clinic.

Can the clinic help with the insurance paperwork?

The site says they understand that insurance claims can be confusing and will help you through the process, working with you, your doctor and your adjuster.

How do I get there by transit?

The closest SkyTrain stop is Marine Drive on the Canada Line, and many buses stop on Marine Drive very close to the Superstore where the clinic is located. The clinic site suggests planning your trip with the TransLink trip planner.

What is active rehabilitation?

A personalized exercise program performed by a kinesiologist and supervised by a physiotherapist. It helps restore normal muscle function and control, and teaches you to exercise independently for long-term success.

Who pays?

Fees are paid by the patient unless coverage is approved and confirmed by your private plan, ICBC, MSP, WorkSafeBC, RCMP, National Defence or Veterans Affairs.
