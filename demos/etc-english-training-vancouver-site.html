Meet Jane, the founder

Jane Solovyov completed her university education in Sapporo, Japan, in 1991 and opened ETC English Training Centre in September 2001. She developed the methodology and curriculum that every teacher at ETC follows.

In her words, the special classes make every learner a confident and, over time, fluent speaker, and each tutorial builds understanding of culture and the skill to explain ideas and opinions naturally in English.

She also publishes free English learning videos on YouTube, including a CELPIP guide and a series on business and professional communication.

Inside the classes

Business communication

  • ETC templates with standard business expressions for different circumstances, in writing and speaking.
  • Expressing thoughts, getting clarity and asking questions in an assertive but professional manner.
  • Recognizing the unspoken side of business culture, taught through role-playing business situations.

General communication

  • Building vocabulary and conversational idioms, and using them smoothly in conversation.
  • Building confidence to share the thoughts and ideas you have.
  • A variety of life situations with role-play to build expressions, confidence and cultural training.

Pronunciation course

  • Focus on the specific sounds that each culture finds challenging.
  • Linking words and sentences.

Exam preparation

  • Customised ETC material for IELTS (General and Academic), CELPIP, TOEFL, TOEIC and other English proficiency exams.
  • Improving your scores in reading, writing, listening and speaking.

Quick answers

Can I take classes online?

Yes. All ETC classes can be taken online from anywhere, using Zoom or Skype.

What is the difference between a private class and a group class?

Private classes are one-on-one. Group classes have 2 learners for 1.5 hours or 3 learners for 2 hours.

Which exams do you prepare for?

IELTS (General and Academic), CELPIP, TOEFL, TOEIC and other English proficiency exams, with ETC's customised material.

Can I get proof of my English level?

Certificates recognizing your English level are available after taking 40 classes.

I am not sure about my level. What should I do?

Conversation classes start at beginner level and go up to advanced. The booking form asks for your level, the schools you have studied at and your goal, so the team can place you.

Does ETC offer anything for companies?

Yes, the ETC Corporate English Program is built for businesses with an international setting where employees do not speak English as a first language.

What does the registration fee cover?

It is a one-time fee that includes access to ongoing learning support, official education receipts, certificates and any correspondence you need from ETC.

Where is the school, and what are the opening hours?

The school's site does not list a street address or weekly timetable. The service advisory page says online Zoom classes run 7 days a week from 9:00 am to 6:30 pm, and the team will confirm the current schedule and in-person options.
