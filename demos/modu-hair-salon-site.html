Our story

Where the name comes from

“ALL” · “EVERYONE” · “EVERYTHING” — MODU (모두) is a Korean word that can mean all of the above. It evokes the feeling and atmosphere the founders envision for the salon.

What makes the salon special is the unique bond the team shares and the contagious love they all have for hair styling.

The team

Four stylists, one welcoming room

FounderHiro

Nearly 25 years as a hairstylist in Korea, Japan and Vancouver. He values lasting bonds with clients built on respect and trust, and offers a personalized, unique experience.

Co-ownerRyo

Based in Vancouver since 2008 after 6 years of experience in Japan. He considers head shape, facial features and hair type, and listens carefully to what you want: easy to maintain and long-lasting.

StylistAustin

Studied hair art design at the John Casablancas Institute in Vancouver (2015). Known for low-maintenance, easy-to-care-for styles and for advice on matching a style to your vibe.

StylistAdah

Started as an assistant in 2010 at JUNO Hair in Korea and rose to senior stylist before settling in Vancouver. Good communication is the key to her clients’ happiness.

In Hiro’s words
“Everyday I am constantly reminded that it is only with one’s heart that one can see clearly. I believe that what is most important to us is invisible to our naked eyes. This is why I wanted my own hair salon, so I could create a special space where there is much more than meets the eye. I welcome you to Mòdú!”

Hiro believes in forging deep interpersonal relationships based on mutual respect and trust — with clients and with coworkers, in a happy and stimulating work environment.

Booking

How appointments work

All appointments are made through e-mail or phone call. A credit card is kept on file for all services. If a reservation is cancelled within 48 hours of the appointment, the card is charged $100 for chemical services and/or $50 for haircut services.

All chemical services require a complimentary consultation with the stylist of your choice beforehand, so you and the stylist are on the same page.

Good to know

Quick answers

How do I choose a stylist?

Each of the four stylists has a profile: Hiro (personalized experience), Ryo (considers head shape and hair type), Austin (low-maintenance styles) and Adah (strong on communication). Prices depend on the stylist’s level of experience, so ask when you book.

Do you do barber cuts?

Yes — the menu lists a barber cut (base $55–75) alongside the haircut (base $70–105).

Which days are you open?

Tuesday through Sunday, 10:30am to 7pm. Closed Mondays.

Where exactly are you?

626 Abbott St, Vancouver, BC V6B 0E1 — in the heart of the city.

Can I send photos or questions before booking?

You can e-mail the team at moduhairsalon@gmail.com or message on Instagram (@moduhairsalon); the assistant here can also pass on a request.

I only want a quick trim — do I still need to book?

Appointments are made by e-mail or phone, so contact the team first (604-428-7601). The assistant can note your request and the team confirms the time.

Is there a fee if I need to cancel?

Only within 48 hours of your appointment: $100 for chemical services and/or $50 for haircut services, charged to the card on file.

Can the assistant give me an exact price?

It gives the base prices from the menu. The final price depends on the stylist’s level and on your hair, so the stylist confirms it at your consultation.

Can I book online?

Appointments are arranged by e-mail or phone. The assistant can collect your request and pass it to the team, who confirm the time with you.

Do I need a consultation for color or perm?

Yes. Every chemical service starts with a complimentary consultation with the stylist you choose.

Is wash & style included?

It is included complimentary with a cut. Technical services do not include wash & style, which is available on request for an additional charge.

Why are prices shown with a “+”?

They are base references. Prices depend on the stylist’s level of experience and on the integrity, length and thickness of your hair.

What is the cancellation policy?

Within 48 hours of your appointment: $100 for chemical services and/or $50 for haircut services, charged to the card on file.
