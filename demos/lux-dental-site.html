Emergency

Is it a dental emergency?

Any dental issue that needs immediate treatment to relieve pain or save a tooth counts. If you are unsure, call and the team will tell you honestly. Waiting can make the issue worse, and an infection can become a wider health problem.

  • Severe or persistent toothache
  • Facial or jaw swelling
  • Infection, or a bad taste that will not go away
  • A lost, loose or fractured tooth
  • A lost or dislodged crown or filling
  • Bleeding or painful gums
  • Injury to the mouth, lips or tongue after a fall or sports impact

Restorative

Damaged tooth? We can restore it.

Whatever has happened to your tooth, there is almost always a way to bring it back to full function. Not sure which treatment you need? That is what the exam is for: the team diagnoses the problem, explains the options in plain language and lets you decide.

  • You have a cavity that needs filling.
  • An old filling has cracked, fallen out or gone dark around the edges.
  • A tooth hurts when you bite down, or reacts sharply to hot and cold.
  • You have been told you need a root canal and you are anxious about it.
  • A tooth has cracked or chipped.
  • You are missing one or more teeth and want the gap closed.

Quick answers

Are new patients welcome?

Yes. New patients are always welcome, and dental emergency appointments are available.

Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan?

Yes, CDCP is accepted. Eligible patients can access government-funded dental coverage, so ask the team whether you qualify.

Do you bill my insurance directly?

The clinic accepts direct billing on most dental insurances.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe or persistent toothache, facial or jaw swelling, infection, a lost, loose or fractured tooth, a lost crown or filling, bleeding or painful gums, or injury to the mouth. Call 604-670-1256 and the team will tell you honestly.

How long does Zoom whitening take?

About an hour in a single visit, with a take-home touch-up kit included.

Do all dentists charge the same fees?

Not necessarily. In BC the dental fee guide is only a suggestion, and each office sets its own fees. Lux Dental follows the BCDA Fee Guide, and the team can walk you through costs after an exam.

Is a consultation free?

Free consultations are listed for dental implants, surgical dentistry, Invisalign, veneers and MINISH veneers. The team can confirm what applies to you.

Can I get a second opinion?

Yes. Some patients come in for a second opinion on treatment another dentist recommended, and the team will tell you honestly what needs doing and what can wait.

Can my dentist refer me for a CBCT scan?

Yes. Referring practices can download the CBCT referral form from the website, complete it and email it to the office.
