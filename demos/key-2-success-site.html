Which one is for me?

Pick by situation

A quick guide built only from the descriptions on the school's price list.

I have never driven

The 10-lesson package is designed for beginners, with structured lessons that progress as you do. Each lesson counts as 3 hours in the log book, for up to 10 lessons.

I have some hours already

The 5-lesson package (intermediate) builds confidence, and the 3-lesson package (advanced) refreshes knowledge and practises in the test area.

My test is booked

Book the trial test with an assessment sheet, then car hire for the test: an extra lesson that starts 1 hour before the exam.

I hold a licence from another country

Ask about overseas licence conversion. Students who transferred a licence say the instructor went through every requirement for the driving test.

I drive, but I am rusty

Refresher courses are on the list, and a single 1-hour lesson is open to all levels.

I want more than the test route

City driving covers highways, one-way streets, shared zones and parking. Country driving covers full or partial North-Western Sydney routes.

Questions

Quick answers

Do you pick me up?

Yes. Pickup and drop-off are free before and after each session.

Can I convert my overseas licence?

Overseas licence conversions are on the list of services. A trial test with an assessment sheet is also available.

How does the log book count?

Each hourly lesson is equivalent to 3 hours in your log book, for up to 10 lessons.

Are the cars safe for nervous learners?

The cars are the latest models and fitted with dual controls for your safety.

Can I book on weekends?

The school lists its hours as Monday to Sunday, 6am to 8pm.

What is the difference between a lesson package and a single lesson?

A single lesson is $70 for 1 hour. The 3, 5 and 10 lesson packages ($205, $335 and $650) are 1-hour lessons bundled at a lower per-lesson cost, each aimed at a different level: advanced, intermediate and beginner.

What does the trial test include?

It is 1.5 hours, similar to the real driving test, and ends with an assessment sheet that shows your results and what to work on.

How fast does the school reply?

The site says enquiries are answered within 24 hours, by phone on +61 438 423 331 or by email.

Where does the school teach?

The base is Waterloo Road, Macquarie Park NSW 2113, and country driving covers North-Western Sydney routes. The team confirms whether your suburb is covered.

Is there an offer for a first lesson?

The site advertises a first lesson at $65 with an asterisk. The conditions are not published, so ask the team when you book.

Do lessons help with the test route itself?

Yes. The school knows the testing tracks well and talks you through how you will be assessed, then offers a trial test with a scoresheet so there are no surprises on the day.

I am transferring a licence from overseas. Where do I start?

Start with a trial test and feedback session, which shows how you score against the real assessment, then add lessons or a pre-test lesson as needed. The team can confirm the steps for your country.
