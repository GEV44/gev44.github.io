About

Started by one bike ride in 2015

Alan Moore is the founder of LetsDoThisHouston LLC, a bike tour and special events company in 3rd Ward, Houston. Born in Chicago and raised in Memphis, he graduated from Prairie View A&M University with a bachelor's degree in Social Work in 2010, and his first job was at Boys and Girls Country Group Home, working with at-risk youth and teaching life skills.

Those same kids inspired him to start riding bikes. After a bike ride through the city in 2015, LetsDoThisHouston was born: bike tours that show Houston in a different way, mixing music with local businesses and historical landmarks. The first event, Bike and Brunch, was hosted with the local bike shop Tour De Hood, led by Dr. Veon McReynolds, and grew through social media and word of mouth.

Questions

Good to know

What is the cancellation policy?

No refunds. All deposits are final. If an event is cancelled, it is rescheduled for a later date.

Do I need to bring my own bike?

No. The company provides the bikes, and for team building also helmets and a tour guide. The Night Light ride needs a bike rental.

Is there a minimum group size?

It varies by tour, from none for the self-guided tour to 8, 10, 13 or 15 for others.

How do I book a historical tour for a class?

Email info@letsdothishouston.com with 'Historical Bike Tour' in the subject line, plus the event date and estimated number of guests.

How long are the rides?

From 2 hours for the E-Bike tour, history tour and school tour, to 2 hours 30 minutes for the Mural and Hip Hop/R&B rides and 3 hours for Night Light, Brunch and team building.

Do you have memberships?

The site has Memberships and Loyalty pages. Ask the team about the current options.

Is there a mobile hoops option for events?

Yes. Mobile Hoops HTX brings a basketball court to your event. Call or text 832-963-1212 for pricing.

Do you rent bikes without a guide?

Yes, the Self-Guided Bike Tour is $20 per hour, with a city guide of places for food and photos and no minimum group size.

Where do the rides meet?

At 3409 Emancipation Ave, Houston, TX 77004 in 3rd Ward. The private bus tour pick-up is 111 Yale St.

Can I choose my own music?

On the Night Light Bike Ride, yes, you can curate your own playlist.
