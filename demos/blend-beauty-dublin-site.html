FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments are recommended to guarantee your preferred time and stylist, but walk-ins are welcome. Message us on WhatsApp to check same-day availability.

How much does balayage cost?

Balayage starts from €149, depending on hair length and the amount of lightening needed.

Where are you located?

73 Capel Street, Rotunda, Dublin 1, D01 C9X0, a two-minute walk from the Luas and Dublin's main shopping streets.

Do you do BIAB and Shellac nails?

Yes. BIAB, Shellac, manicure and pedicure are all available, alongside hair and facial services.

Are you good for Brazilian and Latin clients?

Yes. Our team includes Brazilian hairstylists specialising in balayage, morena iluminada and Brazilian keratin treatment.
