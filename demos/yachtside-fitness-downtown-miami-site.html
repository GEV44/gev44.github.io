First visit

Three steps aboard

  1. 1Choose how to start. A day pass, a book of passes or the Open Gym membership.

Questions

Can I try it for a day?

Yes, day passes are available, in books of 1, 4 or 10.

Can I bring a friend to try the gym?

Ask the front desk. Day passes are sold in books of 1, 4 or 10, so a friend can try a single day.

Is there parking?

The Open Gym membership includes a gate card for access to the secure parking lot.

Who trains here?

Captains and crew from yachts docking worldwide train alongside locals from Brickell, Spring Garden and Little Havana.

Do you have classes?

FitCamp, a HIIT and circuit class, runs on the open floor.

Is there a class for groups?

FitCamp is a HIIT and circuit class on the open floor. Ask the front desk for class times.

Do you have a physical therapist?

Dr. Evan Motlong is a certified physical therapist on the trainer team.

Can I train remotely with a coach?

Jonathan Barthelemy is a certified online coach. Ask the front desk how to start.

Can I get Spanish-speaking help?

Ask the front desk. Reviews on the site include Spanish-speaking members.
