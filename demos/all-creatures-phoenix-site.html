About us

All Creatures Animal Clinic in Phoenix, Arizona, is a companion animal hospital accredited by the American Animal Hospital Association. An experienced team of veterinarians and veterinary technicians has cared for pets since 1987. The clinic also offers diet and nutrition guidance and an online store for pet products.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

Yes. Same-day and next-day appointments are offered for all of your pet's needs, based on availability. Call or text (602) 493-5090.

What animals do you treat?

Dogs and cats, exotic pets such as ferrets, rabbits, guinea pigs, hedgehogs and rodents, and reptiles such as bearded dragons and tortoises.

What if my pet has an emergency after hours?

Call Phoenix Veterinary Referral at (602) 765-3700 (4015 E Cactus Rd, Phoenix) or VetMED at (602) 697-4694 (20612 N Cave Creek Rd, Phoenix).

How do I pay?

Payment in full is due at the time of service. They accept cash, credit card, checks from established clients, CareCredit and ScratchPay. Other payment plans aren't offered.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please call or text at least 24 hours ahead. An $88 no-show or late-rescheduling fee applies if you cancel without 24 hours' notice, and if you're more than ten minutes late you may be asked to reschedule.

Do new clients get a discount?

New clients get $25 off their first visit with promo code FURBABY (limited time, one visit per new client, expires 12/31/26, exclusions may apply). A completed patient intake form is required for all appointments.
