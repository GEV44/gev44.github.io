The OROKO standard

Time is limited. Travel shouldn't be.

First-hand knowledge and the collective experience of the team inform every holiday OROKO designs. Your Travel Designer takes time to understand what matters to you, then develops the plans with thoughtful preparation and personal support throughout.

Anticipated, not requested

Service that meets you before you ask, from early check-ins to front-row seats. Your journey is shaped by insight, not a template.

Crafted, not claimed

Comfort and quality proven in practice. If a hotel, guide or route doesn't meet the standard, it isn't included.

Meaningful, not manufactured

Experiences that are sincere, not staged: thoughtful touches and rare local insight that make a journey personal.

How we work

It starts with a conversation

Tell us what matters

Where you want to go, who is travelling, your pace and your passions. Choose a good time to chat: morning, midday or late afternoon.

Your designer shapes it

Built from scratch, never templated, drawing on first-hand knowledge of the hotels, guides and routes, with flexibility built in.

Support at every step

Thoughtful preparation before you leave and personal support throughout, whether it is a major adventure or a milestone moment.
