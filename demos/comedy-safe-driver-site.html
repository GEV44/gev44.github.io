Texas in detail

Ticket, insurance, record, e-file

Ticket dismissal

Ask your court for permission by the due date on your citation, enroll with your case details, finish the course and turn in the certificate before the deadline. Eligibility resets every 12 months.

Insurance discount

No court permission needed. Finish the course and send your insurer the certificate. Amounts vary by company.

Type 3A driving record

If your court asks for it, add it at registration.

E-file to the court

For many Texas courts the certificate is sent electronically. The system checks your court during registration.

Good to know

Refunds, security and time limits

30-day refund window

A full refund can be requested within 30 days of purchase if you have not completed the course, pass or fail, and no exclusions apply. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

When refunds are not available

Once the course is completed or a certificate is generated or e-filed, after the maximum failed attempts allowed by the state, after failed identity validation, or if the course is not completed within 120 days.

Non-refundable extras

E-file, mailing, downloadable delivery and driving record fees once fulfilled, and some payment processing charges.

Security

Checkout runs over HTTPS and sensitive data is encrypted in transit. Access to personal data is limited to authorized staff.

Other states

Florida BDI is 4 hours and statewide where eligible. New Mexico is recognized by many courts, so confirm with yours. Missouri and Virginia driver improvement programs are also listed.

Questions

Quick answers

Can I start on my phone and finish on a laptop?

Yes. You can switch devices anytime and your course progress follows your account.

Do I need court permission for the insurance discount?

No. For the discount path you finish the course and send your insurer the certificate. Confirm the amount with your carrier.

What if the e-file option is missing?

Then your court does not support it. You can still choose download or mail and follow your court instructions.

Is the site secure for payments?

Yes. Checkout runs over HTTPS and payment processing is handled by trusted providers.

Which browsers work?

Most modern browsers on phone, tablet and desktop, including Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.

What if I get disconnected?

Your progress auto-saves. Log back in and resume where you left off.

Is there a final exam?

Yes, a 20-question multiple-choice exam where required, with up to three attempts at no extra charge. State rules may limit retakes.

How long do I have to finish?

Up to 120 days from purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 30 days of purchase if you have not completed the course and no exclusions apply. Fulfilled extras such as e-file, mailing and driving record are non-refundable.

Which court rules apply to me?

Courts have the final say, so always follow the instructions on your citation or deferral agreement.
