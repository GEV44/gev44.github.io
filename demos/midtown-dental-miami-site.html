Services

The latest technology and treatment options

Tap a service to read what the practice explains about it. Whether a treatment suits you is something Dr. Nelson decides after examining you.

Dental implants

Implants are permanent prosthetics that look natural and can be used for cosmetic purposes or for full-mouth restorations. A titanium-based cylinder replaces the missing tooth root, and a crown is later placed on it. Implants can also support full or partial dentures.

Most patients with adequate bone mass can have implants. An x-ray and CT scan are typically used to check bone. The practice lists better esthetics, less bone loss, better denture retention and preserving natural tooth structure as benefits.

Veneers

A thin shell of porcelain or resin bonded to the surface of the teeth, changing their shape, shade and position. Veneers are among the most conservative cosmetic treatments, and often very little or no tooth structure is removed.

Crowns and bridges

A crown (cap) restores a tooth that has lost a lot of structure, from full porcelain to full metal. A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth. Both are made to match the color, height, texture and appearance of your teeth.

Root canals

Treatment removes infected tissue from inside the tooth, then disinfects and seals the space, usually followed by a crown. The practice notes that modern techniques and materials make root canal treatment faster and more comfortable.

Extractions

A routine procedure in most cases, done under local anesthetic, sometimes with nitrous oxide for anxiety. Where several teeth are involved, the dentist or surgeon may recommend sedation, and the practice works with specialists.

Cleaning and consultation

A hygienist inspects the gums and gently removes hardened plaque, and the dentist may follow with a brief consultation, possibly with x-rays. The practice recommends a professional cleaning once every six months.

Inlays and onlays

Lab-made restorations for cavities too large for a simple filling. Porcelain and composite versions are cosmetic alternatives to fillings, and they are more conservative than crowns.

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening comes as tray whitening (a custom tray and gel used at home) or in-office whitening, which can give significant results in one session. Dr. Nelson can help you choose between them.

Invisalign

Invisalign clear aligners are one of the practice's featured services. Ask the team whether they suit you.

Quick answers

Do you offer evening or Saturday appointments?

Yes: Wednesday runs 10am to 7pm and Saturday 8am to 12pm. Friday is 9am to 1pm.

What about the office itself?

The practice describes a modern sterilization system that surpasses OSHA requirements, and dental units that use distilled (bottled) water instead of tap water.

Do you treat children?

The practice describes its care as gentle and family-oriented, for the adults and children of the community.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call 305-598-6070, which the website lists as the emergency contact, 24/7. This page's assistant can't give medical advice.

How often should I have a professional cleaning?

The practice recommends a professional dental cleaning once every six months, with brushing at least twice daily and flossing once a day at home.

Can the practice help if I have no insurance?

Yes: the Platinum Membership Plan is a reduced-fee option for families and small businesses without dental insurance, and a payment plan is available through a third party.

Are crowns and bridges long-lasting?

The practice says most crowns and bridges will last for life with good oral hygiene and regular check-ups and cleanings. Dr. Nelson can tell you what to expect in your case.

Which methods of whitening are there?

Tray whitening, with a custom tray and gel worn at home for a few hours a day, and in-office whitening, which can be done in one session. Dr. Nelson can help you decide which suits you.

Does the practice work with specialists?

Yes: periodontics (gum diseases), implant dentistry and endodontics (root canal treatments), as well as other specialists.
