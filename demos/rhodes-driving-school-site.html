常见问题Quick answers

教练用什么语言教学？Which language do instructors teach in?

以普通话和英语简明扼要地讲解技术技能。Skills are explained clearly in Mandarin and English.

我需要报多少小时？How many hours do I need?

学校会根据你的驾驶经验精确规划学时，确保课程高效、物有所值。套餐从 5 小时到 30 小时。Hours are planned from your driving experience. Packages run from 5 to 30 hours.

可以上门接送吗？Can you pick me up?

可以安排定点上车或上门接送。10 公里内包含在价格中。Yes, a meeting point or door pick-up. Within 10 km it is included in the price.

中途学不完怎么办？What if I stop part-way?

在有效期内，剩余课时可转让他人使用，但需补齐剩余课时的散钟差价。Within the validity period, unused hours can be passed to someone else, who pays the difference to the single-lesson rate.

想了解哪个套餐适合你？Not sure which package suits you?
