FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are preferred to keep wait times low, but walk-ins are welcome for urgent care and routine services.

How do I request an appointment?

Use the form on our website. A staff member confirms by phone, and availability varies.

Can you issue a travel certificate?

Yes, USDA certificates for international pet travel. Check your destination's rules and bring medical records if you're new.

Do you offer dental cleanings?

Yes, professional cleanings and, if needed, extractions with pain management.

Do you welcome new clients?

Yes, call (305) 262-8888 or (786) 218-1890 to schedule your pet's first exam.
