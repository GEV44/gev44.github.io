Car learners

The 12 EDT lessons

Essential Driver Training is mandatory for first-time car learner permit holders. Each lesson is one hour. Do lessons 1 to 8 in order; 9 to 12 can be taken in any order. Leave two weeks between lessons so you can practise. Your instructor signs each one off in your logbook, and you bring that logbook to every lesson.

  1. Car controls and safety checks
  2. Correct positioning
  3. Changing direction
  4. Progression management
  5. Correct positioning
  6. Anticipation and reaction
  7. Sharing the road
  8. Driving safely through traffic
  9. Changing direction, more complex situations
  10. Speed management
  11. Driving calmly
  12. Night driving

First steps to a permit

Learner permit checklist

  1. Pass the driver theory test (40 multiple choice questions, 35 right to pass)
  2. Eyesight report
  3. Permit application form
  4. Two signed passport photos
  5. Hand it in at your motor tax office

The permit is valid for two years. You need L plates and an accompanying driver with two years of full licence.

Step one

The driver theory test

You must pass it before you apply for a learner permit. It covers the rules of the road, risk perception, eco-driving, hazard awareness and good driving behaviour. Candidates with special requirements can be accommodated.

Ways to apply: online at prometric.com, by phone on 1890 606 106 (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm), or by post.

Bring one original ID

  • Passport or national ID card
  • Employee or student identity card
  • Garda National Age Card
  • Medical card issued in the State
  • Birth or marriage certificate
  • Immigration Certificate of Registration

Late arrivals cannot sit the test and lose the fee.

Professional drivers

Driver CPC, explained

Driver CPC came in across the EU in 2008 for professional bus drivers and 2009 for truck drivers. It sets common standards for training and testing and aims to reduce deaths and serious injuries on Irish roads.

New to the profession?
You take the Driver CPC theory and practical tests before getting your Driver CPC.
Licence from before 2008 or 2009?
Bus and truck licence holders from before then are automatically entitled to Driver CPC. This is called acquired rights. Periodic training is compulsory.
On the road

Learner permit rules

What you must do

  • Display L plates at the front and rear, at least 15cm high, red on white, clearly visible
  • Carry your learner permit whenever you drive
  • Drive only with a qualified accompanying driver, someone with a full licence for the same category for a continuous two years

Good to know

  • A first permit is valid for two years
  • After you pass the test you apply for a full driving licence
  • You can start EDT lessons at any point while you learn, and practise with an accompanying driver in between
  • The completed EDT logbook has to be shown before your driving test

Questions

Quick answers

Do I need a learner permit to start?

You need one to drive on a public road. The first step is the driver theory test, then the permit. Bring the permit to your lesson.

Manual or automatic?

Both are available. The enquiry form asks which you need.

What should I wear?

Sensible, narrow, thin-soled shoes give you feel for the pedals. Revise the rules of the road beforehand.

Can my employer or sponsor pay?

Sponsors are welcome. Tell the team when you enquire and they will confirm how it works.

What does the first HGV lesson involve?

A driving lesson assessment. The instructor evaluates your skills and experience, establishes training needs and then makes recommendations and a quote.

Can I choose a female instructor?

The school has male and female ADI instructors. Ask the team when you book.

I am nervous.

That is normal. The ADI instructors set the pace for you.
