      Common questions

      Anything the site does not say is left for the team to confirm.

      Is there a call centre?

      No. The site says all travel is handled by a local in-house team, and that you get free 24/7 support from experts who know your business.

      How are fees structured?

      An all-inclusive, transparent model with a competitive one-time service fee and no extra charges for changes, cancellations, after-hours support or reporting. Figures are prepared by the team for your program.

      Which booking tools do you use?

      SAP Concur and Deem, as a Tier 1 partner of both, configured to your policies and approval workflows.

      How is our data protected?

      Worldgo is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified and PCI DSS compliant.

      Do you handle crews, groups and events?

      Yes. Crew travel, custom groups, meetings, conferences and events are listed services, plus private jets and charters.

      Can we use you for personal travel too?

      The site mentions a Vacation Club with exclusive personal travel deals. Terms come from the team.

      Do you work in both Canada and the US?

      Yes. Offices in Vancouver and Toronto, plus Seattle for the US, and a unified Canada and US team. Canadian enquiries go to sales@worldgo.ca and US ones to sales@worldgo.us.

      Can you track emissions?

      Their mission includes working towards a net-zero future and helping clients track and reduce travel emissions. Reporting tools cover air, hotel and car data.

      What happens to unused tickets?

      Automated credit tracking means no unused ticket goes to waste. A reviewer says the team kept track of their pre-covid travel credit.

      Do you offer a mobile app?

      Yes. It compiles your plans into one itinerary, with flight updates, check-in, weather and booking on the go, on the App Store and Google Play.

      What does the consultation involve?

      The site calls it a no-obligation consultation. The assistant here collects the same basics as their form: company, travellers, annual spend and contact. Nothing is sent from this demo.
