Procedure day, explained

Surgery

Monday to Friday. Patients are admitted on the morning of the scheduled procedure, and surgery is run like a human hospital: sterile theatre, anaesthetic monitors and recovery agents. Pre-anaesthetic blood tests are done in the in-house lab, with results in minutes.

X-rays

Most pets are admitted for a few hours and fasted, because sedation or a general anaesthetic usually gives the clearest images. Results are instant, and the same goes for dental radiography.

Puppies and kittens

Puppies have three vaccinations at roughly 6-8, 10-12 and 14-16 weeks. Kittens have three at 8, 12 and 16 weeks. Puppy Preschool can start after the first vaccination.

Quick answers

What if it is an emergency?

Call (03) 9416 3788 directly. The nurses will say whether your pet is seen urgently, prioritised before others, or needs external emergency care. After hours, CARE (Centre Animal Referral and Emergency) is at 5 Hood Street, Collingwood, (03) 9417 6417.

Which animals do you see?

Small animals: cats, dogs, rabbits, guinea pigs and some reptiles. If the clinic cannot help, the team has an alternative for you.

Where do I park?

Ample parking at the front of the clinic, with access from Elliot Street or George Street. Tram and bus services are close.

Why is my pet fasted for X-rays or surgery?

Radiography usually needs sedation or a general anaesthetic for good images, so pets are fasted and stay a few hours. Surgical patients are admitted the morning of the procedure. The team gives exact instructions when you book.

Can you come to my home?

Yes, home consultations run Monday to Friday, generally between 11 am and 3 pm, priced by distance radius to the clinic.

When should I de-sex my pet?

The site recommends about six months for dogs and four to six months for cats, though each patient may vary. De-sexing surgery is done five days a week under general anaesthetic.

Do I need a booking for a vaccination?

Every vaccination visit includes a full health exam by a vet, so it is booked as an appointment. Request one here or call the nurses.

How do I get repeat medication?

Use the Medication request form on the clinic's website, or call the nurses on (03) 9416 3788.

Do you board cats?

The clinic does not list boarding. Its Community page recommends The Cattery Company for cat holidays.

Does the clinic do grooming?

Dog grooming and puppy school are run by sister business Joni & Co, for any breed or size.
