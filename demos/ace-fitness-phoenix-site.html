FAQ

Straight answers

Are technicians licensed and insured?

Yes. ACE technicians are factory-certified, bonded and insured. ACE is also a founding member of NAHFTA, the North American Health and Fitness Trades Alliance.

Do you handle warranty claims?

Yes. If equipment is under manufacturer warranty, ACE manages the claims process: paperwork, coordination with the manufacturer, and the repair.

What is the difference between a service call and a PM agreement?

A service call is reactive: something breaks and you call for a repair. A preventative maintenance agreement is proactive, with scheduled visits to inspect, lubricate and calibrate before problems become breakdowns.

Can you build a fitness center from scratch?

Yes. Complete buildouts include equipment consultation and selection, authorized procurement, delivery, installation and ongoing maintenance, for multifamily developers, hotel brands, corporate real estate teams and municipalities.

What if it breaks between visits?
