Before you arrive

Questions

How many people can stay in a room?

All four room types list a maximum capacity of 2 people.

What is in every room?

The room details list a bed (Queen or King), a TV, a coffee maker and a safe.

Which room has the most space?

The Master Queen Suite is the most spacious accommodation. The King Suite is listed at up to 456 sq ft and the Queen Suite at 434 sq ft.

Where exactly is the hotel?

In South Beach, Miami Beach, in the South of Fifth neighborhood, a few minutes' walk from the beaches.

Is the website available in Spanish?

Yes, the hotel's website has an English and a Spanish version.

Is it a quiet hotel?

With 17 rooms, it is described as intimate and surrounded by peace and tranquility, in the calm South of Fifth part of South Beach.

What is there to do nearby?

Beaches, water sports, biking, jet skiing, yachting, live music, local restaurants and Art Deco architecture.

Can I see rates here?

Rates and live availability are in the hotel's booking engine, which this demo links to rather than copying.

Which suite should we choose?

For solo travelers or couples, the Queen Studio. For a separate living area, the Queen Suite. For a King bed, the King Suite. For the most space, or to celebrate, the Master Queen Suite.

Are there good restaurants close by?

Yes, the site describes exclusive restaurants run by locals just steps from the hotel, in one of South Beach's most select gastronomic areas.

What about check-in times, parking and pets?

These are not listed on the pages this demo is built from, so the team will confirm them for you.
