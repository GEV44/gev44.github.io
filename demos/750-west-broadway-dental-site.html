About us

Under its current and earlier names, 750 West Broadway Dental has served families from the same Fairmont Medical Building location for over 25 years. The team practises patient-centered care, takes the time to diagnose your needs thoroughly, and offers transparent fees. The clinic is near the Canada Line station and the B-Line route.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP)?

Yes, we welcome CDCP patients.

Can I come in for a dental emergency?

Yes. We reserve time for emergencies and accept walk-ins. Call 604-879-9241 with details.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Arrive 15 minutes early and bring valid ID and your insurance card if you have one.

Do you bill my insurance directly?

Yes, we offer direct billing to insurance.

How much will my treatment cost?

We offer the exact cost upfront or the closest possible estimate for treatments involving external costs.
