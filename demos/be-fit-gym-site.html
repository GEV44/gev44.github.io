Quick answers

Or ask the assistant in the corner.

Is there a contract?

The month-to-month membership has no contract and can be cancelled at any time. There is a $60 sign-up fee.

Can I try a class without joining?

Yes. The Yoga, Power Spin and Boxing classes are open, and a day pass is available for non-members.

What are the weekend hours?

Saturday 7 AM to 7 PM and Sunday 8 AM to 6 PM. Check for holiday schedules.

Do you have personal training?

Yes. Contact the trainers to schedule a conversation about which routine fits you, for beginners or advanced programs.

What does the sign-up fee cover?

The month-to-month plan carries a $60 sign-up fee. On prepaid plans it is reduced or waived: 50% off on 3 months, waived on 6 months, and no sign-up fee on 12 months.

Where are the classes held?

Yoga and Power Spin are in the Be Fit Gym Studio. Boxing is in The Dungeon.

Is there a women's boxing class?

Yes, Saturdays at 11 AM. The 12 PM class that follows welcomes everyone.

Where can I park?

The website does not say, so the team will confirm. Call (832) 830-8310 before your first visit.

I have never trained before. Is that okay?

Yes. Trainers build routines for beginners and for advanced lifters, and the easiest start is a tour of the facility.

Your first visit

Three easy ways to get started, no pressure.

1. Take a tour

Come by 1601 Live Oak St during gym hours and see the facility. Weekdays from 5 AM, Saturday from 7 AM, Sunday from 8 AM.

2. Try a class

Power Spin on Tuesday and Thursday at 7 PM, Yoga on Saturday at 9 AM, Boxing on Saturday at 11 AM and 12 PM. Non-members can use a day pass.

3. Talk to a trainer

Contact the trainers to schedule a conversation about the routine and plan that fits you. Pro training, Body, Extreme, Burn or one-on-one.
