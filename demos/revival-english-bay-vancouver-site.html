Good to know

Is there a restaurant or breakfast?

There is no on-site restaurant or bar and breakfast is not included, but Denman Street's cafes and restaurants are steps away, and every suite has a kitchen.

Is WiFi free?

Yes, complimentary high speed WiFi is available throughout the property and in all guest suites.

Is there laundry?

Yes, there is a guest laundry facility on site, and every suite has a full kitchen.

Is housekeeping included?

Housekeeping is available upon request and regularly scheduled for every third day of your stay.

Is the neighbourhood walkable?

Yes. The West End is one of Vancouver's most walkable neighbourhoods, with grocery stores, pharmacies, dining, bike lanes and transit connections.

Is there meeting space?

Not on site. The hotel partners with nearby event venues, meeting spaces and planners for corporate groups, events and weddings.

Is the hotel wheelchair accessible?

The property has wheelchair access points and an accessible Bay King suite with a private terrace.

About Revival English Bay

Revival English Bay is a boutique all-suite hotel in Vancouver's West End with 66 individually designed accommodations, two blocks from English Bay Beach. Each unit has a unique layout, large windows and residential-inspired design.

What suites are there?

There are six suite types: the Davie Double Queen Suite w/Terrace, the Bay King Suite w/Terrace, the Bay King Suite w/Terrace (Accessible), the Pacific One Bedroom King Suite, the Pacific One Bedroom King Suite w/Terrace, and the Bay Two Bedroom (Connect) w/Terrace Suite, the largest with two bedrooms, two living areas, two kitchens and two terraces.

Do the suites have kitchens?

Yes. All rooms and suites include fully equipped kitchens with a full-size refrigerator, stovetop, microwave, cookware, dishes and utensils.

Which suites have terraces?

Select suites have private terraces: the Davie Double Queen, Bay King, Pacific One Bedroom King w/Terrace, and the Bay Two Bedroom suites.

What is in each suite?

Each suite includes a 40 inch flat screen TV, air conditioning, high speed WiFi (complimentary), a full kitchen, coffee and tea facilities, iron and ironing board, hair dryer and bath amenities. Housekeeping is available on request and regularly scheduled every third day of your stay.

Check-in and front desk

Check-in and the front desk run around the clock: the hotel is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and contactless check-in and check-out are available. For exact check-in and check-out times, the team will confirm.

Parking and getting around

Self-parking is available adjacent to the hotel for an additional fee. Bus service runs along Denman, Davie and Robson Streets, and Mobi by Rogers e-bike rentals are nearby.

Is there a restaurant or breakfast?

There is no on-site restaurant or bar, and breakfast is not included. But Denman Street, one of the best dining areas in the West End, is steps away, with cafes and restaurants, and grocery stores and pharmacies are close.

Where is the hotel and what is nearby?

Revival English Bay is at 1771 Comox St, Vancouver, BC V6G 1P5. English Bay Beach is a 4 minute walk, Stanley Park is about 2.2 km, and downtown Vancouver is about a 20 minute walk. The Seawall, Sunset Beach, Davie Village and Robson Street are all close.

Do you welcome long stays?

Yes. Long stay guests are welcome. The full suites are equipped for extended stays of any length, with full kitchens and living areas. The hotel also hosts corporate groups, events, weddings and relocation travel.

Groups, events and weddings

Revival welcomes corporate groups, events and weddings with its 66 full suites. There is no on-site meeting space, but the hotel partners with nearby event venues, meeting spaces and planners.

Fitness and laundry

There is a 24-hour fitness facility and a guest laundry facility on site.

Accessibility

The property has wheelchair access points, and there is an accessible suite: the Bay King Suite w/Terrace (Accessible), with a king bed, kitchenette and private terrace.

Is it good for families?

The Davie Double Queen suite has two queen beds and a terrace, which suits families and friends travelling together. The one bedroom suites have a fold-out sofa bed in the living area, and the Bay Two Bedroom is the largest.

Rates and policies

The website doesn't list rates, cancellation terms or a pet policy, so the team will confirm them for your dates: stay@revivalvancouver.com.

How do I contact the hotel?

You can email the team at stay@revivalvancouver.com, or leave your details here and they will get back to you.
