Emergency dentist

Same-day and Saturday emergency care

Severe toothache, broken tooth or lost crown? The practice offers same-day appointments when available. Call first so the team can find the soonest slot.

Toothache

The site suggests rinsing with warm salt water and taking ibuprofen for pain and swelling while you wait for your appointment.

Knocked-out tooth

Time matters. The site advises holding the tooth by the crown, not the root, keeping it moist in milk, and getting care ideally within 30 minutes.

Broken tooth or lost crown

Save any broken pieces, avoid chewing on that side and contact the office for an appointment.

Swelling or abscess

Contact a dentist promptly and do not apply heat. Facial swelling with fever, trouble swallowing or breathing needs emergency medical care.

Good to know

Common questions

Do you accept new patients?

Yes, new patients of all ages are welcome, whether for a routine checkup or advanced treatment.

Do you provide emergency dental services?

Yes, for sudden pain, broken teeth, infections or other urgent dental issues. Call the office so the team can find the soonest appointment.

What should I expect during my first visit?

A detailed dental examination, X-rays if required, and a consultation with the dentist. The team also creates a personalized treatment plan based on your needs.

Are dental implants safe?

The practice describes dental implants as a safe, reliable and long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth. Whether they suit you is a question for the dentist.

Do you treat children as well?

Yes, the practice provides family-friendly care for patients of all ages, including children.
