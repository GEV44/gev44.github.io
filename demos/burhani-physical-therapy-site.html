Your first visit

What to expect

As described on the clinic's service pages.

1. A full evaluation

Your therapist reviews your history, assesses your movement and strength, and talks through your goals.

2. A plan built for you

Treatment typically combines hands-on manual therapy, targeted therapeutic exercise and education, based on your diagnosis and stage of healing.

3. Regular check-ins

Progress is reassessed regularly and the plan is adjusted as you improve, with a home program so progress continues between visits.

Quick answers

Questions patients ask

Does the clinic accept Medicare?

Yes: Traditional Medicare and many Medicare Advantage plans, including Wellpoint, Community Health Choice, Molina and Blue Cross Blue Shield. Aetna Medicare (50%) and CIGNA Medicare (after authorization) are also accepted.

Will my insurance cover physical therapy?

Most plans cover physical therapy with a referral from your physician. The clinic works with most major carriers, and its billing team handles pre-authorizations to maximize your benefits.

Who treats me during my visit?

Your therapist, personally, in a private curtained area. Sessions are not handed off to aides or a crowded gym floor.

Do you see children?

Yes. Pediatric physical therapy and pediatric occupational therapy support infants, children and teens with developmental, congenital and acquired conditions.

How fast will the clinic reply to a message?

The contact page says inquiries are answered within 24 business hours. For something sooner, call 281-758-2727.

I am not sure which service I need.

That is what the first evaluation is for. Your therapist reviews your history, assesses how you move, and discusses your goals before building a plan.

Ready to start your recovery?

Tell the assistant what you need and the front desk takes it from there.
