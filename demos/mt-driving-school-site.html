Quick answers

Do you offer road tests, not just lessons?

Yes. The school is an authorized third-party DPS road test provider, by appointment only.

Is there a class for adults?

Yes: a 6-hour in-person permit class and a 6-hour online permit class. The published description notes the course is required for people aged 18 to 25.

Does a package include pickup?

Free pick-up and drop-off is offered to students who select a package that includes it. Hourly in-car training is without pickup.

What car do I drive?

Lessons use small Toyota Camrys.

Can I take the test in my own car?

The road test price covers a test using the school car or the student car.
