Pink slip, step by step

How a rego inspection goes

1. Check your renewal notice.
All vehicles more than five years old need a safety inspection as a condition of registration renewal, and the renewal tells you if yours does.
2. Go to an Authorised Inspection Station.
The pink slip has to come from an AIS. Sydney City Auto Care is one.
3. Drop the car off in the morning.
The team works on it while you are at work, and the station is a one-minute walk.
4. Results go to the RMS.
The eSafety check is transmitted electronically, so there is no paper to carry.
