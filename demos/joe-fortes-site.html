About us

Joe Fortes Seafood & Chop House has served classic American cuisine in downtown Vancouver since 1985, named after the city's first official lifeguard. The kitchen uses Pacific Northwest ingredients and daily local deliveries, with an oyster bar, wine room and rooftop garden.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Can I bring my own wine?

Yes: 1 bottle (750mL) for every 4 guests, with a $45 corkage fee per bottle plus taxes. Tell Guest Services when you book.

How large a group can you host?

Groups of up to 60 guests, with group menus and an events team.

Where can I park?

Nearby public lots or metered parking; allow extra time at peak hours.

Do you take happy hour reservations?

Yes, indoors only, for up to 12 guests; reservations are highly recommended.

Do you serve brunch?

Saturday and Sunday from 11am to 3pm.
