Billing & claims

Direct billing, ICBC and WorkSafeBC

Confirm your coverage with your insurer before booking. The team can walk you through the rest.

ICBC covered treatment

Hurt in a motor vehicle accident as a driver, passenger, pedestrian or cyclist? Your recovery treatment may be covered by ICBC.

The clinic has been treating ICBC clients for four years, with 1-on-1 physio using manual therapy and exercise prescription.

WorkSafeBC

Work-related injury or disease? Physio may be covered through WorkSafeBC, including sprains, strains, fractures and low back pain.

Since April 1, 2026, kinesiology / PTSW support sessions can be part of approved claims. They need your physio's approval first.

Quick answers

Before you book

Do you direct bill my insurance?

Most major insurers are direct billed when your plan coverage allows it. Please confirm your coverage with your insurer before booking.

Can ICBC cover my physio after a car accident?

If you were hurt as a driver, passenger, pedestrian or cyclist, treatment may be covered by ICBC. The clinic has treated ICBC clients for four years.

What is new for WorkSafeBC claims?

As of April 1, 2026, WorkSafeBC allows kinesiology / PTSW support sessions as part of approved claims. Sessions must be approved by your physio before attending.

What does IMS feel like?

The practitioner explains the treatment and the sensations you may feel first. The clinic describes minor discomfort, with positive results typically within a few days.

How do I book?

Online through the clinic's booking page, by phone at (604) 262-0909, or tell the assistant what you need and the team follows up.
