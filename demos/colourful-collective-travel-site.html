Your booking confirmation shows your pick-up point and exact time. Arrive 10 minutes early. Street parking in the CBD is limited and metered, so public transport is the easy way in. Hotel pick-ups are not offered on small-group tours, but staying in the Hunter Valley? There are pick-ups at several local accommodations on Hunter Valley tours.

Saver or Flexible?

Two ticket types on the small-group tours. Fares are on each tour page.

Flexible

Fully refundable up to 24 hours before departure, and no fee for date changes. The pick if your plans might shift.

Saver

The lower-priced option, non-refundable, with a date-change fee of A$40 per person.
Weekends, public holidays and December to February fill first, and Saturday Hunter Valley tours often sell out weeks ahead. A digital confirmation on your phone is all you need.

Quick answers

What if the weather is bad?

Tours run in most weather. They only cancel in conditions that are unsafe or would ruin the experience, and you are then offered a full refund or a new date.

Is lunch included?

It varies. The Blue Mountains All Inclusive Signature tour includes a sit-down lunch at Scenic World, and Hunter Valley premium wine tours include a two-course lunch. Check the inclusions on your tour page.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Yes: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, Halal, dairy-free and nut allergies. Tell them at booking.

Are the tours accessible?

It depends on the tour. The vehicles have no wheelchair ramps, so contact the team before booking and they will advise honestly.

What should I bring?

A water bottle, sunscreen, a hat, comfortable closed-toe walking shoes and a light layer. The Blue Mountains and Canberra can be much cooler than Sydney.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, online in any denomination, valid for any day or private tour.
