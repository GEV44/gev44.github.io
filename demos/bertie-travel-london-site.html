How a trip comes together

  1. Tell us the plan

    Call or email with your route, dates, group and any special requests, such as dietary needs, pets or unusual baggage.

  2. We find the best flights and places to stay

    Using a global network of contacts and membership of top UK and US hotel consortiums for preferred benefits.

  3. Smooth transfers

    Chauffeur cars to the airport, a personal meet and greet at the other end and onward travel.

  4. Someone on call

    A personal mobile number, so help is a phone call away from emergency passports and visas to travel safety advice.

Why Bertie?

Founded in 2006 by Anna Roberto

Based in London, Bertie Travel brings over 20 years of experience to clients across media, entertainment, advertising and corporate, as well as independent travellers.

Outstanding customer service is at the heart of it. No matter how big or small the request, the team is available 24/7 to take the stress and complexity out of your travel arrangements.

Keeping things simple for you is the approach: meeting your deadlines and budgets, surpassing expectations and building long-term relationships rather than just crunching numbers or hitting targets.

Questions

Before you get in touch

Do you only work with media clients?

No. Media is the speciality, but corporate clients and independent travellers are welcome too.

Can you help if a flight is cancelled?

Clients say the team keeps working on it even when flights are missed or cancelled. Call the personal number you are given.

Are you part of a large chain?

No, the site says it is a fully independent agency, now associated with Nous Travel for back-up and support.

Can you arrange hotels as well as flights?

Yes, from designer city hotels to villas, cabins and bush camps.

Do you work outside office hours?

Yes. You get a personal mobile number and the team is available 24/7.

Can you handle a whole film crew?

Yes, whole film crews and large groups are listed, with special requests and dietary requirements.

Can you fly my pet?

Yes, the agency arranges pet travel on flights across the world.

Are prices on the site?

No. Send the details of your trip and the team will outline options and costs.

Where are you?

London, with a global network of contacts. No street address is listed on the site.
