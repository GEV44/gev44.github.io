Our philosophy

Contemporary dentistry, timeless values

Expert dentistry with a human touch

Exceptional dentistry goes beyond technical skill. The team combines clinical excellence with a deeply personal approach: taking the time to understand your concerns, explaining your options clearly, and making sure you feel comfortable at every visit. The goal is for you to feel respected, well cared for and confident in your smile.

A practice you can rely on

Loyalty is earned through consistency, transparency and genuine care. Patients appreciate knowing their dentist and building a long-term relationship with a practice they trust. By listening carefully and tailoring treatment to individual needs, the team delivers dentistry that stands the test of time.

Part of the family

Whether you visit for a check-up and clean, cosmetic bonding or a more complex procedure, you are treated with warmth, respect and continuity of care. As a family dentist in Melbourne, the practice welcomes patients of all ages and focuses on long-term oral health.

Gentle, conservative, caring

What sets the practice apart is the environment it has built: a dentistry experience that is gentle, conservative and deeply caring, delivered in a calm, welcoming setting in the heart of Melbourne.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Can I book a free cosmetic consultation?

Yes. The practice invites you to book a free cosmetic consultation. Request one with the button above and the team will confirm a time.

Is there a late evening or Saturday option?

Tuesday is the late day, 2 pm to 8 pm, and Saturday runs 9 am to 2 pm by appointment. Monday is 9 am to 6 pm, and Wednesday to Friday 8 am to 4 pm.

How can I pay for treatment?

Flexible payment options are available through Zip, Afterpay and Humm, and the site lists American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Mastercard, PayPal and Visa as payment methods.

Do you offer oral surgery?

Wisdom teeth removal is listed under general dentistry, and Dr. Shady Moussa is a qualified and registered Oral Surgery Specialist on the team.

Is there a hygienist or oral health therapist?

Chloe Newton is an Oral Health Therapist with full adult scope. Her hygiene appointments go beyond a professional clean, with comprehensive gum health assessments, tailored advice and individual prevention plans.

What if my enquiry is urgent?

Please call the clinic on (03) 8669 0500 so the team can assist over the phone right away.
