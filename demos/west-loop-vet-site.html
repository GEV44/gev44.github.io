About us

West Loop Veterinary Care practices to AAHA guidelines and Fear Free techniques, and is a Certified Cat Friendly Practice. The team offers wellness care, surgery, diagnostics and urgent care at its West Loop and Streeterville hospitals in Chicago.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do new clients get a discount?

New clients get their first exam free.

Where do I park?

A Loading Zone in front of the hospital on Randolph for short stops, plus metered and street parking nearby.

What if there's an emergency after hours?

Contact Veterinary Emergency Group (872) 710-5226 or Premier Medical Group 773-516-5800.

Do you offer telemedicine?

Yes, by Google Duo, Skype or FaceTime for non-urgent cases. Call 312-421-2275.

Can I leave my pet for the day?

Yes, Day Stay appointments are available and you'll get a full report.
