FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What happens at my first appointment?

You talk through your activity and goals, have a complete assessment, go through your options and start a custom plan. The number of sessions is agreed during the assessment.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

In many cases you can see us directly. Some insurance plans or conditions may need a referral, so check with your insurer first.

I'm not sure what's wrong. Can I talk to someone first?

Yes, you can book a free injury discovery call or request a call back.

Do you offer sports massage?

Yes, sports and deep tissue massage by CORU registered physiotherapists, as single sessions or a discounted bundle pack.

How can I contact you?

Call (01) 482 6902 (24/7 line), send a WhatsApp, request a call back through the website, or e-mail info@sportsphysioireland.com.
