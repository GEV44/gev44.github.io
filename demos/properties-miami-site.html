About

Started by investors, in 2002

"Each realtor-associate is assigned to a specific area of Miami where they live and interact daily."

  • 2002 PropertiesMiami.com launched by three investors who each held a real estate license, tired of listing sites that did not give them the tools they needed.
  • One year later The founders opened their own agency, first to serve investors, with a group of 10 realtors divided by areas around Miami.
  • Today Realtor-listed properties plus exclusive opportunities with private sellers, buyers and investors.

Quick answers

Do you speak Spanish?

The contact page calls it a Spanish-speaking friendly real estate agency.

Do I pay anything to start a flat fee listing?

The site says you create a free account and fill in the MLS flat fee fields, and that no payment is required at that stage. The team confirms the fee options.

What types of property can I search?

Condos, apartments, pre-construction buildings, single-family houses, townhouses, foreclosures and anything listed for sale or rent in Miami.

How fast will someone call back?

The contact form promises a response in 5 to 10 minutes between 8 AM and 10 PM Eastern.

Can I rent without visiting in person?

The site mentions remote rental applications. The agent confirms how that works for the building you pick.

I am an investor, is this for me?

Yes, the agency was founded by investors and focuses on investors first. Ask about foreclosures, pre-construction and private-seller opportunities.

Can agents join the team?

There are pages for joining the brokerage, a 100% commission brokerage model, and an agent MLS website.
