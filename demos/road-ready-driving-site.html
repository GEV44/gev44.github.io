Good to know before you enroll

Details straight from the school's program pages.

Teen program

  • Driving is a privilege, not a right: the state approved curriculum is built around responsible habits, and the school says teens have the highest accident rate, so the goal is to make them the smartest drivers on the road.
  • Behind the wheel and classroom can run at the same time as soon as the student has the learner permit.
  • A certificate is issued for the learner permit, and another for the driver license after the program.
  • Students who are 16, have finished the program and held the permit for 6 months can schedule the road test with the school.
  • The school lists the dates of the next classroom start on the program page; the team confirms current dates.

Adult program

  • Texas law (SB 1317) requires a six-hour driver education course for license applicants 18 and older, and applicants 25 or under must complete an approved course.
  • The class covers the whole Texas Drivers Handbook: road signs and road rules, in a traditional classroom, every Saturday from 9 am to 3 pm.
  • The certificate (ADE 1317) is issued the same day; you take it to DPS with your other documents for the learner permit.
  • Adults 25 and older can take the course too, which removes the written test at DPS. It helps new residents, people relocating from other countries and corporate employees.
  • A photo ID with your date of birth is required to join the class.

Quick answers

Who can take Teen Driver Education?

Students who are at least 3 months away from their 15th birthday and under 18, with legal status in the U.S.

When can a teen take the road test?

At 16 or older, with a valid learner permit held for at least 6 months and a completed approved teen driver education program.

When does the adult permit class meet?

Every Saturday from 9 am to 3 pm. Bring a photo ID with your date of birth.

How do I pay?

Online with a credit or debit card or PayPal, or cash at the office.

Do you pick students up?

Yes, with no fee within 3 miles of the office, and special arrangements for students outside Houston.

Do you offer online courses?

The site lists Texas Parent Taught Drivers Ed, Texas Defensive Driving and Texas Adult Drivers Ed online.
