Good questions

Can you value my flat without visiting?

The site offers instant online valuations for sales and lettings as an indication. For the most accurate figure an agent visits your home and explains how it would be marketed.

I live abroad. Can you manage my rental?

Yes, full tenancy management is popular with landlords living overseas or outside London. It covers legislation, repairs and issues during the tenancy.

Do you handle offices as well as homes?

Yes. Commercial investments, commercial rentals and serviced offices, with a separate commercial line.

Where can I read about tenant fees?

On the Tenant Fees page of the Hudsons website. I don't quote figures here.

I'm buying a leasehold flat. Any reading?

The Guides section includes The Ultimate Guide to Purchasing a Leasehold Flat in Central London.

Is there anything for clients beyond property?

The Hud Hub lists local cafes, restaurants and salons the team likes, with discounts for Hudsons clients.

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes. The office is open Sunday 10:00am to 4:00pm, and Monday to Saturday 8:00am to 8:00pm.

Which areas do you work in?

The West End and Central London, with area guides for Soho, Marylebone, Fitzrovia, Bloomsbury and Covent Garden.

Do you help with probate property valuations?

There is a Probate Valuations page on the site. Ring the office to talk it through with the team.

Can I speak to someone about block management?

Block management services in London are listed under Landlord services. Ask the assistant to pass your details on, or ring 020 7323 2277.
