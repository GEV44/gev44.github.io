Whether you dream of long, flowing locks or want something to try for a special occasion, extensions offer endless possibilities. Our stylists customise and apply Bellami premium extensions to produce natural-looking results.

  • Instant length and volume — your dream hairstyle without the wait.
  • Versatility — try long and flowing, a trendy bob or a full updo without permanent commitments.
  • Natural appearance — professionally applied extensions blend into your own texture.
  • Confidence — more length and volume can make you feel more radiant.
  • Thin or damaged hair — a quick, aesthetic way to address it.
  • Low maintenance — with the right care they are easy to live with day to day; your stylist explains aftercare.

Keratin smoothing treatment

Keratin is the primary structural component of hair. The treatment starts with a meticulous cleanse so the keratin solution can penetrate deeply, restoring lost protein from within. Heat, typically a flat iron at controlled temperatures, then seals the keratin in to create a protective shield against frizz.

  • Frizz reduction
  • Enhanced shine
  • Manageability and styling ease
  • Temporary straightening

By rejuvenating the hair's natural keratin content, the treatment repairs damage, minimises breakage and reinforces the hair's structure and resilience against environmental stressors. Sealing the keratin in leaves a smooth, glossy finish.

Balayage, ombre & highlights

Good to know

What is balayage?

From the French balayer, to sweep or paint: colour is painted onto the hair to create a soft, blended, natural-looking highlight with a lived-in look.

Why is it so popular?

It needs less maintenance, because the grown-out roots look natural, and it is personal — placement, gradation and colour follow your hair and your features.

How often should I come in?

Maintenance can be anywhere from 3 to 6 months depending on the person; we recommend a toner at least every 8 weeks, as brassy tones can begin to peek through.

Ombre, balayage or highlights?

They are different styles and techniques with the same goal — lightening the hair — and can be combined for your desired look.

What does a toner do?

A toner refreshes the tone of lightened hair and keeps brassy shades from peeking through between balayage visits — part of the every-8-weeks maintenance we recommend.

Will the colour suit me?

Our stylists always consider your skin tone and natural base colour to decide which tones will work best for you, and no two balayages should look the same.
