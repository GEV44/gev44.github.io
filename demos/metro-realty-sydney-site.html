How a sale runs

From first chat to offers

  1. A confidential chatTalk through your circumstances, your property and your ideal timeline. Do your research and ask every question you need; the team is there to guide, not rush you.

Questions we hear

Can I take my time deciding to sell?

Yes. Metro asks you to take your time, do your research and ask all the questions you need. The team guides rather than rushes.

Will I get a report after each open home?

Yes. After every open home the owner gets a detailed report with buyer feedback and interest levels.

What does Metro say about its approach?

Results over theatrics: meaningful strategy, personal and intelligent service, and technology that adds transparency for clients.

Do you only sell, or manage too?

Both, with equal strength. Many agencies lean toward one side; Metro runs an award-winning sales team and experienced property managers.

Do my online ads shrink after 30 days?

No. Metro only uses unlimited campaigns, so your premium ad does not turn into a pumpkin after midnight.

Can a sale happen without public advertising?

Sometimes. Metro matches properties with qualified buyers through its off-market channels and private network.

How will I hear about my campaign?

Weekly updates on online listing performance, buyer feedback, offers and negotiations, with strategic advice on next steps.

How does Metro handle tenant applications?

Smart tenant screening with fast, secure online applications, and a no arrears policy using full direct debit facilities.
