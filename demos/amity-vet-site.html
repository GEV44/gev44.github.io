About us

Amity Veterinary Clinic is a modern clinic in Al Barsha, Dubai, with a pleasant and relaxed atmosphere where pets and owners feel safe and welcome. It has its own operating rooms, laboratory and X-ray facilities, and the team brings over 80 years of combined experience and speaks more than six languages.
