Good to know

Quick answers

What if my pet has an emergency after hours?

Contact the team immediately on the emergency number for guidance, and bring your pet to the clinic for prompt attention.

Do I have to call before coming in an emergency?

Not mandatory, but calling ahead lets the team prepare for your arrival.

How long is a consultation?

Most last between 30 and 60 minutes, depending on the case. You can raise several concerns in one visit.

Can I visit my pet in hospital?

Yes, owners are encouraged to visit during designated hours. The team gives updates during visits or calls.

Do you offer medicated baths?

Yes, for pets with specific skin conditions or sensitivities.

Is microchipping safe?

The clinic describes it as a quick, safe procedure. A visible collar tag is still recommended for immediate identification.

This page and its assistant never diagnose. For any health question about your pet, please speak to the vets directly.
