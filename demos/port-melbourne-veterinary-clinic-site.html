Who we are

A local clinic that has stayed local

Port Melbourne Veterinary Clinic and Hospital opened in 1997 on Bay Street. The clinic is purpose built and fully equipped, with a wide range of surgical procedures and advanced diagnostics on site.

The vets stay current by attending seminars and conferences, and the team works closely with referral centres across Melbourne. When your pet needs a specialist, they can arrange a consultation at the clinic or refer you to one they trust.

Emergency

If something is wrong right now

During opening hours call (03) 9646 5300 and the team will direct you. Depending on the situation you may be sent straight to an emergency hospital. Outside hours, use one of the centres below.

  • Distress or drooling after eating a bone or other object
  • Excessive coughing, vomiting or diarrhoea
  • Collapse from fatigue
  • Straining to urinate or defecate
  • Overheating or heatstroke
  • Poison (rat bait, snail pellets, human medication)
  • Faster, harder or excessive panting or breathing changes
