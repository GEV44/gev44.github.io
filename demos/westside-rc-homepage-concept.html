From a crowded menu to four clear doors

The RFP describes a cluttered menu that overwhelms visitors, and a hard mobile experience. This concept shows the direction. The audit would confirm the exact pages.

Before (as described in the RFP)
  • Cluttered menu interface
  • Content-heavy pages
  • Hard to use on a phone
  • No style guide, so colors and banners vary
After (this concept)
  • Four doors: Families, People we serve, Providers, Jobs
  • Contact and language always visible
  • Key pages no more than two clicks from home
  • One purpose per page, plain English
