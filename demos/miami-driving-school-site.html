Teen drivers in Florida

50HOURS OF SUPERVISED PRACTICE

Drivers under 18 need 50 hours of supervised driving, 10 of them at night, and must hold a learner's permit for a year or until they turn 18, whichever comes first. Pro Driver Package 1 covers all of those hours.

Ages 15 to 17 can also take the 6-hour DETS course and the Florida online permit exam through us.

Quick answers

The assistant in the corner knows these too, and can start a booking request.

Do I need an appointment?

For the driver license package, the road test and the TLSAE course, no appointment is needed. Come to the office Monday to Saturday, starting at 9 AM. For the knowledge exam, appointments and walk-ins are both welcome.

Who can take the knowledge exam here?

First-time drivers, teen or adult, and non-U.S. residents who are eligible for a Florida license.

Is the car included for the road test?

Yes. The school car is provided for the road test in every package that includes it, and in the road test option.

Where do the lessons take place?

On real roads in a new car with dual brakes. Each lesson lasts 2 hours and we pick you up and drop you off at your home or another place you choose.

Do I need a permit before my first lesson?

The student must have a valid driver's license or a learner's permit. If you do not have one yet, you can get it through the school.

Which package is meant for insurance?

The Bronze, Silver and Gold packages with road test include a certificate of completion for insurance.
