Refunds & cancellations

Plans change. The process is short.

Everything happens inside your account, with no phone calls needed.

  1. Log inUse the email you booked with. Guest checkouts get an account and a sign-in code by email.
  2. Open the bookingYour exact terms show right there: a refundable-until date, or your change-of-mind and credit dates.
  3. Request the refundFull or partial. Deals with a change-of-mind window show a Cancel booking button instead.
  4. Team reviews and confirmsConfirmed by email. Approved refunds can take up to 10 business days to reach your card.

Cancellation terms vary by deal and refunds go back to the original payment method. No-shows are not refundable. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law are not limited.

Good to know

Questions before booking

Do escapes include flights?

No. TravelPearls is land-only, so you can use frequent-flyer points or your preferred airline.

Are there booking or membership fees?

No platform surcharge and no membership fees. Only hotel-set amounts, such as peak-date or extra-guest surcharges, can apply and they are shown on the escape page.

How do refunds and cancellations work?

Terms are negotiated per property and shown when you choose a room. Request a refund from My Bookings; approved refunds can take up to 10 business days to reach your card. Some deals have a change-of-mind window followed by 12 months of credit.

Can I change my dates instead?

Often yes. Email hello@travelpearls.com.au and the team will do their best, subject to availability and supplier approval.

Which currency do you charge in?

Australian dollars only, for prices, balances and refunds.

I cannot find my confirmation.

Log into My Account with the email you booked with. Guest checkouts get an account automatically, using a sign-in code sent by email.

Can I ask for a bedding setup that is not listed?

Email the team and they will confirm availability with the hotel directly.

What standard of property do you work with?

Five-star and recognised premium boutique only. Each property is personally inspected by the team or sourced through a long-standing relationship with the hotel; mid-range and budget stock is not listed.

How are the rates exclusive?

The team works directly with hotel revenue managers to secure private allotments during shoulder and value windows. Each advertised rate is benchmarked against public booking sites before it is published.

Where is TravelPearls based?

At 55 Pyrmont Bridge Rd, Pyrmont (Sydney), and operated entirely by Australian travel specialists. Concierge support runs during AEST business hours.

Is paying online safe?

Bookings run through Stripe with fraud protection, and your card details are never stored on TravelPearls servers.
