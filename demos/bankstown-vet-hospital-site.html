Ask the assistant

New here?

New clients are welcome by phone or online. Bring what you have:

  • Vaccination records
  • Desexing history
  • Current medications
  • Recent blood tests, imaging or specialist reports
  • Insurance details

Paying and insurance

Visa, Mastercard, EFTPOS, cash and mobile payments, due at the time of service. VetPay finance may be available for approved clients. Surgery needs a deposit and the vet gives an estimate first. Insured? Pay, then claim from your insurer; the hospital supplies itemised invoices and paperwork.

Quick answers

The assistant in the corner knows these too.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, consultations are by appointment, which keeps waiting times down and gives each pet the right amount of time. Book online any time or phone 9790 1101.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give 24 hours notice for consults and 48 hours for surgery or procedures. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged a failed appointment fee.

Can the vet diagnose over the phone?

No. Without examining your pet an accurate diagnosis and treatment plan is not possible, so the team will ask you to bring your pet in.

What if it is an emergency after hours?

The hospital does not provide overnight monitoring. Call SASH Alexandria 8913 4000, Sydney Uni Vet Teaching Hospital 9351 3437, Animal Referral Hospital 9758 8666 or VSOS Miranda 8376 8787.

Does my pet have a history of aggression?

Please tell the team when booking. The time may be adjusted or extra staff allocated for everyone's safety.
