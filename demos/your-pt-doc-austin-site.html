Your path

From pain to performance

  1. A free discovery call

    A free 15 minute call with a Doctor of Physical Therapy. You tell your story, what you have already tried and what you want to get back to. By the end you will know whether it is the right fit and what the next step looks like.

  2. Identify the root cause and build your plan

    A comprehensive 60 minute evaluation and movement assessment, one-on-one. Together you define where you are now, where you want to be and the exact steps to get there. You leave the first visit with a clear, personalised recovery plan.

  3. Get back to what you love

    Personalised one-on-one care, plus guidance through a strength app, to rebuild strength, confidence and resilience, and to stay active and strong long term.

Your physical therapist

Dr. Gabriel Dunham, PT, DPT

Gabriel grew up in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, and immigrated to the United States at the age of 9, an experience that taught him resilience and adaptability. He has been an active member of the soccer community since 2006, playing for Pflugerville High School and collegiate soccer at the University of Mary Hardin Baylor.

His own sports-related challenges drive his commitment to helping others. He combines clinical expertise with empathy, and being multilingual in English, Spanish and Portuguese helps him break down communication barriers that often exist in healthcare settings.

Questions

Booking and what to expect

How do I get started?

Book a free 15 minute discovery call to discuss your goals, what has been tried before and whether it is a good fit. If it makes sense, a comprehensive evaluation is scheduled from there.

Do you take insurance?

It is a cash-based practice. HSA and FSA are accepted, and superbills are provided so you can submit them to your insurance for potential reimbursement. Payment plans are offered too.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Comfortable clothes you can move in. Imaging reports or previous evaluation notes are welcome but not required.

Do you offer telehealth or in-person only?

Both: in-person care at the Austin location and telehealth. Many patients benefit from a hybrid approach.

How long are sessions?

Initial evaluations are 60 minutes. Follow-up sessions are typically 45 to 60 minutes of focused one-on-one time.

Who will I see?

Every session is doctor-led by Dr. Gabriel Dunham, PT, DPT. The site says there are no rotating techs and no rushed appointments.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking is available on site at 15201 Burnet Rd, Building C, with easy access from Burnet Road.

How soon will I hear back?

The site says messages get a reply within one business day. You can also call or text (737) 259-6850.

Do I need a referral?

The practice has a blog post on direct access in Texas. Ask Dr. Gabriel about your own situation.
