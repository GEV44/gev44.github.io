01 · about

Great hair is an extension of who you are

Located in the heart of Phoenix's Melrose District, Humble Brag was created by stylists for both clients and creatives: a space where elevated artistry meets genuine connection.

Our approach is rooted in precision, creativity and respect for individuality. Whether you're maintaining your signature look or evolving into something new, the team brings thoughtful consultation, advanced techniques and a client-first mindset.

What makes us different is the way we show up, for our clients and for each other: collaboration over competition.

A humble brag isn't about showing off. It's about knowing your worth.

05 · good to know

Before you book

Cancellation policy. We ask for a credit card to hold your appointment. Less than 24 hours notice is charged 50% of the reserved service, and a no-show is charged 100%. It lets us offer the slot to standby guests and keeps our stylists' schedules full.
Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Consultations are complimentary and we can give you a firm price once we've seen your hair.

Why are prices shown with a plus sign?

They are base prices. The total depends on the treatments needed, the length of your hair and other challenges.

Do you sell products?

Yes, we carry shampoos, conditioners, masks, serums and styling products. Online shopping is on the way. See the team in the salon for details.

I'm a stylist. Can I join?

We have a limited number of chairs to rent in a community of artists. Use the form on our Join Our Team page or email us, and tell us whether you're licensed, a student or just interested.

Can I book online?

Yes. The Book Appointment button on our website opens the salon's Square booking page. You can also call 602-900-7360, or send a request through the assistant and the team will confirm.

Which hours should I trust?

Our home page and contact page list different hours, so please call before a first visit and the team will confirm the day you want. Holiday hours may vary.

Do you do color correction, extensions or perms?

Yes, and they are priced upon consultation, because every head of hair is different. The consultation is complimentary, so you'll know the number before anything starts.

Do you do bridal and special-occasion hair?

An updo is $65 and a bridal updo with a trial run is $100 (base prices). For a wedding party or a specific date, call the team so they can plan time and chairs.

What is a "humble brag", anyway?

It's that quiet confidence you carry when you know you look and feel your best, without needing to say a word. It's for our clients and our stylists alike. Since 2018 we've built a space where that confidence comes from genuine connection, elevated artistry and a culture of support.

Where exactly are you?

4200 N. 7th Avenue, Phoenix AZ 85013, in the Melrose District of Central Phoenix, steps from Copper Star Coffee. The SunKissed Beauty studio is inside the salon, at the corner of 7th Ave. and Mackenzie; if you're heading to Marisol, take a seat in the main lobby and someone will assist you.
