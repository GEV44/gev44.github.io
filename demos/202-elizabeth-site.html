Good to know

Quick answers

Check-in and check-out

Check-in is at 3 PM and check-out at 11 AM. Early arrival or late check-out depends on the day's business and is not guaranteed, so please tell the team in advance.

Cancellation

As a small boutique hotel the policy is strict: cancellations within 24 hours or less of arrival incur 100% of the accommodation cost. When booking you will see which options offer free cancellation, and travel insurance is recommended.

Parking

Please contact the hotel for parking options. Complimentary parking comes with the Date Night and Le Love Cinema packages.

Getting around

Central Station takes you to Bondi Beach; from Circular Quay you can catch a ferry to Manly. Local public transport makes the itinerary easy.

Is it quiet?

Yes. Rooms are soundproofed for privacy, even though the CBD is minutes away.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. 202 Elizabeth is a dog-friendly hotel, with a puppy welcome drink and doggy menus on request. Specific pet fees and rules are not on the website, so the team confirms them when you book.

What is on the garden menu?

Artisanal coffee, a variety of fresh cold-pressed juices and locally made pastries, served among the greenery of the courtyard garden.

Is the hotel sustainable?

Every room is designed with sustainability in mind, and the amenities are hand-selected and vegan.

Do you have a restaurant?

The site describes a garden menu and a rooftop with cocktails, but no separate restaurant. The team can point you to nearby favourites in Surry Hills.

What is a room like if I want peace and quiet?

Every room is soundproofed, with large windows and dreamy beds. The Oxford adds the hotel's largest balcony; The Lizzy sits on the top floor.
