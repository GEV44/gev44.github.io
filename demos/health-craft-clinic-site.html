Services

Six ways the team can help.

01
Physiotherapy & active rehab

Reduce pain, improve physical function and prevent injuries through manual therapy and exercise. Sports injuries, work injuries and chronic pain management.

  • Assessment
  • Manual therapy
  • Active rehabilitation
  • Education
02
Pelvic floor physiotherapy

Private, comfortable and compassionate care for bladder and bowel control, sexual function, and abdominal and back muscle support.

  • Pelvic floor assessment
  • Incontinence treatment
  • Pre and postnatal care
03
Acupuncture & TCM

Relieve pain, reduce inflammation and support overall well-being with a holistic approach, from acupuncture to tuina, cupping, moxibustion and gua sha.

  • Acupuncture
  • Tuina
  • Cupping
  • Moxibustion
  • Gua sha
04
Osteopathy

Gentle, whole-body manual therapy for a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions, including back pain, neck pain and headaches.

05
Clinical Pilates physiotherapy

Controlled, guided exercise that improves core strength, flexibility, balance and posture, and supports injury rehabilitation.

06
Orthotic & brace fitting

Evaluation, recommendation, measurement, adjustment and education for proper use and comfort.

Your first visit

What to expect.

Initial assessments take 45 to 60 minutes; follow-ups are usually 30 to 45.

Initial consultation

We discuss your health history, current concerns and goals.

Assessment

A thorough physical examination to understand your condition and contributing factors.

Treatment plan

Together you create a plan tailored to your needs and lifestyle.

Begin treatment

Your first treatment session.

Bring with you

Your health card, any referral letters, relevant imaging or reports, a list of current medications, and comfortable clothing that allows movement and assessment.

Cancellations

24 hours' notice is needed to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee.

FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No referral is needed for physiotherapy, acupuncture or osteopathy, although some insurance plans may require one for coverage. Check with your insurer.

How long is each appointment?

Initial assessments are typically 45 to 60 minutes. Follow-up treatments are usually 30 to 45 minutes.

Do you offer evening appointments?

Yes, Monday to Friday until 7:00 pm. The clinic is closed on weekends.

What languages do you speak?

The team is fluent in English, Cantonese and Mandarin. The clinic's site is also available in Traditional and Simplified Chinese.

How do I book?

Use the assistant button on this page, call (604) 563-4411, or use Book Now on the clinic's website. The clinic is open Monday to Friday, 11:30 am to 7:00 pm.

Is parking available?

Metered street parking on Broadway and surrounding streets, and the clinic is easy to reach by public transit.

What is the cancellation policy?

24 hours' notice. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee.
