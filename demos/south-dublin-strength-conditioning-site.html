01 · Ethos
No egos. Hard-working, open-minded people. Results, and fun.
BarbellBodyweightGymnasticsKettlebellsOlympic lifting

Whether you are just beginning or more experienced, SDSC helps you build and keep a positive approach to health and fitness. The focus is a strong foundation, both in physical capacity and mental fortitude.

The methods vary, from barbell training and bodyweight movements to gymnastics, kettlebells and Olympic lifting, always with appropriate intensity and the belief that fundamentals done well are the key to any goal.

Weekend warrior, part-time or professional athlete, or someone taking the first step in their fitness journey: it is for you.

How it works

Week oneA free one-to-one assessment: you meet the coaches and talk through your goals and any worries.
Movement screeningThe coaches look at how you move, so training starts from where you are, not from a generic template.
Coached sessionsSessions at 9:30am, 12pm and 5-9pm on weekdays. The workout is planned, you turn up and get stuck in.

06 · Good to know

Questions

I have never trained. Is that okay?

Yes. Start with a free one-to-one assessment. You meet the team, talk through your goals, and they take it from there.

Which membership should I choose?

Eight classes a month is €150 and unlimited is €199. If you are unsure how often you will come, talk it through in your assessment.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking is available on-site at 10-11 Greenmount Avenue.

I travel for work. Can I keep training?

Members get the True Coach app for travel and distance workouts, and online support from the coaches.

Do you do nutrition?

Nutrition, behaviour and exercise goal setting is part of the membership, with monthly InBody scans to track progress.

Are weekends covered?

The website lists Monday to Friday, 6:30am to 9pm. Weekend hours are not published, so please ask the team if you need Saturday or Sunday.

I have an injury or a medical condition.

Raise it at your free assessment so the coaches can build around it, and check with your doctor too. The assistant on this page does not give medical advice.

How do I get in touch?

Call 086 864 9732, email info@southdublinsc.ie, or use the free assessment form with your name, email, phone number and preferred day and time.
