About us

24th Street Dental Biltmore is a family-owned and operated dental office in Biltmore, Phoenix, not a large corporate chain. It offers every dental specialty in one office and cares for the entire family. The team includes eight dentists, a master ceramist, hygienists and lab technicians.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How much is the new patient special?

$69, and it includes X-rays and an exam with the dentist. Same-day appointments are available.

Can I get a second opinion on a treatment plan?

Yes. Bring in your treatment plan for an expert review and second opinion at no charge, and the team will let you know if they can match or beat the estimate.

What if I have a dental emergency when you're closed?

Call or text 602 468 1135. When the office is closed, the phone is forwarded to a member of the family's personal cell so they can help right away.

Do you have a plan for patients without insurance?

Yes, two loyalty savings plans with no insurance forms or co-pays: $320 per year per person for two cleanings, or $595 for four cleanings. CareCredit financing is also offered.

Do you help with dental anxiety?

Yes, sedation dentistry is offered, along with the complimentary Comfort Menu of noise-cancelling headphones, weighted blankets and neck pillows.

Is there a cosmetic consultation?

Yes, a complimentary cosmetic consultation with the dentist (limited to one per week), and a Free Smile Virtual Consult online.
