Quick answers

Are you open on Tuesdays?

No, the office is closed on Tuesdays and Sundays. We are open Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and select Saturdays.

What if my child has a dental emergency?

Call (305) 428-2878 right away during office hours. We provide same-day appointments for urgent cases such as a chipped or knocked-out tooth, severe toothache, bleeding gums or infection.

My child is nervous. What do you do?

We slow down, listen and keep the visit positive. The office has TV shows, cartoons and a toy wall, and children's sedation is available for kids with dental anxiety, sensory sensitivities or longer procedures.

Do you see children with special needs?

Yes. Special needs dentistry offers customised plans, a calming environment and behavioural support, with free parking and Medicaid for children.

Do you offer braces for adults?

Orthodontics is for children, teens and adults, with clear braces, metal braces and aligners.

We do not have insurance. What are our options?

The in-house membership plan is designed for families without insurance. There is also a new patient special. See the prices above.
