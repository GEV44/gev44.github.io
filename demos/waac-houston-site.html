About us

West Alabama Animal Clinic treats each animal as if it were their own, with consistent, compassionate pet care. The clinic focuses on preventative medicine for dogs and cats and has served its Houston neighborhood since 1988.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What pets do you treat?

Dogs and cats.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 7:00am-6:00pm and Saturday 8:00am-2:00pm. Closed Sundays.

Do you see emergencies?

During business hours, yes. Please call 713-528-0818 before arriving. After hours, current clients can text the same number for triage help; if your pet can't wait, they recommend VERGI in Houston.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash, debit, check with valid ID, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, AmEx, CareCredit and Scratchpay. Payment is due at the time of service.

What do boarding pets need?

Pets must be free from fleas, ticks and intestinal parasites and up to date on required vaccines.
