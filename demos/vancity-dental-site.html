Treatments

What the team can do for you.

Pick a topic. The text comes from the clinic's own service pages.

Diagnostic check-ups

Toothache and gum disease are preventable if caught early. The dentists look for small cavities and failing fillings, using digital x-rays only when necessary. Check-ups are recommended every six months, and small problems can sometimes be restored without freezing.

  • iTero digital scanner: a quick 3D scan with no radiation, used instead of plaster impressions
  • Digital dental x-rays
  • Oral and gum exam at every visit

Restorative dentistry

Damage to enamel lets bacteria in, so a chipped or decayed tooth should be repaired soon to prevent infection. The dentist suggests the most suitable technique for the damage, aiming for a tooth that looks natural and is strong enough to chew on.

  • Small to medium cavities and chips: tooth-coloured composite fillings
  • Larger cavities in back teeth: an inlay or onlay in porcelain or gold alloy
  • Badly damaged teeth: a crown, often all-ceramic
  • Cracked, chipped or discoloured teeth: dental bonding, typically without sedation or anesthetic

Periodontal and preventative care

Your gums are checked at every exam. Teeth are scaled and polished to remove calculus and lower the risk of gum disease. Most people need a professional cleaning every six months, ideally booked with the check-up.

Endodontics (root canal)

Root canal treatment can save a badly infected tooth: the infected pulp is removed, the chamber is cleaned and sealed, and the tooth is restored later, usually with a crown. If you have a constant toothache, call the clinic.

Invisalign

Custom clear aligners worn at least 22 hours a day, swapped every two weeks. Treatment averages about a year. An initial consultation tells you whether it is right for you; the team takes x-rays, photos and impressions to plan it.

Dental implants

Implants act as artificial tooth roots in the jawbone. They can support single crowns, bridges or removable implant-supported dentures that clip firmly in place, and they help prevent jawbone loss.

Conscious sedation

For dental fear, a long gap since your last visit or lengthy treatment. A sedative pill is taken about an hour before the appointment; you stay conscious but deeply relaxed, and may remember little. Every patient is carefully screened first.

Cosmetic dentistry, whitening and Botox

Cosmetic work is custom-designed for natural results. An initial consultation starts with what you would most like to change about your smile, and each treatment is fully explained.

  • Replace old silver amalgam fillings with tooth-coloured composite or porcelain
  • Porcelain or all-ceramic crowns for worn or damaged teeth
  • Porcelain bridges for missing teeth
  • Veneers: thin custom porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth
  • Teeth whitening
  • Botox for fine lines, given by a dentist trained in facial anatomy

Good to know

Quick answers.

How often should I come in?

The clinic recommends a check-up and a professional cleaning every six months.

I am nervous about the dentist.

Everyone at the clinic works to put nervous patients at ease, and conscious sedation is available when that is not enough.

Is conscious sedation safe?

The clinic takes every precaution and screens each patient very carefully first. The sedative is oral, the dose can be adjusted, and you stay conscious, though you may remember little of the treatment.

Will treatment hurt?

The clinic's promise is painless dentistry with modern technology, and some small cavities can be restored without freezing. If you are anxious, tell the team when you book.

Why do my gums need checking at every visit?

Gum disease is a chronic bacterial infection that can cause bad breath, bleeding gums and eventually tooth loss, and anyone can develop it. The dentists check your gums at every exam, and regular scaling and polishing lowers the risk.

What if I have old silver fillings?

They can be replaced with tooth-coloured fillings made of composite resin or porcelain. Dr. Komagata is especially interested in mercury-free, biocompatible materials.

Do you do x-rays?

Digital x-rays, only when necessary, plus the iTero scanner for 3D images with no radiation.

Do you work with my insurance?

The team contacts your insurance online when possible. Have your policy information with you and ask the front desk.

How do I get a CDCP appointment?

Read the clinic's CDCP information page for eligibility and how to apply, then request an appointment and tell the team you are a CDCP patient.

I have a toothache.

Please call 604.687.1137 so the team can advise. A toothache that is constant, or fever and swelling, should not wait.
