Before you come

Common questions

Where do I start if I am new?

The clinic recommends a private initial consultation. It involves a thorough assessment to discuss your needs and goals, followed by a personalised program built around you.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Friday 7am to 8pm and Saturday 8am to 4:30pm at Sydney CBD, Bondi Junction and South Yarra. Sunday hours are not published, so the team will confirm them.

How big are the Pilates groups?

Semi private sessions have a maximum of 4 people and run for 50 minutes. Private sessions run for 60 or 30 minutes, and duet sessions for 60 minutes. All sessions are physiotherapist led.

Can I claim a rebate?

The site mentions on-the-spot rebates from Medicare and Private Health Insurance, marked with an asterisk. Because conditions apply, the team will confirm what is available for your situation.

Can I book online?

Yes. The clinic takes bookings through its online booking page, or by phone. The assistant on this page can also pass a request to the team.

What does the clinic mean by boutique?

Small groups, modern facilities, a hands-on approach, non-invasive treatment and a commitment to recovery, with clear communication and trust between patient and physiotherapist.
