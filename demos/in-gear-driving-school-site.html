Quick answers

Manual or automatic?

Manual lets you drive both types after you pass. Automatic can suit you if gears are hard, traffic makes you nervous, or you want to focus on observation and control.

Can I choose a female instructor?

Yes. IN Gear has male and female instructors.

Where do the lessons start?

Home, college or work pickup.

Can I book on a weekend?

The school lists Mon to Sun, 8am to 11pm. The team confirms your exact slot.

What skills does the pre-test run cover?

The school lists reversing around the corner, the turnabout (3-point turn), parking and observation techniques, with mock test practice and a final run before your test.

What if I have no car for the test?

Pre-test car hire is part of the service: a dual control, fully insured car, manual or automatic, at the test centres listed above.

Do you help new drivers with insurance?

After 12 lessons you receive a stamped certificate for a 1 year no claims discount, and the school has a Save on Insurance page too.

Do I need to be a complete beginner?

No. The school also offers refresher and motorway lessons, and road safety and advanced lessons.

How do I pay or book a block?

The assistant takes your request and the team confirms packs, block booking discounts and payment with you directly.
