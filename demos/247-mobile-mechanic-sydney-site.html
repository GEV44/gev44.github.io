Good to know

FAQ

Do you give a warranty on the work?

Yes, 12 months on all services and the parts they install. If there is an issue in that time, call them.

How do I book a call-out?

They are an on-call service. Call, describe the issue and they meet you at your place.

Do you use genuine parts?

You can choose between genuine parts and quality parts. Tell them your preference and they fix the car accordingly.

How long will I wait?

They try to reach you as soon as possible. It depends on your location and the day, and they can give a rough estimate when you call.

Do you work on all makes and models?

The site says they work on all makes and models, and that their mechanics can fix any car or truck.

Do you come out evenings and weekends?

Yes. They describe the service as Monday to Sunday, 24 hours, and say they will come to your home or office even on evenings and weekends.

Can I get a quote online?

The site says you can get a quote and book a service online, any time. You can also call, or send the details through the assistant.

What equipment do they use?

The latest diagnostic equipment, so the fault is found properly and the car is repaired or serviced in a timely way.

Where do the repairs happen?

At your place. The service is mobile, so the mechanic brings the tools to your home, workplace or roadside spot rather than you taking the car in.

What if I am not sure what is wrong?

Describe the symptoms when you call or in the request. The team can arrange a diagnostic computer check to find the fault.

Can I book a pre-purchase inspection or log book service?

Yes, both are on their services list. Use the assistant to send a request or call to arrange a time.
