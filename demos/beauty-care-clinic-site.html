Questions

Good to know

Do you offer a consultation?

Yes. The clinic invites you to phone for a complimentary consultation: 0424 586 096.

Which treatment is right for my skin?

That is for the team to advise at a consultation. This page and the assistant do not give skin or medical advice.

Do you work on Sundays?

No. The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm, and closed on Sunday.

How long do lash refills take?

On the menu: Classic Natural refill at 2 weeks is $45; Classic Full $55; Hybrid $55; Russian $65; Mega 8D $95; Mega 12D $155. Three-week refills cost $10 more.

Which skincare brands do you use?

Fleur De Mer, Sincerity and Algologie, with homecare plans to extend your results.

Payment, deposits, parking?

These are not on the website, so the team will confirm. Call 0424 586 096.
