Classes

Pick a style, every one scales to your level

CrossTraining

Safe, efficient, functional movements done with high variety and intensity. Workouts bring in powerlifting, Olympic lifting and gymnastics, adapted to any fitness level, in an inclusive and fun atmosphere with a competitive, supportive community.

Highly trained coaches on the floor

Powerlifting

Dial in your squat, bench and deadlift technique, or learn the lifts for the first time. Competitive lifters follow an 11-week periodised program that builds toward a meet, with competition rules taught and an in-house mini powerlifting meet.

Downtown Vancouver's powerlifting specialists

CardioBlast

A 45-minute bootcamp with full-body bodyweight, kettlebell and dumbbell work, tailored to your experience. Fun but intense: show up, sweat, work hard, maybe laugh a little.

45 minutes

Strength + Conditioning

A full-body class without the competition of CrossTraining. A strength portion of compound barbell, kettlebell and dumbbell lifts, then a conditioning portion of intervals or a circuit that isn't scored.

Strength first, then conditioning

Team Training

Private sessions for your sports team or work team, useful for sport-specific training and team building. Beside Waterfront SkyTrain, so it fits a work lunch break.

Tell us about your group

Kids Strength

A class for children (around ages 7-14) to build strength and confidence in how they move.

Mom & Baby Fitness

An 8-week specialty program for postpartum recovery and fitness. Bring your baby, rebuild your strength and meet other moms without needing a babysitter. Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10am and 11am, in focused blocks.

Questions

I have never lifted. Is this for me?

Yes. Beginners are welcome. Most people start with the free intro and the 4-session Onramp so they are comfortable with technique before joining classes.

Do I have to compete in powerlifting?

No. Competing is optional. If you do want to, the program builds toward a meet and meet-day coaching is available.

I have an old injury.

The coaches scale movements and work around injuries. Mention it in your intro, and check with your doctor or physio for anything medical.

I am visiting Vancouver. Can I train?

Fill in the visitor form on the website or message the team on WhatsApp and they will email you about booking classes.

Is there an app?

Yes, a Thunder and Lightning Athletic Club app is available on the App Store and Google Play.
