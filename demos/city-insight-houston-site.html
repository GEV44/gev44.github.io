Buyer's guide

From first meeting to closing.

Before you search

Meet a local expert about style, price and location, and get pre-approved for a mortgage. Bring paystubs, W2s and bank statements.

Search for homes

Your agent schedules showings and uses tools that make sure you see every available home that meets your criteria.

Make an offer

Your agent prepares the offer based on the price and terms you choose. It may take a few tries to get it right.

Contract

The contract sets a timeline to obtain financing and time to inspect the home. Your agent explains your rights and responsibilities.

In escrow

You and the seller have agreed on price and terms, and the home is effectively held for you until closing.

Closing

Order the appraisal, inspect, review terms with the lender. A title company or attorney facilitates the transfer of funds and ownership.

Good to know: as the buyer, your agent's commission is paid by the seller in almost all circumstances, which means your representation costs you nothing, according to the buyer's guide.

Questions

Quick answers.

Does it cost me anything to have a buyer's agent?

According to the buyer's guide, the agent's commission is paid by the seller of the home in almost all circumstances, so your representation costs you nothing.

What documents do I need to get pre-approved?

Paystubs, W2s and bank statements. Knowing what you can afford early is crucial to a successful home search.

How does the contract protect me as a buyer?

In most cases the contract gives you a timeline to obtain financing as well as time to inspect the physical condition of the home. Your agent explains your rights and responsibilities before you sign.

What happens when the home is in escrow?

You and the seller have agreed to the price and terms, and the home is effectively held for you until closing. During this time you order the appraisal, conduct an inspection and review terms with the lender.

What if I am relocating to Houston?

Relocation is one of the specialties listed for agents on the site, alongside buyer's agent, listing agent, foreclosure, short-sale and consulting work. The cost of living calculator and neighborhood safety ratings help you compare areas from afar.

Do I have to be pre-approved before I search?

The guide recommends it. Meeting a local expert to talk through style, price and location, and getting pre-approved for a mortgage, comes before the home search begins.

Can the agents help if I rent instead of buy?

Yes. The Rent section covers apartments, houses and townhomes, with renting tips, a moving checklist and neighborhood safety ratings to help you decide before you sign a lease.

How do I get a price estimate for my own home?

Use the Home Value Estimator on the Sell page. It gives a quick, free estimate, shows nearby sales and market trends, and gets more accurate when you add the lot size, year built, bedrooms, baths and any improvements.

Can I get alerts for new listings?

Yes. The Buy section offers property alerts, and it also lists new listings, open houses, new construction, condos, lots and the luxury collection so you can browse by type.

Is there a live seminar?

Yes, the site lists a live home buying seminar. The team will confirm the next date.

Do you have a referral program?

The Events page mentions a referral program for every agent you refer who joins the team.

Can I list my own property?

Yes, there is an Add Property option on the site for submitting a property.
