Our story

A Vancouver address for over a century

1913

Opened on March 15, designed by Canadian architect W.T. Whiteway, who also designed the Sun Tower. Lodging, dining and entertainment in one place, next to the new Hudson's Bay flagship.

1940s

Known as the Sportsman's Hotel. NHL players made it home, and owner Coleman Hall built a St. Regis hockey team before going on to own the first Vancouver Canucks.

2000s

Bought by the Macdonald family, who set out to marry the heritage with a contemporary, big city feel.

2008

The 100th anniversary year brought an $11 million top-to-bottom renovation. Every winter each room is taken out of service, stripped, deep cleaned and refurbished.

Art on the walls

More than twenty-two original pieces by five artists hang through the public spaces, mostly abstract, from Hilario Gutierrez, Linda Murphy, Simon Addyman and others.
