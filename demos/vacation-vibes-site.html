Inside the Short Escape

Day by day, no guesswork.

01
Arrival and a cultural evening

Welcome at Bandaranaike International Airport, transfer towards Kandy with a stop at the Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage, then a traditional Kandyan dance and drum show. Overnight in Kandy.

02
Temple visit and the road south

The sacred Temple of the Tooth, then onwards to the southern coast, known for golden beaches and a laid-back atmosphere.

03
Southern coast exploration

Beaches, wildlife and historic landmarks along the south, including Galle Fort.

04
Departure

Transfer for your flight home. Hotels listed for the trip: Earl's Regent and Water's Edge Villa.

The real site has Track Booking and Track Visa pages in its menu.

Yes. Group tours with expert guides and corporate travel for meetings, incentives and team retreats are listed under services.

The signature experiences (Leopard Safari in Yala, Nine Arches and scenic train ride, Sigiriya rock climb, tea plantation visit and southern beach sunset) each have a Customize Trip option on the real site.
