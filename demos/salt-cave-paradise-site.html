About us

Salt Cave Paradise is a modern saltwater therapy and relaxation centre by the sea in the heart of Balbriggan. It is part of a larger organisation with saltwater therapy and halotherapy at its heart, which also runs Salt Cave Halotherapy, set up in 2018.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What should I bring?

For the Salt Water Cave and Jacuzzi bring swimwear, a towel and flip-flops. For the dry salt rooms you stay fully clothed.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please give 48 hours' notice. Deposits are non-refundable but can be transferred to a future booking.

How do I get there without a car?

Take the Irish Rail train from Connolly (35-40 minutes); both buildings are a short walk from Balbriggan station.

Is there parking?

Two spaces at Convent Lane, normally full; street parking nearby and the train station car park.

How do I book for a group of 4 or more?

Call 087 961 9852 or email info@saltcaveparadise.ie.
