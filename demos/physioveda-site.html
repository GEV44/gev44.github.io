Good to know

Questions people ask

Can I book on the same day?

Same-day bookings are listed as available. Call or WhatsApp 800 8332 or leave a request with the assistant.

Which clinic is open on Sunday?

Al Karama is open every day, 9am to 9pm. Al Wasl is open Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm.

Do you treat children or elderly patients?

The site mentions elderly care at home. For children, please ask the team.

How much is a session?

Prices are not published on the site. The team will confirm by phone or WhatsApp.

Is there parking?

Parking is not described on the site. One patient review mentions limited parking, so please ask before you travel.

Do you treat stroke or neurological conditions?

Yes, stroke and neurological rehabilitation is listed, with a focus on balance, coordination and independence.

What happens at the first visit?

Care starts with an assessment, then an individual plan, and progress is reviewed as your needs change. For posture correction that means a detailed posture and movement analysis; for sports massage, a short consultation about your concerns, fitness level and injuries.

Do you do home visits for massage as well?

The sports massage page says professional sports massage at home is offered. Area coverage and availability are confirmed by the team.

Are the therapists licensed?

The site states the physiotherapists are DHA-licensed, with experience across musculoskeletal, sports, neurological and post-surgical conditions.

Who founded the clinic?

Physioveda was founded by a physiotherapist to offer patient-focused, evidence-based care with empathy as well as expertise. The mission is to enhance quality of life through safe, effective and ethical treatments.

Can you tell me what is wrong before I come?

No. Diagnosis needs a physiotherapist's assessment. The assistant does not give medical advice.
