Do you accept dogs?

Yes, dogs are always welcome. Under 25 lbs, house trained, leashed in public areas and never alone in the room. One dog per room, with a one-time 50 dollar cleaning fee at check-out.

How do I get here without a car?

The Yaletown-Roundhouse Canada Line station is steps away, with direct service to Vancouver International Airport. The hotel Bike Fleet is at guests' disposal and the False Creek Water Taxis are a short walk.

Is there parking?

Secured 24/7 valet in the private garage under the hotel. Garage height is 6 ft 6 in. Some special offers include complimentary valet.

What if I cancel late?

Late cancellations and no-shows are charged one night plus taxes. Prepay and Save bookings are non-refundable, and very busy dates may need full prepayment.

Is smoking allowed?

No. OPUS is 100% non-smoking, and in-room parties are not permitted.
