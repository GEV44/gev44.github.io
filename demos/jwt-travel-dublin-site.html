Questions people ask

Quick answers

Can a school tour be adapted to our class?

Yes. All itineraries can be adapted to your classroom requirements, and the team supports you with pre-tour preparation, risk assessment and on-tour assistance.

What does a Camino guided tour cover?

A week-long, fully escorted walk of about 100 km into Santiago de Compostela, so you can receive your own Compostela. Choose the Camino Frances, the Coastal Camino Portugues, the Camino Ingles or Finisterre and Muxia.

Is there an easier Camino option?

Yes: a 9-night Easy Walk Camino tour and three Camino Coach and Walking tours, back by popular demand.

Can supporters travel with Leinster Rugby?

JWT organises official travel packages for supporters to European Champions Cup and United Rugby Championship away games, as the official travel partner of Leinster Rugby.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Petriva Ltd T/A JWT Travel holds Travel Agent licence TA 0804 and Tour Operator licence TO 273 from the Irish Aviation Authority.
