About us

South Dublin Vets is a well-equipped small animal practice. Mark Heffernan took over in Terenure in 2006 and opened the Dundrum clinic in early 2011. Every animal that comes through the door is treated like one of the team's own.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Where are your clinics?

Terenure Veterinary Hospital at 30 Whitehall Rd, D12ET82, and Dundrum Veterinary Clinic in Dundrum Village Centre, Main Street, D14K8H4.

What do I do in an emergency?

Call Terenure on (01) 455 5362 or Dundrum on (01) 296 5876.

Can I renew my pet's prescription without an appointment?

Yes. Use the prescription renewal option on our website.

How do I register my pet?

Register your pet with us on our online platform.

Do you offer dental care for pets?

Yes. From preventative products to a routine scale and polish and, where there is significant decay, tooth extraction.

Do you refer for specialised surgery?

Yes. We perform many surgeries ourselves and work with a number of excellent centres for more specialised cases.
