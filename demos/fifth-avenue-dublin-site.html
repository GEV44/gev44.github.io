FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What is a Silent Appointment?

A treatment where you enjoy comfortable silence. You control the conversation, and only your treatment is discussed. Choose it when you book at no extra charge.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, online and in the salon. They have no expiry date and can be used at any Fifth Avenue branch. They are non-refundable.

Can I book for a group?

Yes, with a minimum €10 deposit per person. Email what each person wants and arrive 15 minutes early. No group discounts are offered.

What if I arrive late?

Please arrive 5-10 minutes early; arriving late may shorten your treatment but the full price still applies.

Do you have special offers?

Yes, monthly special offers; check the Offers page or ask the team what's on this month.

Which brands do you use?

Essie, OPI, CND, Yon-Ka and Carita, plus Fifth Avenue's own body scrubs, hand creams and foot creams.
