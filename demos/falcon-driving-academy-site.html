Test your rules of the road

Questions from the Falcon tips page. Tap to reveal.

When can you overtake on the left?

When the vehicle ahead of you is turning right; if you are turning left immediately after overtaking; in slow moving lanes in traffic.

What rules apply to a box junction?

Do not enter unless your exit is clear, but you may enter when you are turning right (one car per turn).

What is the safe distance behind another vehicle?

A 2 second gap, a 4 second gap in wet weather and a 10 second gap on snow and ice.

What is the minimum tread depth on a tyre?

1.6 mm, and 1 mm for classic cars and motorbikes.

How far from a junction or bend can you legally park?

5 metres. From the kerb, the legal distance is 45 cm.

When should you dip your headlights?

Rain, fog, snow, oncoming traffic, behind another vehicle, in a well lit area and in a tunnel.

What is the maximum speed in a built up area?

50 kph. The national speed limit is 100 kph.

What if you are dazzled by headlights?

By oncoming vehicle: slow down, look to the left of the road or stop if necessary. By following traffic: use the night mode in the centre mirror.

What is a clearway?

A busy road where drivers should not stop or park, except buses and taxis.

Where should you not park?

Bends, tunnels, bridges, double yellow lines and bus stops.

What regulations apply to the use of the horn?

Do not use it from 11.30pm to 7:00am except in an emergency.

What does an amber traffic light mean?

Stop if safe.

How do you recognise a zebra crossing at night?

Flashing amber beacons.

What do white zigzag lines mean?

No parking and no overtaking. You will see them at pedestrian crossing areas.

What is coasting?

Driving with the clutch pressed in, or driving in neutral.

What do red flashing lights mean?

Stop, a train is coming.

What does a red triangle indicate?

An accident or a broken down vehicle ahead.

What does a green light mean?

Go, if the way is clear.
