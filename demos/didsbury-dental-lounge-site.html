About us

Didsbury Dental Lounge is a multidisciplinary clinic offering premium dental care both through the NHS and privately, including general, cosmetic and orthodontic dentistry. Its principle is Dental Care, Done Right: the highest-quality treatment for every patient, in a way that keeps nervous patients comfortable and safe.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How much is a new patient exam?

£65 for a new patient exam with small X-rays included; a routine exam is £55.

Do you offer sedation?

Yes, sedation is available from £295 per hour, and the clinic offers specialised care for nervous patients.

Can I get an emergency appointment?

Yes, an emergency appointment is £59, with prompt diagnosis and treatment to relieve pain.

Is financing available?

Yes, 0% financing and membership plans are available.

Which areas do you serve?

Fallowfield, Didsbury, Withington, Burnage and South Manchester.
