FAQ

Before you come in

Does sending an enquiry confirm my appointment?

No. Sending an enquiry does not confirm an appointment; the team replies to arrange one, and the site says within 24 hours.

Are medical certificates available?

The GP page lists standard certificates such as MC1, MC2, driver licensing reports and fitness-to-work documents. School and sports certificates for children are issued on the same day on the paediatrics page.

Is there a first-visit process for children?

The first visit includes a health assessment and developmental check and a discussion of any current concerns, in a child-friendly environment.

How are allergies tested?

Skin prick tests, patch tests and specific IgE blood panels are listed, with results typically available the same day; allergen immunotherapy is offered for proven allergies.

What can I expect at a first dental visit?

A comprehensive examination, digital X-rays and a personalised treatment plan, according to the dentistry page. Most procedures are completed in-clinic the same day, and every step is explained in your language.

Which doctor should I choose for my family?

That is for the team to advise. Children from birth to 18 go to paediatrics, adults to the GP or a specialist, and the language list above shows who speaks what.

Can the assistant give medical advice?

No. It answers practical questions only. Anything about symptoms, medicines or diagnosis is for the doctors.
