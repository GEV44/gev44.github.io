Questions learners askStraight answers from the school's own FAQ. Anything not covered here, the assistant passes to the team.

Do you teach manual?

No. The school only teaches automatic cars.

Can I drive a manual if I pass in an automatic?

No. You get code 78 on your licence, automatic only. All electric and hybrid cars are automatic, though.

Is automatic easier for nervous drivers?

There is no changing gears and no stalling, so there is less to focus on. The test standard on the day is the same as any other B licence test.

How many hours before I can pass?

The RSA recommends 50 to 120 hours on the road. It depends on the individual, and the instructor can talk you through it.

What do I bring to a lesson?

Your learner permit or licence, an eye test, and the EDT logbook from the RSA, which the instructor provides to be stamped.
