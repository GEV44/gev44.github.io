How it works

From first call to road test

New students are taken by phone or e-mail at the moment. Here is the path the academy's website describes.

  1. Call or e-mailReach the team on 778-952-8488 or vansafedrivingacademy@gmail.com.
  2. Assessment lessonA 1.5-hour lesson for new students. The instructor checks your driving and estimates how many lessons you will need.
  3. Choose your lessonsSingle lessons or a bundle. Payment by PayPal or e-transfer once the number of lessons is settled.
  4. Road test with a vehicleBundles with a vehicle include the car for your road test and a 1-hour warm-up before it.

Good to know

Policies in plain words

Cancelling

48 hours' notice is needed. Less than 48 hours carries a $50 surcharge; less than 24 hours means the full lesson amount.

Paying

PayPal or e-transfer, after the instructor has worked out how many lessons you need. Prices don't include taxes or PayPal fees.

Staying safe

Surfaces that students touch are sanitized, and masks are optional in the vehicles under current mandates.

The full policy list is a PDF on the academy's VSDA Policies page.

FAQ

Before you call

What do I need to start?

A valid Class 7 learner's licence or higher, and a willingness to learn.

Can I book online?

Not at the moment. The academy takes new clients by phone or e-mail.

How many lessons will I need?

The assessment lesson answers that: an instructor checks your driving and estimates the number of lessons.

Can I use your car for my road test?

Yes, with a bundle that includes a vehicle. New students do the assessment lesson first, so the instructor knows you are ready.

Do you help with an overseas licence?

Refresher and update lessons for overseas changeover licences are offered.

Do you teach for a commercial licence?

Class 4 restricted lessons are listed on the Bundles page.
