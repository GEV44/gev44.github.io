Which test should you take?

The free online placement test has three sections. If you know nothing at all, skip it and enrol in Beginner 1 (or Beginner A, Beginner Semi-Intensive I or Beginner Super-Intensive). Take your best guess: if the result says you are not ready for that level, the school points you to the right one and you can retake it there.

Beginner test

You have had little exposure to the language, you visited Spanish speaking countries only for short periods, or you know a few isolated words but cannot yet form basic sentences.

Intermediate test

You understand basic vocabulary (greetings, weather, colours, numbers, food, sports, occupations, common places) and speak slowly, mostly in the present tense. You may have lived in a Spanish speaking country for more than a month.

Advanced test

You have an extensive vocabulary, narrate the past comfortably in both the preterite and imperfect, and are starting to use expressions like "espero que" or "si tuviera". Often six months or more in a Spanish speaking country.

Habits that make it stick

Advice from the school's FAQ, because fluency is a long road and the staff say so plainly.

Be consistent

Ten minutes every day beats one hour every week, so your brain can absorb and retain what you practise.

Practise out loud

Use what you learn with Spanish speaking friends or relatives. If you do not know any, the school can help you connect with native speakers.

Keep momentum

Take the next level right away instead of leaving a long break. Three or more courses back to back show a tremendous improvement.

Quick answers

Ask the assistant at the bottom right for anything else.

Which level should I start with?

Take the free online placement test. It recommends a level, or choose Beginner 1 if you are starting from scratch.

How big are the classes?

Very small: usually 8 to 10 students, so it is worth registering early.

Is there a book?

A digital workbook is included. Printed copies are optional and cost extra on Amazon.

How long until I can hold a conversation?

Committed students hold conversations after about 6 months, and one or two courses already give useful knowledge. Don't leave long breaks between levels.

Can I get a refund?

Submit the refund request form at least one week before the start date. New students have 24 hours after their first class. The cancellation fees are on the refund policy page.

How long are the group classes?

Each level is 6 weeks long for both the once a week and the twice a week classes. Semi-intensive and super-intensive levels run 4 weeks.

Should I choose once a week, twice a week or intensive?

It depends on how much you want to learn and how fast. Twice a week gives more practice and exposure, once a week suits a busy schedule at a slower pace, and the intensive formats suit travel plans or a rapid start.

What does the class cover?

All classes focus on conversation, with discussion and examples from Latin America and Spain. The course content page lists each level in detail.

Do you teach online?

Yes: live online classes and on-demand video lessons with quizzes, plus the same workbook and podcasts.
