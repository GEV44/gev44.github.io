About us

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

When is happy hour?

Daily from 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM, with a late-night happy hour from 9 PM to close Sunday to Thursday and 10 PM to close Friday and Saturday.

Do you serve brunch?

Yes, brunch is served daily from 11 AM to 2:30 PM.

Do you have vegan, vegetarian or gluten-friendly options?

Yes. Please confirm dietary needs with your server and let us know about any allergies. A gluten-friendly crust and vegan cheese are available on request.

Can I book a private event?

Yes, we offer private dining and events with personalised menus. Group bookings: chgroupbookings@socialcorner.com.

Where are you?

1050 W Cordova St, Vancouver, BC V6E 2E9, in Coal Harbour.
