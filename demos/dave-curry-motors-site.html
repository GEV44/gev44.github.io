Pre-NCT checks, done before the test date

Servicing and pre-NCT checks are the two things the garage says it specialises in. A check before the test is the cheapest way to find the small faults that fail a car: lights, tyres, brakes, steering and the rest of the safety list.

  1. Ring or e-mailTell the office the make of the car and that it is for a pre-NCT check.
  2. Bring it inAndrew and his staff go through the car against the safety checks.
  3. Know before you testYou hear what is fine and what needs attention first.

Timings and what is included in a pre-NCT check are confirmed by the team when you book.

The checklist in plain words

The sheet is long, so here it is in four groups. These are the garage's own checklist items, sorted by what they keep an eye on.

Stopping and steering

Brakes, brake lines, the power brake unit, clutch and brake pedals, the parking brake, steering and suspension.

Engine, fluids and cooling

Engine oil and filter, air cleaner element, fluid levels, cooling system, fuel system, drive belts and the automatic transaxle oil level.

Electrical

The battery and the wider electrical system, which is where warning lights and starting trouble tend to begin.

Tyres, body and safety

Tyres, body condition, chassis and body, door locks and hinges, the exhaust system and the general safety checks.

Diagnostic equipment for all makes

"We are proud to be able to offer full engine and gearbox diagnostic equipment for all makes of cars." When a warning light comes on or the car runs rough, the engine and gearbox can be read properly instead of guessed at.
