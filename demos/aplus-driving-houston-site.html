The same answers the assistant gives, listed for easy reading.

How does the Teen class package work?

The Teen class package is a 32-hour course over 16 days for ages 15-17. Students receive their permit on the 3rd day. The package also includes 14 hours of driving with the school's instructors and 2 permit tests. The classes can be taken in any order, at the student's own pace.

What is the Adult permit class?

The Adult permit class is a 6-hour class. It is required for ages 18-24 and highly recommended for ages 25 and above. It is held on Saturdays, 10am to 4pm. You get a certificate of completion for the Texas Driver's License Permit.

How do I get the permit?

After the class you receive a certificate of completion, which is what you need to get a Texas Driver's License Permit. Teens receive their permit on the 3rd day of the Teen course.

What are Behind the Wheel lessons?

Adult Behind the Wheel lessons prepare you for the DPS road test with experienced, qualified instructors. In-car practice covers parallel parking, freeway driving, turning, regular parking and more. Lessons come in 2, 4 or 6-hour blocks.

Can I use your car for the DPS road test?

Yes. The school offers the use of its training car for road tests, and you can practise for the DPS road test in the lessons. The road test can be booked pre-paid or on demand; the team will confirm current options when you call.

What are the rates?

Rates listed on the school website: Adult permit class $50; Teen package $250 (shown as a special); Behind the Wheel 2 hours $140, 4 hours $260, 6 hours $360; DPS road test $65 pre-paid or $100 on demand. Rates can change, so the team will confirm the current numbers at (281) 215-3233.

What are the hours and schedule?

The school offers convenient scheduling six days a week. The Adult permit class runs on Saturdays, 10am to 4pm. For the exact Teen class schedule, the team will confirm the current timetable.
