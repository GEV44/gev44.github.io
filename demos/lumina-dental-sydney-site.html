FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you have an offer for new patients?

Yes: a 1-hour comprehensive check-up and clean, no-gap with an eligible health fund or $249 without. New patients only; terms apply.

Do you accept the Child Dental Benefits Schedule?

Yes, Lumina Dental bulk-bills eligible children under the CDBS.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, interest-free payment plans are available.

Can you see emergencies the same day?

They do their best to accommodate emergencies, including same-day appointments.

Is the cosmetic consultation really free?

Yes, there's a free consultation for cosmetic and advanced treatments, with no cost or obligation.
