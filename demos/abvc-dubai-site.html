About us

Al Barsha Veterinary Clinic (ABVC) has over 15 years of veterinary care in Dubai. The team offers preventive care, surgery, an in-house pharmacy, house calls, pet medical boarding and shared emergency care through the Dubai Veterinary Emergency Care (DVES) network.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call 04-3408601 (ext. 1). Don't use email, SMS or WhatsApp for emergencies.

How much is a house call?

A one-off call out fee of 250 excl. VAT plus the normal consult fee for each pet.

When can I book a house call?

By appointment, Monday to Friday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Not available for emergencies, surgery or X-ray.

How do I register my pet in Dubai?

Dubai residents register each pet through the Dubai Municipality portal with the help of a veterinary provider.

Is there a loyalty program?

Yes, ABVC Treats gives 2%, 4% or 7.5% credit depending on yearly spend.
