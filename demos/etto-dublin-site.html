About us

Etto is a small restaurant at 18 Merrion Row in Dublin 2. The a la carte menu changes a little each day and the wine list focuses on interesting grape varietals and small producers. Because the restaurant is very small, bookings are capped at six people.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What is the largest group you take?

Bookings are capped at six people. Please do not book multiple tables for a bigger group.

Why do you take card details?

We take credit card details for all bookings because of no-shows and late cancellations, and we try to avoid charging a cancellation fee.

How far ahead can I book?

Bookings normally open 60 days in advance.

How much do I have to order?

At lunch, a minimum of two dishes per person including a main. At dinner, three dishes including a main.

When are you closed for Christmas?

We close after dinner on Wednesday December 23rd and reopen at 5:30pm on Thursday January 7th.
