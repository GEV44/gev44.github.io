More than a classroom

The school believes the most important learning also happens outside the classroom, in Sydney's international community. Teachers lead real-life activities, and students practise with both native and non-native speakers of English.

There are parties, language exchange events and excursions through the year. The student advisors speak more than 7 languages, and the school can arrange airport pick-up before you arrive. It also designs custom study tours for high school, university and community groups.

Quick answers

Can I start any week?

General English, IELTS, EAP and High School Preparation start every Monday. Cambridge FCE starts in March and September. For other courses the office confirms the next intake.

Is La Lingua registered for international students?

Yes. The school is a CRICOS provider (code 02117D) and each English course has its own CRICOS course code. For visa questions, talk to the student advisors.

What if I do not know my level?

For language courses the school offers a free level check by phone. For English courses, tell the office your goal and they will advise.

How do I enrol?

Use the school's enrolment forms or contact the office. The assistant on this page can collect your enquiry step by step and pass it to the team.
