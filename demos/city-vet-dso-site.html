FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need an appointment or do you accept walk-ins?

An appointment is advisable, but walk-ins are accepted depending on availability.

What vaccinations are required for boarding?

Dogs: rabies, distemper, parvovirus and Bordetella. Cats: rabies and feline distemper.

How often should we do vaccines, flea and tick, deworming?

Vaccines are typically annual. Flea, tick and deworming treatments are usually every 3 to 6 months.

What is the recovery time for spay or neuter?

Usually around 7 to 10 days, with a follow-up visit to check for complications.

Do you have an eye specialist?

No, but all our vets can check eye issues and advise on treatment or referrals if necessary.
