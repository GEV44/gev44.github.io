How we treat

Hands-on, then you move.

The 45 minute initial consultation covers a detailed assessment, hands-on therapy, pain management and an exercise program with video demonstrations.

Dry needling

A thin sterile needle into tight muscle to cut tension and pain and get you moving again. Several of the team hold dry needling certification.

Shockwave therapy

Acoustic waves that stimulate healing in stubborn pain. Often used for:

  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Tennis elbow
  • Shoulder tendinopathy
  • Chronic lower back pain

GLA:D for arthritis

An evidence-based exercise and education program for hip and knee osteoarthritis, a non-surgical option. A free GLA:D information pack is on the clinic site.

Before you come

Quick answers.

Can I be seen today?

Same day appointments are offered at the Richmond and South Yarra clinics. Call (03) 8395 4050, send a text or book online.

Do I need a referral?

For Medicare rebated visits you need a GP’s Chronic Disease Management plan. For TAC, no GP referral is needed once your claim is accepted. WorkCover needs your claim lodged and a Certificate of Capacity from your GP.

What happens in the first appointment?

A 45 minute initial consultation: a detailed assessment, hands-on therapy, pain management and an exercise program with video demonstrations.

Where do I park at South Yarra?

There is street parking outside the clinic on Garden Street, free for 2 hours, opposite the Jam Factory.

Can I see a doctor as well?

Yes, through partner Clinic Online: same day online GP consultations with an Australian registered GP.
