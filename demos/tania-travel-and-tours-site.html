The ship

MSC World America

2,614 cabins for up to 6,762 guests, so there is room for the whole family and the whole guest list.

30+ places to eat

From fine dining to casual, including the Butcher's Cut steakhouse and Eataly specialty restaurants.

World Promenade

Ocean views and an LED light show, with live music venues and nightlife on board.

Play on deck

MSC SportPlex with a roller-skating arena, the Luna Park Arena arcade, an aquapark with waterslides and a rock climbing wall.

Ports: snorkeling and glass-bottom boat tours in Roatán, the Maya Chan Beach Club in Costa Maya, Lucha Libre shows in Cozumel, and Ocean Cay MSC Marine Reserve with its beach clubs and sunsets.

Questions families ask first

What does the quinceañera package include?

The listed highlights are a venue, DJ and entertainment, and decorations, on a luxury cruise ship with the quinceañera, her family and friends.

Which sailings are open for 2027?

Saturdays June 19, July 17 and July 24, 2027, each 7 days on MSC World America from Miami. The agency also lists 2028 quinceañera sailings.

What is not included in the cruise price?

Port taxes, government fees, federal fees, airfare and gratuities.
