Good to know

Quick answers before you book

Do I need a referral?

No. You only need a GP referral for a workers compensation or motor vehicle (third-party) claim, and workers compensation clients need to call to book.

How soon can I be seen?

There is a good chance of an appointment on the day you call or the day after.

What should I bring and wear?

Any relevant x-rays or scans, your referral if you have one, your health fund card and your Medicare card if you have a GP plan. No special clothing is needed, as shorts and gowns are provided. Bring running shoes for a running assessment, or gym gear for a class.

Will it hurt?

If you are in significant pain, the area is treated very gently. For old stiff conditions a firmer approach may cause some discomfort. Tell your physio about any pain and they will not push beyond what you can tolerate.

Do you manipulate the neck?

All the physios are manipulative physiotherapists, but they do not manipulate the cervical spine (neck) because of the risk to the vertebral artery. With consent they may manipulate the mid and lower back.

How long is each session?

The first is 45 minutes, later ones are usually 30 minutes. Extended 45-minute sessions are available for complex problems or two treatment areas.

Is the clinic accessible?

Yes. A lift runs from street level or the parking level, there is wheelchair access into the clinic, and assistance animals are welcome.
