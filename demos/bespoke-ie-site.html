Property valuations

Know what your property is worth

Whether you are selling, letting, refinancing, managing a portfolio or simply curious, an accurate valuation is the best place to start. Bespoke does not rely on automated valuations or generic estimates. Here is what the team weighs up:

  • LocationYour neighbourhood, amenities, transport links and current demand.
  • Property type and sizeLayout, accommodation, floor area and overall appeal.
  • Condition and presentationQuality, specification and any improvements or upgrades.
  • Recent comparable salesTransactions involving similar properties in your area.
  • Current market conditionsBuyer and tenant demand, available stock and wider trends.

Questions

Before you call

Is the valuation really free?

Yes. The site describes a free, no-obligation market valuation for anyone thinking of selling or letting.

Who regulates Bespoke?

Bespoke is licensed by the Property Services Regulatory Authority, licence 001671, and Sean O'Donoghue is a RICS-registered valuer and SCSI affiliate.

Can you manage just one apartment?

Yes. The lettings and management service is described for landlords with one property or a larger portfolio.

What areas do the listings cover?

Dublin, South Dublin and Wicklow.

What does tenant referencing involve?

Appropriate checks and documents, which may include proof of identity, previous landlord references, proof of employment and income, and relevant financial information, so landlords can make informed decisions.

Do you work with owners' management companies?

Yes. Block management is provided on behalf of OMCs, working with the board of volunteer directors on maintenance, contractors and administration of the development.

Why not just use an online estimate?

Bespoke says it does not rely on automated valuations or generic market estimates, because every property differs in location, condition, specification and local demand.

What are the opening hours?

Not published on the site. Phone (01) 661 9670 and the team will confirm.
