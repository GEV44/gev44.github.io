Why Granville Island

A quieter Vancouver, still minutes from downtown

Staying on the island means the Public Market, Kids Market, theatres and galleries are steps away, and the seawall runs right past the hotel. The hotel describes itself as a calm, intimate base for couples, creatives and families.

Many rooms overlook False Creek or the marina, and the hotel has been named among Vancouver's best by Condé Nast Traveller, Tripadvisor and Travel + Leisure.

Quick answers

What guests ask first

Is the hotel close to downtown?

About a 5 to 10 minute drive or a short water taxi ride. Bus #50 False Creek runs from Gastown, and False Creek Ferries and Aquabus serve the island.

Is it a good fit for families?

The hotel calls itself family friendly: Doubles and Premium Balcony Doubles sleep up to 4, and the Kids Market, theatres and water taxis to Science World are close.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, it is a pet friendly hotel; the pet friendly room takes up to 2 pets. Any fee or house rules are confirmed by the team.

What times are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is 4 PM and check-out is 12 PM.

How do I get from the airport?

Limousine service, car rental pick-ups, taxis and Uber are available from the airport.
