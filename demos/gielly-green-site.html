FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do you take walk-ins?

Booking in advance is recommended, but the team always does its best to accommodate walk-ins, subject to availability.

Can I arrange an out-of-hours appointment?

Yes, out-of-hours appointments can be arranged through reception.

Do you offer bridal hair and makeup?

Yes, with trials and on-the-day services, in-salon and, by arrangement, on location. Use the Bridal & Events inquiry on the Contact page.

Is there a private space?

Yes, there is a private space at the Marylebone salon for clients who prefer discretion, whether for religious beliefs or privacy.

Can I choose or change my stylist?

Yes, you can browse team profiles or take the Find Your Artist quiz, and you can switch stylists any time.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, gift vouchers are available online and in the salon, and there is a loyalty program.
