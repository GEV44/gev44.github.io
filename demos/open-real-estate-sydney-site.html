Questions people ask first

Do you manage Airbnb properties?

The About page lists Airbnb management as part of full-service property management, together with tenant sourcing and maintenance.

How do I apply for a rental?

The Rent menu has an Apply Online option. The team can confirm what you will need to supply.

Can I see what nearby homes sold for?

The Sell menu has a Sold Properties page. For an appraisal of your own home, request one here.

What can I do through the owner and tenant portal?

The Manage menu links to a sign-in for owners and tenants of managed properties. The team will confirm what you can do there.

Do you help investors?

Yes. Investment Consulting covers market research, tailored strategies and portfolio growth support, and the sales and management teams work with investors.

Can I rate the service?

The site has a Rate Us page and a Testimonials page.

Where exactly is the office?

68A Sorrell Street, North Parramatta NSW 2151. There is a map link in the Visit section above.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 9.00 am to 5.00 pm. Weekend times are not listed on the site, so the team will confirm if you need one.

Do you handle both sales and rentals?

Yes. The menu has Buy, Sell, Rent and Manage, and the team is described as sales experts and property managers working together.

How do I get a valuation of my property?

Ask for a Market Appraisal. You can request one with the assistant, and an agent will confirm a time.

Is the agency independent?

The About page describes Open Real Estate Sydney as a boutique real estate agency. Ask the team about its structure.
