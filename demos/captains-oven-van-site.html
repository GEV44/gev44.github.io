About us

Siblings Nevin Fernandes and Suleyman Ebcin fell in love with pizza making in Naples, Italy, and opened Captain's Oven with a simple philosophy: quality first. Dough is pressed fresh daily, vegetables are roasted in-house, and everything is baked in a 3-ton handmade stone oven shipped from Italy.

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How fast do the pizzas cook?

About two minutes, like traditional Neapolitan pizza, which gives a chewy but crisp crust.

Do you deliver?

In Vancouver we deliver within 3 km for a minimum purchase of $25, with a $3.95 delivery charge.

I have a gluten allergy. What can I eat?

We offer a gluten-free crust, but Captain's Oven is not a gluten-free environment.

What can vegans and vegetarians eat?

We offer vegan cheese and you can customize your pizza to be vegan or vegetarian. Salads can be customized too.

Do you cater?

Yes. Large orders can be prepared for pickup or delivery, and a catering menu is available for birthdays, weddings and corporate events.

Do you have gift cards?

Yes, gift cards can be purchased inside any of our restaurants.
