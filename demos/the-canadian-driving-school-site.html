ICBC update: no second road test from October 19, 2026

Eligible N drivers will not take a second Class 5 road test. ICBC reviews your record instead, and you then get a Class 5 with a 12-month restriction. Your Class 7 test is still required, and that is the one Joe prepares you for.

Packages and courses

Pick the lesson that fits where you are

Beginner package

10 hours in the car plus a structured theory component. Vehicle control, road awareness and defensive habits, aimed at the Class 7 (N).

Road test preparation

Mock road tests, practice on real test routes, ICBC scoring criteria and common mistakes, with detailed feedback. Class 5 or Class 7.

Defensive driving

In-car training and theory to spot hazards early and make better decisions, for new and experienced drivers.

Driver refresher
