Questions

Before you call.

Does it cost anything to use Ping?

No. The advice is always free of charge and independent.

Do you only show offices from one operator?

No. Ping works across the whole flexible and serviced office market, so access is unbiased across hundreds of options in Dublin and other major cities.

How quickly can I see offices?

A tailored shortlist comes within 60 minutes of the discovery call, and viewings of the best fits are arranged within 24 to 48 hours.

Can you help if my lease is ending?

Yes, there is a Stay vs Go enquiry for exactly that. Call or email the team and they will talk it through.

Are you regulated?

Yes, Ping Offices is fully licensed by the Property Services Regulatory Authority (PSRA).

What size of team can you place?

Searches run by headcount from 2-4 people up to 50+, in serviced, managed, co-working and commercial lease space.

Can I rent a small office on a short term?

Ping helps with flexible and short-term space, including temporary and swing space offices. The team confirms the terms of each option in your proposal.

What happens after the viewing?

You get a post-tour review within 24 hours, and Ping assists through negotiation and agreement.

I am a landlord. What does it cost to list?

Zero listing fees and zero upfront costs. Ping handles marketing and enquiry management and sends pre-qualified tenant leads.
