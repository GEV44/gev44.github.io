Good to know

Quick questions

Do I need an appointment with Zen?

Yes. Because of popular demand for his cuts, reserve in advance with your desired date and time.

What is included in a cut?

Shampoo, blow and eyebrow cut. For colour, perm and treatment services, shampoo and blow are an additional charge.

Is there a kids discount?

Ages 17 to 15 get 15% off, 12 to 7 get 20% off, and 6 to 1 get 40% off (no shampoo).

Where do the perm solutions come from?

All solutions are from Japan. Ask about body perm, point perm, nuance wave, digital perm, hard wave or Japanese straight perm.

Can I protect my colour-treated hair?

Bleach and highlight services come with Olaplex care, and Hoyu and Olaplex treatments are on the menu. Your stylist advises what suits your hair.

What if I am new to the salon and want a bridal up-do?

The salon asks new customers to contact them before booking. The bridal up-do is 90 minutes and the rehearsal is a separate appointment.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday 10am to 7pm, Sunday 10am to 5pm.

Can I book online?

Yes, online booking is available on the salon site, or you can phone 604-876-8762.
