FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

What's included with every membership?

24/7 facility access, all lifting equipment, infrared sauna access and a private, non-crowded training environment.

Can I cancel my monthly membership?

Yes. The monthly plan has no contract and can be cancelled any time with 30 days' notice. 6-month and annual plans are prepaid and non-refundable.

How does 24/7 access work?

Once your membership is active, the GymMaster app opens the door. You can also pick up a physical key fob during staffed hours.

Can I bring a guest?

Yes. Every Friday from 8am-10pm, members can bring 1 guest for free.

Can I see the gym before signing up?

New visitors can buy a day pass after hours by scanning the QR code at the entrance and following the steps in the app.
