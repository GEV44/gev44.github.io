About

A salon built around your hair, and your schedule

Complete Beauty Bar has been in business since 2014, conveniently located minutes from downtown in Houston's Third Ward. The salon offers hair extensions, custom hair coloring, treatments, braiding and other natural styles, and has licensed barbers on staff for men's grooming.

Every stylist goes through extensive training and uses professional-grade products. Guests can feel comfortable whether they pre-book with a favorite stylist or show up as a walk-in.

Good to know

Quick answers

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours, and you can also pre-book with your favorite stylist.

Do you do men's cuts?

Yes. Licensed barbers are on staff for men's grooming, and men's braids and color are on the menu too.

Can you work with natural hair?

Natural styles are a big part of the menu: twists, bantu knots, wash and go, rod and roller sets, trims and deep conditioning.

Do you install extensions and wigs?

Yes: sew-ins, quick installs, micro links, tape-ins, clip-ins, wig construction and wig installs.

Can I pre-book with a specific stylist?

Yes. Guests can pre-book with their favorite stylist, or show up as a walk-in.

Do you color installed extensions?

Color of installed extensions is on the color menu, along with balayage, highlights and corrective color.

Do you do kids' hair?

Kids' cornrow styles, with or without added hair, are on the braid menu.

Are you open on Sunday or Monday?

The website lists Tuesday to Saturday only. Call 281-674-8610 to confirm anything else.

What is the price of a service?

Prices depend on the service and stylist. Please check with the salon when you book.
