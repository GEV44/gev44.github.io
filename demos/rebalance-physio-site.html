FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Can I claim on my health insurance?

Fees can be submitted to private health insurers and to Revenue for tax relief. Physio sessions are claimable under the physiotherapy benefit on all plans, and you get a receipt on the day.

What is diagnostic ultrasound and how do I book it?

In-house musculoskeletal ultrasound done by a physiotherapist. When booking online, choose option 03 - Diagnostic Ultrasound Physiotherapy Assessment.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give 24 hours' notice if you can't make your appointment, otherwise a cancellation fee applies.

How do I pay?

Fees are paid on the day of treatment, and all major credit and debit cards are accepted.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

Yes, the team can send you the details.

Do you treat vestibular conditions?

Yes, such as BPPV, concussion and Meniere's disease, with a 60-minute assessment and 30-minute follow-ups.
