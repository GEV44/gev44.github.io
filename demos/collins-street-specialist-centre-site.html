Inside the clinic

The centre describes itself as one of Australia's most technologically sophisticated dental clinics. Here is what that means for a visit.

Seeing more, with less

The Planmeca ProMax 3D Max produces detailed 3D imagery at limited radiation doses for precise treatment planning. A 2D system with a child mode keeps X-rays as safe as possible for kids, and one-shot imaging suits anyone who finds it hard to sit still.

Comfort first

AirFlow prophylaxis uses water, air and fine powder for a more comfortable, thorough clean. Lasers (Fotona LightWalker, NV and VersaWave) support gum and cosmetic procedures with greater patient comfort than traditional methods.

Made on site

A 3Shape TRIOS 3 intra-oral scanner and an in-house laboratory keep planning and restorations close together, with KaVo DIAGNOcam and DIAGNOdent helping to detect decay.

Orthodontist or general dentist?

Orthodontists focus on correcting the position of the teeth to improve the bite and the appearance of the jaw and face. An improper bite can affect appearance and can also indicate jaw dysfunction or problems with underlying bone structure, including overbite, underbite, cross-bite and crowded or impacted teeth. At the centre, orthodontic treatment is carried out by a registered specialist orthodontist, and your general dentist stays part of your care.

Payment plans

Common questions

Do I need a referral?

No. You do not need a referral to see any of the specialists. Call (03) 9650 2726 to discuss your needs and make an appointment.

How do I book?

Call the team on (03) 9650 2726, or fill out the contact form on the website and they will get back to you as soon as possible.

What treatments are offered?

All six main fields of dental specialisation: oral and maxillofacial surgery, endodontics, orthodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics and paediatric dentistry.

Is there a payment plan?

Yes. Interest-free plans, Payright, TLC and Supercare are all described on the payment plans page. Eligibility is confirmed by the team.

Where exactly is the centre?

Level 8 of the Manchester Unity Building at 220 Collins Street, Melbourne CBD, on the corner of Swanston and Collins Street.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Friday from 8am until 6pm, and Saturdays from 8:30am until 1:30pm.

What if my case involves more than one specialty?

That is what the centre is set up for. With the specialists in one place, cases can be peer reviewed and planned together, and patient co-ordinators maintain dialogue between your clinicians and you.

Does the centre take clinical photographs?

The technology list includes intraoral and extraoral cameras and a photography studio, used to document and plan treatment.

Are children seen here?

Yes. There are four specialist paediatric dentists, and the 2D imaging system has a child mode for safer X-rays.
