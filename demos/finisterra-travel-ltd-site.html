Custom trip planning

Eight steps from idea to departure

“We start with how you want to feel.” Most itineraries on the site are fully customisable for 1 to 20 people.

  1. StartSubmit the Travel Questionnaire.
  2. ConversationThe team asks a few more questions about the what, when and where.
  3. Sample outlineYou review a first itinerary outline.
  4. Planning feeA planning fee is requested to go forward and applied to your final payment.
  5. Fine-tuningThe itinerary is adjusted and the ingredients added.
  6. Final itineraryA 25% deposit is requested.
  7. BookingsEverything is booked and final payment is due 60 days before departure.
  8. GoOff you go, with 24-hour support.

Before you commit

The practical bits

Cancellation

Deposits are non-refundable from July 1, 2022; more than 60 days out you may apply it to a future trip. Under 60 days, all monies paid are non-refundable. Air tickets are 100% non-refundable.

Insurance

Proof of travel medical insurance is mandatory. Finisterra is licensed to sell Manulife policies and encourages Cancel for Any Reason cover.

Passport & visas

Passport valid for 6 months beyond your return date. Visas and documents are each traveller's responsibility.

Currency

Trips are priced in US dollars. The team can also tell you what cash to bring for your region.

FAQ

Little questions, big questions

Do you charge a planning fee?

Yes, a non-refundable per-trip planning fee because curated trips take a lot of time to plan. It is applied toward your final balance.

Are the itineraries customisable?

Most are, for 1 to 20 people, although pricing varies. Contact the team for a custom trip.

Do I need vaccinations?

It depends on the destination. The team cannot give medical advice, so check with your doctor or a travel clinic at least 6 weeks before departure.

How much luggage can I bring?

Airline rules vary. Most allow 1 checked bag per person up to 50 lbs; one larger bag plus a backpack is the usual advice.

Why are prices in USD?

Finisterra is a Canadian company, but the US dollar is a world currency and the Canadian dollar fluctuates against it.

Can you help with Canada and British Columbia trips?

Yes, for example a stay at Great Bear Rainforest for grizzly viewing, kayaking the Broken Group Islands or polar bear viewing in Manitoba.
