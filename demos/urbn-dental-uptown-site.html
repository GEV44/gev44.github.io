FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

The office works with most insurance plans and can confirm your coverage before your visit. Tell the team your insurer and they will check it.

What if I don't have insurance?

There is a dental discount plan that makes care more affordable; ask the team how it works.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, financing is available with Cherry. You can see if you qualify in about 60 seconds and pay over time, with plan lengths that fit your needs, including 0% APR options.

Can I get in for a dental emergency?

Yes, same-day emergency care is offered for severe tooth pain, a broken or knocked-out tooth, swelling or an injury. The office is open weekdays and Saturdays, so call ahead or book online.

Do new patients get anything?

New patients get a free Quip electric toothbrush after their first dental cleaning; ask the team for details.

I'm nervous about the dentist. What can you do?

The team listens, answers your questions and stays with you at every step, and sedation dentistry is available.
