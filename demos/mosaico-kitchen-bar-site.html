01 / ABOUT

More than a meal

Step into the world of culinary wonder at Mosaico, a gastronomic destination in Brickell. Each bite promises an exploration of unique, bold flavors, and the cocktail bar serves daring concoctions of spirits, fruits and mixes designed to pair with the menu.

There is also an impressive selection of fine wines, sparkling varieties, bottled beers and premium liquors to complete the evening.

The restaurant is a tribute to the history of Miami's Brickell region. Its architecture and aesthetic echo the legacy of the Tequesta people, the area's inhabitants for over 2,000 years, while the interior philosophy embraces Brutalism: raw, clean materials like concrete, steel, glass and wood, with structural elements in plain sight.

Design that you can eat in

"Beautifully merging modernity with nature and a hint of coziness": the avant-garde interior was curated by Angel Sanchez of Sanchez Colman. Come for brunch with the family, a dinner for two, or a lunch with colleagues.
