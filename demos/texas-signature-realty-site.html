Quick answers

Is there really a 100% commission?

Yes. The site states that you keep what you earn and the brokerage does not take a cut. The flat fees above apply.

Which cities do you cover?

Houston is the base. The brokerage also sponsors agents and serves clients in Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin and San Antonio.

What is included for agents?

A free premium Dotloop account, CRM and IDX, lead generation tools, and E&O coverage with no extra fee.

Where can I find forms?

The 2026 W-9 and the Commission Disbursement Authorization (Word and PDF) are on the site.

I am a buyer. Will an agent pressure me?

The site says agents never pressure you. They want you to find the home that fits, and they explain each step of the process.

Do you handle commercial leases?

Yes. Commercial and residential leases are both listed, along with leasing and tenant advisory.

Do I pay anything to leave my current brokerage and join?

The site lists the fees above and nothing else: no monthly fees, a flat fee per transaction, an annual fee once a year, and a lease fee per lease deal. For anything about your own situation, the team answers on a confidential call.

Does the brokerage help new agents get leads?

The CRM and IDX included with the brokerage cover client relationships and lead generation. The team can explain how agents use them when you call.

Can I work with an agent if I am relocating to Texas?

Yes. Agents serve Greater Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin and San Antonio. Tell the assistant your target city and an agent will follow up.

What are your office hours?

They are not listed on the website. The team will confirm, or call (832) 876-2093.
