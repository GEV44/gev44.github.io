Same-day crowns

One visit instead of two, and no temporary

A traditional crown means preparing the tooth, taking impressions, sending them to an outside lab (usually around two weeks) and wearing a delicate temporary in between.

Agave Dental designs and places crowns the same day with computer-aided dentistry, so you do not have to schedule a second visit.

If the dentist makes you nervous

Up to one in five Americans avoids the dentist out of fear, so you are in good company. Sedation dentistry is offered, and you stay awake and able to ask questions.

Nitrous oxide

“Laughing gas” breathed in through the nose. The dose is easy to adjust and it wears off quickly, so with nitrous alone you can usually take yourself to and from your appointment.

Oral sedation

A deeper level of relaxation. You remain alert during the appointment, though some patients get so comfortable they drift off. Ask the team about the details and whether it suits you.

Cleanings with your hygienist

Gentle but thorough cleanings every six months give you a fresh start, with tips on habits and technique that fit your own teeth.

Children from the first tooth

The American Dental Association suggests seeing a dentist about six months after the first tooth appears. The team uses child-friendly, positive communication.

Your first visit

Plan on an hour to an hour and a half.

Fill in the new patient forms online or print them: medical history, medications, dental history and insurance.
Bring X-rays if your previous dentist took any in the last six months. If not, we take a new set.
The dentist does a thorough exam and an oral cancer screening, then talks through findings and any treatment recommendations.
A cleaning may happen the same day or at a follow-up, depending on your teeth and gums. Patients under 18 come with a parent or guardian.

Quick answers


Are you taking new patients?

Yes, new patients are always welcome.

Which days are you open?

Monday to Thursday, with an early 7:00 am start on Tuesday and Wednesday. Friday is by appointment only.

What if I don’t have insurance?

Ask about the Smile Savings Plan, or monthly payment options through CareCredit and Sunbit.

Do you offer a patient portal and online bill pay?

Yes. The website has a patient portal and a pay-my-bill link.

How often should I get a cleaning?

Every six months is the recommendation the practice follows, and some patients benefit from more often. The dentist will tell you what fits your gums and teeth.

Do you work with other practices’ referrals or specialists?

The team says it rarely has to refer patients elsewhere, because implants, Invisalign, root canals and gum care are handled in house.

I need care for my child. Do you see kids?

Yes. Pediatric dentistry is part of the practice.
