Before you try it yourself

Common DIY mistakes.

Trying to fix something on your own might save money short term but often leads to bigger problems. These are the ones the shop sees most.

DIY collision repair with off-the-shelf materials

Cheap fillers, spray paint and bondo kits rarely hold up and often make things worse. A small dent can turn into a much costlier job when done incorrectly.

Online diagnoses instead of proper testing

Forums can help, but generic advice often leads to unnecessary part replacements or ignored warning signs. A check engine light can mean a dozen different things.

Poor brake repairs

Mismatched pads, improperly installed calipers and warped rotors from backyard jobs. Braking is one of the most critical safety systems in your car.

Ignoring suspension and alignment

Delaying these repairs can cause uneven tire wear, poor fuel economy and a less responsive drive.

Temporary fixes on electrical problems

Electrical tape over frayed wires and jerry-rigged connections cause shorts, dashboard errors and power drain.
