Dining

An Italian table, a Texas welcome

Remi

We saved you a seat

The signature restaurant, helmed by Executive Chef Maurizio Ferrarese: Italian dining shaped by the seasons, housemade pastas and exceptional ingredients. Espresso in the morning, candlelight at night, and a courtyard that makes "just one drink" feel wildly optimistic.

In-room dining

Some nights are better spent in

Favorites from Remi, brought to your room daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Call the front desk.

At the Chef's Table

Private culinary experiences

A hands-on experience led by Chef Maurizio for private groups of ten to twenty, at Alba or Remi: Pasta Fresca, The Art of Risotto, Mozzarella Pulled by Hand, or a chef-led demonstration class followed by a tasting. Suited to corporate outings, client entertaining, birthdays and wedding weekends.

Kemo Sabe

Western style from Aspen, in Houston

A collaboration with the Aspen atelier for private and corporate events: Trunk Shows, The Hat Experience and Custom Boot Making. Original Grit tequila, in cocktails such as the Kemo Sabe Margarita, is poured at Remi and Bar Remi.

Experiences

While you're here

Long lunches, poolside afternoons, cocktails at Remi and occasions worth marking.

Tea d'Alba

The hotel's beloved afternoon tea in the newly renovated Alba Tea Room: Italian charm meets English tradition, with fine teas, finger sandwiches, freshly baked scones and desserts, each with a signature Italian twist.

Sunday Supper

Sundays at Remi. Gather four or more and salads, four entrees and three sides arrive in the middle of the table, made to pass and share. Kids dine as the hotel's guests.

Mahjong Mondays

Mondays in The Library: an evening of mahjong, bottomless sips and good company. Bring a group or pull up a chair. All levels welcome.

Holidays at Hotel Granduca

Seasonal dining such as an Autumnal Afternoon Tea, Thanksgiving at Remi, a Holiday Tea and Breakfast with Santa. Dates are on the hotel's Holidays page.

Meetings, weddings and galas

The art of the occasion

Eight distinct venues behind the gates, from intimate salons to a courtyard that holds a thousand: private dinners, cocktail receptions, charity galas, board dinners and milestone celebrations.

Weddings unfold among magnolias and stone fountains in a private courtyard of more than 10,000 square feet, with a seated dinner for up to 150 and a standing reception for up to 1,066, and guests staying under one roof.

Quick answers

Are pets welcome?

Yes, pets are welcome and encouraged. The team can confirm any specifics.

Is parking complimentary?

Self-parking is complimentary. Valet parking is also available.

Can I eat in my room?

Yes: in-room dining from Remi, every day from 7am to 10pm.

What are check-in and check-out times?

They aren't listed on the website, so the team will confirm them for your booking.

Can the hotel host a wedding?

Yes, with eight venues and 121 rooms and suites on site. Tours are arranged through the sales team.
