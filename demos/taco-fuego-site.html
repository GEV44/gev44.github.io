FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Is the food halal?

We offer halal options, including our beef and chicken. We also serve some non-halal items such as al pastor, plus alcoholic beverages, so tell us what you plan to order and the team can confirm.

Can I order online for pickup or delivery?

Yes, at participating Taco Fuego locations through the online ordering page on tacofuego.com.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes, we offer vegetarian options made fresh to order. Check the menu for the latest selections.

Do you cater events?

Yes: birthdays, weddings, office lunches, family gatherings and corporate events. Catering pickup is available at POST Houston and Spring Branch, and you order online.

Where are your other locations?

Spring Branch (the original, with dine-in and a full bar), Richmond (late night), Cypress and Katy, plus POST Houston downtown.

How can I reach the team?

Email admin@tacofuego.com or use the contact form on tacofuego.com.
