Good to know

Why learn Arabic in the UAE?

It makes you feel at home, helps you build real relationships with locals and native speakers, and gives you an edge in employability and career growth.

I only want to chat with friends. What do I need?

The alphabet and its pronunciation, plus basic words, phrases and greetings for restaurants, malls and daily life. Speaking with real speakers teaches more than books alone, and mistakes are part of it.

Can I learn online?

Yes. Registered students get e-learning modules, and every fee plan lists online classes with audio and video support.

How do I start?

Request a free demo class. The team will contact you to confirm the details.

Can my company enrol its employees?

Yes. Corporate training is held on your premises, with days and times set around your needs.
