How classes work

Small room, real conversation

Teachers come from Mexico, Spain, Argentina, Chile, Venezuela, Colombia and more, so you hear more than one accent.

~4students per class on average

100+active students, on site and online
1991first language school founded in Budapest
The school teaches only Spanish, so the programme can go deep on the language's dialects, expressions and cultural nuances, from Spain and Mexico to Argentina, Colombia, Cuba and Puerto Rico. Lessons cover speaking, listening, reading and writing, and each module ends with a short test so you can see your progress. The goal is not only grammar: it is to help you use Spanish correctly and with confidence, at work, at home and when you travel.

Questions

Before you enrol

I have never studied Spanish. Where do I start?

Complete-beginner groups start on the Monday dates in the fall calendar (Nov 2 and Dec 7 next). Register at least one week ahead.

How do I pay?

After you book, an invoice is e-mailed so you can pay online. You can also pay by card at the school terminal during office hours, by bank transfer or PayPal. The school is cashless.

Does the school prepare for exams?

It is an authorized SIELE centre, and Micaela specialises in DELE preparation. Grace prepares students for the AP Spanish exam.

Can I study in the evening?

Yes. The General Evening course runs Monday to Thursday, and office hours extend to 7:30 PM by appointment.

Do you teach English too?

English lessons are listed among the school's courses. Ask the team for the current format.

How big are the classes?

About four students on average. The Super Intensive needs a minimum of two students to run, which keeps every group small and personal.

What do I get with a course?

Flashcards, learning apps, notebooks and handouts, plus a test per module and a certificate. The textbook "Español para Extranjeros" by Antonio Hernández Galán is extra, except in the Super Intensive.

Is there a course for children?

Yes: private Spanish for ages 3 to 13, with early care and after care on request. Ask the team for the current weeks and weekly fee.

What if I need a translation or interpreter?

The school offers certified Spanish and English translation for immigration, legal, medical, educational and corporate use, and consecutive interpreting for meetings and events. Request a quote by e-mail.

Can my company train its staff?

Yes. Corporate programmes range from conversational Spanish for serving Spanish-speaking customers to advanced business Spanish for negotiations and presentations.

How far ahead should I register?

At least one week ahead for the general and Saturday courses, and about one month ahead for the Super Intensive and online courses, which are in high demand.

Are there school breaks?

Yes. This fall there is a Thanksgiving break from November 25 to 28 and a winter break from December 23, 2026 to January 4, 2027.

Can I try before I commit?

The school offers a free consultation. Use the assistant to leave your details and the team will get back to you within two business days.
