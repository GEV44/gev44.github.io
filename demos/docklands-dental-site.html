Your first visit

One hour, no clock-watching.

Dental appointments often feel rushed. Here the first one is deliberately long, so you leave knowing more about your mouth and with a plan that suits you.

  1. We listen first

    What matters to you, what worries you and what your goals are, not only what is wrong with a tooth.

  2. A thorough examination

    Teeth, gums, jaw and the soft tissues of the mouth. Screening for oral cancer is standard.

  3. You see what we see

    Clinical information, digital photography, videos and digital radiography are used so the explanation is clear.

  4. A plan that works for you

    There is no single right treatment plan, only the one that works for you, agreed together.

Dental anxiety

Nervous about the dentist? That is normal here.

Dental fear can come from a bad childhood experience, sounds and smells, or a general feeling of vulnerability. The team has lots of experience with anxious patients and starts with ways to build trust. Where care is too stressful, dentists trained in sedation dentistry can help, after a careful assessment.

Questions

Things people ask before booking.

Anything not on the website is left to the team to confirm.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. The website says new patients are welcome at the Grand Canal Dock practice in Dublin 2.

How long is a first appointment?

An hour. The team uses it for a full examination, oral cancer screening, and a conversation about what matters to you and what worries you.

What are your opening hours?

They are not published on the website, so the team will confirm them. Call 01 636 0192 or email hello@docklandsdental.ie.

Do I have to be sedated if I am nervous?

No. Sedation is a tool for when care is too stressful, it needs an assessment first and is not for everyone. The team starts with other ways to build trust.

Who places the implants?

Specialist oral surgeon Dr Marie O'Neill at ISCD, the specialist practice Docklands Dental works with. Planning and the teeth made to go on top are done at Docklands Dental.

Are the dentists registered?

Yes. Every clinician is registered with the Dental Council of Ireland and the team page lists registration numbers so you can check them yourself.

Can I see the prices?

There is a Price List page on the practice website. For payment options or insurance questions the team will confirm.
