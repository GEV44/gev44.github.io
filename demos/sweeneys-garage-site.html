How a service day works

Drop in the morning, collect by evening

1. Book or call

Choose the garage nearest you and tell them the car and the service level. Call, email or book online.

2. Drop off

Bring the car in the morning. The 10 point check is free, and the 25 point check is part of the standard and full service.

3. Told before extras

Long-life spark plugs, a cabin filter, extra oil or a fuel filter on some diesels are charged extra, and you are advised before any of it goes ahead.

Ready for collection by the end of the day. If a part is not in stock, a separate appointment is arranged. Work and parts carry the 12 month guarantee, and the garage can tell you which parts were fitted.

Quick answers

Questions

What days are you open?

Monday to Friday 8:30am to 6pm and Saturday 9am to 5pm at Dublin City Centre, Ballyfermot and Finglas. Bray closes at 5pm on weekdays. Sunday is closed.

Do I need an appointment for the free pre-NCT check?

No. You can walk in, call or book online.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes, services and parts come with a 12 month guarantee.

Can you collect my car?

Within 2 km of the Ballyfermot, City Centre, Finglas and Bray garages, subject to availability, with a minimum charge of €50.

How long does a service take?

Drop the car in the morning and it is normally ready by the end of the day. If a specific part is unavailable, a separate appointment is needed.

Do you work on vans and hybrids?

Yes, cars and light commercial vehicles, and there is a dedicated hybrid car service.
