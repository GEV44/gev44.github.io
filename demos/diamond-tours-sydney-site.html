The Blue Mountains aren't just the destination, they're family history

Diamond Tours is run by Rob and Alena Michail, and Alena's family story is woven through these mountains. Over a century ago her ancestor Victor Levitt ran the Hazelbrook General Store and Post Office, the heartbeat of the early mid-mountains community. He served in both World Wars, and locals named Victor Falls, a Hazelbrook waterfall, after him.

Since 2008 the family has shared its backyard with guests from around the world: small groups, comfortable vehicles, and guides who know every lookout, story and shortcut.

"When we say local, we mean it."Millions of eucalyptus trees release a fine oil mist that scatters sunlight and gives the mountains their blue haze. The day is timed to reach the lookouts before the crowds.

Good to know

Straight from the Diamond Tours FAQ. Anything not listed here, the assistant will pass to the team.

How far are the Blue Mountains from Sydney?

About 90 minutes to 2 hours by road. On the tour it takes roughly 1 hour 45 minutes to reach the first stop, with stories from your guide on the way.

Are there entry fees to the National Park?

All National Park and entrance fees are covered by Diamond Tours.

What should I wear?

Layers and comfortable walking shoes. The tours include short walks to lookouts. Bring a camera, sunscreen, a hat and water.

Best time of year?

It is a year-round destination. Spring and autumn are popular for mild weather and landscapes, and the day is tailored to the season.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes, children of all ages are welcome. All children require a seat and must be booked. No prams or strollers, as there is no luggage space.

Can I customise my tour?

Both private tours can include specific stops or activities. Tell the team your preferences when booking.

What is the cancellation policy?

Small group tours cancelled more than 24 hours before are fully refundable (within 24 hours a fee may apply). Private tours cancelled more than 7 days before are fully refundable (within 7 days a fee may apply).

Small group or private?

Small groups are more social, max 20 guests. Private tours are tailored to your group alone.
