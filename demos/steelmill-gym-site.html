Before you come in

Quick answers

When can I train?

Members have 24-hour access, every day. The front desk is staffed Mon-Fri 10 AM-8 PM and Sat-Sun 10 AM-4 PM.

How do I become a member?

Memberships are available on site during operating hours. Stop by and the team will set you up.

I am under 18. Can I join?

Yes. Bring a parent or guardian with you for the paperwork and gym access.

Can I try it first?

The 1-day guest pass is $9.99 during staffed hours, with access to all equipment.

Is there a contract?

The monthly plan is month to month with no long-term contract. The yearly plan is a one-year contract. There is no annual fee and no cancellation fee.

Do you offer online coaching?

Yes. All three trainers offer 1-on-1 in-person and in-gym training, plus online personal training and online coaching, with flexible scheduling to fit early mornings or late nights. Session prices are not listed, so the team confirms them.

How do the prep meals work?

Sign up online, choose a 1-month, 1-month recurring or 3-month subscription, talk through your goals with a dietitian, then pick up at the gym or have the meals delivered. Menus change every week and the meals are easy to heat.

Is there a military or first responder discount?

Yes, Steelmill offers one. Ask the team about it when you visit or call 281-888-3132 during staff hours.

Where can I buy the gym gear?

Tees, tanks, sweatshirts, wrist straps and Velcro patches are only available at Steelmill Gym. Pick them up when you come in.
