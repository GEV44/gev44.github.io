Odjibik opened in Yorkville, Toronto in 2013 and now has four locations around the Greater Toronto Area.

The chairs

Master stylists

International training, brought to Gastown. Ask the assistant about either of them, or request one by name when you book.

F

FABIANO

Master stylist · Brazilian tech · Makeup artistry

Born and raised in São Paulo, Brazil, with more than a decade behind the chair. He works in colour, precision cuts for men and women, keratin smoothing, styling and professional makeup.

His specialties include making textured hair more manageable and building blonde tones with precision. With makeup too, a full makeover can happen in one visit.

F

FERIT

Master stylist · Blonde · Balayage master

From Istanbul, Türkiye, with over 20 years of experience at some of the city's top salons. Naturally dark, thick hair is common there, so he has developed methods for strong blonde results even on challenging textures.

His work spans highlights, balayage and precision haircuts, customised to each client.

Good to know

Questions

How do I book?

Use the Book Appointment button on the salon's website, call (604) 566-6565, text (647) 965-3232, or send a DM to @mprivesalon. Or tell the assistant here what you are after and it will pass a request to the team.

Can I get extensions in one visit?

Extensions start with a consultation. The length and colour of the hair is chosen afterwards, a full deposit for the extension hair is required, and the installation happens after one business week.

I want a big colour change. Where do I start?

Colour correction (6+ hours, from $500+) requires a consultation first. Balayage and highlights are done by the colour team, and Ferit is the balayage master.

Where do I park?

Metered street parking in Gastown is usually limited to 2 hours. Two public lots have no time limit: Advanced Parking Lot #171 at 80 Powell St (about 100 metres from the salon) and EasyPark Lot #31 at 160 Water St (about 8 minutes on foot).

Are you hiring?

The salon is recruiting stylists, from senior stylists on tiered commission to new talent with in-salon training. Send a resume to mprivesalon@gmail.com and a DM on Instagram @mprivesalon.
