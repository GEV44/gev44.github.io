FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Are the prices on your site final?

All listed prices are starting prices, subject to change, and vary by service provider and the amount of product used. Taxes and gratuities are not included. A complimentary consultation is offered for all services.

Is there parking?

Metered pay parking is available right outside and nearby. The PayByPhone location number for 1260 Hamilton Street is 60882.

What is your cancellation policy?

A minimum of 48 hours notice is requested to cancel or change an appointment. Changes within 48 hours are charged 50% of the original appointment, and no-shows are charged a no-show fee.

Do you do colour corrections?

Colour corrections vary for each guest and can take more than one session, so a complimentary consultation is required before scheduling.

Do you offer hair extensions?

Yes: volume, infinity and flex weft, plus tape-in, I-tip and K-tip methods, and Bellami clip-in extensions. Contact the salon for a complimentary consultation.

Can I buy professional hair products?

Yes, the website's shop carries brands including K18, Olaplex, Davines, DevaCurl, ghd, Kevin.Murphy and Wella Professional, and gift cards are available.
