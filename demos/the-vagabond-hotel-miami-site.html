Quick answers

Can I check in early or late?

Early check-in may be available on the day, subject to room availability, and a fee may apply. The front desk is staffed 24 hours, so you can arrive at any time, even after midnight. They will store your luggage if you arrive early.

Is breakfast included? Is there a restaurant?

No free breakfast, but complimentary coffee is in the lobby 24 hours a day. The hotel's restaurant space is under renovation and not operating right now. The MiMo District has several breakfast spots within walking distance.

Are children and pets welcome?

Children are welcome; guests under 21 must be accompanied by an adult. Pets are not allowed, except service animals as required by law.

Is there an elevator?

No. The hotel has two floors, with rooms on both around the courtyard pool. You can request a ground-floor room when booking, subject to availability.

Can I host a group or an event?

Yes: private events, meetings, group room blocks and full-property buyouts. Call 305-400-8420 or email enquiries@thevagabondhotelmiami.com.

What payments do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards. Personal and company checks are not accepted. The name on the card must match the guest checking in, and a valid government photo ID is required. A refundable incidental authorization is placed at check-in.

Is smoking allowed?

Rooms are non-smoking; there is a designated outdoor smoking area. A cleaning fee applies if someone smokes inside a room.

How far is the hotel from the airport, the beach and the cruise port?

By the hotel's own figures: about 2 miles to Wynwood, 2.5 to the Design District, 5 to PortMiami (15-20 minutes), 6 to Miami Beach (15-20 minutes) and 8 to Miami International Airport (15-20 minutes), depending on traffic.

Do rooms have coffee makers and mini-fridges?

Keurig coffee makers are in Electra Deluxe and Catalina King (ADA) rooms. Mini-fridges come with the deluxe rooms. Other room types do not have in-room coffee makers, but coffee is free in the lobby.

Is there a gym or a business center?

Yes to both: an on-site fitness room, and a small workstation in the lobby for email and printing boarding passes. Ask the front desk for photocopies.

Can the hotel receive my package?

Yes, for registered guests, for a fee per package per day. The charge applies only to packages delivered before your arrival.

Is there a best-rate guarantee?

Booking through the hotel's website or by phone gets you the best available rate, avoids third-party fees and puts you in direct contact with the team before and during your stay.
