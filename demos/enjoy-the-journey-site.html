It’s in the details

What makes the tours different

Hassle-free

Itineraries are inclusive and the team handles the planning. Taxes and flights are included.

Black car service

All multi-day tours include black car home pick-up and drop-off. Surcharges apply from Abbotsford and Chilliwack; the team confirms the amount.

A consultive team

Book online or through the booking agents, who look after the tour all the way.

Competitive group prices

Small groups on 24-seat buses, built around active seniors.

Travel insurance

Licensed and bonded in-house agents suggest a policy for the chosen trip, with Manulife or Tips Travel Insurance, and support with the claim.

Community

Partners include Destination BC, Tourism Langley, Tourism Abbotsford and the BC Retired Teachers Association.

Many ways to save

Discounts and cancellations

Loyalty program

Receive a 5% discount if you travelled on any Enjoy catalog tour within 12 months of the booking date.

Method of payment

Receive a 2% extra discount if the balance payment is made by cheque or e-transfer.

Understand the rules

  • Multi-day tours: cancellation within 30 days is 100% non-refundable unless otherwise specified.
  • Day tours: cancellation within 21 days is 100% non-refundable.
  • Cancellation plan: cancel up to 48 hours before departure for a full refund less the cost of the plan, bought at the time of booking.
  • Deposits are non-refundable unless a plan or cancellation insurance is purchased.

FAQ

Good to know before you book

Who are the tours for?

Active seniors. Tours are escorted and inclusive, with social environments and bucket-list destinations.

Is a gratuity expected?

Gratuities for tour managers are customary and always greatly appreciated.

Which phone extension do I use?

Extension 1 for bookings and information, 507 for day tour operations, 508 for multi-day tour operations. Insurance quotes are on 508 as well.

Can I visit the office?

Yes, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm, at Unit 15, 2414 Main Street, Vancouver.

Do the tours include a tour manager?

Multi-day tours run with tour managers from the team (the site lists Lacey June Berry, Sel Jogi and Lucy Miringu), and the guides, drivers and agents are part of the same small team.

Can I book day tours through my community centre?

Yes. Day tours booked with community centres start from the centre’s address, otherwise you choose a Vancouver, Burnaby or Surrey transit or car park location.

What kind of destinations do you visit?

Pacific Northwest destinations and beyond: local day tours around Vancouver, multi-day trips across Canada, and bucket-list journeys such as Thailand and Mexico.

Can the itinerary change?

The company reserves the right to make changes to tour itineraries.
