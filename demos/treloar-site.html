How care works here

From the clinic's FAQ: no surprises about what happens after you book.

1. Assessment

Your practitioner carries out a thorough initial assessment during the first session. Initial visits run about 30 minutes to one hour.

2. A plan with you

You discuss the treatment plan and agree on your individual goals for your wellness journey.

3. Treatment and exercise

Follow-up visits are 20 to 30 minutes, unless an extended follow-up is arranged or you are referred to a kinesiologist for active rehab exercises.

4. Your doctor stays informed

With your permission, the clinic sends your doctor an initial report. If a physician referred you, they receive the assessment findings too.

Empowerment and education

The clinic aims to give you knowledge and tools for a healthier, more active lifestyle, not only a treatment room.

Innovative treatments

Treatments follow current healthcare research and techniques, to speed recovery and improve quality of life.

Questions patients ask

How many appointments will I need?

It depends on your injury or reason for visiting. At the first session your practitioner does a thorough assessment and agrees a treatment plan and goals with you.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

In most cases, no. Some insurers require one for extended coverage, so check with your carrier. For WorkSafeBC and ICBC claims, ask your case manager.

Does MSP cover physiotherapy?

Only beneficiaries with premium assistance status, up to a combined 10 visits per calendar year, with a surcharge per appointment. Contact the clinic for the amount.

How long is an appointment?

Initial visits are about 30 minutes to one hour. Follow-ups are 20 to 30 minutes unless an extended visit is arranged.

What is the cancellation policy?

48 hours notice for Clinical Pilates, 24 hours for all other services. Late cancellations are charged 60% of the appointment cost. Monday appointments: cancel before 2pm Saturday at Kerrisdale or 3pm Saturday at Cambie.

Who are the physiotherapists?

The team is expected to go beyond basic physiotherapy skills and work towards becoming Fellows of the Canadian Orthopaedic Manipulative Physiotherapists Association (FCAMPT). Many have additional training in IMS, acupuncture, vestibular and concussion rehabilitation, shockwave therapy, pelvic floor therapy and Clinical Pilates.

Is there parking?

Yes, ample metered street parking at both locations and parking lots close by.

Do you bill my insurance directly?

Most extended health insurers, plus WorkSafeBC and ICBC. Contact the location you are attending before your appointment. If direct billing is not available you receive a detailed receipt for reimbursement.

What should I bring?

Your CareCard number. Shorts for back, hip, knee, ankle or foot treatment. Sport shoes or orthotics for lower-limb sports injuries. Claim details for WorkSafeBC or ICBC.

Will you contact my doctor?

Only with your permission. The clinic then sends an initial report so your family physician knows what you are being treated for and for how long.

Do you have Pilates studios?

Yes. The clinic lists a Cambie & 8th Pilates studio and a Kerrisdale Pilates studio, with Clinical Pilates delivered by physiotherapists. Extended 55 minute sessions are listed at Cambie.

Can I book online?

Each clinic has its own online booking page: one for Cambie & 8th and one for Kerrisdale. You can also phone the reception team, who are happy to help with any appointment question.

Which services are in-home or specialised?

The clinic lists in-home care, cancer (oncology) physiotherapy, vestibular and concussion rehabilitation, pelvic floor therapy, running assessment and bike fit. The reception team confirms availability for each clinic.
