AboutTaught by people trained to teach Chinese

Founder Isabella Yi holds a Master of Linguistics and Applied Linguistics (TCSOL) and taught Mandarin in China and abroad before opening MoTai. Teachers hold degrees in Chinese language teaching, and anyone teaching children needs a valid Working With Children Check.

Classes meet in MoTai's own classroom, not shared with other schools, with refreshments provided. MoTai is a member of the Australia China Business Council and an official partner for the Oral Chinese Test (OCT).

Business MandarinFor careers and Chinese-speaking customers

The pathway: finish Adult Beginner and Elementary (6 modules, 120 hours), take Business Mandarin Foundation (3 modules, 60 hours), then Intermediate to Advanced (12 modules, 240 hours).

Employers can ask for a customised corporate program covering language, business etiquette and cultural awareness. Private and corporate lessons can be delivered at your office in Sydney or Parramatta CBD with no travel cost. MoTai also offers NAATI translation services for Chinese.

FAQGood to know before you enrol

Can I join in the middle of a term?

Beginner A: generally the next term is advised if you will miss three sessions; with two missed you can still enrol and catch up with private sessions at extra fee. Other levels: anytime during the term, subject to placement assessment.

What if I miss a group lesson?

Group classes cannot be rescheduled. MoTai tries to place you in another class of the same course, or you can use a weekly tutoring session. Private lessons can be moved with two days written notice.

What is the refund policy?

80% refund if requested before the course starts. No refund once it has commenced. Full refund only if MoTai cancels, or cannot place you in any of your three preferred class times.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or major credit card. American Express and JCB carry a 1.4% surcharge, waived for group classes enrolled online. Fees are GST inclusive and a tax invoice is provided.

Is there a minimum for private lessons?

Generally 10 hours is the minimum private booking. Contact the centre if you need another arrangement.

What is a tutoring session?

An extra weekly support session, not a class: ask questions, get help with homework or practise speaking. Standard Group and private students can attend up to two a week, Starters Group students one. Students of different levels can join together.

Can lessons happen at my office?

Yes. Private and corporate lessons in Sydney or Parramatta CBD are delivered on site with no travel cost. Other locations add travel cost.

How do I know which course to join?

If you studied on your own or with friends, start at Beginner Module A. If you studied at school, a short complimentary assessment finds your level and the team recommends a course.
