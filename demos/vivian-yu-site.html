Meet Vivian

Her mother's dream was a cozy little home in Vancouver. Realtors told them their budget was not enough.

That is why Vivian went into real estate: so every client with even the slightest ambition of owning a home knows how to reach the goal and never misses the opportunity. Looking back, she says that budget was more than enough for a two-bedroom ten years ago, and still is today.

For sellers

Selling your home just got easier

Six steps, each one planned with you. Nothing is a surprise on the day your home goes live.

Get your home's value

  1. Personalized home assessment

    What made you choose to live in your home? What makes it special? Vivian uses the answers to position your property in its finest light.

  2. Decide on your price

    Together you choose a price that is both competitive and market-driven, with the same page on everything from the start.

  3. Prepare for showings

    A walk-through points out what to do before thousands of eyes see it. A home stager can help, then a professional photographer shoots HD photos, drone and video for a listing video.

  4. Advertise your home

    Targeted digital marketing, social media advertising, e-mail marketing and personalized 1-to-1 outreach to the right buyer.

  5. Host an open house

    Planned around your property type, location and style, to turn digital attention into face time and create real buzz.

  6. Close and celebrate

    Vivian handles the title, closing services and inspection so closing goes smoothly. Then you celebrate.

Common questions

Before you call

How does Vivian price a home for sale?

You decide together, after the assessment, on a price that is competitive and market-driven.

Does she help with the mortgage side?

She introduces buyers to a mortgage partner so you can set your buying range with confidence, and the site has a mortgage calculator.

What if I need to sell before I buy?

The buyers page covers this. Ask Vivian how to time both, and the team will confirm the best order for your situation.

Which areas does she cover?

Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Coquitlam, New Westminster and Surrey are featured on her site.

Can I see sold homes and market stats?

Her site has a sold gallery and market stats for the REBGV and FVREB boards. For how they apply to your street, request a call.

Is the home valuation really free?

Yes, the site offers a free home valuation. Tell the team about your property and they will arrange it.

Can I just talk first, with no commitment?

Yes. The conversation comes first, and her approach is to explain timelines, budgets and taxes before you decide anything.

Do I need to be ready to move to start talking?

No. Plenty of people call while they are still exploring. Vivian explains timelines, budgets and taxes first, so that when you do decide, you already know the path and the numbers behind it.

How do I stay updated on new listings?

Buyers get listing alerts from Vivian's search, then view homes and open houses with her. You can also join her e-mail list from the site footer.

What are the office hours?

Hours are not published. Call or text and the team will arrange a time.
