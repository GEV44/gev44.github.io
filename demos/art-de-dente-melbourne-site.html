FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed for general dental appointments; just get in touch directly.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes, we welcome new patients of all ages. Call or book online and we'll find you a time.

Which health funds do you work with?

We accept all health funds with on-the-spot HICAPS rebates and are preferred providers for Medibank, Bupa, HCF, NIB, CBHS and HIF.

Do you offer payment plans?

We offer a range of payment options and discuss them before treatment starts, with costs explained in advance.

Do you offer after-hours emergency care?

No. For after-hours emergencies contact The Royal Dental Hospital on 03 9341 1000, and call 000 if it is life-threatening.

Where exactly are you located?

Level 17, 190 Queen Street, Melbourne CBD, an easy walk from Flagstaff and Melbourne Central.
