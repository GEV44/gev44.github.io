Care for every stage

Puppy & kitten care. The hospital has a dedicated Puppy & Kitten Care page covering first vaccines and what to expect. New clients get an extended first appointment so there is time for all your questions.
Adult wellness. Regular wellness checks are the most important way to keep pets from developing serious conditions such as dental disease, heartworms, arthritis and diabetes. Flea, tick and heartworm preventives are available at the on-site pharmacy.
Senior pets. As cats and dogs reach their seventh year, special attention should be paid to their health. The team offers senior pet care, prescription diets and pain relief such as laser therapy where appropriate.

Boarding, done thoughtfully

The hospital placed first for Best Pet Boarding Facility in the 2016 Reader’s Choice Awards, and boarding is now reserved for current clients so every guest gets the care it deserves.

Dogs

Spacious climate-controlled suites built with safety first: powder-coated aluminum enclosures over a hand-laid epoxy floor, a large comfortable bed, and calming music throughout. Every dog is walked at least twice a day in a large back yard.

Cats

A Cat-Friendly Practice with a state-of-the-art feline facility completely segregated from the rest of the hospital, so cats relax without hearing dogs bark. The feline area is climate-controlled for a better stay.

Guests eat Royal Canin Gastrointestinal Low Fat dry food to ease the transition, or you can bring your pet’s own diet divided into individual meals in plastic bags. Feeding, grooming, medicine if needed, hands-on care and playtime are all included in the stay.

Pharmacy & nutrition

A complete on-site pharmacy for medications and products. The team reviews each prescription with you and answers questions about giving it, and can special order what you need.

Quick answers

Can I board my pet with you?

Dogs have climate-controlled suites with a bed and music, walked at least twice daily in a large back yard. Cats stay in a separate climate-controlled feline area. Boarding is restricted to current clients.

What food do boarders eat?

Guests are fed Royal Canin Gastrointestinal Low Fat dry food, or bring your own pre-portioned in plastic bags for each meal.

Do you have a pharmacy?

Yes, a complete on-site pharmacy with medications, flea/tick/heartworm preventives and prescription diets; special orders and home delivery are available.

Do I need to fill out forms before my first visit?

You can, to make the first appointment smoother: a client information form gathers your pet’s health history and your contact and billing details. There is also a drop-off questionnaire for busy mornings.

What if my pet needs surgery?

Surgery and anesthesia are available in-house. A surgery release form is online so the paperwork is done before the day, and you always get a treatment plan before anything is performed.

Is there a client portal?

Yes — the website links to a patient portal and an online pharmacy.

Where do you serve?

Oak Forest, Timbergrove/Lazybrook, Greater Heights, Garden Oaks and Spring Branch, and the wider Houston area.

Who treats my pet?

Seven veterinarians share the hospital; the front desk can tell you who is available.
