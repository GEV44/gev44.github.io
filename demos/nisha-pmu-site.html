FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes, a non-refundable £100 deposit is required to book. It is redeemable towards the treatment.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for all semi-permanent, brow lamination, henna brows and lash lift & tint treatments. It is £20, redeemable towards treatment.

How long do ombré brows last?

Typically up to 8 years, depending on lifestyle, sun exposure, skin type and medical history. A colour refresh every 18-24 months is recommended.

What is the difference between lip neutralisation and lip blush?

Neutralisation balances dark or uneven undertones; lip blush then adds a soft, natural tint.

How often should I have glutathione shots?

Maintenance shots or drips every 4-6 weeks are recommended.
