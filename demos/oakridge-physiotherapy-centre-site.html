Who is paying?

Start with how your visit is covered

No referral is needed for physiotherapy itself. Pick the way your visit is funded to see what the clinic says to expect.

Motor vehicle accident (ICBC)

With an ICBC claim number you no longer need pre-approval from ICBC. The clinic treats whiplash, back strains, headaches, soft tissue injuries, fractures and chronic pain after an accident.

  • ICBC funds 25 physiotherapy sessions, 12 kinesiology sessions and 12 acupuncture sessions
  • Claim more than 12 weeks old? Tell the clinic and they will work with you and ICBC
  • Approved visits need no payment from you for the allocated number of visits
New claim: no doctor's referral for the first visit unless your adjuster says so

Work injury (WorkSafeBC)

The clinic works with you through WorkSafe BC claims for work-related injuries.

  • To activate your claim you need to have seen your doctor
  • Approved WorkSafeBC injury claims need no payment from you for the allocated number of weeks
Doctor visit first, then call the clinic

DVA claims need a doctor's referral with a specific diagnosis. Once approved, the allocated number of visits is fully covered with no payment from you. Medavie Blue Cross (Veterans Affairs, Canadian Armed Forces and RCMP) is also on the direct billing list.

Frequently asked questions

What happens at my first appointment?

Your physiotherapist does an initial assessment to give a working diagnosis, works with you to identify your needs and devises a treatment plan. Treatment focuses on a hands-on approach, with education, exercise and electrotherapy as determined by your physiotherapist.

How long are appointments?

Physiotherapy visits are 30 minutes, although your time in the clinic may run 30 to 60 minutes. Prolonged visits are booked for 60 minutes.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No referral is required for physiotherapy. Some extended health plans may require one to activate payment, so check with your insurer. ICBC, WorkSafeBC and DVA each have their own rules, shown above.

Will my plan cover the visit?

Contact your insurer to learn what your plan covers. The clinic can direct bill listed carriers, and if a visit is partly covered it bills the covered amount and asks you to pay the balance.
