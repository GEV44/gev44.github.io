PRSI, tax relief and students.

PRSI dental benefit

The clinic submits your PRSI claim in-clinic. The scheme covers one annual exam and subsidises a basic cleaning. You are likely eligible if you are an employed worker, a self-employed person or retired, with a minimum number of PRSI contributions that varies with age. The clinic does not accept medical card schemes.

20% tax relief (Med 2)

Many treatments, including crowns, bridges, veneers, implants, bone graft, post and core and root canals, qualify for 20% tax relief under Revenue's Med 2 scheme. A signed Med 2 form is given on request. Whitening is considered routine care and does not qualify.

10% student discount

Show a valid in-date Irish or international student ID at check-in, have your clinical examination, and 10% comes off private fees at checkout. It does not apply to PRSI treatments, medical card schemes or emergency dental care. The clinic is next to the Abbey Street Luas stop, handy for Trinity College Dublin, RCSI and TU Dublin.

FAQs.

General information as published by the clinic. It is not personal medical advice: for your own teeth, ask the dentist.

How often should I visit my dentist?

At least once a year, more often if you have difficulties.

How often should I have a professional cleaning?

With good oral hygiene and a healthy mouth, your dentist will likely suggest a professional cleaning at least twice a year. Many dental insurance policies cover two cleanings per year.

Is tooth whitening safe?

Whitening should be carried out by a dentist only, as hydrogen peroxide should be handled with care by a qualified dentist. Any staining may be due to an underlying condition that your dentist can diagnose.

What is a dental implant?

A titanium or zirconium screw on which a false tooth is supported, screwed directly into the jaw bone. Implants anchor fixed false teeth or support a removable denture.

What is a crown?

Like a jacket or cap that fits over a prepared tooth, giving it the same shape as the original. It can be made of metal alloy, porcelain, or a combination, and is fixed in place with cement.

What is a bridge?

Like two crowns with a false tooth between them, cemented in place to replace a missing tooth. A Maryland bridge is instead glued to the backs of the teeth on either side of the space.

What does root canal treatment mean?

It removes a dead or infected nerve from inside the tooth. The clinic says the procedure is painless: the dentist cleans the space with small files and fills it, so the tooth can be kept rather than extracted.

I have broken a piece of a filling.

The clinic's advice: make an appointment as soon as possible. In the meantime avoid eating on that side, and consider a temporary filling kit from a pharmacy until you can be seen.

Does my dentist need to know if I may be pregnant?

Yes, tell your dentist before treatment. Most dentists prefer to defer elective procedures until after pregnancy, particularly in the first and third trimesters, and X-rays are best avoided but may be taken in an emergency.

Why do my gums bleed when I brush?

The clinic's general explanation is that plaque left beside the gums lets bacteria infect them. For your own mouth, please book an examination.
