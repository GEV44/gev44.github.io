Which slip does your car need?

Both slips are done on site and sent to RMS electronically.

Registered cars

Pink slip

The eSafety check (previously known as the pink slip) is your annual registration inspection. When the vehicle passes, the report goes to RMS electronically.

  • If it fails you get a "Repairs Needed" report and 14 days to complete the repairs.
  • A follow-up check after the repairs is done by the centre at no extra charge.
  • Rego is then renewed online through Service NSW.
Valid for 28 days
Unregistered cars

Blue slip

For unregistered vehicles: it verifies the vehicle is safe and meets design and identity standards before it can be registered in NSW.

  • Registration expired more than 3 months ago
  • Vehicle brought in from interstate or overseas
  • No number plates, or a written-off vehicle approved for re-registration
  • Significant changes such as a new engine, or a defect notice to clear
Valid for 42 days

Also covered: vehicle design checks for clearance of defect notices (previously the white slip), and heavy vehicle inspections. Trailers under 250 kg and caravans under 411 kg tare do not need an eSafety check.

Pink slip visit, step by step

How an eSafety check works at the centre, as described on the site.

  1. Book or pop in.Use the online booking form, call (02) 9565-2999, or come in during office hours.
  2. Inspection.The authorised inspection station runs the Vehicle eSafety check on your car, ute or van.
  3. Report to RMS.If the vehicle passes, the report is sent to Roads and Maritime Services electronically.
  4. If it fails.You get a "Repairs Needed" report and 14 days to complete the repairs. After that, a follow-up check from the centre at no extra charge.
  5. Renew rego.Renewing is as easy as visiting Service NSW, which lets you renew your rego online. The check is valid for 28 days.

Quick answers

Or ask the assistant at the bottom right.

How long is a pink slip valid?

An eSafety Vehicle Inspection (pink slip) is valid for 28 days.

My car failed. What now?

You are issued a "Repairs Needed" report and have 14 days to complete the repairs. After 14 days the report expires and a new check is needed. Once repairs are done, a follow-up check from the centre is required at no extra charge.

When do I need a blue slip?

For unregistered vehicles: registration expired more than 3 months ago, a vehicle brought from interstate or overseas, no number plates, a written-off vehicle approved for re-registration, a significant change such as a new engine, or a defect notice to clear. A blue slip is valid for 42 days.

Do you give a quote before repairs?

Yes, always, before any repairs are undertaken.

Can I keep my manufacturer's warranty with log book servicing?

Log book servicing is offered for owners who want to preserve the manufacturer's warranty and still get expert service at competitive rates.

Do trailers and caravans need an eSafety check?

Trailers that weigh less than 250 kilograms and caravans with a tare weight of less than 411 kg do not require an eSafety check.

What is the white slip now?

The centre also does vehicle design checks for clearance of defect notices (previously the white slip), and safety check reports for establishing and renewing light vehicle registration.

Which suburbs do you cover?

The Camperdown workshop serves Camperdown, Annandale, Petersham, Enmore, Glebe, Macdonaldtown, Newtown and Stanmore.

What does it cost?

Prices are not published on the centre's site. The team gives a quote before any repair work.
