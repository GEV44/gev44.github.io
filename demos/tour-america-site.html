Our story

Small team, big ambition

Tour America has been proudly Irish-owned since 1995, starting with a small team and a determination to bring the best of America to Irish travellers. Today the team spans more than 50 travel specialists, and the approach is still personal.

The Ireland-based team is led by founder Mary McKenna. They say there are no call centres and no algorithms, just experienced travel experts who take the time to understand what you want.

Frequently asked

What makes Orlando holidays with Tour America special?

They take the stress out of planning: flights, hotels, theme park tickets and car hire are handled, and the team can tailor the itinerary around Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando Resort, SeaWorld and local gems.

Can I book travel insurance with you?

Yes. Annual multi-trip, single trip, family, couple and individual policies can be added to your booking or booked separately.

Can I book car hire?

Yes, call 01 817 3535. You can collect at most airports; some destinations offer a downtown location.

Can I book excursions?

Yes, see the attractions section of the site or ask your sales consultant.

How do I get the 2026 brochure or the newsletter offer?

Subscribe with your email for the brochure. Newsletter sign-up also gets you 50 euro off your next booking, according to the site.
