FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How do I book?

Online through our booking site, or by calling 02 8084 8355 during opening hours Monday to Saturday. You'll get a confirmation and a reminder 24 hours before.

Is there a waitlist?

Yes, use the waitlist option at the bottom of the calendar on our booking site to be alerted to openings and cancellations.

Where do I park?

We're 4 minutes' walk from St Peters train station. Street parking is limited, so arrive at least 15 minutes early.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule 48 hours ahead. Under 48 hours, a 30% fee applies to rebook; a no-show fee is 100%.

Do new clients book a special service?

Yes, new clients select the New Client service for their haircut; the stylist decides the service for future cuts.

Is card processing included in prices?

No, card processing is not included in the listed prices.
