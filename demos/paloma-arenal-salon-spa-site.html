Before you book

Good to know

How do I get an accurate colour quote?

Ask for a free consultation with one of the stylists before your service. Prices vary with length, texture, thickness and the result you want.

Is there parking?

Yes, complimentary parking on site.

Do you have weekly specials?

There is a Weekly Specials page on the site. Offers are one per person and cannot be combined with other promotions. Ask the team what is on this week.

Can I add to my facial?

Yes. Add-ons such as LED light therapy, microcurrent or a Dermalogica peel must be booked together with a facial.

Do you do kids’ nails?

Yes, kids’ manicure and pedicure are listed for ages 4 to 10.

Which neighbourhoods do you serve?

The salon is in Little Havana and welcomes clients from Brickell, Coconut Grove, Coral Gables, Downtown and Southwest Miami.
