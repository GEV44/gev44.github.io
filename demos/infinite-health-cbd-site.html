FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need a referral?

No. You do not need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist, but bring it if you have one.

Which health funds do you accept?

HICAPS facilities accept most Australian private health funds, so you only pay the gap.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable exercise clothing, your health fund card and any x-rays or scans.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your assessment. A program typically starts at two sessions a week and tapers to one.

Do you accept WorkCover, CTP and EPC?

Yes, all insurance claims and plans except DVA.

When should I arrive?

10 minutes before your appointment to fill in a health history form.
