Parents ask

My child is 4. Is that too young?

No. The Early Years Mandarin program starts at 3.5 years.

When will my child start speaking Mandarin?

Happy Mandarin says children begin speaking from the first month, and that reaching a good level usually takes 3 to 5 years.

Can my child learn to speak only, without reading and writing?

Not recommended. Reading, writing, listening and speaking are taught together so children can communicate properly later.

Are Chinese characters hard for children?

The school's view is no: children see characters as drawings, shapes and blocks, and enjoy reading and writing them.

Do you teach adults and companies?

Yes: group, private and online classes for adults aged 18 and above, and corporate training.

I live outside Dubai.

Online classes are open to students in other Emirates, the Gulf and the rest of the world.
