Before your visit

Do I need a referral?

No, you can book directly. If your insurer needs a referral for reimbursement, check with them first.

How does insurance work?

Pay and claim. You pay at the clinic and receive detailed invoices and medical reports to send to your insurer. Call your insurer first to check physiotherapy is covered.

How do I pay?

Cash, credit or debit card, and bank transfer. Instalments through Tabby, plus discounts for Fazaa, Alsaada, GEMS Rewards and Homat Al Watan members.

What if I need to cancel?

At least 6 hours’ notice. Late cancellations or no-shows may deduct one session from your package.

What is Physio Cure Connect?

The free patient app and web portal: appointments, session history, progress, secure messages to your doctor, invoices, and your home exercise plan.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing such as shorts and a t-shirt. Avoid jeans or anything restrictive.
