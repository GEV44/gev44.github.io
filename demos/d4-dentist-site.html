Quality over quantity

Minimally invasive, preventive dentistry that aims to keep your natural teeth, using the latest technology and techniques.

Masters-qualified dentistsDigital dentistryInvisalign expertiseSustainable clinic

Restoring a chipped front tooth with composite rather than a crown, for example, preserves tooth structure. The clinic also runs with a focus on the environment, from the eco-friendly Dentaqua disinfection system to less waste and energy-efficient habits.

Invisalign

Each aligner is worn about two weeks, 20-22 hours a day. Treatment typically takes 6 to 18 months, then retainers. A complimentary orthodontic consultation is offered.

Same day crowns

Digital design and manufacture, so there is no temporary crown and no second appointment.

New patients

The first visit covers a thorough exam of teeth, gums and mouth, with X-rays and photos if needed, then a personalised plan.
