Good to know

Questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The free 3-day pass lets you try everything the gym offers with no commitment.

Can I cancel any time?

Memberships are month-to-month with no cancellation fees. The start-up fee and the quarterly maintenance fee still apply.

When is someone at the front desk?

Members get access 24/7. Non-members can reach staff Monday to Friday 8am to 9pm and Saturday 8am to 4pm.

What does the Wellness Add-On include?

One daily session each of Vitamin D Lounge, InBody Scanner, Red Light Therapy, BioCharger, CureWave Laser, High-Intensity PEMF Mat and LightWave Therapy, for $59.95 a month on top of Basic.

Is the gym crowded?

Rarely. Access is members-only, and the 15,000 sq. ft. floor is divided into different rooms.

Is it a chain?

No. Tangible Wellness is locally owned and has been operating for over 10 years.

Are the trainers on staff?

They are independent trainers who work out of the gym, each with their own rates.
