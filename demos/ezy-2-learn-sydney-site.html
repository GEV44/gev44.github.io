Quick answers

Or ask the assistant in the corner.

How long does it usually take to learn to drive?

Since the lessons are so focused, most people need fewer lessons than they expect. The team makes a personalised plan to get you ready for your test as quickly as possible, with no rushing.

What kind of car will I learn in?

A modern, comfortable car, automatic or manual, with dual controls for safety. The cars carry a 5-star ANCAP rating.

Is it better to learn in an automatic or a manual car?

It depends on your goal. Automatic is usually easier and faster to learn. Manual gives you more options for future cars but needs more focused practice and time.

What happens if I need to reschedule?

The school asks for 24 hours' notice if you need to change your booking.

Can I book a female instructor?

Yes. Experienced female instructors are available for learners of every level.

Do you help with the NSW driving test?

Yes: test-route practice, parking, hazard awareness and reverse parking, plus car hire for test day.

Where will my lessons take place?

Across Western Sydney and beyond, from Liverpool, Campbelltown and Oran Park to Bankstown, Campsie and Lakemba. You will learn the exact skills the examiner looks for.

Why choose EZY 2 LEARN?

Experienced instructors, safe driving techniques, personalised training and driving test preparation, to help learner drivers build confidence and pass their NSW driving test.

Do you offer both automatic and manual lessons?

Yes. Whether you are a beginner or preparing for your NSW driving test, qualified instructors will help you develop the skills to drive safely and confidently.

Do you offer test-day car hire?

Yes. A modern, dual-controlled car for your actual driving test, with a 45-minute lesson, so you skip the vehicle stress.
