The NSW logbook

Learner licence holders under 25 who do not hold an international licence are recommended to log:

120hours of driving
20of them at night
5–15professional lessons on average

How many lessons you need depends on your experience and confidence. Professional lessons sit alongside supervised practice with a parent or guardian, and Road Vibes can help you fill up the logbook.

Quick answers

Do you teach manual?

No. Road Vibes currently provides lessons in automatic cars only.

Is there a female instructor?

Yes. Check the instructor list, or say you prefer one when you enquire.

What is in the basic / 10-hour package?

Vehicle control, turning practice, roundabouts, traffic lights, road driving, lane changing and parking.

What does the pre-test assessment include?

Driving around the test route, then a mock test and assessment.

How do I book a driving test?

Follow the instructions on the website, or contact the admin team on 02 3816 0949.

How do I contact my instructor?

Instructors cannot be contacted directly. Message the admin team through chat or call 02 3816 0949.

How much do you charge?

Pricing varies based on the suburb and the instructor you choose. The Pricing page has the details, or the team can confirm it for your suburb.

How many lessons do I need before the NSW driving test?

It depends on your driving experience and confidence. On average most learners take 5 to 15 professional lessons alongside supervised practice with a parent or guardian, and the recommended logbook is 120 hours including 20 at night for learners under 25 without an international licence.

What services does Road Vibes offer?

Driving lessons, test preparation lessons, international licence conversion lessons, car hire for the driving test, instructor training, dual-brake car hire, car roof signs, large blind spot mirrors and a booking platform website for driving schools.
