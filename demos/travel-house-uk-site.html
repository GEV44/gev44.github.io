About Travelhouse UK

A global network, a London team

Travelhouse UK says its aim is to be the ultimate source of unsurpassed quality and unmatched value for global destinations and worldwide airlines, for European consumers and vendors.

It is part of a global distribution network that connects airlines and hotels for the sales promotion of cheap flights from the UK. Customers can choose from many airlines and stay anywhere from five-star deluxe hotels to cheap bed and breakfasts, with bookings, schedule changes, cancellations and payments handled online or through an agent.

Umrah packages

Hotels, transport and flights together

Travelhouse UK offers Umrah packages for 2026 from London, Birmingham, Manchester and Glasgow and many other UK airports. Each package includes hotels, transport, direct or indirect return flights, and after-sales guidance.

The site lists 3 star, 4 star and 5 star options in Makkah and Madinah, including Emaar and Pullman Zamzam hotels. Use the package enquiry or the assistant to ask for the current options for your dates.

Questions

Quick answers

Is Travelhouse UK genuine?

It is a London-based travel agency. All bookings are financially protected through ATOL (3517) and ABTA P7124, and the registered office is 12 Stephen Mews, London W1T 1AH.

What if my flight is cancelled?

You can have a refund, or rebook or reroute with the same airline. The site advises keeping the ticket for a date change without penalties, or exchanging it for a travel voucher valid for up to one year.

Can I choose meals and seats?

Yes where the airline allows it, including disability aids. Agents can also forward hotel requests such as early check-in, late check-out and extra bedding.

Can I change the date?

One free date-change voucher comes with all new bookings, terms and conditions apply, and airline change policies are honoured.

How long has the agency been trading?

The FAQ says it has been operating for more than two decades.

Can I book through an agent or only online?

Both. Flights and all-inclusive holidays can be booked on the official website or directly through travel agents.

Do you only sell flights?

No. Besides cheap flights from the UK there are all-inclusive holidays, city breaks, hotels and Umrah packages.

What does an Umrah package include?

Hotels and accommodation, transportation, direct or indirect return flights and after-sales guidance, with 3, 4 and 5 star options in Makkah and Madinah.
