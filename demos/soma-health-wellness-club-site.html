How we work with you

It starts with
how you move.

Before any program is written, the coaches look at how your body moves, recovers and performs, then turn the data into a weekly plan that keeps being measured and refined.

  1. Posture + movement analysisHow your body moves, where it overworks, underperforms or overcompensates, so training is purposeful and injury-aware.
  2. Body assessment + health metricsScans for lean mass, fat, hydration, bone density and visceral fat, giving an accurate baseline to customise your program.
  3. Functional strength + stability testingCore, joint and muscle control tested to find what to strengthen from the inside out.
  4. Training plan + app accessA realistic, evolving plan, plus app access for tracking, coaching support, nutrition tips, sleep and heart rate monitoring.

Group sessions

Find your flow,
strength and balance.

Small groups, expert instructors and a studio built for reformer work. Not sure where to begin? New or returning members are pointed to Foundations Reformer Pilates or Yoga.

  • Foundations Reformer Pilates

    Open level

    Alignment, control and breath on ALLEGRO 2 reformers. A full-body, low-impact class for beginners through to experienced clients, with options to progress or regress.

  • Intermediate Reformer Pilates

    Confident reformer clients

    More complex, flowing sequences, deeper core and glute engagement, and a steady build in intensity and endurance.

  • Strength

    Open level

    High-intensity strength combining floor weights with reformer work: 15 minutes of weight training, then slower, heavier reformer sequences for legs, glutes, core, back and upper body.

  • Stretch + Flow

    Recovery and longevity

    Breathwork, stretching and mobility to counteract long hours of sitting: posture, hips, spine and shoulders, and key stabilisers that protect the lower back.
