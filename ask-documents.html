    How this works

    Retrieval, ranked passages, cited answer

    The page contains no language model. It finds the passages that match the question and quotes them, so it cannot say anything the documents do not say.

    01

    Retrieval

    The documents are cut into passages of roughly 80 to 150 words, each keeping its section title. The question and the passages are lowercased, stripped of common words and reduced to word stems, so "refund" matches "refunds" and "reimbursed".

    02

    Ranked passages

    Each passage gets a BM25 score: rare words count for more, repeated words count for less, and long passages are not favoured. The best passages are kept in order.

    03

    Answer with citations

    The most relevant sentences from the top passages are quoted, each followed by a number that points to its source passage below. When the match is weak, the page says so instead of guessing.

    What the production version adds

    Beyond keyword matching

    This page shows the principle. A delivered assistant is built around your documents and your channels.

    Better answers

    • Embeddings and reranking, so questions are matched by meaning and not only by shared words.
    • A language model that writes the answer only from the cited passages, with a fallback to the plain passages.
    • An evaluation set of real questions, run on every change, so quality is measured and not assumed.

    Better reach

    • PDF, DOCX and HTML ingestion with a scheduled refresh when documents change.
    • Telegram, WhatsApp or a widget on your website.
    • Handover to a person when the assistant is not sure, with the conversation attached.
